## The defect

`Realized 𝓘 S Φ` holds when `Φ = Σᵢ conjChannel (Kᵢ)` for finitely many operators `Kᵢ` each
admissible in `𝓘`. Nothing connects the `Kᵢ`: they need not be the branches of one instrument.
`IsGenInstrument` asks each branch of a family to be realized and the aggregate trace to be
preserved, and `ImplementationGenerated` promotes that into availability. The flow-extension
deferral found what this admits: if a post-selected contraction `c • U`, `0 < |c| < 1`, `U`
unitary, is admissible, then `N` identical branches `(1/√N) • U` are admissible once
`N ≥ 1/|c|²`, and their sum is `conjChannel U`, trace preserving. A branch of one instrument is
taken alone, its companion branches discarded, and replicated until the probability mass is
unity. No physical instrument does that. The defect is structural: it is in the realization
primitive, and it is promoted by `ImplementationGenerated` into every implementation-local
theory.

The owner decision (`FLOW-EXTENSION-AUDIT.md`, the deferral): branch-wise realization is
replaced by a notion in which the branches of a realized family arise together as the outcomes
of one admissible extended implementation, defined only after the tests below are fixed, with
the replicated post-selection as the countercontrol and with a proof, not an assumption, of
whether provenance removes the replication.

## The replacement, as an object to be defined

Fixed now, before the definition is written, so that the tests are not shaped to it:

**The primitive.** At level `n`, on the carrier `S × Fin n`, one *step* of an observer is: attach
a fresh ancilla `Fin m` in the uniform state (the theory's own preparation primitive,
`uniformAttach`, which is available in every finite operational theory), apply one admissible
operator `K` of the class on `(S × Fin n) × Fin m`, read the fresh ancilla out with the native
readout, and report the outcome through a coarse-graining `f : Fin m → O`. Its branches are the
blocks `(1/√m) • ancBlock K k e` for the readout value `k` and the uniformly weighted input `e`,
grouped by `f k`. A family is *instrument-realized* when it is obtained from steps by the
operations every finite operational theory performs on families: sequential composition with
outcome-dependent continuation (the observer chooses the next step from the outcome, the theory's
`availExt_bind`), coarse-graining of outcomes, and the discard of a uniformly attached ancilla.
`InstrumentRealized 𝓘 n O F` is therefore to be an inductive predicate whose constructors are the
step, the native readout, coarse-graining, sequential composition and discard, and nothing else;
in particular no constructor takes an arbitrary finite sum of admissible operators. The
generated theory `instTheory 𝓘` has `availExt n O F := InstrumentRealized 𝓘 n O F`.

**What provenance buys, stated as conjectures for the tests.** In a step whose family is trace
preserving, the admissible `K` is an isometry: the aggregate `(1/m) Σ_{k,e} Bₖₑ† Bₖₑ` is the
ancilla partial trace of `K†K` divided by `m`, which is the identity only when `K†K = 1`, because
the partial trace of the positive operator `1 − K†K` vanishes only when it does. So the
non-isometric elements of a class, the projectors, blocks and attenuated operators the
`Architecture` closure adds, never appear as the operator of a step; they appear only as branches.
A one-outcome family `conjChannel U`, `U` unitary, is instrument-realized only when its branches
are all proportional to `U` (the extremality of a unitary conjugation among completely positive
maps), and each branch is a compression of an admissible isometry.

**The structural invariant for the countercontrol.** For a class generated from permutation
matrices and the gate flows of a layer (the flow-extension object), every element is
`c • E† W F` with `|c| ≤ 1`, `W` a unitary on some finite carrier fixing the all-ones vector, and
`E`, `F` embeddings of the carrier as index subsets: the generators are of this form, and the form
is closed under products (two compressions compose to one compression of a permutation-conjugated
product on a disjoint union), scalars, projectors (blocks of a permutation), blocks, spectators,
relabelling and the adjoint. An isometric compression `E† W F` of a unitary `W` fixing `𝟙` maps
`𝟙` to `𝟙`, so every isometry in the class fixes `𝟙` up to a global phase, and the transition
flow on a carrier with three or more points does not.

## The tests, each with its admissible outcomes

**T1. Soundness.** `InstrumentRealized 𝓘 n O F → ∀ a, Realized 𝓘 (S × Fin n) (F a)`, for an
architecture, by induction on the constructors: a step's branches are contractive blocks of an
admissible operator, and the other constructors are the existing `realized_*` closure lemmas.
Admissible outcomes: the theorem `realized_of_instrumentRealized`, or a constructor whose
branches are not realized, recorded.

**T2. The converse fails.** The replicated post-selection is the preregistered countercontrol,
in the minimal flow extension `flowClass` of `FLOW-EXTENSION-AUDIT.md`: the family
`fun _ : Unit => conjChannel (flow (transition a b) t)` on a carrier with three or more points,
at a time `t` with `e^{−it} ≠ 1`, is branch-realized by `flowClass` (the gadget block `c • flow`
and its contractive replication) and is not instrument-realized. Admissible outcomes: the
theorem `flow_realized_not_instrumentRealized`, from the structural invariant above; or a proof
that the family is instrument-realized, which would mean provenance does not remove the
replication and the defect lies elsewhere, recorded as such. The invariant is to be proved, not
assumed: `flowClass_compression` (every element of the class is a contractive compression of a
`𝟙`-fixing unitary), `isometry_fixes_ones` (an isometric such compression fixes `𝟙`), and the
extremality lemma `unitary_conj_branches` (every branch of a decomposition of a unitary
conjugation is proportional to the unitary).

**T3. The generated theory exists.** `instTheory 𝓘` is a `FiniteOperationalTheory` for every
class that is an architecture, context-stable and label-invariant: identity (a step with `K = 1`
and one outcome), coarse-graining, sequential composition, preparation (the uniform attach
followed by an available operation, as now), readout (the native local Lüders readout of the
level ancilla, obtained as a step whose operator copies the level value into the fresh ancilla,
a permutation), and discard, each a constructor or derived from one. Admissible outcomes: the
structure `instTheory` with every field proved, or a field that the constructors do not supply,
recorded with the constructor it would need. Context stability and label invariance are expected
to be consumed by sequential composition, which acts on the enlarged carrier; that the
architecture's own closures are consumed at all is part of what the test records.

**T4. Survival.** Every theorem of the implementation-locality stack is re-established for the
new primitive or recorded as needing a stronger hypothesis: `validity_of_implementationLocality`
(instrument-realized families are completely positive), `observationalIndependence_of_implementationLocality`
(the spectator extension of an instrument-realized family is instrument-realized, from context
stability), `implementationLocality_of_qm` (exact quantum mechanics is instrument-generated by the
full class: every Kraus instrument is a step of one isometry on a pure ancilla, and a pure ancilla
is obtained from the uniform one by reading it and relabelling, a permutation, by
feed-forward), `countermodel_not_implementationGenerated`, `carrier_general_oiPlusLocal`,
`ReversibleImplementationLocality` and `oiPlusMin_iff_qm`, `DerivedOI` and `SourcedOI`, and the
theorems of `substratumTheory` and `permTheory` that the substratum-interface audit rests on
(`substratumTheory_derivedOI`, `permTheory_sourcedOI`, `bijectionLevel_not_phasesAvailable`,
`configurationLevel_availExt_le`, `routeB_target`). Admissible outcomes, recorded per theorem:
unchanged statement and re-proved; unchanged statement with an added hypothesis, named; or a
theorem that depended on free branch recombination and does not survive, named with the reason.
The expectation, fixed in advance: every survives, since the branches each of them uses are
those of one isometry, and the one theorem whose proof consumed the freedom is the scalar-hull
regression, which is stated for `Realized` and is not migrated.

**T5. The flow endpoint is not settled here.** No theorem of this round asserts or refutes
`SourcedOI ∧ LayerFlowExecutable → PhaseFreeRichness`; the countercontrol of T2 is a statement
about one family in one class, and the endpoint is re-preregistered after this round from the
repaired main. Admissible outcome: absence of any such theorem, guarded.

**T6. Guards.** `R7-INST` pins the inductive definition verbatim, the absence of a sum
constructor, the soundness theorem, the countercontrol, the survival record and this note, and
rejects any statement that the flow endpoint is settled.
