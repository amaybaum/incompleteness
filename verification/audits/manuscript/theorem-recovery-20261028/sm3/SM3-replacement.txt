**Theorem 3.** *Let a site set carry a parity grading $\varepsilon_i\in\{+1,-1\}$, with coefficients in an associative unital ring having no additive 2-torsion ($2a=0\Rightarrow a=0$). Fix a hopping kernel $D$ whose entries vanish between sites of equal parity. For the first-order operator with arbitrary onsite coefficients $A_{ij}=D_{ij}+\delta_{ij}c_i$, exact $\varepsilon$-chirality, $A_{ij}\varepsilon_j+\varepsilon_iA_{ij}=0$ for every $i,j$, is equivalent to $c_i=0$ for every site. If both parity sectors occur and $c_i=m\varepsilon_i+p_0$ has uniform coefficients, this is equivalent to $m=p_0=0$.*

*Proof.* For equal parities, the chiral entry equation is twice an invertible sign times $A_{ij}$, so no additive 2-torsion forces $A_{ij}=0$. For opposite parities it vanishes identically. Thus chirality is equivalent to opposite-parity support. Since $D$ already has that support, its diagonal vanishes and the only new equal-parity entries are the onsite $c_i$, proving both directions. For uniform coefficients, the two parity sectors give $m+p_0=0$ and $-m+p_0=0$; hence $2m=0$, so $m=p_0=0$. Conversely zero coefficients recover the odd hopping kernel. (kernel: `onsite_zero_of_chiral`, `onsite_chiral_of_zero`; coefficient directions: `scalar_center_coefficients_zero_of_chiral`, `scalar_center_chiral_of_coefficients_zero`; `OIBridge/ChiralCenterRecovery.lean`.) $\square$

The operator-level extension classifies every $\varepsilon$-even perturbation $C$, including same-parity offsite terms: if $\varepsilon^2=I$, $D\varepsilon=-\varepsilon D$ and $C\varepsilon=\varepsilon C$, then $\{D+C,\varepsilon\}=0$ is equivalent to $C=0$ under the same no-2-torsion assumption. (kernel: `even_perturbation_zero_of_chiral`, `chiral_of_even_perturbation_zero`.) The first-order onsite statement above therefore follows from a broader grading obstruction, rather than an assumed independence of mass coefficients.
