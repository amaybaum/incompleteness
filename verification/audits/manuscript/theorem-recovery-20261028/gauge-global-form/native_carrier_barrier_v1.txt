NATIVE END-CARRIER BARRIER TO THE CONDITIONAL CENTRAL QUOTIENT
Supplement v1; 2026-10-28. The frozen global-form candidate is unchanged.

Let V=V3 direct-sum V2 direct-sum V1 and G0=U(3)xU(2)xU(1) act in its
native block-diagonal representation g=diag(C,D,t). Consider its action on
the full End(V) by X -> g X g^-1. Its entire kernel is

  Delta={(c I3,c I2,c):c in U(1)}.

Proof, forward direction. If g X g^-1=X for every X, then g commutes with
each diagonal matrix unit E_ii. The equality g E_ii=E_ii g makes all
off-diagonal entries of g zero. Write g=diag(c1,...,c6). Commutation with
E_ij gives ci=cj for every i,j, hence g=c I6. This lies in G0 exactly as
the displayed Delta element. Backward direction: a scalar commutes with
every X. Thus both directions are explicit; no assumption that the
initial kernel element is scalar or central was needed.

Consequently the faithful image of G0 on the FULL native End(V) is G0/Delta.
Its Lie algebra is su(3)+su(2)+u(1)^2, not the Standard Model algebra.
If only the block-diagonal End(V3)+End(V2)+End(V1) modes are retained, the
kernel instead contains all three independent central circles; their
quotient gives PU(3)xPU(2). Which modes and gauge connections survive is
therefore essential, not determined by the block sizes alone.

The conditional quotient in global_form_proof_v1.txt uses

  K={(cI3,dI2,c^2/d)}={(cI3,c u I2,c/u)}.

Its additional circle h(u)=(I3,u I2,u^-1) does NOT act trivially on the
full native carrier. On the cross-Hom spaces its actions include

  Hom(V2,V3): u^-1,    Hom(V1,V3): u,    Hom(V1,V2): u^2.

For any u!=1 the first action is nontrivial. Thus K cannot be the kernel
of this unchanged conjugation action. Amplitude normalization of the
common phase gives Delta; reaching K requires another operational action,
mode selection/dressing, or a justified further redundancy. This is an
exact representation obstruction, not a proof that no observer action
can realize the recovered group.

The full charged-family representation supplies one explicit alternative
action rho composed with F, whose kernel is K because rho is faithful on
H=S(U3xU2). For example, its G0 transformation laws are

  Q:   t (det C)^-1 (C tensor D),
  dR:  t^-2(det C)(det D)^-1 C,
  uR:  t^4(det C)^-3(det D)^2 C,
  L:   t^-3(det C)^2(det D)^-2 D,
  eR:  t^-6(det C)^4(det D)^-3,
  H:   t^3(det C)^-2(det D) D.

These follow by substituting F into the intrinsic H representations; all
determinants are ordinary one-dimensional characters. They are genuine
representations made from determinant lines, native fundamental factors
and singlet characters. They need not occur in the raw End(V): in
particular its SU(3)xSU(2)-singlet subspace consists of the three block
identities, each neutral under all central phases, and contains no
charged eR with the last-but-one character. Enlarging to tensor/exterior
powers can supply determinant characters algebraically; identifying such
a representation with a light physical chiral fermion remains a separate
observer/matter construction. No such dynamics is established here.

This supplement prevents the positive F theorem from being interpreted
as a quotient already realized by the full native link/conjugation
carrier. It preserves the conditional theorem and identifies the next
precise physical recovery obligation.

The companion control uses exact matrix-unit conjugation on all 559,872
elements of a finite noncentral monomial subgroup of G0 (sixth roots),
checks the entire kernel, and checks all K elements on the same grid.
It is finite verification, not a formal proof of the continuous theorem.
