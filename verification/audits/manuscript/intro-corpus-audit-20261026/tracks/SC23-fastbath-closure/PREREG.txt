    for a visible law P on {0,1}^{K+1},
    delta_k := max TV( P(X_{k+1} | X_{<k}=p, X_k=c), P(X_{k+1} | X_{<k}=p', X_k=c) )
    over p != p' (length k) and c with both histories of positive probability.

 D2 (lemma inequality, reading R1): delta_k <= delta_0 * r^(k-1), r := e^{-tau_S/tau_B}, with delta_0

 D3 Model E(d,r) (eroding delay register; coarse-grained channel level):
    visible x_t in {0,1}; hidden register cells c_1..c_d in {0,1}.
    init: x_0 uniform, cells iid uniform, independent.
    step t -> t+1: (i) erosion: each cell independently kept w.p. r, else replaced by a fresh
    uniform bit (channel E_r: uniform-invariant, TV contraction coefficient exactly r per cell —
    every stored record eroded uniformly by r per step); (ii) read: x_{t+1} := c_d;
    (iii) shift/write: c_{i+1} := c_i (i<d), c_1 := x_{t+1}.
    [Note: with this write order, at time t the register holds x_t, x_{t-1}, ..., x_{t-d+1}.]
 D4 M_k := I(X_{<k}; X_{k+1} | X_k) in bits.
 D5 eps_coarse(t) := TV(law of the read cell c_d after erosion at coupling t+1 given the full
    visible record, uniform); eps_full(t) := same for the full register (all d cells).
