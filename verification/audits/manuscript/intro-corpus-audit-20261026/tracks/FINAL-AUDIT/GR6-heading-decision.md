# GR §6 heading: documented no-change decision (main `55e3217b`)

**Finding.** The citations review of #918 noted that GR §6's heading, "Dissolution of the Cosmological Constant
Problem", is stronger than the status the Introduction gives the application ("proposal, not a resolution").

**Owner ruling (amaybaum, 2026-10-27).** "Retain the ambitious heading if the section clearly separates demonstrated
mathematical results from the proposed cosmological resolution. Record a documented no-change decision if the body
already makes that distinction."

**Check, against `papers/GR.md` at `55e3217b`.** The body makes the distinction.
- §6.2 has a paragraph headed "Descriptive distinction established; dynamical decoupling conditional on G3" (:358). It
  sets out what is shown and what is not:
  - Transition data are invariant under Ω_eff → Ω_eff + CI.
  - Two gravitational completions can share every observer-level datum while differing by ρ_C g_μν.
  - "Invisibility to quantum mechanics is not decoupling from gravity; the latter is a coupling theorem and is what G3
    names as open."
  - "Accordingly the descriptive-level distinction is established and the dissolution is conditional."
- The classical-vacuum-energy paragraph and §6.3 carry the same condition: "Conditional on the coupling step, the
  framework removes the need for the 122-digit cancellation", and "conditional on the coupling step (§6.2)".

**Decision.** No change to the heading.

**Related item, not part of this decision.** §6.3 (:368) says the dS observer cancellation is "structurally forced",
and that the type II entropy is "pinned to" log|G_sub|. That is the same class of claim as Main §4.4 (finding D-12), so
it is recorded as a GR mirror site for SC-28 and checked there.
