**3. Revival is still stated as a "prediction" (ch15 :116, :128, :122, :18, plus FULL).**
- **Current text:** at :116, "The framework's prediction is that at the critical chain length, where the chain's return also revives distinguishability, the qubit's $T_2$ … oscillatory behavior with partial revivals". :128 is similar.

**4. GR :591 is scoped wrongly.**
- **Current text:** "The recurrence-cycle P-indivisibility of [Main §2.3] needs, with a full-support visible prior, only that some rooted visible map on the constraint surface is not a permutation, and it does not extend to accessible windows. The constraints correlate interior and exterior data, so the sectors cannot be decoupled entirely; the constraint surface thus enforces (C1) non-perturbatively."
- **First problem: the product structure.** Main's theorem and the Lean `RootedRealization` are both stated on a product $\mathcal C_V\times\mathcal C_H$, with one hidden prior shared by every root. $\Gamma_{\rm phys}$ is not a product. The argument does extend (φ restricted to an invariant finite set has finite order; with a prior per root's fiber, $\Gamma_L=I$ still holds and the lemma applies), but neither Main nor the kernel states that.
- **Second problem: the full-support prior.** It is misplaced. The indivisibility needs no visible prior, because the rows of zero-probability roots are kept. Full support enters only in the equivalence with $M_k>0$.
- **Third problem: correlation is not coupling.** "Correlate data ⇒ (C1)" does not follow. A product dynamics $\varphi_V\times\varphi_H$ restricted to a correlated invariant surface has correlated data and no hidden-to-visible influence. Main :74 grounds C1 at the horizon in the ADM boundary Hamiltonian, not in correlation.
- **Replacement:** "The recurrence-cycle P-indivisibility of [Main §2.3] carries over to the constraint surface, with the hidden prior taken on each root's fiber: it needs only that some rooted visible map is not a permutation, and it does not extend to accessible windows. (C1) holds non-perturbatively through the ADM boundary Hamiltonian, which couples interior and exterior evolution [Main §1.3]."

**6. SM :112 weakens a result that is easy to prove.**
- **Current text:** "…applies to a visible partition of this ring wherever its rooted visible map is not a permutation; no such partition is exhibited here."
- **Problem.** A suitable partition is immediate. Take the visible sector to be one site's two time slices $(x_0(t-1),x_0(t))$. Then $x_0(t+1)=x_{L-1}(t)+x_{R(0)}(t)-x_0(t-1)$, and for $L\ge4$, $R(0)\in\{1,2\}\neq L-1$. Under the uniform hidden prior that row is uniform over $\mathbb Z_q$, so the one-step map is not a permutation.
- **Check.** I ran an exact enumeration at $(L,q)=(4,2),(4,3),(5,2)$. It confirmed the dynamics is bijective and that $\Gamma_1$ is not a permutation; the uniform rows were 1/2 or 1/3. The owner rule treats unnecessary weakening as a defect.
- **Replacement:** "With the visible sector one site's two time slices, the update adds the hidden value $x_{L-1}(t)$, uniform under the uniform hidden prior, so the one-step visible map is not a permutation and the recurrence-cycle P-indivisibility of [Main §2.3] holds for any L ≥ 4 and q ≥ 2; it is not a statement about accessible windows."
