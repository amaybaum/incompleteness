### 1. Mathematics, independently re-checked with exact arithmetic under `python3 -I -B`

- **(2) Landau.** ln g(n) ~ √(n ln n) (Landau 1903) is correct, and so is "no permutation has order near eⁿ".
- **(3) Typical order.** ln ord = (1+o(1))·½ln²n in probability (Erdős–Turán I, 1965). (ln ord − ½ln²n)/√(⅓ln³n) → N(0,1) (Erdős–Turán III, 1967). Both are correct, and both are stated in ch18 as holding only under the uniform model.

  - In sampled D=3 cases, q = 2, 3, 5 reach 7, 26, 124 (attained), while q = 4 and q = 6 reach only 14 and 182. This is consistent with the composite-q caveat.
