  - lcm(1..n) ≈ eⁿ is not Landau's function. Landau's g(n) = e^{(1+o(1))√(n ln n)} is the maximum order of any permutation. The typical order of a random permutation is e^{(1/2+o(1))(ln n)²} (Erdős–Turán).
  - With |S| ~ e^{10^122}, the typical order is about e^{10^244/2} and the maximum about exp(10^61·e^{5×10^121}). Neither is e^{e^{10^122}}.
