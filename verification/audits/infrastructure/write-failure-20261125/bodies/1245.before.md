## Question

GR §3.2's boundary-only dependence lemma (`papers/GR.md:112–134`) needs step (ii)'s conclusion: the deep sector $D$ has negligible effect on the visible–boundary dynamics for $t \ll \tau_B$. #1244 item 3 found that step (ii) is not established as written. It infers small *influence* from small *motion*, and an exactly frozen $D$ can still drive $B$. Five of seven readings of $\tau_B$ have explicit counterexamples.

The repair is the posited hypothesis **H-BD**: $D$'s update permutes $\mathcal C_D$, and for each $(v,b)$ the fraction of deep configurations $d$ on which the $V\times B$ update differs from a fixed reference update $\varphi^{VB}$ is at most $\tau_S/\tau_{BD}$. Then
$$\textstyle\sum_j |T_{ij}(t)-T^{(B)}_{ij}(t)| \le 2t/\tau_{BD}.$$

**Can H-BD, or a statement strong enough to carry GR §3.2's conclusion, be derived from OI's existing premises, from the embedded-observer structure, or from independently motivated physics?** If it cannot, what is the weakest additional premise that does the job, and is that premise hard to vary (§A.29)?

## Candidate routes (to be tested, none adopted)

1. **Causal structure.** With finite propagation speed and nearest-neighbour coupling (A3), $D$ cannot reach $V$ until influence crosses $B$. This gives an exact result for a boundary layer of depth about $ct$ (one cell per step at $c$). Question: can the thin, area-counted boundary of GR's mode count (GR:98, :104) be reconciled with that thickness?
2. **Typicality and measure.** Under the canonical (maximal-entropy) measure, is the fraction of $d$ that changes the $V\times B$ update typically small? For which dynamics, and with what bound?
3. **Observer-relative partition.** Is the $B/D$ split itself defined so that $D$'s influence on $V$ is small, for example by choosing $B$ as everything that influences $V$ within the window? Is that circular?
4. **Slow-bath structure (C2) and the meaning of $\tau_B$.** Main:74 defines $\tau_B$ as a mixing time; Main:340 adds a weak-coupling condition. Can a restated (C2) imply the influence bound, and what does it cost Main?
5. **Horizon physics.** Can de Sitter / horizon thermodynamics (GR S1, $\tau_B \sim H^{-1}$) fix $\tau_{BD}$ independently of the $10^{-32}$ figure?

## Success criteria
- **Derivation:** H-BD, or a sufficient substitute, proved on paper from stated premises, with every hypothesis named and labelled, and independently reviewed.
- **Failing that:** the weakest premise that carries GR §3.2, with its §A.29 preserved list, an independent fix of $\tau_{BD}$, and an independent consequence.
- **Either way:** if $\tau_{BD}$ can only be chosen to give $10^{-32}$, that is recorded as an ad hoc fit.
