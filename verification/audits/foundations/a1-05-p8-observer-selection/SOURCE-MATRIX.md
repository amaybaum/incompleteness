## Source and assumption boundaries

A1 concerns finiteness of the **total** configuration space. Finite record resolution and finite reading sets are separate. On a site set of arbitrary cardinality the stated finite-input dependency rule gives a finite cone for each finite readout and finite horizon. This is a pointwise implication about the update. A comparison of statistical records also needs the initial law on that cone, including correlations relevant to the declaration of the records. The finite periodic, finite-alphabet A4 model still has finitely many total configurations. Removing a redundant premise in that class leaves its finiteness intact. Its bound concerns the process generated by iterating the one-step compression. Recurrence and the finite permutation's spectrum pertain to the exact model. The coverage ledger lists the §4.6 theorem and both corollaries as kernel GAP. The audit r9 variants are proposals. ### P8-M · Invariant probability and compressed Koopman dynamics

For the forward operator step, supply a measurable update $\varphi$ and a normalized probability $\mu$ with $\varphi_*\mu=\mu$. Use the same law for $L^2(\mu)$ and $P_\Sigma=\mathbb E_\mu[\ \cdot\mid\mathcal F_\Sigma]$, the projection onto the declared local observables. The Koopman pullback $Uf=f\circ\varphi$ is an isometry by invariance. $\mathcal K=P_\Sigma U P_\Sigma$ is a mean-preserving contraction. This step requires no inverse. For a finite-support readout, use the declared locally finite dependence cone covering its writes and reads. The cone locates causal influence. The reference law governs statistical correlations already present. [Main §3.3][cone] · [Main §4.6][main-p8] · [A1 recovery matrix][matrix-p8]

State (EM) at the norm and rate the inference uses. For instance, an operator estimate on the centred local observables of the form $\lVert\mathcal K^n\rVert\leq C e^{-n\Delta t/\tau_{\rm mix}}$ bounds $|\langle f,\mathcal K^n g\rangle_\mu|$ by $C e^{-n\Delta t/\tau_{\rm mix}}\lVert f\rVert_2\lVert g\rVert_2$ for centred $f,g$ in its scope. A gap quoted without its operative rate and constants has yet to provide that estimate. The printed memory-time bound is $\tau_B(V)\leq\tau_{\rm mix}(\Sigma_{\rm loc}(V))+O(\varepsilon)$. Its transfer requires a record threshold and an error estimate. The timescale corollary needs the bound, including that error, to lie in the fast regime relative to $\tau_S$. The erasure lemma additionally requires uniform erosion of the relevant records. [Main §4.6][main-p8] · [Main §2.3][erasure] · [A1 recovery matrix][matrix-p8]

## Smallest future checks and negative controls

Fix one existing update and readout, one state specified independently of the desired result, one accessible window, and two distinct histories $h,h'$ with common endpoint $X_k=x$ and masses bounded below by a declared $p_\star>0$. Use one later binary read event $A$. Order $k=2$ is the first order covered by the fast-bath gap estimate. A single event above tolerance can falsify a uniform gap bound. Passing one pair establishes only that comparison.

The shift-register control distinguishes update speed from erosion. It gives a maximal order-2 gap with rapid circulation, and pushing the return outside the window leaves an i.i.d. visible law despite storage. Main's coin gives the independent storage-without-readback control. The fast-bath lemma gives no order-1 estimate and permits nonzero gaps at finite rate ratios. The lower bound on $M_k$ cannot turn a gap upper bound into an information upper bound. Slower equilibria, protected modes and the de Sitter thermal timescale bound the scope of the fast-mixing argument. [Main C2][c2] · [Main §2.3][erasure] · [Main §4.6][main-p8]

The reversal and preparation controls remain those of ARROW-1. A stationary visible law may be chiral in its transitions even though its block entropies and $M_k$ are reversal-invariant. The CNOT example satisfies GR Theorem A.6 and decreases the sum of sector entropies between its first and second steps. Exact de Sitter has constant apparent-horizon area. The measurement convention of preparing future states and recording past outcomes inherits the orientation of the memory protocol. The two-sided entropy inequality and the conditional clock leave the physical time direction, the generalized second law and alignment open. [ARROW-1 recovery][arrow] · [Substratum §5.4][sub-arrow]

**Disposition.** The tests above are specifications for future independent checks. The current evidence supports the conditional P8-M operator step and preserves the finite-model and finite-law results. The normalized physical state remains the first missing ingredient on both paths. Neither outcome changes the physical-source gate.
