### Findings

- **B1 (factual: byte offset).** "At the same byte offset (2305, line 37)" should read byte offset
  2332 (character offset 2305). The design and the tool work in bytes, so the error matters.
- **B2 (factual: not append-only).** The 4-line insertion into the lessons register must be stated.
  The claim "no byte before them changed" must be corrected, as must "append-only" in the table. The
  correct statement is that every change lies at or after H byte 46677, wholly after D296's span.
- **B3 (workflow already has a role in the frozen plan).** PREREG at P, §31.12.7, already defines W =
  `413b0bb8` as the **workflow base** that decides how trials are checked. `preserve_check.py`
  enforces that the workflow blob at a dispatch's head equals W's. B treats the file as an
  unclassified side change and proposes a new `trial-workflow` record without citing this. The
  classification is reasonable: DESIGN-v5.1 sec. 13.1 names the trial workflow as an example class.
  But the record should cite §31.12.7 as the reason, and pin the blob. Under sec. 16.2,
  `roles.side_change_classes` entries are `{path, blob_sha256}`, and B's proposed records carry no
  blob. The values are: workflow at W and at M sha256
  f8c36855cf6e61891cbbd41011714af503b094b9dd63065ac042304817621646 (git blob bca44ac0); at H 5c767c9f...e747.
  `.claude/settings.json` at M has sha256 ad4130d0d5f61f0a2807e488706c925cfc6766b9a4cefc2cf04612bb4bb65b9b
  (git blob b7f9b0a6).
- **B4 (option 1 is underspecified against sec. 13.1 and against c.7093128173).** Option 1 re-resolves
  "the ledger base for AGENTS.md" to the blob at the new freeze's `main`. Under DESIGN-v5.1 sec. 13.1,
  base drift is defined by paths that changed between `H` and `M`, not by a ledger's per-file base
  hash. The sec. 13.1 invariants also require that every `base_sha256` equal **H's** blob and that X
  be a single-parent child of H. Re-basing one file's `base_sha256` while H stays `53c6601a` would
  therefore violate the invariant and would not remove the drift. Option 1 is coherent with sec. 13
  only if the scientific base itself moves to a later `main` commit H′. That move then carries all of
  the following:
  - `roles.scientific_base` = H′;
  - the HP segment E..H extended to E..H′ (sec. 3, sec. 16.6: segments must end at
    `scientific_base`). The added commits change no corpus path, which should be shown;
  - every ledger `base_sha256` re-verified at H′, of which only AGENTS.md's changes;
  - X, T0′ and T1′ rebuilt as children of H′ (the route reruns trials anyway);
  - R's tree re-pinned at H′;
  - the hard-coded H checks in the plan's checkers (for example `preserve_check.py` line 1245),
    which are replaced in the amendment;
  - the sec. 13.4 sequencing safeguard.

  c.7093128173 also states "No change to the ledger base H ... is authorized" and describes H as
  the "immutable scientific base". Option 1 therefore needs the owner's explicit decision on that
  point. The in-principle approval of decision 8 may be that decision, but it should be recorded as
  such (see A, correction 4).
- **B5 (option 2 misreads sec. 13.3).** Sec. 13.3's foreseeability restriction applies to records
  "written after F". A reconciliation record written before F and frozen with the amended plan, for
  a side change already known, is not restricted by it. Combined with the landing composition rule
  of sec. 13.1, under which L's ledgered bytes equal M's bytes with C's splices applied at
  M-translated offsets and each `old_sha256` is re-verified, this keeps H immutable, consistent
  with c.7093128173. B's statement that option 2 "fits poorly if the freeze happens after today" is
