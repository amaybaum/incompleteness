## Definitions and table grammar

**F1** means each spatial site takes values in a finite alphabet. **F2** means the complete spatial site set is finite. Under the declared fixed site-product construction **F1 + F2 ⇒ A1**, but neither alone does. **F3** means finite visible distinctions on a specified finite observation horizon, not a bound on hidden states. **F4** means a finite backward causal dependence region for a finite readout W and finite K under a finite-range or locally finite-input update. F4 is *not* an autonomous finite closed system; equality of statistical outputs with another model also requires matched initial joint law (including correlations) on that cone. **A1** is total finiteness of ALL complete configurations, a separate open posit, not a consequence of finite access, cone locality or boundary entropy. A1 alone says nothing about bijectivity.

## Dependency graph — only forward arrows

- F1 + F2 + fixed spatial product => A1. A1 + full bijection => one common finite microscopic order; with earlier overlap and shared rooted prior => recurrence-scale P-indivisibility. No accessible time bound follows.
- F3 + finite K + stochastic joint law => one constructed finite D(K) => fixed-basis representation. **Not** a selected physical same-carrier family for all K, or coherent instruments.
- Finite-range update + finite W + finite K + matched initial cone law => F4: same window observations. **Not** a closed patch, A1, all-time equality, global recurrence or a common rooted return.
- Genuine C4 history readback => positive *multi-time conditional memory*. Genuine C4r/C4e rooted revival => *rooted P-indivisibility*. Neither premise follows from finite access alone, and history memory does not automatically yield rooted revival.
- Invariant normalized measure => Poincare pointwise recurrence (almost everywhere). **Not** common rooted identity return. F11's synchronized near-return bound is an independent sufficient condition.
- Local six-link + independently supplied H-link/H-cust/condensate => conditional local stabilizer; chosen charged action => conditional group/charge equations. **Not** a native physical observer, Yang–Mills/QCD, or complete quantum apparatus.
