## Definitions and table grammar

**F1** means each spatial site takes values in a finite alphabet. **F2** means the complete spatial site set is finite. Under the declared fixed site-product construction **F1 + F2 ⇒ A1** ([SubstratumInterfaceAudit.a1_of_finite](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/411c838889ce79e78f9061b396b9af3d02c695e1/verification/lean-mathlib/OIBridge/SubstratumInterfaceAudit.lean#L108-L111)); this is a **one-way implication only**. Neither alone establishes A1, and no cited result derives F1 or F2 from A1. **F3** means finite visible distinctions on a specified finite observation horizon, not a bound on hidden states. **F4** means a finite backward causal dependence region for a finite readout W and finite K under a finite-range or locally finite-input update. F4 is *not* an autonomous finite closed system; equality of statistical outputs with another model also requires matched initial joint law (including correlations) on that cone. **A1** is total finiteness of all complete configurations, a separate open posit, not a consequence of finite access, cone locality or boundary entropy. A1 alone says nothing about bijectivity.

In axis columns **R** = required by the **cited proof as scoped** (not logically necessary under all other methods), **S** = supplied *in the stated model* but unnecessary to the local implication, **C** = sufficient only with the named additional premises, **N** = neither supplied nor required by the stated implication (or explicitly not implied), **U** = not audited. Source-paper ladder labels F1/F2/F11 are independent of THESE F1–F4 axes. Class tags: **FINITE-ONLY**, **A1-FREE CONDITIONAL**, **CAUSAL-PATCH EQUIVALENT ON WINDOW**, **PHYSICAL SOURCE GAP**, **UNCLASSIFIED**; mixed tags and mixed-axis entries (such as N in P7-B versus R in original GR counting) distinguish separately cited branches. - Local six-link + independently supplied H-link/H-cust/condensate => conditional local stabilizer; chosen charged action => conditional group/charge equations. **Not** a native physical observer, Yang–Mills/QCD, or complete quantum apparatus.

## The NR-1 causal-cone / wraparound distinction (non-negotiable)

Under NR-1's selected mod-q wave rule, full (current,previous) window readout and matching torus-uniform versus infinite-Haar priors, the **rooted maps agree exactly for every r≤t** if ext_j(W)+2t<L for **each coordinate j** ([NR-1.md:295–302](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/411c838889ce79e78f9061b396b9af3d02c695e1/verification/audits/foundations/nr1-near-return-20261116/NR-1.md#L295-L302)). This is finite-time equality of observables, not the existence of a finite autonomous reversible patch. The torus's near-return witnesses obey n≥(L−max_j ext_j(W))/2 for nonnilpotent alpha; its C4r/C4e revival witnesses obey t≥the same bound with prime q, alpha≠0, lattice-convex W ([NR-1.md:304–311](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/411c838889ce79e78f9061b396b9af3d02c695e1/verification/audits/foundations/nr1-near-return-20261116/NR-1.md#L304-L311)). Thus, **under (NN) for near-returns and prime q, α≠0 and lattice-convex W for C4r/C4e**, the torus witnesses lie outside the certified prewrap agreement region. **This is not a universal claim:** if α has a nonzero nilpotent primary component, lattice-independent witnesses may lie *inside* that region (NR-1:337–380). As L→infinity, the earliest witnesses under those assumptions diverge; this does not exclude nilpotent-primary-component exceptions. NR-1 additionally proves all-horizon P-divisibility on Z^d for prime q/nonzero alpha/Haar/convex W ([NR-1.md:237–255](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/411c838889ce79e78f9061b396b9af3d02c695e1/verification/audits/foundations/nr1-near-return-20261116/NR-1.md#L237-L255)), but does NOT cover all nonconvex windows, non-Haar laws, nonlinear readouts or composite q ([NR-1.md:828–847](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/411c838889ce79e78f9061b396b9af3d02c695e1/verification/audits/foundations/nr1-near-return-20261116/NR-1.md#L828-L847)). The nilpotent primary-component exceptions are nonpropagating and not credited ([NR-1.md:355–380](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/411c838889ce79e78f9061b396b9af3d02c695e1/verification/audits/foundations/nr1-near-return-20261116/NR-1.md#L355-L380)).
