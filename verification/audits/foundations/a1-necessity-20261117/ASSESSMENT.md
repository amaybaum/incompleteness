Its downstream diagnostic leaves these four physical chains unrecovered. We reuse that diagnostic without rerunning the cosmological literature screen or its quantitative checks.

**Quantifiers and scoped controls.** Per-window finite representation covers the specified finite visible trajectory law (and the specified finite action-labelled family where the intervention theorem is invoked), not all operations of one common physical universe. Main's all-time rooted classification concerns periodic row-stochastic families with Γ₀=I; it is not all-time gluing for arbitrary multi-time laws. NR1's infinite-lattice negative result requires its stated prime alphabet, nonzero coupling, Haar prior and lattice-convex readout window; composite/nilpotent cases are not ruled out. None of these controls proves universal impossibility or algorithmic undecidability.
