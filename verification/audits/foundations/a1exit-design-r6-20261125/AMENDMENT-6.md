### B2. R6.RECON.24 explicit-resolution schema

Options:

- keys: the tool writer's `{splice, start_M, end_M, sha256, resolved, resolved_sha256}`, which the owner accepted with the meanings quoted in sec. 0.7;
- source of the resolved bytes: inline, in a lossless encoding (chosen), or read from `l` through an offset map (rejected: it makes the bytes the review confirms depend on a second mapping);
- confirmation: the review item `<path>:resolution:<splice>`, its verdict carrying `confirms` equal to the window and `resolved_sha256`.

Proposal: 0-based byte offsets into the entry's `m` blob, end exclusive, at UTF-8 character boundaries; base64 (RFC 4648 sec. 4, canonical) for `resolved`, chosen over UTF-8 text because no normalization on the way can alter it silently; the review confirmation bound to the window and `resolved_sha256`. Safety: every hash, boundary, containment, splice identity and the composed content are checked mechanically; a malformed resolution never applies. Controls: RESOLUTION-SCHEMA (both directions), with countercontrols for windows, hashes, encoding, replacement content, splice identity and review confirmation.
