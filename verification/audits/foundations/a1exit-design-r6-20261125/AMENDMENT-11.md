## 1. The dependency to remove

R6.EVID.8 and M8-3 make live REP-BUILD evidence due on the tool candidate. Staging r2 §2 requires a verified preparation base B holding the two roots, with complete first-parent walks from B to the snapshot S and to the run's main anchor. M8-9 derives the allow root from final H, and DP-1 requires H to be chosen after the tool lands. A check due for that landing cannot consume the resulting root and snapshot. Moving the check later while still making B an ancestor of the original tool landing leaves the first-parent condition impossible. Amendment 9 already moves the 75 named OBL-3 rows. REP-BUILD is outside that list. The Package 6 template split resolves the distinct content dependency through the class pin. ## 3. Dependency and requirement preservation

The proposed order is well-founded by construction. Fixed tool sources and the reviewed pre-landing evaluation precede T_land. T_land precedes final H and the derivation of reviewable records. Both roots can be prepared at B after H by the ordinary authorized staging route, with the descriptor recorded after B exists. Both S and M_rep can then extend from B by first parent. Exact input and review records precede the candidate F and its validation. The OBL-16(a) result refers to the candidate F and is retained outside the bytes that define it. Designation and execution follow successful validation and applicable authority. The class record pins only the fixed unfilled template and the actual T_land scope, with the existing exclusion of the class and its own dependent records. No prerequisite refers back to the object that contains it.

This is a proof of ordering, conditional on permissible staging, eligible roots and records, and exact checks holding. It neither selects B nor assumes an allowance re-derived at final H exists. A changed pre-landing tool image, an unauthorized ref, failure to admit the fixed representative or final-H scopes, or missing verified history leaves the corresponding phase blocked.
