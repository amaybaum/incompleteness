# UNIFY-1A — Ontology and common physical foundation

**Paper-only assessment for [#1602](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/issues/1602).** U1 and conceptual U2. OI sources are pinned to [main at 942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/commit/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a). The research findings await independent review.

**Goal link.** Determine which distinctions between physical geometry and operational quantum mechanics follow from the existing OI evidence, and which missing bridge would distinguish the surviving common-foundation hypotheses.

**Verdict — categorical separation underdetermined.** The record-law equivalence and the use of supplied causal geometry in the designated GR construction support a distinction between descriptive roles. The former quantifies over representations of a supplied finite law. The latter supplies geometry before defining its particular trace-out. These results leave open whether a physical metric is primitive, is derived from the substratum, or exists only as an effective description in a class of observers and regimes. They also leave open the physical origin of coherent quantum operations. The closest existing proposal is already in Substratum §5. It assigns visible statistics and boundary thermodynamics different roles in a common construction, with that construction's native realization explicitly open. ## 2. Distinguish the three proposed levels

1. **Underlying histories.** Specify admissible histories $X$, an update or solution law, and a physically justified family of preparation measures. Apparatus and observers belong to the histories. The deterministic representative is a candidate for this level.
2. **Physical structure.** Specify candidate quantities defined on histories, such as $G(x)$ for a metric or causal structure and $M(x)$ for material degrees of freedom or conserved currents. Define them up to the redundancies of the model. Their meanings and claimed observer independence are part of the hypothesis.
3. **Observable records and descriptions.** For observer $o$ and permitted finite protocol $p$, let $R_{o,p}$ map the applicable histories to available records. Conditioning and pushing the preparation through this map give outcome laws. Quantum states and instruments, when recovered, organize the laws and their operational composition. An observer's estimated geometry can be another description at this level.

### A test on one observer interface

For a deterministic per-run record map $R_{o,p}:X_p\to\mathcal R$, with $X_p$ the declared admissible history domain of the protocol, let $F:X_p\to Y$ be a physical quantity. At the chosen resolution, use a specified physical binning $b_\delta:Y\to Y_\delta$. The binned quantity is exactly recoverable from the available record precisely when

$$
R_{o,p}(x)=R_{o,p}(x') \ \Longrightarrow\ b_\delta(F(x))=b_\delta(F(x')) \quad (x,x'\in X_p).
$$

This is equivalent to $b_\delta F=\widehat F\circ R_{o,p}$ on $X_p$. A factorization gives the equality. Conversely every realizable record has a common value of $b_\delta F$ on its preimages, defining $\widehat F$. An equal-record pair with different values is an obstruction for this interface. For ensemble inference the input is the family of outcome laws of all declared probes. Equality of those laws gives an analogous equivalence on the model or preparation family. The ensemble criterion and per-run recovery address different questions.

One matched comparison would use the same finite retained record for two held-out targets. The geometry target is a bin of the proper time, in a fixed physical unit, along a specified clock trajectory between two matter–clock coincidences. This presupposes a metric and observer-independent identification of the events on the compared histories. The matter target can be a charge class of a specified probe at one of those events, provided a gauge-invariant charge and the relevant sector are defined. Use the same admissible history domain, retained record, readout window and preparation and intervention restrictions for both tests. A witness of nonrecoverability is a pair of histories in that domain. Report the binning separately for each quantity. A record that already includes a calibrated report of a target makes its factorization immediate on the domain where that calibration holds. The meaningful case asks what can be inferred without reading the target directly.

An independent issue arises even if an observer could infer exact continuum light cones. A rescaling $g'=e^{2f}g$ leaves the causal cones invariant but changes proper time along a fixed marked timelike path by the factor $e^f$ in the integrand. Identifying causal reachability with Lorentzian cones would therefore still leave the clock scale to be fixed. A native dependency bound is weaker than such an identification.

The equivalence theorem itself fixes no such comparison. Given any finite reversible representative on a domain with an observation map, append a decoupled two-valued variable carried unchanged by the update, with a normalized independent distribution. This preserves the observed law. A quantity on the enlarged history that reads the auxiliary variable can differ on an equal-record pair, while a quantity defined by a function of the original record factors. This is an example about the logical information in a record-law statement. Diagnosing a disagreement for a real clock or charge requires $G$, $M$, and $R$ on a physical history domain. Main's partition lemma and Structure's decoupled enlargement describe the corresponding fixed-interface freedoms. [Partition relativity](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a/papers/Main.md#L82-L90) · [Fixed-interface enlargements](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a/papers/Structure.md#L43-L54)

**Consequence for U1.** A quantity can be observer-independent in its definition and register different values on histories equivalent for a finite observer. That is how geometry can be physically real under H1 or H2 and still subject to observational incompleteness. Full agreement of two observer estimates also leaves open whether they recover a common physical quantity or make equivalent predictions in a limited regime.

## 3. Common architecture and H1–H4

A common foundation must supply a law and apparatus on one history space. For a proposed deterministic representative, write $x=\Phi_p(s)$ for the history of a prepared initial configuration $s$ under a realizable protocol $p$, and $\nu_\pi$ for the measure associated with a preparation $\pi$. Define the candidate record law by pushforward,

$$
P_{o,p,\pi}=(R_{o,p}\circ\Phi_p)_*\nu_\pi .
$$

The common data are the dynamics, the rule for preparing $\nu_\pi$, the physically available interventions and their composition, material probes, and the detector-record maps. For joint predictions, multiple records must be available from compatible interfaces on the same histories. For a quantum bridge, seek a preparation map $\nu_\pi\mapsto\rho_{o,\pi}$ and outcome maps $\mathcal I_{p_j,r_j}$ on one observer carrier. On a sequence of declared interventions, the comparison law would take the form

$$
P_{o,p,\pi}(r_1,\ldots,r_n)
=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[(\mathcal I_{p_n,r_n}\circ\cdots\circ\mathcal I_{p_1,r_1})(\rho_{o,\pi})\right].
$$

For an adaptive protocol the choice of $p_j$ depends on the earlier recorded outcomes. The preparation assignment, outcome maps and their composition have to respect the declared operational equivalences and composite constraints. A response table constructed separately from each desired experimental family provides an existence representation at the record layer. Reachability and the operational relations on one physical dynamics need their own derivation. [Intervention dilation and algebraic gap](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a/papers/Main.md#L490-L498) · [Finite-envelope scope](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a/papers/Main.md#L574-L605)

## 5. Constraints and possible conflicts

- **Bell and CHSH.** On a declared Bell domain, a setting-independent pre-setting ensemble and factorized responses given a complete state $\lambda$ give
  $P(a,b\mid x,y)=\int P(a\mid x,\lambda)P(b\mid y,\lambda)\,d\nu(\lambda)$.
  With dichotomic outcomes on the counted trials and the stated sampling rule, $|S_{\rm CHSH}|\le2$. Main realizes those assumptions for the specified fixed nearest-neighbor cone, setting locality and readout. Bell violations, including Hensen et al.'s event-ready experiment, constrain that branch. A compatible extension has to explain its response structure and operational no-signaling, or make explicit which premise of the comparison is changed. Relabeling the outputs with a unitary representation leaves the CHSH value intact. [Internal Bell bound](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a/papers/Main.md#L364-L435) · [H-Bell](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a/papers/Substratum.md#L121-L141) · [Bell](https://journals.aps.org/ppf/abstract/10.1103/PhysicsPhysiqueFizika.1.195) · [CHSH](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.23.880) · [Hensen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature15759)
- **Contextuality and coherent control.** Generalized noncontextuality requires operationally equivalent procedures of a given type to have the same ontological representation. Spekkens' preparation, transformation and unsharp-measurement no-go results use the corresponding quantum operational equivalences. An action ledger can retain the procedure label, so matching selected outputs leaves the status of those equivalences open. The same caution applies to phase-sensitive noncommuting operations and to the Born probabilities over alternative measurements and composites. H1–H3 must account for these structures on the relevant domain. The fixed-basis law or configuration-level control ceiling supplies a scoped statement about an interface. [Spekkens](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.71.052108) · [Operational extension](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/942bd5ea1fd09f4223012b0755bce2d6da07c44a/papers/Main.md#L490-L498)
- **Fixed finite dynamics and time.** With one finite bijection $\Phi$ and fixed prior and readout, $\Phi$ has finite order. Passive rooted laws recur exactly at that order. This excludes exact all-time reproduction of nonperiodic passive targets for that class, while leaving the per-horizon theorem and finite-precision comparisons available.
