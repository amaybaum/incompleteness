## What would let the test start

Supply one independently specified field on a fixed finite A4 representative and its readout. Name the site-labelled real functions of that readout, their span and the spatial action used to compare them. The functions and the law must be fixed before looking at their image under $P_\mu U P_\mu$. Then the predeclared checks can test closure, normalization and nonnegativity, symmetry, nearest-neighbour weights and $p_0=0$. The expected negative control is SM's correlated-hidden swap, which has a self-transition despite microscopic lack of a self-input. The source gate stopped before the kernel check or the control run.
