# A1-08 · Source of the scalar observer field

**SOURCE GAP.** The corpus defines real, site-indexed functions of the binary readout. In particular, the lattice trace-out code uses the Ising encoding $2b-1$ on both registers, and Main uses the same signed coordinates up to sign as members of its family of diagonal characters. Their documented purposes are to form correlation probes and to represent the configuration channel. The corpus supplies no independent observation rule identifying any of those functions or their span with the scalar field of SM §4.1. ## Inherited setting and source criterion

A1-06 supplies the model family, the cut and the reference law. In Main's notation the configuration is `(u,v)`, with one present and one retarded bit per site of the finite periodic cubic lattice. The update is $(u'_x,v'_x)=(\sum_{z\sim x}u_z+v_x,u_x)$ over $\mathbb F_2$. The spatial readout is $X_R=(u,v)|_R$ for a declared visible set $R$ and the law $\mu$ is normalized counting on the full phase configurations, with uniform conditionals on the hidden fibres. Main discusses a cubic block in $d=3$ as a case of this family. The field audit uses the cuts already named by the candidate sources. [A1-06][a1-06] · [Main's binary update and readout][main-model] · [Cubic block][main-block] · [Reference law][main-law] · [A4][substratum-a4] · [Typed wave rule][lean-wave]

The source sought is a physical or observational prescription for real functions $f_x$ on the values of $X_R$, a site index set and the induced spatial action on $\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{f_x\circ X_R\}$. The prescription has to be justified on the same update, readout and law independently of the spatial kernel it would later be used to test. SM Lemma 1c conditions on all of $\sigma(X_R)$ once a cut and a law are supplied. Corollary 1b assumes the scalar spatial representation of its operator. The full readout algebra and the chosen scalar field space are distinct inputs to that identification. [SM Theorem 1a and Lemma 1c][sm-projection] · [SM Corollary 1b][sm-branch]

## Real functions already defined on the binary readout

### Signed site values in the trace-out code

`exact_L2.c` uses $q=2$, a periodic $L=2$ cubic torus and the same directed nearest-neighbour sum with the retarded bit and a copy into the previous register. The periodic $+$ and $-$ directions of each axis coincide at this size. Its cut is the plane $x_1=0$, with sites `0, 2, 4, 6` in its indexing, and its visible record reads both time slots there. The exact trace-out loops over all hidden bit assignments at each visible root and weights the visible roots uniformly. These are the product-counting law and its hidden conditionals for this cut. [Lattice and bit layout][c-layout] · [Microscopic step][c-step] · [Cut and readout][c-cut] · [Weights][c-count]

The source explicitly defines `spin(b) = 2*b - 1` to form Ising-like correlations. For $x$ on the plane the two instances are $a_x=2u_x-1$ and $b_x=2v_x-1$. The temporal probe sums $a_x b_x$ over the plane. The other probes sum oriented triples of present signs, take the product of four present signs on the plane, or sum squared differences of present signs in the positive $y$ direction. Their span is the real span of those four aggregate observables. The elementary present and retarded site spans are identified below. The static operator-conditioning record uses the same signed observations and counts all states, with the stated purpose of comparing distributions of probe values. [Signed encoding and temporal probe][c-spin] · [Spatial probes][c-composites] · [Static conditioning design][c-static] · [Exact enumeration][c-static-count]

The code also supplies a $q=2$, $L=3$ case with six distinct directed neighbours per site and the same Main update. Both registers are read on the nine-site plane $x_1=0$. `sample_main.c` uses the same local signs for its probe statistics. `effective_lagrangian.c` names the signed functions of the initial visible registers as terms of an operator basis, including both time slots and two-site products, and targets uniform visible sampling and uniform hidden averaging. This is a sampling prescription for the reference law on the named cut. The $L=2$ record gives exact evidence for the use of counting. [Larger binary update and cut][c-bench] · [Larger probe definitions][c-bench-ops] · [Operator-basis purpose][c-effective-intent] · [Both registers on the plane][c-effective-cut] · [Sampling of the two factors][c-effective-prior]

The bit extractors return the integers $0$ and $1$. Read as real indicators, those are the uncentred coordinates corresponding to the signed readout, e.g. $u_x=(1+a_x)/2$. The signs are fixed before evaluation of these diagnostic observables. Their formula uses the value of a single register and takes no adjacency or weights as input. Some composite probes subsequently use particular neighbours and directions. This explains the probe coding independently of the target kernel. The missing physical step is a reason to use the present signs, retarded signs, their on-site products, or another specified family as the SM scalar amplitude.

### Diagonal characters in Main and Lean

Main defines $Z^c|i\rangle=(-1)^{c\cdot i}|i\rangle$ inside the Weyl basis. Here $i=X_R$ labels the entire visible phase configuration of the inherited $q=2$ update, and $c$ ranges over its $2|R|$ bit positions and their linear combinations over $\mathbb F_2$. The channel uses the uniform hidden factor of the reference law. Selecting the mask at the present or retarded slot of $x$ reads the corresponding real sign $\chi_{x,u}=(-1)^{u_x}$ or $\chi_{x,v}=(-1)^{v_x}$. The mask supported on both slots gives the product. These are special cases of the source's general character, including the traces' Ising values up to overall sign per one-bit character. Lean defines the pairing, its coordinate specialization and the real sign character used in the complex Weyl matrix. [Main's character formula and its use][main-weyl] · [Lean coordinate pairing][lean-coordinate] · [Lean sign and Weyl matrix][lean-character]

The complete real character span is the function space on $X_R$, of dimension $2^{2|R|}$. The source's character sum gives orthogonality for the uniform visible marginal. The present and retarded site families each span a copy of $\mathbb R^R$ inside it, and the on-site products span a third such copy. Combined, these three nonconstant families span a $3|R|$-dimensional subspace. These are statements about the sourced functions and their static spans. Main's use of all masks is a configuration-channel calculation and offers no identification of a specific site span with SM's field. [Main channel normal form][main-weyl] · [Lean character orthogonality][lean-ortho]

### Spatial action of the sourced site functions

On the uniform cubic reference family let a lattice motion $g$ permute sites and preserve the current/retarded slot labels. On the full phase space the reindexing is $(g\cdot s)_x=s_{g^{-1}x}$. Pullback by $g^{-1}$ sends each sign at $x$ to the same sign at $gx$ and sends a character mask $c$ to its reindexed mask $g c$. Each of the three site spans has the same permutation action, including under cubic signed coordinate permutations. The normalized counting law is preserved by these site relabellings. On a fixed cut $R$ this is an action on the visible algebra only for motions with $gR=R$. Other motions compare functions on $R$ with functions on $gR$. For the plane probes, in-plane translations preserve the cut, and translation normal to it moves the cut. A cubic rotation exchanging the normal with a tangential axis moves the cut too. This action is read from the coordinate and probe definitions. It gives no selection of one of the equivalent site spans and no full-lattice equivariance assertion for the fixed-cut compression. ## First missing source

The first missing object is a source-backed assignment of a particular real scalar site family to the actual observed phase registers, with a reason from the observation or physical rules to use that family as the SM scalar field. Its observable span and spatial action must be specified on the same cut and uniform reference law. The Ising values and characteristic signs meet the definability part and have an ordinary site action wherever the cut allows it. The corpus uses them as coordinates and probe factors. SM's conditional real branch takes the physical field as input. The surviving gap lies between those levels, before closure or normalization of the scalar compression is tested.

**Disposition.** SOURCE GAP for the pinned corpus. A1-06's one-step kernel test and correlated-hidden-swap control remain blocked at this input. The paper audit stops at field sourcing. Physical source remains **NOT PASSED**.
