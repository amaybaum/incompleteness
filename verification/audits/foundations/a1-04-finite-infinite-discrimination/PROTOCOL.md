# A1-04 · Finite-resource tests of total carrier cardinality

**NO SEPARATOR WITHIN SCOPE for the paper comparison. SOURCE GAP for a physical test of total finiteness.** A finite cyclic register and an infinite stream give identical complete log laws at both settings. The shorter register is distinguishable from that stream once its wrap falls within the short observation schedule.

## 1. Carrier classes

Both classes specify an autonomous discrete-time reversible update, a normalized preparation law and a measurable readout of one declared bit. Here a carrier is the space of total microscopic states of the model, including the rest of the system outside the readout. A physical interpretation must account for any clock, controller, preparation source and record store that can feed back into that dynamics. Membership is defined by microscopic state space and dynamics before comparing record distributions.

| Class | Admission independently of a record law |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathcal F$, finite total carriers | A finite nonempty state space $\Omega$, a permutation of it and a probability law for preparation. Each member has a size bound $\lvert\Omega\rvert$. The class has no independently specified common size ceiling. |
| $\mathcal I$, infinite total carriers | One genuinely infinite state space $(\Omega,\Sigma)$, a measurable bijection with measurable inverse, and a countably additive normalized preparation law. For example, an unbounded site set with a finite alphabet can supply an infinite product carrier. The one-bit readout remains local to the specified interface. |

An unbounded family of finite approximants lies in $\mathcal F$ member by member. Exact record-law equality is compatible with different preparation measures on the two microscopic spaces. When invoking a stationary spectral calculation, the reference measure for that calculation must also be invariant. A finite instantaneous alphabet is available at the model interface by stipulation. Its all-time record capacity is a separate question. [Main §1.2](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/papers/Main.md#L40-L60)

## 2. Protocol and resource budget

Fix the following budget for the paper comparison. The two settings give short and long read schedules under the same preparation and free dynamics. The recording budget is sufficient to keep the full record at either setting.

| Component | Shared rule and bound |
| --- | --- |
| Preparation | One attempt to arm the readout and obtain the preparation ensemble for that model. Use the same declared preparation label at both settings, with one bounded preparation slot $T_{\rm prep}$. Record ready, rejected or deadline. The paper controls specify the ensemble and ideal status explicitly. |
| Setting | Choose $K=4$ or $K=8$ before preparation. The setting fixes only the number of scheduled updates and is kept in the record. Compare laws at the same setting. |
| Intervention | Leave the state to its autonomous update, with the identity as the sole state intervention. A read schedule gates the recorder. The paper protocol assumes a passive readout of the bit. Physical passivity and timing are source requirements. |
| Readout and precision | At ticks $0,\ldots,K$, resolve one binary value of $b_t$, at a fixed step resolution $\Delta t$. Record any ambiguous, absent or late reading as a failure, then stop. Every tick slot carries one of $0,1,\mathsf{failure},\mathsf{padding}$. |
| Complete outcome | Keep the setting, preparation status, all scheduled bit or failure records, stop reason and stop tick. Encode run termination as scheduled, preparation failure, read failure or deadline. A failed attempt stays in the comparison. |
| Time and storage | At most eight free updates and nine timed reads, with deadline $T_{\rm prep}+8\Delta t$, and at most 32 retained bits for the complete log. The setting uses one bit, the preparation status two, nine slots use 18, the stop reason uses two, and the stop tick uses four. A preparation failure can use tick zero and a separate stop reason. No reset, repeated trial or unbounded external setting stream is used. |
| Precision for probabilities | Use $\eta=1/16$ in total variation as the maximum calibration error per model's complete-log law when considering a physical separation. This is a tolerance for evaluating a stated law and would need a separate calibration source. It is independent of the one-bit read resolution and supplies no empirical estimate from one attempt. |

For each setting let $P_M$ be the model's law on complete attempted logs, including failures. A sufficient class separator at this precision is a log event $E$ with
$$
\inf_{I\in\mathcal I}P_I(E)-\sup_{F\in\mathcal F}P_F(E)>2\eta
$$
or the reversed inequality. The two deviations of at most $\eta$ then leave a strictly positive probability gap for $E$. For the identifiability check, one pair that agrees exactly at both settings rules out a uniform discriminator for these two classes under this protocol. Contrasts on a narrower pair must identify the extra model restrictions used.

## 4. Exact record comparison and controls

Use the existing SUBSTATE-1 shift-XOR models. The finite carrier $F_q$ has a cyclic register of $q$ binary cells and the visible bit $b$, with $2^{q+1}$ total states. The infinite carrier $I_R$ has a bi-infinite binary register and the same visible bit. In both the update shifts the hidden register left and maps $b$ to $b\oplus x_0$, where $x_0$ is the pre-shift bit. The readout is $b$. In the mathematical control the preparation draws the visible bit independently of the fair hidden bits, using the finite uniform law or its infinite product counterpart. The preparation is ready and each timed read and the scheduled stop succeed in the ideal log. Both updates are reversible and preserve their respective normalized laws. [SUBSTATE-1 model definitions](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/BOUNDARY-CONTROLS.md#L9-L57) · [Finite cyclic counterpart](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/BOUNDARY-CONTROLS.md#L87-L106)

For $K\le q$, the first $K$ increments $b_{t+1}\oplus b_t=x_t$ use different cells in both models. Every $K+1$ bit word therefore has probability $2^{-(K+1)}$ in each, since the word determines $b_0,x_0,\ldots,x_{K-1}$. The setting, ticks, success tags and padding are supplied by the same schedule. Thus $F_9$ and $I_R$ have the same **complete** ideal log law for $K=4$ and $K=8$, with equal zero mass for failed logs. If a common conditional recorder kernel acts on the same word and setting, including possibly word-dependent failures, the same equality follows for its outputs. Equality of failure rates alone would be insufficient because a failure could carry different information about the underlying history. [Exact finite-prefix comparison](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/BOUNDARY-CONTROLS.md#L93-L106)

| Control | Complete-record implication |
| --- | --- |
| Decoupled shift with the same readout | In case D, the read bit stays $b_0$ while the hidden register evolves. Both the finite cyclic and infinite forms give the same two constant successful logs at each setting, each of mass $1/2$. |
| Coupled shift with delayed wrap | $F_9$ and $I_R$ agree under both settings despite hidden-to-visible transfer at each step. Agreement extends through horizon $q$ for $F_q$. The first disagreement is at $q+1$. |
| Coupled shift with early wrap | In the $K=4$ setting, use $F_3$ and $I_R$. On an attempted log define $E$ to require a successful record and $b_4\oplus b_3=b_1\oplus b_0$. The two increments use the same hidden cell in $F_3$ and independent fair cells in $I_R$. Hence the ideal probabilities are $1$ and $1/2$. With a sourced $\eta=1/16$ error bound in each model the gap would be at least $3/8$. This is a contrast of two specified mathematical models under the budget. |
| Infinite carrier with visible return | In case Q, the hidden register is fixed and $b$ toggles according to $x_0$. Its update squares to the identity and its visible two-step return is exact, including at the two scheduled horizons. The existing finite Q control has the same visible log at every horizon, since both records depend only on two independent fair bits, $b_0$ and $x_0$. ## 5. Candidate separators

| Observable candidate | Result within the declared budget | Requirements for a stronger comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Return to the starting bit, or a rooted identity return | A fixed finite permutation has a finite order, so a synchronized return exists on the ideal carrier. Its order need not fall in the window. $F_9$ and $I_R$ give the same distribution of every in-window return statistic. Infinite Q permits synchronized returns. Poincaré recurrence is a statement about state-dependent returns to sets and does not guarantee a common exact time for the visible law. [Main recurrence and overlap](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/papers/Main.md#L119-L155) · [Observable return](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/observable-recurrence.md#L10-L56) | An independent ceiling $N_{\max}$ on autonomous total states or a known synchronized return time in observable ticks. For the ceiling, $L=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,N_{\max})$ is a common identity time if the passive dynamics is a permutation. Require $L\le K$, preparation and readable retention through $L$, and a uniform lower bound on the infinite class's successful mismatch $b_L\ne b_0$ large enough to survive errors. With its invariant law, infinite Q also has a Koopman operator satisfying $U^2=I$, whose spectrum is contained in $\{-1,1\}$. A compression to a finite record algebra can have a finite spectrum even on an infinite carrier. Every spectrum estimate built from the bounded matched log has the same law on $F_9$ and $I_R$. [Exact evolution and compression](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/papers/Main.md#L748) · [Infinite toggle model](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/BOUNDARY-CONTROLS.md#L59-L67) | A physical readout coupling the proposed spectral feature to the log, fixed frequency or time resolution, and a class-uniform bound on the resulting finite-log statistic, including all finite approximants allowed by the hypotheses. Spectral statements about a chosen closure or projected operator give no such bound on the total carrier. |
| Finite memory, repetition of words or saturation of predictive histories | The one-bit alphabet bounds instantaneous read resolution. The infinite stream has $K+1$ bits of joint entropy at horizon $K$, and the matched finite register has the same entropy in the window. The stream is i.i.d. and supplies this example without conditional predictive memory. Main's capacity bound gives a lower bound on the hidden space of a faithful finite deterministic realization from the observed conditional past–future information. The passive predictive quotient describes the distinguishable future traces under its known size, support and access assumptions. Invisible states inside one trace fibre are not counted by the quotient. [Capacity bound](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/papers/Main.md#L510-L516) · [Passive quotient and fibres](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/papers/Substratum.md#L329-L361) | A uniform upper bound on the finite competitors and readback that tests beyond it with controlled preparation, retention and precision. With $N$ possible initial states, a noiseless autonomous passive block has at most $N$ possible words. Its law is at TV distance at least $1-N/2^{K+1}$ from the fair stream when $N<2^{K+1}$. This follows by taking the complement of that particular finite model's word support. It is a bound against a specified law or support, not a prechosen single event uniform over arbitrary unknown supports. |

The absence of a class separator here follows from the exact shared law, so any test or post-processing of the complete log has the same distribution on at least that pair. Even without restricting to eight updates, the cyclic family supplies a matching finite model for each fixed passive horizon. The matching model can grow with the horizon. One fixed finite permutation cannot reproduce the fair stream for all horizons, since its return time would give $b_L=b_0$ with probability one. Repeated interventions, external inputs or resource readouts require a fresh check of which laws remain matched.

## 6. Physical stop and evidence needed

The first physical source gap is a common, sourced preparation and passive timed readout for the candidate realizations. SUBSTATE-1 chooses the bit, coupling and preparation law in its mathematical controls and explicitly leaves their identification with actual OI dynamics open. A physical test needs a specified cut and update, a preparation procedure with the stated law or a bound on its error, calibrated $T_{\rm prep}$ and $\Delta t$, reliable retention, and the joint law of success, failure and timing on those same operations. The bound $\eta$ must cover changes to the complete law, including losses and disturbance of the state by the reading process. A new intervention would need its own physical source and branch-support check. [Control model scope](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/BOUNDARY-CONTROLS.md#L69-L85) · [SUBSTATE-1 operational sourcing](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/0283ba315e8c8a8f1465a91eea8613f9aa213ab5/verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/observation-context-protocol.md#L54-L84)

The physical cardinality question also needs model restrictions that rule out shared log laws and give a demonstrated uniform gap within the resource budget. Bounds on accessible modes, on a dependency patch, or on a finite surrogate do not give a uniform bound on total carriers. A sourced apparatus for the short-register contrast would test that register against the specified stream. An inference about total finiteness would require independent grounds to restrict the admissible finite and infinite models accordingly. The assessment stops at these missing inputs. Scientific acceptance and integration are separate decisions.
