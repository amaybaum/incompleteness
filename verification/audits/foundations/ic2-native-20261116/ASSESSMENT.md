## 1. First stop and precise meaning

The first ingredient in the declared check order is **a natively executable and completely accounted communication interface for the one-bit game**. The existing IC₂ source explicitly appends this as a mathematical operational extension: a native Alice-to-Bob message channel, independent input source, clock and local operations are not supplied by the original isolated-wing grammar. Treating that extension as available under the actual OI dynamics would require an independent availability premise, not a consequence proved from embedded observation. The owner's stop condition is therefore met at this ingredient. No apparatus candidate, substitute premise or further derivation is attempted.

This does **not** demand that every OI observer be universally restricted to one bit. The message budget is a condition defining a particular experiment: exactly one binary message on the stated schedule, with no uncounted operational communication through clocks, heralds, flags, failures or later joining of records. Declaring that budget is mathematically legitimate. What has not been supplied is a native implementation and access account satisfying it, and a native information theorem for the allowed resource class. For an m-bit version the analogous contract would bound the communication alphabet by 2^m and count all admitted side channels; no m-bit theorem is attempted here.

Nor does absence of a sourced implementation prove that the game is impossible or that IC₂ holds vacuously in a physically adequate sector. An unavailable game supplies neither an experimentally usable bound nor quantum coverage. The result is a stopped source check, not a native counterexample to IC₂ or a universal non-derivability theorem.

## 3. What the existing arguments do and do not decide

There are two separate obligations: **making the test physically available**, and **deriving an information bound for that native operational class**. Resolving I1 alone would not resolve I5. D §6.1's common classical record R is not provided for free by saying the entire substratum is classical and deterministic: that proof also restricts the interactions and the effect of the query before decoding. The adopted Bell branch allows ontic parameter dependence while requiring operational no-signalling separately (Main §3.3; Substratum H-Bell). Equating operational no-signalling with classical pointwise locality would insert the very extra premise the source distinguishes.

D §5.2's already-written PR counterprotocol yields J=2 under its supplied inputs, operations and appended message channel. It shows why the declared one-bit alphabet and the tested weak/no-signalling conditions do not themselves give the classical-local proof's conclusion. It is **not** a newly native OI counterexample: the globally coupled map, fair preparation, restricted access and operational extension are specified model data. This check does not certify that they satisfy every native OI source/interaction requirement. Conversely, D §6's quantum preservation calculation is a comparator, not an OI derivation.

The retained conditional SEL-MENU theorem adds an access diagnostic: if a passive-data selector accepts the full model of the theorem's full/restricted pair, it also accepts the indistinguishable restricted partner, which lacks the required operation under the stated non-realizability hypothesis. This does not prove closure of every OI class under arbitrary menu deletion. It prevents passive-law agreement alone from being counted as the required operation certificate.

No implication from IC₂ back to OI, to a unique physical sector, or to the full quantum set is claimed. No equivalent alternative information principle is introduced after the stop. Q-A and foundational assessment r7 provide the question and candidate map; the particular source-stop finding here rests on the primary arguments just traced. The older assessment's broad “no discriminator” phrasing is qualified by Q-A: the conditional IC₂ discriminator exists, but its native source and derivation remain open.
