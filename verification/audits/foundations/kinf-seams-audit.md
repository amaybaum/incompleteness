# K∞ seams — the obligations upstream of the elementary ball

**Read-only bookkeeping audit.** Base: `bd5de8f541fa94162da1017720970e9e95de8b22`. It constructs no object, freezes no
theorem and changes no status. It names, in `verification/ROADMAP.md`, the obligations that the route to the
elementary ball currently consumes, each pinned to the kernel object that carries it. The obligations are seams of
the present proof route, not independent axioms; later theorems may discharge several of them together.

## 1. The label K∞-R is ambiguous

In the landed records the label K∞-R names two different predicates:

- `OrbitGeneration.lean` and the OG-1 record use it for `BoundaryTransitive Ω G`: a member of `G` carries any
  boundary state of `Ω` to any other (`OrbitGeneration.lean:78`, the countercontrol `not_boundaryTransitive_flow` at
  `:620`, and the hypothesis lists of `seedOrbit_ball3_eq` and `supportingEffectComplete_of_sharp_transitive`);
- the ROADMAP's K∞ entry at the base uses it for field-neutral drivability, `ElementaryDrivability Ω`
  (`KInfFoundations.lean:264`): a continuous one-parameter group of affine automorphisms preserving `Ω`, a member of
  it that is an involution moving some state (the NOT), and an automorphism `J` of `Ω` off the flow's axis.

The landed records are left as they stand. The queue uses **K∞-Drive** for the second and **K∞-Trans** for the first.

## 2. A drive's flow does not give boundary transitivity

`not_boundaryTransitive_flow` (`OrbitGeneration.lean:620`): the flow of `ball3Drive`, a drive of the ball, preserves
the ball and carries a sharp seed, and `Set.range rot3` is not boundary transitive on `ball3`. The words generated by
the same flow together with `J` are boundary transitive on `ball3` (`boundaryTransitive_ball3Drive`,
`OrbitNormalization.lean:667`, with `driveWords3 = words (Set.range ball3Drive.flow ∪ {ball3Drive.J})`). That is a
property of one drive of a body that is already the ball. No theorem derives `BoundaryTransitive Ω G` for any `G` from
`ElementaryDrivability Ω` on a general body; `preservesBody_drive` and `preservesBody_driveWords` give body
preservation only. K∞-Drive and K∞-Trans are therefore separate obligations.

## 4. The finite route as currently built

```
K∞-Stage (SC∞, elementary scope, finite rank)  →  finite-dimensional completed body and chart
K∞-Act                                          →  reversible affine actions preserving the body
K∞-Drive, K∞-Trans, K∞-Seed, K∞-V4              →  the elementary ball (TRB-1) and its sharp family (OG-1)
                                                →  the effect cone of the ball (EFF-1)
K2 (local tomography, the composite cone, compatible local actions)
K∞-Copy and the native gate (frame, the two NOT relations, two-sided positivity, entangling)
                                                →  d = 3 (DIM-1)
Kₙ (the operational architecture at every finite carrier)
K3                                              →  finite operational quantum theory
```
