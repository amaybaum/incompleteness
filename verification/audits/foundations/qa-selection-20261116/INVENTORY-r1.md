## 1. Outcome and current target

The inspected record supplies conditional discriminators, insufficiency results and explicit controls. It does **not yet identify a premise both independently justified from native OI and shown to exclude a specified nonquantum alternative while providing the required quantum coverage**. This is a bounded inventory result, not a theorem that such a premise cannot exist, nor a conclusion in favor of the assessment's null option C0.

There is an important qualification to FOUND-ASSESS-r7 §0 and Q-A's phrase “None removes a non-quantum law while keeping QM.” SEL-DISC-1 already gives a conditional information-causality discriminator: its declared PR protocol has information sum 2, while its classical-local and quantum-local comparators satisfy a bound of 1. What is missing is the independent native justification and executable protocol, not all conditional exclusion mathematics. The narrower negative statement in the preceding paragraph preserves this distinction. This inventory leaves the assessment and all accepted sources unchanged.

The owner decision [D-CQ, #1169 comment 6891338304](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/issues/1169#issuecomment-6891338304) governs the programme target: C must be defined without quantum outcomes, cover perceived quantum phenomena, and agree with performed experiments. Whether C is contained in the quantum zone Q is open. Excluding PR is **not** a programme goal. The authorized Q-A inventory asks whether any discriminating consequence is already supported; it does not reinstate universal soundness, exact equality C = Q, or a requirement to adopt IC₂. Stronger-than-quantum predictions in unperformed experiments are not rejected merely for being stronger than quantum mechanics.

Four questions are therefore recorded separately:

| Test | What must be supplied | What does not supply it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formulation | A rule intelligible without specifying desired quantum outcomes | Defining C as the models that give the desired table |
| Native justification | A derivation or independently justified physical restriction on the actual dynamics, sources and accessible operations | A mathematically consistent model, a model axiom, or a favorable comparator |
| Discrimination | An exclusion at an explicit preparation, intervention, record and resource interface | Missing an operation in one implementation, or matching one passive marginal |
| Quantum coverage and empirical agreement | The required phenomena from one common realization and justified correspondence, with all admitted histories and failures accounted for | Quantum notation, a separately fitted model for each experiment, or compatibility with only a classical fragment of QM |

“Nonquantum” below distinguishes **insufficient coverage** (e.g. a diagonal subtheory lacking quantum controls) from **excess behavior** (e.g. a PR table outside the specified quantum Bell class). These are different failures. A classical fragment of QM is not itself a forbidden probability law.

## 6. Decision and stopping point

The first missing scientific link is a justified restriction on **what the native observer can prepare and do**, together with its source law and retained records, that has a discriminating consequence while preserving the required quantum phenomena. The inspected passive-law representations do not supply that link. D6 shows what a conditional discriminator can look like; D3 and D4 show why adding apparently innocuous access or preparation assumptions can change the conclusion or remove quantum coverage.

No existing row completes all four tests in §1. This inventory therefore stops with an explicit evidence gap, not a selected new premise or a new proof assignment. It does not decide whether Q-A is ultimately achievable. Any next proof/source/literature unit needs its own concrete scope; completing P1 or later scoping P12 does not automatically provide that authority.

The authorized parallel administrative action keeps G's three non-A1 findings in existing [#1211, comment 6910248680](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/issues/1211#issuecomment-6910248680): the claimed M1/mixing dependency, the temporal-realization/lattice bridge, and the observer-versus-substratum gauge action. No duplicate issue, source correction or status promotion is made. P6 and P7 remain inactive. Physical source remains **NOT PASSED**; full OI–QM reconstruction remains open.
