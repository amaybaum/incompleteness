"Of undetermined cardinality" is added only where the cardinality is the point of the sentence.
- **W2 (finite-model mathematics).** A result that uses finiteness of the whole system is stated of "a finite reversible realization" (Main §3.4 term), "a fixed finite reversible representative" (Main §1.2 term, kept where already used), "a finite bijective $(S,\varphi)$", "a finite model" (Introduction, book), or with "$S$ finite" as an explicit hypothesis. - **W4 (A1–A6).** (a) As the hypothesis set of the reconstruction theorem and its lemma: "finiteness of $S$ and A2–A6". (b) As the name of OI's specific class: "A2–A6". (c) "A1 (finiteness)" as an item of a per-condition comparison: "finiteness of $S$ (a hypothesis of Theorem 23)". (d) "violating A1", "A1 is supported by E3" and similar: removed or replaced by the actual premise. A1–A6 statements about weaker sets stay true when narrowed to A2–A6 only in the entailment direction ("not entailed by A2–A6" follows from "not entailed by A1–A6"); every such row was checked for direction.
- **W5 (removal).** RX content is deleted where stated; the derivation of what survives stays (§A.30); no "formerly", no notes in the text (§A.27).
