## The question

The same arrow as the flow-extension audit, asked on the migrated semantics:

> `SourcedOI ∧ LayerFlowExecutable ⟹? PhaseFreeRichness`.

The flow-extension audit preregistered the least class `flowClass σ` as the test object and
deferred its endpoint because branch-wise realization admitted the replication of a post-selected
contraction into the unitary it scales. That loophole is closed: availability in an
implementation-generated theory is instrument realization by the class (`InstAvail`), and the
instrument-realization audit proved, for every class whose admissible unitaries fix the all-ones
vector up to a scalar, that every instrument-realized unitary conjugation fixes it
(`instAvail_unitary_fixes_ones`), and that the closed-form class `onesClass` of contractive
compressions of ones-fixing unitaries is such a class (`isometry_fixes_ones`), an architecture,
context-stable, label-invariant and dagger-stable, containing every permutation matrix and every
gate flow of an involution (`onesClass_arch`, `onesClass_contextStable`, `onesClass_labelInvariant`,
`onesClass_daggerStable`, `onesClass_permMatrix`, `onesClass_gateFlow`), in which the conjugation
by the transition flow at a quarter turn on a carrier with a third point is not instrument-realized
(`flow_realized_not_instrumentRealized`).

So the least class is not needed to decide the arrow. The theory generated by `onesClass` carries
every ingredient `SourcedOI` and `LayerFlowExecutable` ask for, as single admissible steps, and the
invariant of the instrument-realization audit says what it cannot carry. This round tests that
theory. The least class remains the object of the minimality and classification question, which
this round does not ask.

**What is retained from the flow-extension audit.** Its (L1) invariant, that every gate flow and
every permutation matrix fixes the all-ones vector, is in the kernel as `gateFlow_mulVec_ones` and
`permMatrix_mulVec_ones`; its extremality lemma is `kraus_of_conj_unitary`; its gadget identity is
`gadget_block`. Its (L2) positive outcome by post-selection was never a result and is not cited.

## The objects

**The carrier and the layer.** The base carrier `Fin 2` and the involution `σ := Equiv.swap 0 1`,
which moves `0`. At level `n` the layer is `levelPerm σ n` on `Fin 2 × Fin n`, an involution, and
its gate flow is `gateFlow (levelPerm σ n) t`. As in the lift audit, the gate flow enters
availability only through
`LayerFlowExecutable T σ := ∀ n t, T.availExt n Unit (fun _ => conjChannel (gateFlow (levelPerm σ n) t))`,
and nothing is read off the continuous path. The general form is stated for every finite carrier
and every involution; the two-state carrier is the instance on which the endpoint is decided.

**The test theory.** `onesTheory A := genTheory onesClass onesClass_arch A`, the theory the
ones-fixing class generates on a carrier `A`: availability at every level is instrument
realization by `onesClass`. It is a countermodel candidate and not a minimal one: it contains the
sourced class `permClass` (every architecture containing the exchanges contains it,
`permClass_le_of_exchanges`), every gate flow, and every contractive compression of a ones-fixing
unitary along ones-compatible isometries.

**The target.** `PhaseFreeRichness T` as literally stated: at every level `n` with at least two
states, some pair `a ≠ b` with `conjChannel (flow (transition a b) t)` available for every real
`t`, and every exchange of distinct states available. A unitary reached up to a global phase
counts, since a global phase acts trivially on its conjugation channel.

**The level of the negative, fixed now.** On a two-state carrier the transition flow of the one
pair carries the all-ones vector to `e^{−it}` times itself (`flow_mulVec_ones_apply`: the entry is
`cos t − i sin t` on the pair and `1` elsewhere, and there is no "elsewhere"), so it fixes the ray,
lies in `onesClass` as `e^{−it} • (e^{it} • flow)`, and its conjugation is one admissible step. The
obstruction is therefore absent at level one on `Fin 2`, and no negative is claimed there. It is
present at level two: the carrier `Fin 2 × Fin 2` has four states, every pair `a ≠ b` has a third
point `c`, and at `t = π/2` the vector `flow (transition a b) (π/2) 𝟙` has entry `−i` at `a` and
`1` at `c`, which is not a multiple of `𝟙`. Since `PhaseFreeRichness` requires the driven pair at
every level with at least two states, failure at level two is failure of the predicate. The
negative theorem is preregistered at level `n = 2`, and the obstruction is not to be relocated to
whichever level happens to work: if the mechanism fails at level two, the outcome is recorded as
such.

## The mechanism, fixed in advance

**Positive half.** Every conjunct of `SourcedOI (onesTheory A)` and every instance of
`LayerFlowExecutable (onesTheory S) σ` is a single admissible isometric step, `InstAvail.op`:
reversible implementation locality from the four stability properties of the class
(`genTheory_reversibleImplementationLocality`), embedded observation from label invariance
(`genTheory_embeddedObservation`), the exchanges from `onesClass_permMatrix`, the read-write
operators from `readWriteOperator_eq_perm` and `onesClass_permMatrix`, the gate flows from
`onesClass_gateFlow`, the layer involution at every level being an involution.

**Negative half.** An available unitary conjugation in `onesTheory` is instrument-realized by
`onesClass`, so its unitary fixes the all-ones vector up to a scalar
(`instAvail_unitary_fixes_ones`, `isometry_fixes_ones`); the transition flow at a quarter turn on
a pair with a third point does not (`flow_mulVec_ones_apply`); so no pair at level two is
continuously driven, by `flow_realized_not_instrumentRealized` applied to each pair with any third
point of the four.

**The consistency with the lift audit.** Q3′ of the lift audit derives phase-free richness from
executability under the substratum's availability, `SubstratumAvail`, whose isolation identity
consumes a sign diagonal and a phase diagonal at every level. The countermodel must therefore fail
`SubstratumAvail`, and the mechanism says exactly where: the quarter phase `phaseGate a` carries
`𝟙` to a vector with entry `i` at `a` and `1` elsewhere, off the ray on any carrier with two
states, so `onesTheory` carries no quarter phase at any level with two or more states, hence fails
`PhasesAvailable`, `DerivedOI` and `SubstratumAvail`. The missing resource is named by the
invariant: a relative phase, a diagonal unitary that moves the all-ones vector off its ray.

## What the outcomes mean, fixed in advance

**If F1–F4 hold.** `SourcedOI ∧ LayerFlowExecutable ⇏ PhaseFreeRichness`: on the faithful sourced
baseline, with the executable layer flow adjoined at every level and time, the continuous mixing of
one pair is not supplied, and the theorem that says why names the resource, a relative phase. The
implication of the lift audit's Q3′ is thereby sharp in its hypothesis: the substratum's
availability, not the executability alone, supplies the mixing. This is a statement about the
migrated semantics, in which availability is instrument realization; it is the answer the
flow-extension audit deferred, reached on the repaired objects.

**If a test fails.** The failing conjunct or level is the finding, recorded with its reason, and
the arrow stays undecided by this round.

**What no outcome establishes.** The lift audit's preregistered Q3, from `DerivedOI` and
executability: the countermodel has no phases, so it says nothing about a theory that has them,
and that question stays open as the lift audit records it. The least class `flowClass σ`, its
definition, its canonicity, or whether the gadget block lies in it: not defined, not asked. That
`onesTheory` is minimal among countermodels. Whether the observer-level lift is derivable. Route A
in either direction. Bare OI. Any manuscript sentence.
