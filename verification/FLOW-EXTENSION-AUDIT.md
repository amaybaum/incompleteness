**Status: preregistered, no proof attempted.** The sections below fix the objects, the levels

## The question

The substratum-interface audit determined the faithful sourced baseline: the observer theory of
a substratum is the theory generated by the scaled partial permutations, it satisfies `SourcedOI`,
and it carries no diagonal unitary beyond a global phase (`SUBSTRATUM-INTERFACE-AUDIT.md`). The
lift audit proved that an executable layer flow gives phase-free richness under the substratum's
full availability, by an isolation identity that consumed a sign diagonal and a phase diagonal
at every level (`LIFT-AUDIT.md`, Q3′). Those diagonals are not sourced. The generic implication
from `SourcedOI` to `LayerFlowExecutable` is already refuted: `permTheory` satisfies `SourcedOI`
and, being generated by a configuration-level class, fails `LayerFlowExecutable` for every moved
layer (`configurationLevel_not_layerFlowExecutable`). The question of this round is the other
arrow, on the faithful baseline and with no relative phase available:

> `SourcedOI ∧ LayerFlowExecutable ⟹? PhaseFreeRichness`.

The canonical test object is the minimal generated extension: the sourced implementation
repertoire `permClass` with exactly the one-parameter layer-flow family that
`LayerFlowExecutable` requires adjoined, closed under the existing architecture operations and
nothing more. If it fails phase-free richness it is the strongest natural countermodel, and it
names the structural resource still missing; if it has phase-free richness, the executable layer
flow alone supplies the mixing on the faithful baseline.

## The objects

**The layer.** A finite carrier `S` and an involution `σ` of `S` moving some `x`; at level `n` the
layer is `levelPerm σ n` on `S × Fin n`, and its gate flow is
`gateFlow (levelPerm σ n) t = unit (permMat (levelPerm σ n)) t = 1 + (e^{iπt} − 1)·(1 − P)/2`,
`P` the permutation matrix of the layer. As in the lift audit, `unit g t` enters availability only
through `LayerFlowExecutable T σ := ∀ n t, T.availExt n Unit (fun _ => conjChannel (gateFlow (levelPerm σ n) t))`,
and nothing is read off the continuous path.

**The target.** `PhaseFreeRichness T` as literally stated: at every level `n` with at least two
states, some pair `a ≠ b` with `conjChannel (flow (transition a b) t)` available for every real
`t`. A conjugation by a unitary is unchanged by a global phase, so a unitary reached up to a
global phase counts; a unitary reached up to a non-scalar diagonal does not. A generic
non-monomial unitary, a continuous subgroup, or the flow on several pairs at once is not the
target.

**Two levels at which "generated" is asked, frozen now.** The diagnostic below found that the
answer depends on which one is meant, so both are preregistered and the reading of a split is
fixed in advance.

- **(L1) The dynamical repertoire.** The group `flowGroup S σ n` generated in the unitary
  matrices on `S × Fin n` by the gate flows `gateFlow (levelPerm σ n) t` for every real `t` and
  the permutation matrices of `S × Fin n`. Its ingredients are exactly products, inverses (from
  the group law `gateFlow_group` and the inverse permutation), time changes, and conjugation by
  sourced permutations. This is the reading in which the positive route was to be attempted.
- **(L2) The architecture closure.** `flowClass σ`, the least implementation class containing
  `permClass` and, on every carrier of the form `S × Fin n`, the gate flows of the layer, and
  closed under the operations of `Architecture` (identity, products, all complex scalars, the
  ancilla readout projectors, the ancilla blocks), under uncoupled spectators, under relabelling
  and under the adjoint; `flowTheory σ := genTheory (flowClass σ) _`. Availability of a unitary
  conjugation in a generated theory is membership of the unitary in the class up to a nonzero
  scalar (the extremality of a unitary conjugation among completely positive maps, to be proved
  as `unitary_avail_iff_mem_smul`). Because the class is closed under every complex scalar and
  under blocks of words that contain a readout projector, (L2) contains **post-selected and
  rescaled** operators: `c · E† W E` where `W` is a word on an ancilla-extended carrier containing
  a projector onto one ancilla value, `E` the embedding of one ancilla value, and `c` any scalar,
  including one of modulus greater than one. Nothing in (L1) is of that form.

The difference between (L1) and (L2) is one closure of the formalism, `Architecture.smul`,
stated in round fifty-nine for every complex scalar and used in the generated theory for the
`1/m` normalization of a discard. Whether a unitary that is reached only as a rescaled
post-selected block is faithfully "available" is a question about the formalism, not about the
substratum, and this round decides nothing about it: it proves what each level gives and records
the split, if there is one, for an owner decision.

## The diagnostic, recorded as a diagnostic and not as evidence

Before this preregistration was written, a numerical check on carriers of size two to six
found: the real Lie algebra generated by `i·(1 − P)/2` over all involutions `P` of one cycle type
annihilates the all-ones vector in every case, has the dimension of `u(N − 1)` for one
transposition, and does not contain the single-pair transition generator modulo `i·1` for any
carrier of size at least three; and on the base carrier `Fin 2` at level two, the product of one
direct gate flow at time `−t` with the level-zero blocks of two gate flows on the doubled carrier
at times `t` and `−t`, for fixed-point-free involutions pairing one chosen pair inside level zero
in one gadget and the other pairs inside level zero in the other, is exactly a single-pair
transition flow at angle `−πt` up to a scalar. These numbers fix the mechanisms below; they
prove nothing and are not cited as outcomes.

## The three questions, in order, each with its own outcomes

**Q1. Generated repertoire (L1).** Characterize `flowGroup S σ n` by an invariant. The
preregistered invariant, with its mechanism: every gate flow and every permutation matrix fixes
the all-ones vector `𝟙` on both sides (`P 𝟙 = 𝟙`, `𝟙ᵀ P = 𝟙ᵀ`, so `(1 − P)/2` annihilates
`𝟙` from both sides), hence so does every element of the group and every element of the real
Lie algebra it generates. Admissible outcomes: the theorem `flowGroup_fixes_ones` (every element
`U` of the group has `U 𝟙 = 𝟙` and `𝟙ᵀ U = 𝟙ᵀ`), or a kernel refutation. A normal form beyond
the invariant is welcome and not required. Whether the Lie algebra is exactly the annihilator of
`𝟙` is not asked.

**Q2. Isolation (L1).** Does `flowGroup S σ n` contain `flow (transition a b) t` up to a global
phase, for some pair and every real `t`? The preregistered mechanism for the negative: the
transition flow on one pair has row sum `e^{−it}` on the pair and `1` elsewhere, so no scalar
multiple of it fixes `𝟙` when the carrier has a third point, while every element of the group
does. Admissible outcomes, decided per level: for every level whose carrier has at least three
points, the theorem `transition_not_in_flowGroup` (no scalar multiple of the flow at a time with
`e^{−it} ≠ 1` lies in the group) or a word in the group's generators equal to the flow up to a
global phase; on a two-point carrier the gate flow of the moved pair is the transition flow up to
a global phase (`gateFlow_eq_flow_two`), recorded as the one positive instance. If the negative
outcome holds at every level from the second on, then no theory in which the available unitaries
at level `n` are exactly `flowGroup S σ n` has phase-free richness, and the Lie-algebra route
(`HControl`, `control_of_lieRank`) is closed at (L1): the control Lie algebra of the gate-flow
drift with permutation controls annihilates `𝟙` and is not `su(D)`.

**Q3. Operational endpoint (L2).** Three parts, in order.

1. *The class.* `flowClass σ` is defined in closed form and proved canonical: it is an
   architecture, context-stable, label-invariant and dagger-stable, contains `permClass` and the
   gate flows (`flowClass_arch`, `flowClass_permClass_le`, `flowClass_gateFlow`), and every
   such class containing them contains it (`flowClass_le_of`). `flowTheory σ` then satisfies
   `SourcedOI` and `LayerFlowExecutable` (`flowTheory_sourcedOI`, `flowTheory_layerFlowExecutable`).
2. *Extremality.* `unitary_avail_iff_mem_smul`: in a theory generated by an architecture, the
   conjugation by a unitary `U` is available exactly when `c • U` lies in the class for some
   nonzero `c`. Mechanism: `U X U† = Σ Kᵢ X Kᵢ†` for every `X` forces each `Kᵢ v` to be
   proportional to `U v` for every vector `v`, so `U⁻¹ Kᵢ` has every vector as an eigenvector and
   is a scalar.
3. *The endpoint.* Admissible outcomes, exactly one of which is reached by theorem:
   - **positive by dynamics**: `flow (transition a b) t` lies in `flowGroup`, so in `flowClass`, at
     every level; then `flowTheory_phaseFree` and the general theorem
     `phaseFree_of_sourced_layerFlow : SourcedOI T → LayerFlowExecutable T σ → PhaseFreeRichness T`
     if its proof goes through availability-level operations alone. This outcome is excluded if
     Q2 is negative at the levels from the second on.
   - **positive by post-selection**: the flow is not in `flowGroup` but `c • flow (transition a b) t`
     lies in `flowClass` for a nonzero `c`, through a rescaled block of a word with a projector;
     then `flowTheory_phaseFree` holds by the gadget (`gadget_eq_flow`, the preregistered
     mechanism: one direct gate flow at time `−t` times the level-zero blocks of two gate flows on
     the doubled carrier at times `t` and `−t`, the first pairing every pair but one inside level
     zero, the second pairing the chosen pair inside level zero, both fixed-point-free) and the
     general implication `SourcedOI ∧ LayerFlowExecutable → PhaseFreeRichness` is **not**
     established, since a theory satisfying `SourcedOI` is generated by a context-stable,
     label-invariant class that need not be closed under scalars, and post-selection with
     rescaling is not an availability-level operation of a finite operational theory.
   - **negative**: `flowTheory σ` fails `PhaseFreeRichness`, certified by an invariant of
     `flowClass` that the transition flow violates; then it is the minimal countermodel and the
     missing resource is named by the invariant.

**The reading of a split, fixed in advance.** If Q2 is negative and the endpoint is positive by
post-selection, the round has found that the executable layer flow supplies the mixing on the
faithful baseline **only through the formalism's scalar closure**: dynamically, products and
conjugations of the layer flow and the sourced permutations never isolate one transition, because
every such operator fixes the all-ones vector, and the isolation is performed by a block of an
ancilla-extended word that a readout projector has cut, multiplied by a scalar of modulus greater
than one. Whether that closure is a faithful part of operational availability, or is to be
restricted (to scalars of modulus at most one, under which a unitary block of a contraction word
forces the projector to act trivially and the (L1) invariant returns), is an owner decision about
`Architecture` that this round does not take. In that case the sharp residual question is not
"is executable layer flow sufficient" but "is post-selection with rescaling part of what the
observer can do", and every kernel statement of the round is to be read on the level it names.

## What the round does not do

Insert a sign diagonal or a non-global phase into any (L1) word. Change `Architecture`,
`Realized`, or any existing module. Derive `LayerFlowExecutable` from anything; it is a
hypothesis throughout. Read availability off the CT2 path. Narrate anything in a manuscript.
Propagate Route B. Refresh the transfer bundle.
