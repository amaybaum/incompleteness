## The question

`OIPlusElem` is reversible implementation locality, elementary transition richness and embedded
observation. Reversible implementation locality is implementation locality with a **dagger-stable**
class, and dagger stability is consumed at exactly one place: it supplies inverse accessibility
(`inverseAccessibility_of_generated_daggerStable`), which is the `hstar` hypothesis of the
round-fifty reachability theorem (`universalReachability_of_lieRank_unconditional`). That theorem
turns membership in the reachable **subgroup** — words in the flows, the controls, their inverses
and the phases — into availability, and `hstar` is what makes the inverses of the controls
available.

> Does Lie-rank richness already imply full composite unitary control, using only the
> composition and coarse-graining structure every finite operational theory carries — with no
> inverse accessibility?

## The fork, as preregistered

**Outcome A — direct redundancy.** `LieRankRichness T → HasCompositeUnitaryControl T`. Then
inverse accessibility is derived from control (`reversibleRichness_of_control`), dagger stability is
removed from the characterization, and the package is `OIPlusPos`.

**Outcome B — only inverse derivation.** Direct control fails, but implementation locality,
embedded observation and Lie-rank richness force inverse accessibility.

**Outcome C — independence.** A `FiniteOperationalTheory` with Lie-rank richness and the relevant
locality and observer structure but without full composite unitary control, the missing control
exhibited by a named unavailable unitary in the style of `diagGen_not_control`.

The order of attack is A, B, C. A holds, so B and C are not reached, and no countertheory is
built: the fork's two falsifiers of A — a theory whose positive orbit directions span less than
`su(D)` although the control Lie algebra with inverses is full, and a submonoid of the unitary
group with a neighbourhood of a point but none of `1` — are excluded by `skew_mem_posSpan` and
`nhds_one_of_nhds_mem` respectively.

## The mechanism, made exact

The route is the compact-semigroup / interior route the repository names as the candidate
derivation, in three steps, none of which uses an inverse of a control.

1. **Positive words suffice locally.** `posReach H U` is the submonoid of the unitary group
   generated by the flows `e^{-itH}` (all real `t`), the controls `U_g` and the phases; every
   element has an available conjugation channel by composition alone (`avail_of_mem_posReach`).
   The positive powers of a unitary recur — the sequence lies in the compact closed unit ball, a
   subsequence converges, and the ratio of two far-apart terms tends to `1`
   (`exists_pow_tendsto_one`) — so `m^{p_k - 1} → m†` along a subsequence
   (`exists_pow_pred_tendsto_star`), the only form in which an adjoint is ever used. The real span
   of the positive orbit directions `Ad(m)(-iH)`, `m ∈ posReach`, with the phase direction, is
   closed and `Ad(posReach)`-invariant, hence `Ad(m†)`-invariant as a limit
   (`adStar_mem_posSpan`), hence preserved by the one-parameter groups
   `e^{t Ad(m)(-iH)} = m e^{-itH} m†` (`exp_posDir_conj_mem_posSpan`); the round-fifty derivative
   step makes it a Lie subalgebra (`bracket_mem_posSpan`, `posLie`) containing the control Lie
   algebra (`controlLie_le_posLie`), so it contains every skew-Hermitian matrix when
   `𝔏 ⊇ su(D)` (`skew_mem_posSpan`). Conjugation by a positive word is a real-linear
   automorphism preserving the phase-free span (`map_adEquiv_posSpan₀`), so a tuple of positive
   words `A_1, …, A_n` can be chosen greedily, by induction on the codimension, with the directions
   `Ad(A_1 ⋯ A_j)(-iH)` spanning it (`exists_nested_spanning`). The word
   `A_1 e^{-it_1H} ⋯ A_n e^{-it_nH}` is a positive word for every `t` (`wordMap_mem_posReach`) and
   has strict derivative `h ↦ (Σ_j h_j Ad(A_1 ⋯ A_j)(-iH)) · B` at `t = 0`, `B = A_1 ⋯ A_n`
   (`wordMap_hasStrictFDerivAt`). With a phase factor and a Hermitian complement, the round-fifty
   `Ψ` with the word in place of the product of orbit exponentials has surjective strict derivative
   (`psiW_hasStrictFDerivAt`, `psiDerivW_surjective`), so by the inverse-function theorem it maps a
   neighbourhood of `0` onto a neighbourhood of `B`, and a unitary in the image has zero Hermitian
   part and lies in `posReach` (`posReach_mem_nhds_totalProd`).
2. **From a neighbourhood of `B` to a neighbourhood of `1`.** For `v` near `1`, a positive power
   `B^k` lands where `v B^{-k} B` is in the interior, and `v = (v B^{-k} B) B^{k-1}`
   (`nhds_one_of_nhds_mem`, recurrence again).
3. **A neighbourhood of `1` generates the group.** A symmetric neighbourhood of `1` generates an
   open, hence clopen, subgroup of the connected unitary group; the subgroup it generates is the
   submonoid it generates (`Subgroup.closure_toSubmonoid`); every unitary is a phase times an
   element of the identity component (`exists_phase_joined`); so the submonoid is everything
   (`eq_top_of_nhds_one`, `posReach_eq_top`).

`universalReachability_of_lieRank_positive` is the round-fifty conclusion with the `hstar`
hypothesis deleted from the signature. The analytic inputs are those of round fifty — the strict
derivative of the matrix exponential and the inverse-function theorem — plus compactness of the
closed unit ball of the finite-dimensional matrix space. No closed-subgroup theorem, no
Lie–Trotter, no general orbit theorem.

## The theory-level consequence

| result | statement |
|---|---|
| `control_of_lieRank` | `LieRankRichness T → HasCompositeUnitaryControl T` |
| `inverseAccessibility_of_lieRank` | on a well-formed theory, `LieRankRichness T → InverseAccessibility T` |
| `oiPlusPos_iff_qm` | `OIPlusPos T ↔ ExactAllFiniteEndomorphicQuantumOps T`, nonempty carrier |
| `oiPlusPos_iff_oiPlusElem` | `OIPlusPos T ↔ OIPlusElem T`: the dagger clause is idle in the package |
| `carrier_general_oiPlusPos` | the equivalence quantified over every nonempty finite carrier |

`OIPlusPos` — implementation locality, elementary transition richness, embedded observation — is
the package this round records; `MINIMAL-REPERTOIRE-AUDIT.md` cuts its repertoire further. Each conjunct is stated at the level of
implementations or of the observer architecture, and none mentions reversal, an adjoint, or a
Lie-algebraic, reachability or availability condition. Dagger stability remains true of exact
quantum mechanics (`reversibleImplementationLocality_of_qm`) and remains the primitive from which
inverse accessibility was first derived; what changes is that the characterization does not need
it as a hypothesis.

## What this note does not claim

That `HControl` is necessary for positive reachability. That the minimal elementary repertoire,
context stability given generation, or the empirical addition of off-diagonal controllability are
touched. That the converse from inverse accessibility to dagger stability holds. That anything here
bears on the OI-N freeze, the concrete-cut freeze, or CT3. That anything here concerns non-compact
or infinite-dimensional groups.
