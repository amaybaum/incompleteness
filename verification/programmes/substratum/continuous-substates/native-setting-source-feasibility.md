# Physical-source feasibility of the late-entry coordinate

Research baseline: independently accepted, CI-green `0085faeb`.
This new bounded source audit awaits independent review. It does not run
native field simulations, introduce a source, or alter preparation/access.

**Result:** the late-entry geometry remains valid, but no selectable setting
operation is supplied by the audited passive-native/read contract. Public
record labels cannot prepare the private independent coordinate. The original
reference preparation also cannot supply the required mR-sensitive early
bit at J2 or N4. Public access and a usable outgoing instrument remain open.
These scoped barriers justify stopping before a delivery simulation search.

## Setting preparation: exact limitation of record-derived labels

Let B be the original current triple at

    p=(0,S,S+160), tau=L+3, first packet-boundary contact L+4.

The accepted geometry shows B lies outside the incoming packet and all old
public-port cones through tau. The same distance comparison excludes it
from old public ports through the interaction at L+4: the earliest possible
old-port contact is L+83. It also lies outside the old q9 read at L and the
supplied whole-V q9 read at L+2, even if that read is generously included
without adopting Native A's different source contract.

Under the retained product-complement prior, B is uniform on eight triples
and independent of the complete admitted history H and original incoming
carrier. This relies on both the prior and the causal exclusion; it is not
inferred from marginal uniformity alone. Suppose a setting label is computed
as C=f(H,Z). For this statement require B independent of the joint pair (H,Z)
at the actual decision boundary; an initially independent seed is insufficient
without that use-boundary justification. If it is unavailable, retain Z's
correlations and make no nonsteering inference from this argument. Under the
stated independence, B remains uniform after conditioning on H,C. For any
prescribed triple b(C), on a positive-mass branch,

    Pr[B=b(C) | H,C]=1/8.

For a prescribed one-bit value the corresponding probability is 1/2.
The proof sums the independent uniform B law over exactly one triple or four
triples. It holds for arbitrary public-policy functions and history laws;
it is not a conclusion from enumerating a few policies. Reading, labelling
or stopping cannot turn that label into preparation of B without a state-
changing operation. The probabilities are mathematical nonsteering bounds,
not a proposed heralded measurement or a native physical failure law.

Setting C=B.A after privately peeking at B is outside the read class and is
still retrospective labelling rather than an intervention. A publicly known
initial correlation between C and B would change the original product-source
law unless physically supplied. An actual native write/preparer could evade
this restricted argument, but its operands, clock, operation and records must
be constructed; the geometric possibility does not supply them.

The exact controls use nonuniform histories correlated with unknown X and
an explicit independent seed: all 256 binary policies on four history tags
and two seed values give match probability 1/2; 512 modular-affine whole-
triple policies give 1/8. These finite controls accompany the general paper
argument. An excluded correlated-prior countercontrol gives perfect bit
matching, demonstrating why the product/independence premise is essential.
That private conditioning is not an allowed herald or preparation.

## Compatible computation: the original early cone lacks mR

The original v14 reference uses the affine field masks
(E.A,E.B,E.D,N.A,N.B,N.D)=(1,5,1,4,1,5) on features
(1,u,mY,mR,a,b), hence

    E0=(1,1 XOR mY,1), N0=(mY,1,1 XOR mY).

None contains mR. Original J/G/K fields depend on u,a,b, not mR.
The current radius-two past of J2 consists of 25 prepared cells, including
exactly J/G/Kplus/Kminus/N; its seven previous-past cells are prepared too.
The two declared mR-selector sources and all compiled sources are too far
to enter. Holding u,mY,a,b fixed and changing mR therefore leaves the entire
local initial data unchanged.

The target bit 1 XOR a*mR changes when a=1. Eight of the sixteen fixed
(u,mY,a,b) source-pairs have different target bits but identical original
local input data. A deterministic native update cannot output the required
bit in both worlds at J2. This is source-dependence logic; no native field
evolution is needed to establish it.

The same issue applies at the proposed early N4 meeting. Its initial current
radius-four cone contains 129 cells: 25 pinned and 104 free. Its previous
radius-three cone contains 63 cells: seven pinned and 56 free. The only
named current sources inside are the same five MR-free sources; no compiled
or bank source is present. The free complement has the original product law
independent of mR. Thus the complete local input law, and consequently every
native field/function of that boundary at tick four, is independent of mR
conditional on u,mY,a,b. It cannot deterministically supply an mR-sensitive
bit across those contexts without another physical mR input.

The earlier successful J2 programme explicitly inserted mR through an affine
N source and swapped K orientation. Its computation remains valid under its
own assumptions, but that source programme failed original-carrier matching.
Inserting the mR-dependent N payload again is a preparation change, not a
delivery proof. A source-profile countercontrol confirms that insertion
breaks the MR-insensitivity premise; it is not performed as a native write.

This rejects importing that early J2/N4 signal into the unchanged reference
preparation. It does not exclude a later computation near the packet after
other physical inputs have arrived, or a separately sourced native actuator.
Mathematical knowledge of the seed does not place its physical operand in
the early cone.

## Public access, failures and outgoing state

At L+4 the late coordinate and affected packet boundary are not declared
public setting/outcome registers. No such permission follows from a private
inverse or the existence of a random coordinate. L+83 is only an earliest
possible old-port influence, not a decoder or an unmasked setting read.
Any later route must retain the actual port banks, masks, full raw fields,
attempt counts, failure branches and timing; earlier terminal branches may
not acquire a new read or offered carrier.

The accepted two-passive-step packet kernel retains its original scope and
product-prior assumptions. It is not a setting-dependent instrument. A
proposed conditioning or physical intervention at a free field requires a
new joint record/carrier/environment calculation and a native successor
accepting the outgoing state. No independent reset, fresh bath or unchanged
carrier is inferred from this source audit.
