## 1. Question and scope

**Q-NC (as authorized).** Can an embedded observer physically select between two distinct native
interactions using accessible information, while the incoming carrier preparation stays fixed and
prior records are preserved?

**What "select" can mean inside P0.** In the fixed preparation P0, every original variable is either
pinned by the source law or free under the product prior. A setting is therefore either a pinned
value, whose second value is a preparation change (§4, Class B), or a function of free original
variables. Main §3.3 (lines 400–402 at D) retains measurement independence as a posit about the
initial condition, and states that settings are functions of the pre-setting microstate when the
setting generators are part of the system. A setting available in P0 is therefore **randomly
selected by native dynamics, not chosen**. It comes from a passive random source with a public
record, and no do(sigma) exists. NC-1 tests the operative form:

**Q-NC′.** Inside the one preparation P0, does a native random setting, publicly recorded before
the window, correspond to two distinct native interactions on the same fixed incoming carrier? Here
"distinct" means a difference in the setting-conditioned kernels that is witnessed on carrier or
record coordinates and depends on the carrier (§5, K-COUPLED). A setting that merely correlates with
an existing variable, through a common cause, does not count.

Q-NC is the first checkable deliverable of #866 lane B, the physical carrier (comment 6525723006). It
serves #914 row 20 (native instrument) and the settings part of row 17. It is the target named in
#1009 comments 6537895636, 6538070106 and 6538289242 and in the later-access result (comment
6543875618).

**Three levels, kept apart. Only the second counts.**

- **Representable.** Some model contains a map realizing the two interactions: the controlled lift
  F(x,s)=(T_s x, s) (#866 comment 6526292899), a private inverse such as the transported coordinate
  chi_H of `native-actuation-boundary.md`, a private packet decoder, a supplied classical actuator.
  Representability establishes nothing here.
- **Available.** The operation is generated by the unchanged native rule from the fixed preparation
  P0 under §2.3, and: (Av1) it uses no supplied write, assignment, reset, inserted key or external
  switch between maps; (Av2) its selection input is in the declared access alphabet before the
  coupling begins; (Av3) no private read, inverse or decoder is used for selection, record or
  boundary; (Av4) every physical field of the setting mechanism is retained, in the records, the
  outgoing boundary or the environment pushforward.
- **Derived.** Availability that follows from the OI premises alone, without the retained candidate
  conventions H-access, H-prior-NC and H-inventory. NC-1 cannot reach this level. Its positive token
  states availability conditional on §2.3, never derivation.

**Out of scope:** a chosen (programmed) setting, noncommutation, contextuality, a quantum
instrument, composites, reuse beyond the single declared successor, horizons beyond the frozen
window, and any new preparation, access, source or physical premise. NC-1 works entirely inside the
nonlinear native-Y construction (rule h). The scalar torus revival candidate (Track A) is a
different model with a different prior, and none of its readouts is identified with a field here.
Noncommuting classical permutations, if they arise, are not evidence of a quantum instrument and
raise no token or flag.

## 3. Common frame

### 3.1 Fixed carrier/apparatus split

The **carrier** is the accepted 42-bit packet K_u: both complete slices on B1(q10). The
**apparatus** is a finite site set A, disjoint from B1(q10), named at stage 1 and identical for both
settings; both its slices are retained at every cut. The **environment** is everything else,
together with every original variable. The split cannot depend on the setting value or on any
outcome.

### 3.2 Layouts

- **Layout I (entry adapter).** The incoming carrier is K0=K_(L+1), with window L+1 -> L+3. The
  outgoing carrier K_(L+3) is the input object of the accepted successor L+3 -> L+5 (`923be23b`).
- **Layout II (in-window).** K0=K_(L+3), with window L+3 -> L+5. At D no accepted successor from L+5
  exists, so a Layout II instance can establish distinctness but cannot pass P-SUCC (§5).

### 3.3 Setting source, setting record and matched incoming preparation

**Setting source (bijection criterion, fixed here and not chosen at stage 1).** The setting record Z
is a whole raw triple of a declared public port, recorded at a tick before the window opens. Its
setting source Xi_A is the single original-time bank triple on that port's reserved bank line
whose unique straight first arrival gives Z. For every fixed value of all other original variables,
the map Xi_A -> Z is then a bijection (P-BIJ). This is the criterion of `native-interface-selector.md`
for G and of `native-random-setting-test.md` for R. Xi_A must be unpinned and lie outside the 1479
sources, the 17 named exceptions and the 25 ready balls (CP-UNPIN). sigma in {0,1} is a coordinate
projection of Z, fixed at stage 1. Z_rest is the rest of Z and is retained: no field of Z is summed
out. C_sys denotes C of H-prior-NC: every original variable outside Xi_A, xi and eta_succ, including
the source context and every hidden carrier-relevant variable.

**Matched incoming preparation (M).** For both values s, the conditional law of
(Z_rest, K0, W^-, C_sys) given sigma=s is the same. W^- is the complete accessible history through
the incoming cut other than Z: old q9 at L, every port raw triple (later raw triples of Z's own port
included), every flag and timing value, and V where it lies before the cut. M is stronger than
equality of the (K0, W^-) marginals. It excludes a setting correlated with a hidden carrier-relevant
variable, which is the measurement-independence posit of Main lines 400–401 at D. M is never
assumed. It is established by Lemma 3 from measured preconditions: cone separation from K0 only
(P-GEO-IN), and pads for every W^- coordinate that Xi_A reaches (P-PADW).

**One preparation.** Both setting values occur with positive mass inside the single preparation P0.
No source law, pin, prior or inventory entry differs between the arms.

### 3.4 Records, boundary and residual

- **Record alphabet Y_rec.** At each tick of the window: the whole raw triples of all eight ports
  and, in Layout I, the stipulated whole-V read at L+2. V is the copy of the current q9 field inside
  the outgoing packet, not an independent draw (the V-copy constraint of `53bf7ac8`). Every flag and
  timing value of the record contract is included. A read not attempted on a terminal branch records
  ⊥, and earlier records stay. No new flag or acceptance predicate is introduced; an instance that
  needs one ends in GAP (acceptance flag).
- **Outgoing boundary B_out.** The outgoing carrier and both complete slices of A, at the outgoing
  cut. **Residual.** The global two-slice cut at the outgoing cut is retained as the native
  pushforward of the original state, never replaced by an independent bath. The private triangular
  inverse appears only inside proofs and is never an operation. NC-1 compares no residual field
  directly; equality or difference on the compared object says nothing about the residual.

### 3.5 Compared objects

Let X' = (Z_rest, K0, W^-, Y_rec, B_out), and let k0 range over the incoming carrier values together
with ⊥, the value on the 16 terminal source contexts. For s in {0,1} let
mu_s(x) = Pr(X'=x | sigma=s), and let mu_s^k0 be its restriction to K0=k0, with the mass of the
restriction retained. No branch is renormalized. With TV(m,n) = (1/2) sum |m-n| over unnormalized
measures, TV_win(k0) = TV(mu_0^k0, mu_1^k0), and TV_in(k0) is the TV of the (Z_rest, K0, W^-)
marginals.

**Distinctness** means that TV_in(k0)=0 for every k0, that M holds, and that TV_win(k0)>0 for some
k0. Under M this is equivalent to a difference between the setting-conditioned kernels
Pr(Y_rec, B_out | sigma, Z_rest, K0, W^-) on a set of positive mass. Distinctness alone does not
separate an interaction from a common cause. NC-AVAILABLE additionally requires a carrier-coupled
witness (§5, D5). The same native rule, record contract and successor apply to both arms. NC-1
tests the joint law at the outgoing cut. A later continuation also reads the residual, so equality
on X' does not imply equality after a continuation. NC-1 claims neither equality nor difference
after the successor.

### 3.6 Three lemmas (paper proofs)

**Lemma 1, controlled lift** (#866 comment 6526292899; elementary). For bijections T_s on X, the map
F(x,s)=(T_s x, s) is bijective and conserves s. Hence: (a) writing two interactions as F with s an
initial register is representability only; (b) changing s between successive couplings on one
outgoing carrier needs a further sourced operation, so NC-1's positive token is single-use;
(c) T_0 T_1 ≠ T_1 T_0 is evidence for no token.

**Lemma 3, matched preparation** (elementary; proof given). The pads are split as xi_W (pads of
history coordinates) and xi_Y (pads of output coordinates). Suppose:
(a) P-BIJ holds;
(b) no cell of Xi_A, xi or eta_succ reaches a K0 cell, and no cell of xi or eta_succ reaches Z;
(c) the W^- coordinates that Xi_A or xi reach form a set W_pad, ordered so that each is a bijection
of its own pad in xi_W, given the earlier ones and (Xi_A, C_sys), onto a range not depending on
them. Every other W^- coordinate is a function of C_sys;
(d) H-prior-NC holds.
Then M holds.

*Proof.* Fix c in C_sys. By (a), (b) and (d), Z = f_c(Xi_A) with f_c bijective and Xi_A uniform, so
Z is uniform and independent of C_sys. Since sigma is a coordinate of Z, sigma and Z_rest are
independent and Z_rest is uniform. By (c) and (d), W_pad is uniform given (Xi_A, C_sys), hence
independent of (Z, C_sys). K0 and the other W^- coordinates are functions of C_sys, by (b) and (c).
So the law of (Z_rest, K0, W^-, C_sys) given sigma=s is the uniform law of Z_rest, times the law of
(K0, W^- outside W_pad, C_sys), times the uniform law of W_pad. None of these factors depends on
s. ∎

**Lemma 2, pad.** Split the compared output as Y=(Y_1, Y_2), where Y collects Y_rec and B_out.
Suppose:
(i) every coordinate of Y_1 is y1(C_sys), a function of C_sys alone;
(ii) for every fixed value of (Xi_A, C_sys, xi_W), xi_Y -> Y_2 is a bijection onto a range not
depending on that value (triangular order);
(iii) xi_Y is uniform and independent of (Xi_A, C_sys, xi_W) (H-prior-NC);
(iv′) M holds, and Z, K0 and W^- are functions of (Xi_A, C_sys, xi_W);
(v) Y does not depend on eta_succ.
Then mu_0^k0 = mu_1^k0 for every k0.

*Proof.* By (ii) and (iii), Y_2 is uniform and independent of (Xi_A, C_sys, xi_W). By (iv′) it is
therefore independent of (sigma, Z_rest, K0, W^-, Y_1). By (i), the remaining coordinates are
(Z_rest, K0, W^-, y1(C_sys)), and by M their law given sigma=s does not depend on s. The joint law
given s is that law times the uniform law of Y_2, the same for both s. ∎

Lemmas 2 and 3 are sufficient conditions. Failure of their preconditions is neither a difference
nor a confound.

## 4. Candidate classes, fixed before any evaluation

In each class the carrier, layout, record alphabet, boundary and residual are as in §3. Each class
states what makes it native and available. No class is preferred because of its expected outcome.
Every class yields at most a randomly selected setting.

**Class A: internal setting from a public random record** (Main §3.3, lines 400–402 at D). Z and
Xi_A are as in §3.3. The native continuation of Xi_A reaches the carrier within the window,
possibly through A. Class A is a passive random source: sigma is selected by the original bank
triple and recorded publicly; it is not chosen, and no do(sigma) exists.

The setting is native and available iff P-BIJ and CP-UNPIN hold; the field that acts is the native
continuation of Xi_A, with no supplied transport, write or relabelling; Xi_A reaches no K0 cell
(P-GEO-IN); and every W^- coordinate Xi_A reaches is padded (P-PADW). The two values are sigma=0
and sigma=1 inside the one P0.

*Remaining instances (paper arithmetic on symbolic coordinates).* For port 5 the bank cell of the
record at S+t is port5+(S+t)e_y=(0,2S+t,0). Its distance to the nearest cell of B1(q10) is
S+t+77 = L+t, since L=S+77; at t=2 this is the L+2 bound of `native-interface-selector.md`.
Separation from K_(L+1) needs t ≥ 2, and arrival inside the Layout I window needs t ≤ 3.
Separation from K_(L+3) needs t ≥ 4, and arrival inside the Layout II window needs t ≤ 5. Once

**Class B: program-register preparation** (Main §3.4, line 576 at D). Pi is a set of original-time
cells outside K0's backward cone, whose value selects, under the native rule, which interaction
acts. By Lemma 1(b) it is single-use. The two values are the two register values inside P0. Under
P0 a register cell is of one of three kinds:

- **Pinned:** a second value needs a pin change, PREPARATION-CHANGE (apparatus). That change lies
  outside the retained contract, needs its own sourcing unit and is not refuted by NC-1.
- **Source-context variable:** the source-context cells lie in the core, within O(T) of q10 and so
  far inside K0's structural backward cone of radius L. This option is therefore empty.
- **Free under H-prior-NC:** a random setting, admissible only if it meets the §3.3 bijection
  criterion for a public record before the window, and then evaluated as Class A. Otherwise its
  value is knowable only by a private read, and the token is PRIVATE-ACCESS.

**Class C: an observer-side subsystem coupled by the native rule.** O is a declared site set,
disjoint from A, B1(q10) and the port sites. Its state at the window opening is a native function
of a Class A record, produced by a native record-forming interaction rather than a write. O then
acts on A or on the carrier through the native rule. The selection is still random.

The setting is native and available iff the map from the record to O's state is native and derived
from frozen sources; O is not padded with respect to that record; O's outgoing state is retained in
B_out; and the Class A conditions hold for the underlying record. Under H-access a port read writes
nothing, so the retained contracts contain no record-forming map into a subsystem distinct from the
read field. If stage 1 cannot write that map from frozen sources, Class C records GAP (native
record-forming observer memory) without evaluation.

## 5. Decision rule (fixed before any computation)

Exactly one token is issued, by the first step that decides. Every step reads structured fields
(§7), and no step refers to an expected value. **An UNRESOLVED token is final within NC-1.** No later
step, flag, amendment or continuation promotes it to NC-AVAILABLE; only a new preregistration can
reopen the instance.

- **D0 (gate).** If any control in §6 fails or is vacuous, if the instance was evaluated before its
  stage-1 freeze, or if the run departs from the frozen instance: **VOID**.
- **D1 (source audit, CP-SRC).** If any element of the setting path is not generated by the native
  rule from P0 (an Av1 failure): **UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR**.
- **D2 (access audit, CP-READSET).** If selection, a compared record or the boundary requires a
  private read, private inverse or private decoder (an Av2 or Av3 failure): **PRIVATE-ACCESS**.
- **D3 (preparation and measurement independence).**
  - (a) If the two setting values need different source laws, pins, priors or inventories:
    **PREPARATION-CHANGE (source)**, or **(apparatus)** when only apparatus cells differ.
  - (b) If a precondition of Lemma 3 fails (P-BIJ, CP-UNPIN, P-GEO-IN, P-PADW or P-PADX), the local
    search (L-search, below) is run on each incoming coordinate (of K0 or W^-) that the failure
    names, at its own record time. An exact witness that the coordinate's law given sigma=0 differs
    from its law given sigma=1 gives **SETTING-CONFOUNDED**. Inside one P0 this is a failure of
    measurement independence, not a change of preparation.
  - (c) If such a precondition fails and no witness results: **GAP (matched preparation)**, naming
    it.
- **D4 (equality).** The stage-1 instance names a split Y=(Y_1, Y_2) and the pads xi_Y; the pad set
  may be empty, in which case Y_1=Y. If P-DET, P-PADX, P-TRI, P-PERM and P-GEO (§8) all hold,
  Lemma 2 gives mu_0^k0 = mu_1^k0 for every k0, ⊥ included: **PASSIVE-ONLY**. This side is a proof
  over the whole window.
- **D5 (local witness).** Otherwise, a failure of P-PERM or P-GEO that involves no output coordinate
  gives **GAP**, naming it. Each output coordinate that a failing D4 precondition involves is
  searched by the L-search, in this order:
  - first, outgoing-carrier and Y_rec coordinates, in the frozen witness order, stopping at the
    first counted witness;
  - then, only if none of those yields any witness, A's coordinates.

  A witness is **counted** only if all three hold:
  - (w1) its coordinate is an outgoing-carrier or Y_rec coordinate;
  - (w2) the deletion test makes it vanish: every member of Xi_A among the search inputs is replaced
    by an independent copy with the same law that does not feed Z;
  - (w3) the coupling test returns K-COUPLED.

  A counted witness proceeds to D6. If the search finds witnesses but none is counted, the token is
  **UNRESOLVED**: a common cause is not excluded. This covers a K-INDEPENDENT or UNKNOWN witness, a difference that
  survives deletion, and a witness on A's coordinates only. No witness on any searched coordinate,
  or a search that does not run, gives **GAP**, naming the coordinates, the failing preconditions
  and the search outcomes.
- **D6 (reusable boundary).** Given a counted witness: **NC-AVAILABLE** if P-SUCC holds, otherwise
  **GAP (reusable boundary)**. P-SUCC requires that:
  - the outgoing carrier is the declared successor's input object;
  - eta_succ is disjoint from Xi_A, xi, A and every window record and output, and reaches none of
    them;
  - eta_succ stays product-uniform given every positive-mass window history and sigma;
  - terminal branches offer no carrier.

GAP always names its missing element and scope, and it is never a general no-go.

**L-search (local to the window; executable).** For a searched coordinate Y* at time t, let τ be
the incoming cut (for D3(b), the cut two ticks before t). The rule has radius one and is second
order, so Y* is a function of the two-slice state at τ on its local cone: the current slice within
distance t−τ of Y*'s site, and the previous slice within distance t−τ−1 (a paper fact). Records
before τ are not inputs.

A written reduction must assign each local-cone input, or each native sufficient statistic that the
rule consumes from it (neighbour-XOR sums and old-local terms), to exactly one of four kinds:
- (r1) a K0 cell, enumerated per k0 class;
- (r2) a setting-carrying input, with its joint law with Z derived exactly;
- (r3) a padded input, uniform and independent of everything else by a named pad;
- (r4) an input whose joint law with (sigma, Z_rest, K0) is derived exactly from P0 and
  H-prior-NC, as unnormalized masses per k0 class.

The search runs only if every input is assigned and at most six triples are enumerated, with the
source context enumerated over its 64 rows. It then evaluates the native rule over every
assignment, in exact fractions, and compares the laws of (k0 class, Y*) under sigma=0 and sigma=1,
averaged over all inputs. A difference at a fixed background value is not a witness. An input that
cannot be assigned, because its law depends on the private complement in a way the derivation
does not reduce, stops the search for that coordinate.

**Asymmetry, stated openly.** PASSIVE-ONLY is certified by proof over the whole window.
SETTING-CONFOUNDED, UNRESOLVED and NC-AVAILABLE can rest only on a local exact witness. At actual
scale most cones carry private-complement dependence, so a local witness exists only where the
reduction closes. A difference that needs a larger input set, or that appears only jointly across
several coordinates, is GAP.

**Coupling test (w3), on a witness.** Y* is a single coordinate: a raw triple, or a flag or timing
value encoded in bits. G is the XOR group of its encoding. For each k0 class κ that has positive mass under both settings,
let P_s^κ be the law of Y* given sigma=s and κ, and let Δ_κ = P_0^κ − P_1^κ.
- **K-INDEPENDENT** if there are a bijection π of the alphabet, fixed across κ and sigma, and
  elements g_κ of G such that the pullback of Δ_κ under π equals the pullback of Δ_κ0 translated by
  g_κ, for every κ. The setting's effect is then a carrier-independent kick seen through a fixed
  relabelling, which a common cause can produce. Fixing π and g is an exhaustive finite check.
- **K-COUPLED** if at least two classes exist and no such (π, g) exists. For example, the contrast
  vanishes for one carrier class and not for another.
- **UNKNOWN** if fewer than two classes exist.

**Reported flag (decides no token).** *Visibility* of a counted witness: ACCESSIBLE for a Y_rec
coordinate, CARRIER-ONLY for an outgoing-carrier coordinate.

**What each token licenses.**

- **NC-AVAILABLE.** A randomly selected, publicly recorded native setting under which the incoming
  carrier's interaction differs in a carrier-coupled way, available under §2.3 for this instance,
  layout and dictionary. It is single-use, not chosen and not derived. It is not a quantum
  instrument, and it shows no noncommutation, contextuality or composability.
- **UNRESOLVED.** Distinct setting-conditioned laws exist, but a common cause is not excluded. This
  token is not a positive.
- **PASSIVE-ONLY.** The setting is sourced and matched but does not act, for this instance only.
- **SETTING-CONFOUNDED.** Measurement independence fails for this instance inside P0.
- **UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR, PREPARATION-CHANGE, PRIVATE-ACCESS.** This instance fails the named
  availability condition.
- **GAP.** Only its named gap.
- **VOID.** Nothing.

No token changes a preserved result, the 42-bit benchmark, a #866 quotient, a manuscript claim or a
status label.

## 6. Controls and countercontrols

Each control can fail, and all run before the token is computed. Fixtures are small exact native
worlds or exact algebraic fixtures. They test the mechanism and the comparator, not the
astronomical preparation.

- **POS (supplied classical actuator).** A supplied XOR of sigma into an unpadded carrier boundary
  field gives TV_win>0 and a witness, and the classifier returns UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR at D1, never
  NC-AVAILABLE.
- **NEG (passive public selector).** A fixture with the structure of `53bf7ac8`, an independent
  public label whose native continuation reaches only padded outputs. D1–D3 pass, D4 applies, and
  the token is PASSIVE-ONLY, with TV_win=0 for every k0.
- **LIFT (controlled lift).** F(x,s)=(T_s x, s) with noncommuting T_0, T_1 and s assigned per arm
  gives TV_win>0. The classifier returns UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR, or PREPARATION-CHANGE when s is pinned
  by a changed source law. LIFT-SEQ: T_0 then T_1 on one outgoing carrier is flagged as needing an
  operation outside F. The noncommutation sets no token or flag.
- **COMMON (common-cause countercontrol).** A native fixture whose bank source drives both Z and a
  carrier-independent XOR kick on a carrier coordinate, passing D1–D4 and P-SUCC. Its witness must
  be K-INDEPENDENT, and the token must be UNRESOLVED, never NC-AVAILABLE. COMMON-π: the same fixture
  seen through a fixed nonlinear relabelling must also be K-INDEPENDENT.
- **A-ONLY.** A difference confined to A's slices must give UNRESOLVED.
- **COUPLED.** An exact algebraic fixture in which sigma selects which carrier bit a record copies
  must give K-COUPLED. It tests the coupling test only, not sourcing.
- **DELETE (source deletion).** On the COMMON-like fixture, an independent copy of the setting
  source takes TV_win to 0. DELETE-COUNTER: a confounded difference survives deletion and gives
  UNRESOLVED.
- **PREP.** Two arms realized by two E/N source laws give PREPARATION-CHANGE (source), whatever the
  output TV. **CONF (hidden confound).** sigma correlated with a carrier-relevant C_sys variable,
  with equal (K0, W^-) marginals, fails a Lemma 3 precondition and gives SETTING-CONFOUNDED.
- **PRIV (private decoder).** A selection input read from q10, a pad or the chi_H coordinate is
  flagged by CP-READSET and gives PRIVATE-ACCESS.
- **MARG.** A joint-only difference with equal per-coordinate marginals: the joint comparator gives
  TV>0 and a marginal comparator 0. If the marginal one does not miss it, the run is VOID.
  **LEAK.** A difference carried only through Z_rest is detected with Z_rest and missed without it.
- **NULL.** Identical arms give TV=0 and no witness. **SEARCH-COUNTER.** For Y = sigma XOR d with d
  uniform, the L-search returns no witness; with d left unassigned, it refuses to run.
  **REDUCE-COUNTER.** An input whose law depends on an unreduced private complement stops the
  search. **PAD.** A fully padded fixture gives TV 0; removing one pad gives TV>0 and fails P-TRI.
- **WPAD.** A later raw triple of Z's port, unpadded, must fail P-PADW. With its own bank pad, it
  must pass.
- **VCOPY.** Detaching V from the q9 field it copies is caught. **SUCC.** Reusing a successor key
  inside the window fails P-SUCC. **MODEL.** Importing a scalar torus readout or prior is refused.
- **BRANCH.** All 64 contexts are carried, 16 at k0=⊥, with unnormalized masses summing to the
  source-context masses. No ⊥ branch acquires a read or a carrier.

## 7. Stages

**Stage 0** is this file and its independent exact-commit review. Any change after review starts is
an append-only amendment beside this file, written before the stage it affects.

**Stage 1 (candidate freeze; separate owner gate).** One file, `native-coupling-nc1-candidate.md`,
in one commit that is a single-parent child of the reviewed stage-0 head or of its last reviewed
amendment. No program is run for it. It fixes:
- the class and the layout, and A (and O where used) as symbolic site sets;
- Z, its port and tick, and the projection sigma; Xi_A then follows from §3.3, not by choice;
- the pads xi_W of W_pad and the Lemma 3 argument;
- Y_rec, B_out and the successor;
- the pads xi_Y and the split (Y_1, Y_2), or "none named";
- the witness coordinate order;
- the setting-path ledger of CP-SRC;
- each class-level GAP, with its missing element.

**Selection rule (outcome-independent).**

- Classes are taken in the order A, C, B. A uses only declared records and native transport; C adds
  a declared observer subsystem; B's one admissible form is evaluated as Class A (§4), so it comes
  last. Within a class: Layout I before Layout II, then the earliest record tick, then the lowest
  port index, then the axis order (+x,-y,-z,-x,+y,+z) of the accepted records.
- An instance is admissible only if:
  - it uses no excluded mechanism of §4;
  - it does not coincide with a preserved result in (setting record, layout, dictionary);
  - its Xi_A meets the §3.3 criterion;
  - the frozen records, or symbolic paper arithmetic, place Xi_A outside K0's backward cone;
  - its earliest symbolic arrival at the outgoing carrier footprint falls inside the window.

  Arrival at A alone does not qualify.
- The first admissible instance is frozen. A symbolic claim that fails at stage 2 gives GAP for its
  named precondition and never changes the instance. If no class has an admissible instance, NC-1
  ends at stage 1 with GAP (no admissible instance), naming the missing element of each class.

**Void clause.** Any computation bearing on an instance before its stage-1 freeze commit voids that
instance for NC-1. Such computation includes a kernel, TV, witness, native evolution or
programmatic geometry evaluation. A voided instance can return only through a new
preregistration. The stage-1 file is independently reviewed before the stage-2 gate.

**Stage 2 (evaluation; separate owner gate).** One execution commit adds
`native_coupling_nc1_probe.py` (standard library, exact integers and fractions),
`native-coupling-nc1-result.json` and `native-coupling-nc1-result.md`. It runs from the repository
root:

    python3 -I -B verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/native_coupling_nc1_probe.py
    python3 -I -O -B verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/native_coupling_nc1_probe.py
    python3 -I -B verification/programmes/substratum/continuous-substates/native_coupling_nc1_probe.py --mutation-suite

The verdict reads exit statuses and JSON fields, never log text (§A.44). Exit 0 is a token, 2 is
VOID, and 3 is a mismatch with the committed JSON, which is committed beside the probe (§A.42).
The L-search is bounded by 8^6 assignments times 64 source rows per coordinate. A run beyond local
smoke scale runs in CI (§A.40). No dispatch, `F`/`E`, readiness or merge is requested. An
independent result review follows.
