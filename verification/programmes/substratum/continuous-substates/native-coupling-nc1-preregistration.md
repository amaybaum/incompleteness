## 1. Question and scope

**Q-NC.** Can an embedded observer physically select between two distinct native interactions
using accessible information, while the incoming carrier preparation stays fixed and prior records
are preserved?

Q-NC is the first checkable deliverable of #866 lane B, the physical carrier (comment 6525723006):
one pinned native reversible carrier with two independently selectable interventions, complete
records and reusable state, all grounded in the same update. It serves #914 row 20 (native
instrument) and the settings part of row 17. It is the target named in #1009 comments 6537895636,
6538070106 and 6538289242 and in the later-access result (comment 6543875618).

**Three levels, kept apart. Only the second counts.**

- **Representable.** Some model contains a map realizing the two interactions: the controlled lift
  F(x,s)=(T_s x, s) (#866 comment 6526292899), a private inverse such as the transported coordinate
  chi_H of `native-actuation-boundary.md`, a private packet decoder, a supplied classical actuator.
  Representability establishes nothing here.
- **Available.** The operation is generated by the unchanged native rule from the fixed
  preparation P0 under §2.3, and: (Av1) it uses no supplied write, assignment, reset, inserted key
  or external switch between maps; (Av2) its selection input is in the declared access alphabet
  before the coupling begins; (Av3) no private read, inverse or decoder is used for selection,
  record or boundary; (Av4) every physical field of the setting mechanism is retained, in the
  records, the outgoing boundary or the environment pushforward.
- **Derived.** Availability that follows from the OI premises alone, without the retained
  candidate conventions H-access, H-prior-NC and H-inventory. NC-1 cannot reach this level. Its
  positive token states availability conditional on §2.3, never derivation.

**Out of scope:** noncommutation, contextuality, a quantum instrument, composites, reuse beyond the
single declared successor, horizons beyond the frozen window, and any new preparation, access,
source or physical premise. NC-1 works entirely inside the nonlinear native-Y construction (rule h).
The scalar torus revival candidate (Track A) is a different model with a different prior, and none
of its readouts is identified with a field here. Noncommuting classical permutations, if they
arise, are not evidence of a quantum instrument and raise no token or flag.

## 3. Common frame

### 3.1 Fixed carrier/apparatus split

The **carrier** is the accepted 42-bit packet K_u: both complete slices on B1(q10). The
**apparatus** is a finite site set A, disjoint from B1(q10), named at stage 1 and identical for
both settings; both its slices are retained at every cut. The **environment** is everything else,
together with every original variable. The split cannot depend on the setting value or on any
outcome.

### 3.2 Layouts

- **Layout I (entry adapter).** The incoming carrier is K0=K_(L+1), with window L+1 -> L+3. The
  outgoing carrier K_(L+3) is the input object of the accepted successor L+3 -> L+5 (`923be23b`).
- **Layout II (in-window).** K0=K_(L+3), with window L+3 -> L+5. At D no accepted successor from
  L+5 exists, so a Layout II instance can establish distinctness but cannot pass P-SUCC (§5).

### 3.3 Setting and matched incoming preparation

The setting record Z is a declared accessible record, or a fixed tuple of them, recorded at a tick
before the window opens. Write Z=(sigma, Z_rest), where sigma in {0,1} is a coordinate projection
fixed at stage 1 and Z_rest is retained; no field of Z is summed out. Xi_A is the set of original
triples on which Z depends structurally. C_sys denotes C of H-prior-NC: every original variable
outside Xi_A, xi and eta_succ, including the source context and every hidden carrier-relevant
variable.

**Matched incoming preparation (M).** For both values s, the conditional law of
(Z_rest, K0, W^-, C_sys) given sigma=s is the same. W^- is the complete accessible history through
the incoming cut other than Z: old q9 at L, every port raw triple, every flag and timing value, and
V where it lies before the cut. M is stronger than equality of the (K0, W^-) marginals. It
excludes a setting correlated with a hidden carrier-relevant variable, which is the
measurement-independence content of Main line 402 at D. M must be shown by a written proof whose
preconditions are measured; it is never assumed.

**One preparation.** Both setting values occur with positive mass inside the single preparation
P0. No source law, pin, prior or inventory entry differs between the arms.

### 3.4 Records, boundary and residual

- **Record alphabet Y_rec.** At each tick of the window: the whole raw triples of all eight ports
  and, in Layout I, the stipulated whole-V read at L+2. V is the copy of the current q9 field inside
  the outgoing packet, not an independent draw (the V-copy constraint of `53bf7ac8`). Every flag
  and timing value of the record contract is included. A read not attempted on a terminal branch
  records ⊥, and earlier records stay. No new flag or acceptance predicate is introduced; an
  instance that needs one ends in GAP (acceptance flag).
- **Outgoing boundary B_out.** The outgoing carrier and both complete slices of A, at the outgoing
  cut. **Residual.** The global two-slice cut at the outgoing cut is retained as the native pushforward
  of the original state, never replaced by an independent bath. The private triangular inverse
  appears only inside proofs and is never an operation. NC-1 compares no residual field directly;
  equality or difference on the compared object says nothing about the residual.

### 3.5 Compared objects

Let X' = (Z_rest, K0, W^-, Y_rec, B_out), and let k0 range over the incoming carrier values
together with ⊥, the value on the 16 terminal source contexts. For s in {0,1} let
mu_s(x) = Pr(X'=x | sigma=s), and let mu_s^k0 be its restriction to K0=k0, with the mass of the
restriction retained. No branch is renormalized. With TV(m,n) = (1/2) sum |m-n| over unnormalized
measures, TV_win(k0) = TV(mu_0^k0, mu_1^k0); TV_in(k0) is the TV of the (Z_rest, K0, W^-)
marginals, and TV_acc(k0) the TV of the pushforwards that drop B_out.

**Distinctness** means that TV_in(k0)=0 for every k0, that M holds, and that TV_win(k0)>0 for some
k0. Under M this is equivalent to a difference between the setting-conditioned kernels
Pr(Y_rec, B_out | sigma, Z_rest, K0, W^-) on a set of positive mass. The same native rule, record
contract and successor apply to both arms. NC-1 tests the joint law at the outgoing cut. A later
continuation also reads the residual, so equality on X' does not imply equality after a
continuation. NC-1 claims neither equality nor difference after the successor.

### 3.6 Two lemmas (paper proofs)

**Lemma 1, controlled lift** (#866 comment 6526292899; elementary). For bijections T_s on X, the
map F(x,s)=(T_s x, s) is bijective and conserves s. Hence: (a) writing two interactions as F with
s an initial register is representability only; (b) changing s between successive couplings on
one outgoing carrier needs a further sourced operation, so NC-1's positive token is single-use;
(c) T_0 T_1 ≠ T_1 T_0 is evidence for no token.

**Lemma 2, pad** (elementary; proof given). Split the compared output as Y=(Y_1, Y_2). Suppose
(i) every coordinate of Y_1 is a function of C_sys alone; (ii) for every fixed value (a, c) of
(Xi_A, C_sys), xi -> Y_2 is a bijection from the pad alphabet onto a set not depending on (a, c);
(iii) xi is uniform and independent of (Xi_A, C_sys) (H-prior-NC); (iv) M holds, and Z_rest, K0
and W^- are functions of (Xi_A, C_sys). Then mu_0^k0 = mu_1^k0 for every k0.

*Proof.* Fix (a, c). By (ii) and (iii), Pr(Y_2=y | a, c) is the uniform mass, constant in (a, c).
So Y_2 is uniform and independent of (Xi_A, C_sys), hence of every function of them, sigma
included. Given sigma=s, the remaining coordinates (Z_rest, K0, W^-, Y_1) are functions of
(Z_rest, C_sys), whose law given sigma does not depend on s by M. So the joint law given s is that
law times the uniform law of Y_2, the same for both s. ∎

The lemma is a sufficient condition for equality. Failure of its preconditions is not a
difference.

## 4. Candidate classes, fixed before any evaluation

In each class the carrier, layout, record alphabet, boundary and residual are as in §3. Each class
states what makes it native and available. No class is preferred because of its expected outcome.

**Class A: internal setting from accessible records** (Main §3.3, line 402 at D). Z is one or
more declared accessible records: a port raw triple, old q9 at L or V at L+2. sigma is a coordinate
of Z. The native continuation of the fields that produced Z reaches A, or the carrier boundary
directly, within the window.

Native and available iff Z is in the H-access alphabet and recorded before the window; the field
that acts is the native continuation of Xi_A, with no supplied transport, write or relabelling; M
holds; and Xi_A lies outside the backward cones of K0 and W^-. The two values are sigma=0 and
sigma=1 inside the one P0, and the interactions are distinct iff the kernels of §3.5 differ. A
record whose source lies in the incoming carrier's backward cone, such as old q9 at L, fails the
admissibility filter of §7.

**Class B: program-register preparation** (Main §3.4, line 576 at D). Pi is a set of
original-time cells disjoint from the backward cones of K0 and W^-, whose value selects, under the
native rule, which interaction acts. By Lemma 1(b) it is single-use. The two values are the two
register values inside P0. Under P0 a register cell is of one of three kinds. **Pinned:** a second
value needs a pin change, PREPARATION-CHANGE (apparatus); that change lies outside the retained
contract, needs its own sourcing unit and is not refuted by NC-1. **Source-context variable:**
admissible only if M holds. **Free under H-prior-NC:** a random setting, admissible only if its
value is recorded in an accessible record before the window, and then evaluated under Class A's
conditions with Z equal to that record; if unrecorded, its value is knowable only by a private
read, and the token is PRIVATE-ACCESS.

**Class C: an observer-side subsystem coupled by the native rule.** O is a declared site set,
disjoint from A, B1(q10) and the port sites. Its state at the window opening is a native function of
accessible records, produced by a native record-forming interaction rather than a write. O then
acts on A or on the carrier through the native rule. The two values are those of O's setting
coordinate inside P0.

Native and available iff the map from the accessible records to O's state is native and derived
from frozen sources; O is not padded with respect to those records; O's outgoing state is retained
in B_out; and M holds. Under H-access a port read writes nothing, so the retained contracts contain
no record-forming map into a subsystem distinct from the read field. If stage 1 cannot write that
map from frozen sources, Class C records GAP (native record-forming observer memory) without
evaluation.

## 5. Decision rule (fixed before any computation)

Exactly one token is issued, by the first step that decides. Every step reads structured fields
(§7), and no step refers to an expected value.

- **D0 (gate).** If any control in §6 fails or is vacuous, if the instance was evaluated before
  its stage-1 freeze, or if the run departs from the frozen instance: **VOID**.
- **D1 (source audit, CP-SRC).** If any element of the setting path is not generated by the
  native rule from P0 (an Av1 failure): **UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR**.
- **D2 (access audit, CP-READSET).** If selection, a compared record or the boundary requires a
  private read, private inverse or private decoder (an Av2 or Av3 failure): **PRIVATE-ACCESS**.
- **D3 (preparation).**
  - (a) If the two setting values need different source laws, pins, priors or inventories:
    **PREPARATION-CHANGE (source)**, or **(apparatus)** when only apparatus cells differ.
  - (b) If the M proof has a measured failing precondition, the bounded search of D5 is run on the
    incoming object: (Z_rest, K0, W^-) together with the C_sys coordinates that the failing
    precondition names. An exact witness that their law differs across sigma gives
    **PREPARATION-CHANGE (correlated)**.
  - (c) If M is not established and no such witness exists: **GAP (matched preparation)**, naming
    the failing precondition.
- **D4 (equality).** The stage-1 instance names a split Y=(Y_1, Y_2) and the pads of Y_2; the pad
  set may be empty, in which case Y_1=Y. If P-DET, P-PADX, P-TRI, P-PERM and P-GEO (§8) all hold,
  Lemma 2 gives mu_0^k0 = mu_1^k0 for every k0, ⊥ included: **PASSIVE-ONLY**.
- **D5 (difference).** Otherwise, take the output coordinates in the frozen witness order. Let Y*
  be the first coordinate that a failing D4 precondition involves: Xi_A or xi reaches it
  (P-DET), or its pad fails P-TRI or P-PADX. A failure of P-PERM or P-GEO that involves no output
  coordinate gives **GAP**, naming it. Let S be every free original variable in its structural
  backward cone, together with the source context. The search runs only if S has at most six free
  triples and the joint law of S with (sigma, Z_rest, K0) is derived exactly from P0 and
  H-prior-NC, as unnormalized masses for each k0 class, in a written derivation with every input
  checked. It evaluates the native rule over every assignment in exact fractions and compares the
  laws of (k0 class, Y*) under sigma=0 and sigma=1, averaged over all of S; a difference at a
  fixed background value is not a witness.
  - **Deletion test.** Every member of Xi_A inside S is replaced by an independent copy with the
    same law that does not feed Z. The witness counts only if the difference then vanishes;
    otherwise **GAP (attribution)**.
  - No witness, a bound exceeded or a failed derivation gives **GAP**, naming the coordinate, the
    failing precondition and the search outcome.
- **D6 (reusable boundary).** Given a counted witness: **NC-AVAILABLE** if P-SUCC holds, otherwise
  **GAP (reusable boundary)**. P-SUCC: the outgoing carrier is the declared successor's input
  object; eta_succ is disjoint from Xi_A, xi, A and every window record and output, and reaches
  none of them; eta_succ stays product-uniform given every positive-mass window history and sigma;
  terminal branches offer no carrier.

GAP always names its missing element and scope, and it is never a general no-go.

**Reported flags (they decide no token).** *Visibility:* ACCESSIBLE if the counted witness is a
Y_rec coordinate, BOUNDARY-ONLY if it lies in B_out, n/a otherwise. *Carrier dependence:*
K-COUPLED if the witness derivation shows a carrier variable entering the sigma-dependent part (two
k0 classes with different sigma-difference measures), K-INDEPENDENT if it shows only setting and
apparatus variables entering it, UNKNOWN otherwise. *Selection form:* RECORD-FUNCTION (Classes A
and C) or FREE-REGISTER (Class B, free and recorded).

**What each token licenses.**

- **NC-AVAILABLE:** one single-use, setting-dependent native interaction on the fixed incoming
  carrier, available under §2.3 for this instance, layout and dictionary. With K-INDEPENDENT it is
  a setting-dependent carrier perturbation, not a setting-dependent read; with BOUNDARY-ONLY the
  difference is not visible in the window's records. It is not derived, not re-settable and not a
  quantum instrument, and it shows no noncommutation, contextuality or composability.
- **PASSIVE-ONLY:** the setting is sourced and matched but does not act, for this instance only.
  **UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR, PREPARATION-CHANGE, PRIVATE-ACCESS:** this instance fails the named
  availability condition. **GAP:** only its named gap. **VOID:** nothing.

No token changes a preserved result, the 42-bit benchmark, a #866 quotient, a manuscript claim or
a status label.

## 6. Controls and countercontrols

Each control can fail, and all run before the token is computed. Fixtures are small exact native
worlds or exact algebraic fixtures. They test the mechanism and the comparator, not the
astronomical preparation.

- **POS (supplied classical actuator).** A supplied XOR of sigma into an unpadded carrier boundary
  field: TV_win>0 and a witness, and the classifier returns UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR at D1, never
  NC-AVAILABLE.
- **NEG (passive public selector).** The structure of `53bf7ac8`, an independent public label whose
  native continuation reaches only padded outputs: D1–D3 pass, D4 applies, PASSIVE-ONLY with
  TV_win=0 for every k0.
- **LIFT (controlled lift).** F(x,s)=(T_s x, s) with noncommuting T_0, T_1 and s assigned per arm:
  TV_win>0, and the classifier returns UNSOURCED-ACTUATOR, or PREPARATION-CHANGE when s is pinned
  by a changed source law. LIFT-SEQ: T_0 then T_1 on one outgoing carrier is flagged as needing an
  operation outside F. The noncommutation sets no token or flag.
- **DELETE (source deletion).** On the POS-like native fixture with a native transport in place
  of the supplied XOR, an independent copy of the setting source takes TV_win to 0.
  DELETE-COUNTER: a confounded difference survives deletion and is returned as GAP (attribution).
- **PREP.** Two arms realized by two E/N source laws give PREPARATION-CHANGE (source), whatever the
  output TV. **CONF (hidden confound).** sigma correlated with a carrier-relevant C_sys variable,
  with equal (K0, W^-) marginals, fails M and gives PREPARATION-CHANGE (correlated).
- **PRIV (private decoder).** A selection input read from q10, a pad or the chi_H coordinate is
  flagged by CP-READSET and gives PRIVATE-ACCESS.
- **MARG.** A joint-only difference with equal per-coordinate marginals: the joint comparator must
  give TV>0 and a marginal comparator 0; if the marginal one does not miss it, the run is VOID.
  **LEAK.** A difference carried only through Z_rest is detected with Z_rest and missed without.
- **NULL.** Identical arms give TV=0 and no witness. **SEARCH-COUNTER.** For Y = sigma XOR d with
  d uniform the search returns no witness, and with d omitted from S it rejects the cone as
  incomplete. **PAD.** A fully padded fixture gives TV 0; removing one pad gives TV>0 and fails
  P-TRI.
- **VCOPY.** Detaching V from the q9 field it copies is caught. **SUCC.** Reusing a successor key
  inside the window fails P-SUCC. **MODEL.** Importing a scalar torus readout or prior is refused.
- **BRANCH.** All 64 contexts are carried, 16 at k0=⊥, with unnormalized masses summing to the
  source-context masses; no ⊥ branch acquires a read or a carrier.

**Selection rule (outcome-independent).**

- Classes are taken in the order A, C, B. A uses only declared records and native transport; C
  adds a declared observer subsystem; B's one admissible form is evaluated under A's conditions
  (§4), so it comes last. Within a class: Layout I before Layout II, then the earliest record tick,
  then the lowest port index, then the axis order (+x,-y,-z,-x,+y,+z) of the accepted records.
- An instance is admissible only if it uses no excluded mechanism of §4; it does not coincide with
  a preserved result in (setting record, layout, dictionary); the frozen records, or a symbolic
  paper comparison, place Xi_A outside the backward cones of K0 and W^-; and its earliest symbolic
  arrival at A or at the carrier falls inside the window.
- The first admissible instance is frozen. A symbolic claim that fails at stage 2 gives GAP for its
  named precondition and never changes the instance. If no class has an admissible instance, NC-1
  ends at stage 1 with GAP (no admissible instance), naming the missing element of each class.
