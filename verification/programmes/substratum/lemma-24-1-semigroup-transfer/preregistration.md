## Why this round exists

The completeness half of Theorem 24 — that *every* observables-preserving transformation of the
dilation-datum substratum lies in the group generated by the four families (i)–(iv) — is
conditional on Lemma 24.1, and the manuscript itself names the load-bearing step: step (2), that
the Stinespring/GNS uniqueness-up-to-environment-unitary, stated for a single CP map, **transfers
to the whole discrete semigroup** `{Φ_t}_{t ∈ ℕ}` generated by one substratum bijection `φ`, so
that fixing the visible channel family fixes the substratum dilation up to the stated gauge
families. Steps (1) and (3) are described there as bookkeeping given (2). The manuscripts flag (2)
for specialist verification; `Structure.md`'s remark explains why the semigroup form is expected to
say more than Proposition 9.7b; `Complexity.md` closes an argument conditionally on it. The
`ROADMAP` ranks it above larger obligations for exactly the reason that a mathematical step
carrying an exhaustiveness claim is what this Lean programme exists to close.

What the kernel already holds is the **single-map** statement and no more: `proposition_9_7b`
gives, for two cyclic pure-reference dilations of *one* channel, a unique environment unitary
relating the *isometries*, and the CNOT pair in `stinespring_scope_probe.py` is the permanent
record that nothing follows about the dilating unitaries off the reference subspace. The semigroup
step is the claim that a *single generating unitary* and *cyclicity* remove that remainder. This
round asks whether it does, formally, at the stated scope — and it freezes, before running, what
"transfer" must mean, because the difference between two readings of the word is the entire
content.

## The objects, FROZEN

Throughout, `V` and `H` are finite types with `|V| = n ≥ 1`, `|H| = m ≥ 1`; `ℋ_V = ℂ^V`,
`ℋ_H = ℂ^H`; a **dilation datum** is a unitary `U` on `ℂ^{V × H}`, and the substratum instance is
`U = φ̂`, the permutation unitary of a bijection `φ` of `V × H`. Every statement is finite-dimensional
matrix algebra in the coordinate idiom of `StinespringUniqueness.lean`.

**The uniform-prior family.** For `t ∈ ℕ`,

    Φ_t(ρ) = Tr_H [ U^t (ρ ⊗ 𝟙/m) U^{-t} ],

the lemma's `Φ_t` with `ω_H = 𝟙/|𝒞_H|`. Written on matrix units, with `U_{vw}` the `m × m` **visible
block** `(⟨v| ⊗ 𝟙) U (|w⟩ ⊗ 𝟙)`:

    Φ_t(E_{ab})_{cd} = (1/m) · Tr [ (U^t)_{ca} ((U^t)_{db})ᴴ ].

Two data agree — `Φ_t = Φ'_t` for every `t` — exactly when these traces agree for every
`a, b, c, d, t`. This is the Gram identity `KrausUniqueness.krausMap_single` already records for
one map, read at every `t`.

**The purification, which is the GNS object.** With `Ω = m^{-1/2} Σ_h |h⟩|h⟩ ∈ ℋ_H ⊗ ℋ_{H'}`, the
uniform-prior family of `U` is the **pure-reference** family of `U ⊗ 𝟙_{H'}` on
`ℋ_V ⊗ (ℋ_H ⊗ ℋ_{H'})` with reference `Ω`; its Stinespring isometry at time `t` is
`V_t ψ = (U^t ⊗ 𝟙)(ψ ⊗ Ω)`, and `Φ_t = stinespringChannel V_t` in the merged module's sense. The
Kraus family of `Φ_t` is the `m²`-member family `m^{-1/2} (U^t)_{·}` indexed by `H × H`. **The
reference state of the substratum is mixed**, exactly as `Main.md` §3.2 and `Structure.md` §10.4
record; the purified environment has dimension `m²`, not `m`.

**Reachable cyclicity — the manuscript's `𝒞_B`.** The span of `{ U^t (ℋ_V ⊗ supp ω_H) : t ≥ 0 }`.
With uniform `ω_H` its support is all of `ℋ_H`, and for a bijection every configuration is reached,
so **`𝒞_B = 𝒞_H` for every substratum instance**, and step (3)'s "non-cyclic remainder" is empty on
every instance. This is recorded here as a fact about the definition, not as a finding.

**GNS cyclicity — the hypothesis the argument's own words name.** The reference subspace
`ℋ_V ⊗ Ω` is cyclic for the `*`-algebra generated by `B(ℋ_V) ⊗ 𝟙` and `U ⊗ 𝟙`. **Frozen
reduction (recorded analysis):** this holds **iff the hidden commutant of `U` is trivial**,

    HiddenCommutantTrivial U  :⟺  ∀ Y ∈ M_m(ℂ), (𝟙 ⊗ Y) U = U (𝟙 ⊗ Y) → ∃ c, Y = c·𝟙.

The reason, recorded so the execution cannot substitute another: the commutant of the generated
algebra tensored with `𝟙_{H'}` is `(𝟙 ⊗ 𝒞_U) ⊗ B(ℋ_{H'})` with `𝒞_U` the hidden commutant; a vector
is cyclic for an algebra iff separating for its commutant; `Ω` is maximally entangled, so
`(Y ⊗ 𝟙 − 𝟙 ⊗ Yᵀ) Ω = 0` for every `Y`, which is a nonzero element of the commutant killing the
reference whenever `𝒞_U` contains a non-scalar; and conversely `(𝟙 ⊗ Z) Ω = 0` forces `Z = 0`.
**The operative predicate of this round is `HiddenCommutantTrivial`;** its identification with
GNS cyclicity is target `ST0(c)`. The two cyclicity notions are **different**, and the gap between
them is one of the round's findings-in-waiting: a hidden permutation commuting with `φ̂` leaves
`𝒞_B = 𝒞_H` untouched and makes the GNS object non-cyclic.

**Hidden conjugation — Reading B, the manuscript's conclusion.** `U' = (𝟙 ⊗ W) U (𝟙 ⊗ W)ᴴ` for a
unitary `W` on `ℋ_H`. On the purified environment this is Kraus mixing by the *single*,
*time-independent*, *product* unitary `W ⊗ W̄` at every `t`.

**Per-time Kraus mixing — Reading A, what single-map uniqueness gives.** For each `t` separately,
a unitary `W_t` on the `m²`-dimensional purified environment with `X'_t = X_t W_t`, `X_t` the
`(n × n) × (H × H)` amplitude matrix of the Kraus family of `Φ_t`. Nothing in Reading A makes `W_t`
constant in `t`, nor of the product form `W ⊗ W̄`.

**The block-word functional.** A *word* is a finite product of visible blocks `U_{vw}` and their
adjoints; its *net power* is (number of plain letters) − (number of adjoint letters); it is
*balanced* when the net power is `0`; its *trace* is `Tr` of the product in `M_m(ℂ)`. Hidden
conjugation preserves every word trace. **Family equality is equality of one specific sub-family
of balanced word traces** — the sorted words `(U^t)_{ca} ((U^t)_{db})ᴴ`, expanded as sums over
internal visible indices of words with `t` plain letters followed by `t` adjoint letters. The
interleaved balanced words are the multi-time correlation data the family does not carry. **The
whole content of "semigroup transfer" is whether the sorted sub-family determines the rest.**

**Decoupled deep-sector enlargement — generator (iii) at the exact-decoupling reading.** Adjoin a
finite `D` with a unitary `δ` on `ℂ^D`: `U ⊗ δ` on `ℂ^{V × (H × D)}`. Its visible blocks are
`U_{vw} ⊗ δ`. **Frozen lemma (recorded analysis):** for a *balanced* word `w`,
`Tr[w(U ⊗ δ)] = |D| · Tr[w(U)]`, because equal numbers of `δ` and `δᴴ` cancel in any order. So a
balanced word whose traces differ between `U` and `U'` certifies that `U' ⊗ δ'` is **not** hidden-
conjugate to `U ⊗ δ` for any `D`, any `δ, δ'` on it. Unbalanced words carry `Tr[δ^j]` and are not
enlargement-stable.

**The generator classes at fixed alphabet and fixed graph, as this round can state them.** (i)
restricted to the visible factor is conjugation by `P_σ ⊗ 𝟙` for a visible permutation `σ`; (i)
restricted to the hidden factor, read as the manuscript's "hidden-sector partial isometry", is
hidden conjugation by a unitary `W` (which includes hidden permutations); (iii) is decoupled
enlargement as above. Generators (ii) and (iv) are lattice-level notions — alphabet size and
coupling-graph statistical class — with **no meaning on a finite toy carrier**, and this round says
nothing about them in either direction.

## The recorded analysis — three pairs, computed before this freeze was written

Three pairs of substratum instances were computed against the objects above before this file was
written, so that no prediction below is a guess and the execution cannot quietly substitute other

**Pair A — the CNOT pair, on `V = H = ℤ₂`.** `φ(v,h) = (v, h ⊕ v)` and `φ'(v,h) = (v, h ⊕ v ⊕ 1)`.
Both are involutions, so the family has period two; `Φ_1 = Φ'_1` is dephasing in the visible basis
and `Φ_0 = Φ_2 = id`, under the uniform prior and also under the pure reference `|0⟩`. Reachable
cyclicity holds trivially (`𝒞_B = 𝒞_H`). The `(0,0)` visible block of `φ̂` is `𝟙` and that of `φ̂'` is
`X`; hidden conjugation preserves each block's trace, and `Tr 𝟙 = 2 ≠ 0 = Tr X`, so **no hidden
unitary conjugates one to the other on the fixed carrier**. Both hidden commutants are
`span{𝟙, X}`, so neither is GNS-cyclic. **The pair is absorbed by generator (i)'s visible part:**
`φ̂' = (X ⊗ 𝟙) φ̂ (X ⊗ 𝟙)`, and the dephasing family is `X`-covariant. It is also absorbed by (i)+(iii):
adjoining `D = ℤ₂` with the swap and conjugating by the hidden permutation `CNOT_{D→H}` carries
`φ̂ ⊗ swap` to `φ̂' ⊗ swap`. No balanced word of length at most six separates the pair, as the
absorption requires.

**Pair B — the controlled-shift pair, on `V = ℤ₂`, `H = ℤ₃`.** `φ(v,h) = (v, h + v)` and
`φ'(v,h) = (v, h + v + 1)`. Same family for every `t` (dephasing when `3 ∤ t`, identity otherwise).
Different cycle types — `(1,1,1,3)` against `(3,3)` — so **no unitary of any kind** conjugates one
to the other on the fixed carrier, and in particular no generator-(i) relabelling of `V × H`. Both
hidden commutants contain the shift; neither is GNS-cyclic. **Absorbed by (i)+(iii):** adjoining
`D = ℤ₃` with the shift and relabelling `(h, d) ↦ (h + d, d)` carries `φ̂ ⊗ shift` to `φ̂' ⊗ shift`.
No balanced word of length at most six separates the pair, as the absorption requires.

**Pair C — the spectral pair, on `V = ℤ₂`, `H = ℤ₃`.** `φ` is the involution exchanging
`(0,2) ↔ (1,0)` and `(1,1) ↔ (1,2)` and fixing `(0,0)`, `(0,1)`; `φ'` additionally exchanges
`(0,0) ↔ (0,1)`. Both are involutions; `Φ_1 = Φ'_1` is the classical symmetric channel
`[[2/3, 1/3], [1/3, 2/3]]` composed with full dephasing, so the visible statistics `T_{ij}(t)` are
non-trivial and identical. **Both hidden commutants are trivial** — a finite linear-algebra fact —
so both are GNS-cyclic. `Tr φ̂ = 2 ≠ 0 = Tr φ̂'`, so **no unitary whatsoever** conjugates one to the
other on the fixed carrier. A balanced word of length six,
`Tr[U_{00} U_{10} U_{01} U_{00}ᴴ U_{01}ᴴ U_{10}ᴴ]`, takes the value `1` on one and `0` on the other,
and the separation persists under both visible relabellings; so the pair is **not** related by
`P_σ ⊗ W` for any `σ`, any `W`, on the fixed carrier **or after any decoupled enlargement**. The
mechanism is a hidden involution acting inside one visible fibre and commuting with `φ̂`, which the
dephasing family cannot see. (Also recorded: on `V = H = ℤ₂`, the 3-cycle `(0,1) → (1,0) → (1,1)`
and its inverse form a same-family pair with trivial hidden commutants separated by the balanced
word `Tr[U_{00} U_{00} U_{01}ᴴ U_{11}ᴴ]`; it is a secondary witness, and it is a *time-reversal*
pair, which the manuscript subsumes under (i) only for the wave rule via the layer swap `T`.)

An exhaustive scratch survey of every permutation of `ℤ₂ × ℤ₂`, `ℤ₂ × ℤ₃` and `ℤ₃ × ℤ₂`, grouped
by family and then by `(σ_V × σ_H)`-conjugacy class, found same-family class pairs with both
hidden commutants trivial only on `ℤ₂ × ℤ₂` (one pair, the inverse pair) and `ℤ₂ × ℤ₃`
(twenty-four pairs), and **every one of the twenty-five** was separated by a balanced word of
length at most six under every visible relabelling — length four sufficing for all but two. The
survey is scratch and is not an execution object; it is recorded so the reader knows the
prediction's provenance, and it is the reason `ST3`/`ST4` are predicted at the strength they are.

### `ST5` — the positive branch, with the hypothesis under which transfer does hold

> For `U, U'` on the same carrier, if **every** block-word trace agrees — the full hidden-tracial
