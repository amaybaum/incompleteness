## Current gap

PR #818 added the roadmap agenda for discriminating empirical access to the hidden sector. Finite-horizon representability alone does not distinguish deterministic hidden structure from fundamental randomness, and none of the proposed routes has an executed test.

- The roadmap names H-GRAV, H-NEQ and H-FIN, plus the cross-cutting classical-memory test. This umbrella note tracks all four.
- Every prediction, source assumption and comparison with data needs its own concrete decision rule before analysis. No new data are analysed here.

## Research question and proposed work

Which proposed route yields a discriminating prediction from named premises, or can be shown not to discriminate at the stated scope?

- [ ] H-GRAV: determine whether the premises select a microstate, hidden-average or emergent-description gravitational completion; derive that completion's predictions and distinguish environmental from additional nonunitary coherence loss.
- [ ] H-NEQ: resolve the posited-versus-dynamical measure reading, then conditionally derive relaxation, initial-deviation amplitude and scale dependence before making a relic or signalling prediction.
- [ ] H-FIN: separate recurrence under bijectivity, rational-probability signatures under a rational prior, model-specific dispersion and finite-capacity signatures; derive accessible magnitudes and discriminating power.
- [ ] Classical-memory test: fix an operational criterion separating fundamental processes from engineered quantum environments before analysing data, so classifications cannot be adjusted after the result.

## Controls and evidence obligations

- [ ] A null/comparison model and a genuinely discriminating observable for each route; representability alone cannot earn discrimination.
- [ ] Explicit coupling, measure, prior, lattice and capacity assumptions, with uncertainty and systematics accounted for.
- [ ] A fixed analysis/decision rule before new data are read; derive experimental reach rather than assuming either detectability or undetectability.
