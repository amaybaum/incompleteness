## 3. Definition: the record-forming interaction

### 3.1 Data

A **record-forming interaction** (RFI) is a tuple `𝔐 = (S, φ, μ, π, Z, Acc, ρ, Out, Res)`, frozen as
a whole:

- **`S = S_Σ × S_A × S_E × S_Z × S_L`**, the complete microscopic state: system `Σ` (the carrier);
  apparatus `A` (probe, amplifier stages, pointer); environment `E` (every other degree of freedom
  coupled within the horizon, heat sinks and stray copies included); setting source `Z` (the degrees
  of freedom whose state selects the operation); ledgers `L` (clocks, controller seeds, resource
  banks, history). The factorization is a declared partition (Main §1.4). `S` is finite, or a stated
  measurable space.
- **`φ`**, one fixed native bijection of `S`: the native rule, or an explicit relation to it. Every
  setting change, readout, reset and inverse is a composition of `φ`-steps under the access; an
  external switch between abstract maps is excluded (N7).
- **`μ`**, the prior on `S`, with every correlation among `Σ, A, E, Z, L`. It and the observation map
  are fixed by PC-1's fork rule (preregistration §4 at `99dce4b6`), applied unchanged: per leg, a
  selection principle with its necessity assessment, or invariance over an admissible class with at
  least two inequivalent members.
- **`π : S → C_V`**, the observation map, a function defining the visible sector (Main:44, Lemma 2 at Main:50).
- **`Z`**, the setting source, with its dynamics under `φ` and its correlation with
  response-relevant variables under `μ`; that correlation is decided, not assumed (§5.6).
- **`Acc`**, the continuation class: the executable adaptive policies over native operations up to
  a finite budget `T`, with controller law, stopping rules and the permitted reference and
  environment interactions; closed under stopping and record-conditioned continuation.
- **`ρ : S → R`**, the accessible readout, factoring through `π`. `R` is the complete public
  alphabet per step: outcome symbols, failure `⊥`, no-event `∅`, heralds, attempt and rejection
  marks, timing ticks.
- **`Out`**, the outgoing system: the post-interaction carrier `Σ'` and any reusable apparatus part,
  with its branchwise unnormalized conditional preparation, offered to the same `Acc`.
- **`Res`**, the resource ledger: fresh resources consumed per use, under #912's conditional
  freshness condition (`P(U | H = h, X_rel = x) = ν` on every reachable positive-probability pair,
  `X_rel` the non-seed variables the stage uses); ready-state supply; information moved into `E` or
  `L` at reset (§3.3).

### 3.2 Record conditions

A value `r_t = ρ(φ^t s)` at an accessible time `t` is a **record** of a property `P` (a partition of
`S_Σ` at `t_0`, or a reference record) when:

- **R1 correlation.** Under `μ`, the law of `r_t` depends on the `P`-cell at `t_0`.
- **R2 persistence.** Under every policy in `Acc` that does not act on the record's support, `r` is
  recoverable at every later accessible time up to `T`.
- **R3 accessibility.** `ρ` is executed by a native readout process; no private decoder.
- **R4 amplification index `k`.** The number of disjoint subsystems of `A × E` each of whose
  accessible readout alone recovers `r`. `k = 1` is the premeasurement level; the classical index is
  the bookkeeping analogue of the redundancy discussed under einselection (Zurek 2003).
- **R5 completeness.** Every attempted, rejected, heralded, failed and successful branch is kept with
  its unnormalized mass; no success renormalization.

Stages: ready → premeasurement (correlation of `Σ` with a probe, in von Neumann's sense) →
amplification (`k` grows) → registration (R2 holds) → outgoing.

### 3.3 Resources, heat and reset

**Lemma R (proved on paper here).** Let `S = S_R × S_O` with `S_R` the record register. No bijection
`g` of `S` satisfies `g(r, o) = g(r', o) = (r_0, o)` for distinct record values `r ≠ r'` and one
`o`. *Proof.* Injectivity. `□`

So a reset of the record to a ready value with everything else unchanged is impossible: the
distinguishing information moves into `E` or `L`. The reset cost is booked as that transfer. Heat
is not defined by the OI inputs at `D` (no energy function or temperature is among them), so `Res`
books information transfer only; Landauer's thermodynamic reading is not used and was not read
first-hand. For finite `S` under a bijection, and for measurable `S` under a bijection that preserves
the reference measure, the entropy of the pushed-forward `μ` is invariant, so a ready supply of
low-entropy pointer states is a resource, entered in `Res`.

### 3.4 Controls and inverses

An operation is **available** only if executed: a finite composition of `φ`-steps on the declared
state, under the declared access, with a sourced setting. The mathematical inverse exists (for the
native wave step, `ψ(x, y) = (Ax − y, x)`, PC★2a P-1) but is an allowed continuation only with a
native implementation (#866 relation, §5.3); a reversal of all of `S` is not an operation of an
embedded observer unless some subsystem executes it. The kernel's semantics is consistent with this:
availability of a unitary conjugation forces a nonzero multiple of the unitary into the
implementation class (`exists_scaled_mem_of_instAvail_unitary`), and inverse availability needs dagger
stability and unitary-ray saturation, which are properties of the class, not consequences of
reversibility. A probabilistic reversal (Koashi–Ueda 1999; Katz et al. 2008) counts with its success
mass, and its failure branches are records.

### 3.5 Absorption, loss of interference and irreversibility, kept separate

- **(i) Absorption.** On the branch, the carrier's conserved occupation is transferred out of `Σ`
  into `A × E` during the interaction (whether it returns is a question for (iii)).
- **(ii) Loss of observed interference**, relative to a declared recombination test `𝒯 ∈ Acc` with a
  sourced phase setting: the `𝒯`-contrast of the post-interaction preparation is below that of the
  pre-interaction preparation. **Decoherence** is (ii) together with R1 between the path and some
  subsystem of `A × E`; **dephasing** is (ii) with no such record.
- **(iii) Effective irreversibility**, relative to `(Acc, Res)`: no policy in `Acc` within budget
  returns the joint post-interaction preparation to one equivalent (§5.3) to the pre-interaction
  preparation; *probabilistically* irreversible: none does with positive success mass.

| (i) | (ii) | (iii) | Example (standard unless marked) |
|---|---|---|---|
| no | yes | no | reversible which-path marking by a two-level marker, undone by repeating the controlled coupling; Scully–Englert–Walther 1991 propose marking by a cavity photon |
| no | yes | no if the kick value is accessible to `Acc` (the inverse kick is then executable), yes otherwise | dephasing by a random classical phase kick: no record of the path anywhere |
| no | yes | yes | redundant environmental copies (`k ≫ 1`) with `Acc` lacking joint control of the fragments (Zurek 2003) |
| yes | no | yes | on the common segment before the first splitter, absorption and re-emission of the carrier, with an irreversibly amplified record of the path-independent absorption time |
| yes | no | no | on the common segment before the first splitter, absorption by a two-level absorber followed by coherent re-emission (vacuum Rabi oscillation; textbook, no source read for this note) |
| no | no | yes | an irreversible record of a path-independent property, such as a passage time recorded on the common segment before the first splitter |
| yes | yes | yes | an absorbing detector in one arm |

**Proposition S (by the table).** No implication holds among (i), (ii), (iii) in either direction,
and (ii) does not imply a record. Consequences: loss of interference is not evidence of record
formation, a record is not evidence of irreversibility, and absorption is evidence of neither.

**Complementarity benchmark (standard; derived on paper).** For
`|Ψ⟩ = (|L⟩|d_L⟩ + e^{iθ}|R⟩|d_R⟩)/√2` with unit marker vectors, a balanced recombination gives
port probabilities `½(1 ± Re(e^{iθ}⟨d_L|d_R⟩))`, so the visibility is `V = |⟨d_L|d_R⟩|`; the
Helstrom success probability for equal priors is `½(1 + √(1 − |⟨d_L|d_R⟩|²))`, so the
distinguishability `D = 2P_succ − 1` satisfies `D² + V² = 1`. Englert 1996 gives the general
inequality `D² + V² ≤ 1`. **Physical-justification clause:** in a candidate, `V` (contrast of a
sourced recombination as a sourced phase setting varies) and `D` (best discrimination over `Acc`
acting on the marker) are both executed quantities on the frozen interface. An overlap
`0 < |⟨d_R|d_L⟩| < 1` assigned to marker vectors, or a partial overlap of two classical conditional
distributions, is not evidence. A classical model need not obey the relation: a non-invasive copy
of the path leaves a classical fringe intact (§5.8, CC1). A complementarity match of `D` and `V` is
a consistency requirement, not a witness.

**Predictability–visibility witness (carrier preparations, not markers).** Catani, Leifer, Scala,
Schmid and Spekkens 2023 treat a different pair of quantities: for a preparation of the carrier
alone (a splitter of reflectivity `r` and a phase `ϕ`), the which-way predictability
`P = |P(L) − P(R)|` and the which-phase predictability `V`. Quantum states obey `V² + P² ≤ 1`; for a
preparation that satisfies their A21 orbit-realizability condition (a quadruple of preparations with
equal predictabilities up to sign and a stated operational equivalence among them), noncontextuality
implies `V + P ≤ 1` (their Props. V.1–V.2, proved in Catani et al., *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 129, 240401 (2022), not yet read; §9), which quantum states with `0 < V < 1` on the curve
exceed. `P` is not the marker distinguishability `D` above: for the §3.5 state the carrier has
`P = 0`. The relation is therefore admitted as a T7 instance (W1, §5.7) on sourced carrier
preparations, only when the orbit-realizability quadruple and its operational equivalence are
certified by §5.3; a bare numeric match to the quantum curve is not admitted, and no bound on
`V + D` is claimed.

## 4. A, B and C

### 4.1 The OI premises in play (at `D`)

- **(P-set)** kinematics: `S` is a set of states (finite under the total-finiteness posit, Main:62).
- **(P-bij)** Lemma 3 (Main:56): `φ` is a function and a bijection.
- **(P-obs)** Definition and Lemmas 1–2 (Main:44–50): the observer `V ⊊ S` has finitely many
  distinguishable internal states, and registered content is `V`'s state, so records are values of a
  function `π` of the configuration.
- **(P-μ)** the prior as a realization datum (Main:60).
- Settings are internal (part of `S`), or exogenous labels written by bijections `I_a` (Main:482),
  or a program-register preparation (Main:582).

### 4.2 The three classes

- **(A)** coarse-grained classical deterministic record formation: `(S, φ, μ, π)` satisfying P-set,
  P-bij, P-obs and P-μ, with records `ρ ∘ φ^t`.
- **(B)** measurement kernels: records drawn by kernels `K(r, s' | s, a)` on a declared state space
  that omits latent variables, with fresh randomness per use.
- **(C)** a non-functional access relation: a relation `R ⊆ S × O` whose realized outcome is
  determined neither by the complete state nor by any enlargement admitted as physical.

### 4.3 Results

**Proposition A1 (records are functions of the complete state; proved on paper here).** Under
P-set, P-bij and P-obs with internal settings, every record `r_t = ρ(φ^t(s_0))` is a function of
`s_0`; with exogenous labels applied as bijections `I_a`, it is a function of `(s_0, a_{≤t})`.
*Proof.* Composition of functions. `□` Relative to the visible state `x_0 = π(s_0)` a record is in
general not a function of `x_0`: that is OI's emergent stochasticity (Main §2.1), class (A).
**"Record not a function of configuration" is new physics only when "configuration" means the
complete state `s_0`.**

**Proposition A2 (which premise C changes; proved on paper here).** Let `S` be the total system
(Main:44), so that no stochastic input enters from outside `S`. Suppose a formed record persists in
the configuration (R2 with P-obs: the record is part of `V`'s later state) and the configuration
evolves by a function (the determinism half of Lemma 3). Then the record is a function of the
complete pre-interaction state, or of `(s_0, a_{≤t})` when settings enter as exogenous labels applied
by bijections `I_a`. *Proof.* A1 applied to the stored value. `□` Hence a C-model
abandons at least one of: **P-bij** (determinism; Main:62's argument for it then needs an answer),
**P-obs** (records as values of `V`'s configuration), **P-set** (states as points of a set, for
example an amplitude-carrying ontic state). Weakening injectivity alone does not help. A C proposal
names which; that is the OI premise it changes.

**Proposition B1 (A and B coincide as classes of finite record laws; proved, Main:482, Main:518, Main:520, Main:558).**
For finite alphabets, a finite action menu and a finite horizon, every B-model's joint law of
action-labelled records is reproduced by an A-model: the state is enlarged by a response table
`u = (u_c)` over history-and-action contexts, with product prior independent of the actions and of
`x_0`, plus ledgers and clock (Main:520 for arbitrary real kernels). Deterministic adaptive policies
are covered because the contexts are history-indexed (Main:558). Conversely A is B with
deterministic kernels.

**Proposition B2 (availability-level reduction; sufficient, not necessary; proved on paper from #912's proof).**
If the B-model's randomness is a physical latent resource `U` in `L` that is conditionally fresh at
every use (#912's condition, §3.1), and `U` is outside the access (or its reads are records), then the
A-model with `U` in `S` is an available realization of the B-model's record law under every policy
in `Acc` whose controller randomness is independent of `U` given the public history. **The converse
fails in general:** a kernel that does not read `U` reproduces its law whatever `U`'s correlations,
and #912 states that statistical freshness "is a sufficient construction method, not necessary for
every possible instrument" (REPORT.md, "Fresh resources"). Failure of freshness can still change a
record law (#912's heralding control, TV 1/2).

So B reduces to A by enlarging the state: at the representation level always (B1); at the
availability level when conditional freshness and controller independence hold (B2), a sufficient
condition that is not necessary.

**Proposition U (finite-law universality of A; corollary of Main:482, Main:518, Main:566).** Every
model, B, C or the quantum benchmark, whose records over a fixed finite adaptive menu and horizon
have a well-defined joint law with all branch masses has an A-realization reproducing that joint
law, including the outgoing carrier's law under the menu's continuations (exactly by Main:482 with
Main:520's arbitrary-kernel form, quantum laws included; Main:566 gives one fixed map per instrument
at accuracy `ε`). **No record-law test on
a fixed finite menu separates C from A.** A separation exists only relative to a frozen constraint
set `K` on the A side:

- **K1** the dynamics is the one fixed native `φ`, not a construction made per law (the battery
  freezes `π` and `μ` for `𝔐★`; Q1 lets them range over their admissible classes, §4.4);
- **K2** causal-cone locality with measurement independence (Main:368);
- **K3** one finite ontic cardinality for every menu (Main:488, Main:560);
- **K4** generalized noncontextuality relative to declared operational equivalences;
- **K5** noninvasive measurability (Leggett–Garg);
- **K6** a bounded resource and reset budget.

*Corollary U1.* A Leggett–Garg violation is reached by A with invasive instruments (Main:486,
`K = 3/2`): it refutes K5 only (the clumsiness loophole; Wilde–Mizel 2012).
*Corollary U2.* Contextuality is reached by A: A-realizations of quantum record laws exist
(Main:482, Main:566), and no preparation-noncontextual ontological model reproduces quantum
statistics (Spekkens 2005, §IV: six pure qubit states and five decompositions of the maximally
mixed state), so on a menu containing that scenario these realizations are contextual. A
contextuality witness refutes K4-models, not A.
*Corollary U3 (literature, not load-bearing).* Bohm's 1952 completion (not read first-hand) is
deterministic with records functions of configuration given the guiding wave; at the level of
phenomenology, coherent recombination therefore does not require non-functional records, and the
cost there falls on P-set (amplitude-carrying ontic structure) and locality.

- **Q3 hard to vary** (§7.3).

*K3 and the classical-dimension obstruction.* For each fixed `N`, Main:560 gives a finite qubit
experiment that no `N`-state model reproduces exactly. This is not Q1: the total-finiteness posit

from a finite ontic state still factorizes as `B = ΞM` with `Ξ ∈ [0, 1]` (Main:560), so a

## 5. The discriminator battery (fixed before any candidate)

### 5.1 One interface

Every test runs on one RFI `𝔐★` frozen at stage 1, with each element of §3.1, a justification grade
per element (derived, motivated or posited). `K★ ⊆ {K1, …, K6}`, the held constraint set used only by
the primitive-proposal track (Q1, §4.4), is frozen at stage 1 before any candidate law is derived, on
paper or otherwise. Changing any element of `𝔐★` or `K★` after an outcome voids the run (§6, 0(iii)).

### 5.2 The joint-law object

For a sourced preparation procedure `P`, a policy `κ ∈ Acc` with its sourced controller law, and the
budget `T`, `L(P, κ)` is the unnormalized measure over complete histories `h`: setting-source records,
records per step (attempts, rejections, heralds, failures and successes), timing ticks and declared
accessible reference records; and, per terminal branch, the outgoing preparation offered to further
continuation. Every decision path uses exact rational or algebraic arithmetic.

### 5.3 Equivalence (#866, restated)

`P₀ ≡(Acc, h, T) P₁` iff every permitted adaptive continuation in `Acc` yields the same joint law of
complete future accessible records and all declared accessible reference records, conditional on `h`;
all branches and masses retained, failures included; no success renormalization; hidden
environmental correlations propagated through declared permitted interactions; a private
mathematical inverse is not an allowed continuation without its native implementation; composability
requires closure under continuation and branchwise equivalence of outgoing conditional preparations;
zero-probability histories are specified rather than conditioned on; finite-budget equivalence is not
all-time equivalence.
(#866 writes the interface as C; it is `Acc` here, since C names class (C) of §4.)
Conventions (review 6541615446):

- **c1** the same adaptive policy and sourced controller law on both sides; a seed is averaged with
  its advertised law only under conditional independence at the use boundary, and otherwise retained;
- **c2** equivalence after `h` does not compare the probability of reaching `h`; unconditional
  comparisons use unnormalized branch measures or the full history law;
- **c3** one common physically admitted domain; no private microscopic read or zero-default
  preparation granted by definition;
- **c4** finite and discrete only.

Measurement and transformation procedures are equivalent when they yield the same `L` for every
permitted preparation and continuation in `Acc`. These relations, over the whole of `Acc` and not a test
menu, certify the operational equivalences of T7.

### 5.4 Coherence before marking (T0, an obligation)

Before any marking test: a sourced recombination test `𝒯` with a sourced phase setting, and a sourced
dephasing process (an executed operation of `𝔐★`; an inserted dephasing is a diagnostic and does not
count, PC-1 §7), such that the pre-marking preparation and its dephased version are **not** equivalent
under §5.3, witnessed by a test that composes two phase settings (single-phase tests excluded, AW13).
Without T0 no later test counts toward outcome (2).

### 5.5 Tests on `𝔐★`, each reported as the whole `L`

- **T1 graded marking.** A family of sourced marker couplings with a sourced strength setting; `V` and
  `D` as executed quantities on one preparation (§3.5). Consistency only.
- **T2 repeat and sequence.** The RFI applied twice, then with an intervening different setting in
  both orders, each order executed by sourced setting changes; joint law of the records and of `Out`.
- **T3 undo.** A policy in `Acc` returns marker and carrier. The result is a sub-label, not a gate:
  `unconditional` when the post-undo preparation is equivalent (§5.3) to the pre-marking preparation
  as a whole joint law, without postselection, and T0's witness returns; `heralded-probabilistic`
  when this holds on a heralded success branch, reported with its success mass (Koashi–Ueda; Katz et
  al.); `none` otherwise.
- **T4 eraser.** A conjugate readout of the marker; conditional fringes with full unnormalized branch
  masses that sum to the marked law. Consistency only: CC4 and CC5 reproduce erasers.
- **T5 composition.** Two markers with independent sourced settings, in both orders; each stage
  checked against one fixed per-stage description (#912 equation (1), fixed decoder on the admissible
  cone); a composed-phase test across markers.
- **T6 two systems.** Two carriers, or carrier and marker, in separated regions with independent
  setting sources: operational no-signalling (each wing's complete record law independent of the
  other wing's setting) decided exactly; measurement independence of `μ` with respect to the setting
  sources decided on the substratum; a preregistered Bell or steering functional; the premise of
  Main:368 (i)–(iv) that fails is identified from the dynamics, not assumed.
- **T7 contextuality.** A preregistered noncontextuality inequality (preparation, measurement or
  transformation; T3's undo enters as a transformation), in a noise-tolerant form with secondary
  procedures in the manner of Mazurek et al. 2016, its equivalences certified by §5.3 over all of `Acc`.
- **T8 source deletion.** For each sourced element (setting source, marker coupling, undo process,
  dephasing process, recombination), the interface with that element replaced by a null native
  counterpart fixed at stage 1. The capability attributed to the element must vanish; if it persists,
  the attribution fails.
- **T9 inverse audit.** Every inverse used in T3 shows its executing process. A supplied `ψ = φ⁻¹` or
  a private decoder is run as CC6 and must be classified representation-only.

### 5.6 Free choice, no-signalling, composability

Setting independence is decided as a property of `μ` on the substratum (AW-MR7), never assumed.
Composability: `Acc` closed under stopping and continuation, branchwise equivalence of outgoing
conditional preparations, and #912 equation (1) at each stage on the declared admissible cone.

### 5.7 Witnesses

- **W2** a T6 Bell violation, reported with the premise it relinquishes; it does not establish W1

There is no law-level witness of the form "no A-model reproduces this law of `𝔐★`": `𝔐★` is itself an
