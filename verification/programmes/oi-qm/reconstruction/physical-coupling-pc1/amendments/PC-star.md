## PC★2. The structural point that governs selection

This is a paper argument about which A★2 premise a coupling can break. It decides no predicate for
any theory.

1. **What the template requires.** A coupling `C` is generated by the native update `φ` (§3, "not
   an external switch"). Every native update in the sources is a bijection of a classical
   configuration set:
   - the finite substratum (Main §1);
   - SM §4.1's wave rule over `ℤ/q`, checked bijective and additive in the log (SC-1);
   - its profinite and torus completions in SUBSTATE-1 and SUBSTATE-2.

   A joint process `g ∈ Proc` is therefore a measurable bijection of the joint configuration space
   `X = Reg × Apparatus × Remainder`.
2. **Premise (ii) is forced for a native `C`.** A physically executed record is the register value
   after a joint process. That value is a function of the joint configuration. Its computed readout
   projectors (screen item 2) are therefore the indicator projectors of the level sets of that
   function (A★2 step 1). No native readout process has non-indicator projectors.
3. **Premise (i) holds by construction.** The native carrier is a set with a measure: finite, or
   profinite with Haar, or a torus with Haar. It carries no amplitude or phase structure beyond
   what the measure algebra and the group structure determine. Characters of the group are
   functions on it, not physically executed readouts (A★3 on Θ-F and Θ-D; AW1).
4. **Premise (iii) is the only remaining freedom.** The operator that a joint bijection induces on
   register-measurable functions is the compressed Koopman operator `f ↦ E[f ∘ g | 𝓡]`, a positive
   unital Markov operator (A★2 step 2). Any other assignment is a function of the baseline image.
   For example:
   - the polar factor (N7, control C13);
   - the generator `i log` (N4);
   - an eigenbasis rewriting (Θ-F, 't Hooft, Elze; §PC★3).

   Screen item 11 admits such a function only with an executing process. An executing process is
   itself a joint process `g' ∈ Proc`, whose physically induced operator is again a compressed
   Koopman image. So within the template, a non-baseline register operator needs a reason that is
   not an executing process: a posit.

**Scope of PC★2.** It covers couplings whose joint process is a measurable bijection of a classical
configuration space and whose records are functions of the configuration. That includes every
native coupling in the sources screened (§PC★3). It is not a theorem about F2 extensions that posit
non-classical sub-state structure or non-function records. Those break premise (i) or (ii) by
posit, and are what G★ item 3's first two branches describe.

## PC★4. The gap, named

**Obstructed condition: G★ item 3, for native couplings.** No source screened supplies a
physically executed register operator other than the compressed-Koopman Markov image of a joint
bijection with set-valued records. In A★2's terms:
- **Premise (ii), indicator readout, is not broken by any native source.** By §PC★2 step 2 it
  cannot be broken by any coupling within the scope stated there.
- **Premise (i), classical sub-states, is broken only by posits.** Each posit screened either fails
  item 4 (Q5, N9's amplitude completion, Q6) or is a gauge re-description (D1).
- **Premise (iii), the induced operator, is broken only by functions of the baseline image with no
  executing process** (N4, N7, Q7), or by chosen dilations (Q8, N6).

**What a future candidate needs.** It must break premise (i) or (ii) by a stated F2 principle with a
physical justification:
- for premise (ii): a record that is not a function of a classical configuration, or an access
  that is not set membership;
- for premise (i): sub-state structure that the measure does not determine, and that is not itself
  linear amplitude transport.

The principle must be stated in `C`, so that it transplants to C1, C2, C10, C11 and C14. That is a
new stage 1, under a new amendment reviewed before any computation.

**Relation to #914.** The gap sharpens rows 12, 14 and 21, and #914 §4 (i):
- the missing map `Θ` of L3 cannot come from a coupling generated by the native update with
  set-valued records;
- it requires a posited non-classical access or sub-state structure, and that posit is the F2
  extension.

This is recorded for the gate, and changes no row.

**Scope.** This is not a no-go. It concerns couplings whose joint process is a classical measurable
bijection with function-valued records, the native sources at `5914c17c`, and the branches named in
the log. It also concerns the candidates screened here (Q1–Q12, with F2-1's 13). The negative is
stated for that class only.
