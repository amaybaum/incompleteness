## Arithmetic and evidence types

- **[X] exact.** Rationals and the cyclotomic fields ℚ(ζ_n) for n ∈ {3, 5, 8, 12, 20, 24, 40, 60,
  120}. Elements are integer polynomials modulo Φ_n with a common denominator. Inverses are computed
  as products of Galois conjugates. The sign of a real element is certified by rational interval
  enclosure: π from Machin's formula, cosines from Taylor series with an alternating-series
  remainder.
- **[N] floating point.** One kind of line only: the decimal value printed beside a certified
  negative Wigner value. It decides nothing.
- **[L] literature.** **[K]** kernel theorem, cited and not recompiled. **[W]** written argument.
- **Exactness self-tests (exact):**
  - Kronecker product = schoolbook product;
  - a·a⁻¹ = 1 and conj∘conj = id;
  - certified signs of √3 − 1.7320 and √3 − 1.7321;
  - Gauss sums G² = (−1/q)·q at q = 3, 5.

## Conventions (amendment A1)

- **Phase space.** z = (x, ξ) = (current, previous) ∈ V^{2N}, V = ℤ/q.
  - Leap L_M: x′ = Mx − ξ, ξ′ = x (SM §3.1).
  - Weyl operators W(x, ξ) = ω^{−ξ·x/2} Z^ξ X^x.
  - Registration is in the computational (x) basis.
- **Canonical Weil representation.**
  - ρ(lower shear (x, ξ) ↦ (x, ξ + Bx)) = diag(ω^{y·By/2}).
  - ρ(J′), with J′ = [[0, −I], [I, 0]], is fixed by the relation J′ = up(−1)·low(1)·up(−1). The
    result is ρ(J′) = s^N G^{−N} F₀, where F₀ = Σ ω^{x·y}|x⟩⟨y|, G is the Gauss sum, s = +1 at q = 3
    and s = −1 at q = 5.
  - ρ(L_M) = ρ(J′)·diag(ω^{−y·My/2}).
  - All entries lie in ℚ(ζ_q).
- **Genuineness, checked exactly.**
  - Every image is unitary and satisfies U W(z) U† = W(Lz) and U^{ord L} = I.
  - At N = 1 the matrix group generated by the images has order exactly |SL(2, q)|: 24 and 120.
  - Sp(4, 3) and SL(2, 5) are perfect, so the genuine lift is unique there. Every image equals the
    commutator lift [U_a, U_b] for symplectic a, b with [a, b] = L_M: 27 of 27 at (3, 2), 5 of 5 at
    (5, 1).
  - At (3, 1), SL(2, 3) has three genuine lifts, which differ elementwise by cube roots of unity.
    The canonical one is fixed by the chirp formula. The other two are global-phase changes, which
    the scans cover.
- **Translations.** τ_k: x_k ↦ x_k + 1, with image X_k = W(e_k, 0), of order q and determinant 1.
- **Branch.**
  - Principal branch, eigenphases in (−π, π].
  - Spectral projectors are computed by Lagrange interpolation over the distinct eigenvalues. Each is
    checked equal to the cyclic-group projector (1/n) Σ λ^{−k} U^k, and checked to be a resolution
    of the identity by orthogonal projectors.
  - Generators are G = Σ θ_k P_k with θ_k rational; the common factor 2πi is dropped.
- **Scans.** Each global phase corresponds to one branch-cut position between distinct eigenphases.
  For C3(c2) the scan also covers each square root modulo global phase, with one sign per eigenspace.

### C3(c3) — density [L]

The statement used is the one the amendment quotes: Campbell, Anwar, Browne, PRX 2, 041021 (2012),
Appendix D, "for odd prime dimension, the Clifford group together with any non-Clifford gate is
dense". The source was not retrievable in this environment, which has no network route, so the
statement is recorded as quoted and was not checked against the text.

Hypotheses, checked at each size:

- q is an odd prime: holds;
- (c1), the full Clifford group on N qudits: holds;
- (c2), a non-Clifford gate: holds.

At N = 2 the Hilbert-space dimension is 9, which is not prime. Applying the statement there requires
"dimension" to mean the qudit dimension q, and requires the appendix to cover N-qudit Clifford
groups; neither was checked.

Token: `E1-DENSE [L]` at (3, 1) and (5, 1); `E1-DENSE [L] [scope at N=2 conditional on the quoted
statement covering N qudits]` at (3, 2).

Supporting only (per A2 it decides nothing):

- g = V·X_1 has |tr g|² ∉ ℤ[ζ_n] at every size. This is an exact certificate that g has infinite
  order modulo scalars, because for unitary g of finite order modulo scalars, |tr g|² is a sum of
  roots of unity.
- The generated symplectic group is transitive on nonzero vectors: 8/8, 80/80 and 24/24. In the
  Weyl basis this makes the Clifford adjoint action irreducible on sl(q^N) [W].
- Together these give a supporting density argument, [X] with [W] and standard compact-Lie-group
  facts, at every tested size, without the scope question of the quoted statement.

## Findings outside the frozen cells (supporting or supplementary; they decide nothing)

1. **T is not local on uncoupled sites (N = 2) [X].**
   - The zero-coupling two-site leap image is exactly ρ(J′)⊗ρ(J′).
   - Its principal logarithm is not of the form g_a⊗I + I⊗g_b + cI for any combination of
     branch-cut positions of the two-site operator and of the one-site operators.
   - With τ_1 and τ_2 it generates the full algebra (80), whereas the local-sum generators reach 16.
   - Its half-period gate has operator-Schmidt rank 2 or 3 for every square root modulo global
     phase, and rank 1 for none.
   - Under T as frozen (the principal logarithm of the whole-system image), two sites with zero
     coupling therefore interact at sub-period times, under every phase convention.
   - The frozen cells do not test locality or no-signalling. The finding does not affect the C3(a)
     token at N = 2, because the coupled subset {L_[0,1;1,0], τ_1} is already full.
   - Assumption-watch: a statement of T has to fix whether the logarithm is taken per subsystem or
     on the composite image. The two give different flows.
2. **Centre-free couplings (supplementary) [X].**
   - SM §3.1's update depends on the neighbours of i and not on x_i. A coupling with a nonzero
     diagonal entry is a self-coupling, while RULES quantifies over every symmetric M.
   - The centre-free couplings alone ([0] at N = 1; [0,a;a,0] at N = 2) generate subgroups of Sp of
     order 4, 48 and 4.
   - With the translations they give groups modulo phases of order 36, 3888 and 100, against the
     Clifford group's 216, 4199040 and 3000. So (c1) fails for that repertoire.
   - The all-duration algebra of {one centre-free leap, τ_1} remains full: 8, 80 and 24.
   - The density route through (c1) therefore depends on self-couplings; the all-duration route of
     C3(a) does not.
3. **The (c2) token depends on the named element.** At q = 5 the canonical half-leap of L_[2]
   normalizes the Weyl group.
4. **Scope of the literature step.** The CAB 2012 statement was used as quoted in the amendment.
   Its source text, and its scope at N = 2, were not checked.
