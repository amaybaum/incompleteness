# F2-1 — consistency and control of candidate E1: rules fixed before the exact runs

Research design on a research branch. Not a native round, not adopted, not for merge without
separate review. This file is committed before any exact computation of the round is run. Thread
H's numerical results (`../f2-research-threads/H-extension-candidates.md`, `work-H/`) are prior
design evidence and are cited as such; they do not count as this round's measurements.

## Framing (owner decisions, 2026-10-14)

- **Target: dense finite quantum mechanics, both directions.** (Up) every experiment the candidate
  allows gives valid quantum predictions; (down) every finite quantum experiment — preparation,
  operations including ancillas, sequential composition, measurement — is approximable to any
  accuracy. Error measure: the diamond norm on channels, with ancillas included; errors of a
  sequence are bounded by the sum of the errors of its steps. Exact availability is a separate,
  stronger target that needs its own continuity or completion principle.
- **Continuity is an added physical hypothesis, and its control content is itemized**: T1 unitary
  evolution between updates; T2 selectable couplings (symmetric couplings, source terms); T3
  selectable durations. The round reports which controls follow from which items.
- **Alphabet.** E1 is investigated at odd prime q as a conditional branch. The q = 2 failure is a
  control: a limitation of the candidate, not a statement that nature requires odd q. SM §2.7's
  q-independence claim is not used in either direction.
- **Complex kinematics is inherited.** R1 below posits complex amplitudes; the field-neutral
  reconstruction stays open. Every result here is conditional on R1.
- **Scope.** The smallest nontrivial case q = 3, N = 1 site; then the two-system case q = 3, N = 2;
  q = 5, N = 1 as a second odd instance. Arbitrary finite dimensions are not addressed: E1's
  carriers have dimension q^N only.

## The candidate's assumptions, each stated

- **R1** The observer's description of N registered sites is a vector in ℂ^{V^N}, V = ℤ/q.
- **R2** A substratum process preserving the symplectic form acts on the description by its Weil
  image, fixed up to phase. The substratum's leapfrog (x(t−1), x(t)) ↦ (x(t), M x(t) − x(t−1)),
  SM §3.1, is symplectic exactly when the coupling M is symmetric.
- **R3** Registration reads the current component of a site, as a projective measurement in the
  computational basis of ℂ^V, through a symplectic meter.
- **R4** The prior on the previous component is the counting measure (Main Lemma 3).
- **T1–T3** as above.

## Cells and decision rules (each verdict is generated from its measurements)

- **C1 consistency (exact over ℚ(ζ_q), and ℚ(ζ_{2q}) where needed).** For every symmetric
  coupling at the tested (q, N): the intertwiner space of the leapfrog's symplectic matrix is
  one-dimensional; and on every word of leapfrog steps and translations up to length 6, the Born
  marginal of the registered current component equals OI's rooted visible law under R4, as exact
  rationals; and the two-time law with one symplectic meter equals the classical two-time law
  exactly. `E1-CONSISTENT` iff every equality holds; otherwise `E1-INCONSISTENT`, with the first
  counterexample printed.
- **C2 normalization and back-action (exact).** Registration probabilities sum to 1 on every
  tested state; after registration, the distribution of the conjugate component is the counting
  measure. `E1-NORMALIZED-BACKACTING` iff both hold exactly.
- **C3 control content.**
  - (a) T1+T2+T3 (all durations; the leaps for every symmetric coupling, the translations and the
    source terms): the complex Lie algebra generated by the principal-logarithm generators has
    dimension q^{2N}, computed exactly. `E1-FULL-ALGEBRA` iff equal.
  - (b) Leaps only, without translations or source terms: the generated algebra commutes with the
    parity x ↦ −x. Countercontrol; expected superselected.
  - (c) The weakest-duration test for the dense target: the integer-time images (R2) together with
    one fixed sub-period duration, the leap at t = 1/2. `E1-ONE-DURATION-NONCLIFFORD` iff that
    operation does not normalize the Weyl group, tested exactly; density of the generated group
    then follows from the Clifford group being a unitary 2-design at prime q together with an
    element of infinite order, and the round reports the infinite-order test with its evidence
    type ([X] if exact, otherwise [N]) and the density inference as literature-dependent ([L]).
- **C4 escape from the known obstructions.** (a) Some generated operation does not preserve
  nonnegative entries in the computational frame; (b) some state reachable from a
  registered-value state has a negative Wigner value; (c) the dense target is met by a countable
  generating set, consistent with `fixedGateTheory` and `countable_not_pairFlowSourced` (the exact
  target would need all durations). Each reported exactly where possible.

## Controls (a failed control voids every verdict)

- **K1** q = 2: the kernel's two-layer images contradict the visible law (recorded as a limitation
  of the candidate).
- **K2** A non-symmetric coupling has no intertwiner.
- **K3** A point prior on the previous component is not reproduced.
- **K4** The interface's bare read-write copy has no Weil image.
- **K5** Flows of the configuration-carrier permutations fix the all-ones ray.
- **K6** Mutation: a sign error in the Weil intertwiner is detected by C1; a dropped generator is
  detected by C3(a).

## Outcome classes

- `E1-CONSISTENT-AND-DENSE`: C1, C2 hold; C3(c) holds with C4(a), C4(b). One consistent extension
  supplies dense control at the tested sizes under R1–R4 and T1 with one duration.
- `E1-CONSISTENT-NOT-CONTROLLING`: C1, C2 hold; C3 fails.
- `E1-INCONSISTENT`: C1 or C2 fails.

## Non-claims

No general-N theorem; no kernel proof; nothing OI-derived; complex kinematics inherited; no claim
about minimality, about field-neutral reconstruction, about dimensions other than q^N, or about
which alphabet nature uses.
