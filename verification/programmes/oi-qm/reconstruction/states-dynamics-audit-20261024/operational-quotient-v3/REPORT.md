# Operational quotient: a working Clifford interface and its next obstruction

Research continuation for #911, 24 October 2026. This is an abstract
representation/access diagnostic. No native implementation, new physical
premise, changed G3 identification, observer restriction or full-equivalence
verdict is adopted. A1 and A2 retain separate targets.

## Result

A different operational map can invalidate an obstruction tied to the old
pointwise/G3 map. The explicit eight-sign control below realizes a Hadamard
as a positive permutation, including the output off-diagonal entry -1/2
that the previous pointwise map excluded. Its complete finite adaptive Pauli
record/outgoing-state law is well-defined on equivalence classes when the
specified fresh reset and private-access contract hold.

This is not an A1 source. Under the same eight-sign moment map and the full
specified preparation fibres, a stochastic implementation of a unitary Bloch
rotation must preserve the sign cube. It is therefore a signed permutation;
the proper rotations give only the 24 single-qubit Clifford actions. For one
rational non-Clifford rotation there is also a direct approximation bound:
the worst error over two explicitly allowed preparations in the final X
record is at least 1/15. Enlarging the output preparation region to the
whole Bloch ball does not remove that bound.

The general lesson is conditional: escaping the old invariant requires an
explicit operational correspondence, and that correspondence has its own
preparation, access and operation-availability constraints. This does not
exclude every operational quotient or OI's separate A2 programme.

## Fixed source and target contract

The hidden carrier is lambda=(x,y,z) in Omega={-1,+1}^3. A subnormalized
nonnegative measure p has mass w and first moments r=(E_p x,E_p y,E_p z).
For normalized inputs the supplied preparation region is

    P_oct = {p in Delta(Omega): |r_x|+|r_y|+|r_z| <= 1}.

Every measure in this region is admitted by the candidate contract, including
measures with the same first moments and different private correlations.
This is stronger than admitting only the six canonical eigenstate priors.
It is not asserted to be the native preparable set. Its subnormalized cone
replaces the right-hand side by w.

The fixed linear map, including normalization, is

    D(p) = (w I + r_x sigma_x + r_y sigma_y + r_z sigma_z)/2.

The image is exactly the Bloch octahedron. Positivity follows from
||r||_2<=||r||_1<=w. Conversely, the six Pauli eigenstate distributions

    mu_(a,s)(lambda) = 1[lambda_a=s]/4

map to its six vertices, and their mixtures cover it.

Allowed transformations are signed permutations of coordinates generated by

    H: (x,y,z) -> (z,-y,x),
    S: (x,y,z) -> (-y,x,z).

They preserve P_oct. A Pauli instrument exposes the chosen sign s=lambda_a
and then has the subnormalized carrier kernel

    K_(a,s)(p) = mu_(a,s) sum_(lambda:lambda_a=s) p(lambda).

Each invocation uses its own initially independent fresh two-bit seed and
initially blank workspace. Their independence must hold conditional on the
whole previous public history. The allowed public record comprises settings,
gate choices, outcomes, fixed completion ticks and a certain-success flag.
Both outcome branches, including zero-probability ones, are retained.
There is no heralding failure in this positive control. Private old-state
workspace and seed registers stay outside the allowed reads and have no
permitted return coupling into later carrier operations. This is part of the
supplied abstract kernel contract; privacy of direct reads alone would not
justify it for a native neighbourhood. No native
clock, settings mechanism, private-access rule or reset is derived here.

## Exact operational equivalence and composition

Define p~q by equality of their laws for every permitted finite adaptive
future full record. For normalized p,q in P_oct,

    p~q if and only if moments(p)=moments(q),

under this contract. Unequal moments are separated by one of the three
permitted Pauli reads. For equal moments, each signed permutation transforms
them identically. Each Pauli outcome has probability (1+s r_a)/2 and leaves
exactly the same unnormalized eigenstate distribution. Induction on a finite
adaptive decision tree then gives the same complete branch law. Known gate
choices, settings, flags and fixed ticks are the same functions of the same
public history, so retaining those labels preserves the induction.

Moreover, direct multiplication of quantum projectors gives

    D(K_(a,s)(p)) = P_(a,s) D(p) P_(a,s).

Thus the induction includes the outgoing subnormalized density operator,
not only the reported sign. For a zero-mass branch it compares the zero
operator without normalizing. No adaptive tree has been silently replaced
by one fixed outcome sequence. Randomized classical policies require their
public/private coin contract as well; independent policy mixtures preserve
the stated finite-tree equality by linearity.

The moment kernel is four-dimensional on unnormalized measures with fixed
mass: the degree-two and degree-three Fourier characters xy,xz,yz,xyz span
it. The checker verifies that reset kernels annihilate those characters and
every Clifford action preserves their zero first moments. This measures
the congruence obligation rather than presuming it from one-read agreement.

## Reversible dilation and access countercontrols

Label the eight carrier states by integers j modulo 8. For a fixed setting
a and fresh two-bit seed r, let g_a(j,r) select the triple with measured sign
lambda_a(j) and the other two signs given by r. The global map is

    (j,b,r,c) -> (b+g_a(j,r) mod8, j, r, c XOR 1[lambda_a(j)=-1]).

Here b is an eight-valued blank output register and c a record bit. The old
j is retained. Given the output, recover j from the retained second
register, subtract g_a(j,r) to recover b, and undo the record XOR to recover
c. This is a bijection on all 512 states per setting. On b=c=0 and uniform
r it gives exactly the specified reset kernel and public sign record. All
ancillas and the entire sequence of fresh seeds are supplied initially;
no seed is injected between measurements. This is an abstract reversible
map, not a construction under the lattice update rule.

The access and reset restrictions are load-bearing. Take p_+ and p_- uniform
on the four triples with xy=+1 and xy=-1 respectively. Both have zero first
moments and decode to I/2.

- Retaining the unchanged carrier after an X read lets a following Y read
  reveal xy. The two complete X,Y record laws have TV distance 1. Their
  normalized post-X moment decoders have determinant -1/4. One-read moment
  equality therefore does not define a congruence for this altered protocol.
- After the proper reset, exposing the retained old Y field alongside the
  public X sign also gives TV distance 1. Exposing just old X or old Z in
  this example gives distance 0. Uniform individual workspace marginals do
  not suffice for a joint access audit.
- Exposing the fresh seed and the sign determines all three new carrier
  signs. The resulting conditional moment decoder has determinant -1/2
  in every seed/setting/outcome case. Even a preparation-independent seed
  label can spoil the outgoing-state contract when it is public.

These controls do not prove that a forbidden native read is available. They
identify exactly what a future native implementation must exclude or handle
in its fixed full-record correspondence.

## Why the old Hadamard witness does not transfer automatically

For the negative-Z eigenstate prior, H sends (0,0,-1) to (-1,0,0), so

    D(H p_Z-) = (1/2) [[1,-1],[-1,1]].

This matrix is positive with determinant zero although its off-diagonal
entry is -1/2. The hidden H map remains an ordinary permutation with no
negative transition probabilities. The earlier pointwise/G3 map and D
identify different objects: negative matrix entries under D are signed
expectations of ordinary probabilities. Therefore the earlier invariant is
not a map-independent no-go for all operational quotients. Its stated
fixed-identification theorem remains intact.

The eight-sign stabilizer model and its Hadamard action are known. Lillystone,
Wallman and Emerson describe them in arXiv:1802.06121v2, pp.2-3, and show that
the stabilizer subtheory has generalized contextuality involving transformations.
No universal noncontextuality or novelty for that simulator is claimed here.
This checkpoint uses it as a controlled alternative-map test.

## A new obstruction under this fixed quotient and preparation domain

Let A be the 3-by-8 sign matrix whose lambda column is lambda. Suppose a
single stochastic hidden kernel T implements a target Bloch rotation R on
every allowed p, in the sense A T p=R A p. T includes any fixed independent
apparatus followed by classical processing; it cannot depend on an unknown
preparation label. Define the eight admissible priors

    nu_lambda = (2/3) u + (1/3) delta_lambda,

where u is uniform. Their first moments are lambda/3, so each belongs to
P_oct. They affinely span the normalized probability hyperplane: their
average is u and delta_lambda=3 nu_lambda-2u.

Exact intertwining on these allowed priors forces, by linearity,

    A T delta_lambda = R lambda.

This conclusion uses point priors only as vectors in a linear identity; it
does not declare them physically preparable. Since T is stochastic, each
coordinate of A T delta_lambda must lie in [-1,1]. Consequently R maps the
entire sign cube into itself.

For an orthogonal R this requires the l1 norm of every row to be at most 1.
Its l2 norm is 1, and the equality

    ||v||_1^2 = ||v||_2^2 + 2 sum_(i<j) |v_i v_j|

forces each row to have one nonzero entry, equal to +1 or -1. Orthogonality
makes their columns distinct. Hence only signed permutations are possible;
the proper rotations are the 24 Clifford actions. This written proof is
conditional on this carrier, decoder and full preparation-fibre contract.
It does not classify arbitrary hidden encodings, different admissible
preparation sections, larger output carriers or native operational quotients.

For the explicit proper rotation

    R = [[3/5,-4/5,0],[4/5,3/5,0],[0,0,1]],

the first coordinate of R(1,-1,1) is 7/5, giving an immediate contradiction.
The target rotation is independently checked by conjugation with
diag(2-i,2+i)/sqrt(5), performed in rational complex arithmetic by using the
unnormalized matrix and dividing its conjugation by 5.

## Quantitative record-level bound, independent of output-state closure

Use the two allowed priors nu_+ and nu_- above for antipodal
lambda=+(1,-1,1) and -(1,-1,1). Their ontic TV distance is 1/3. Under the
target R followed by an X read, the Bernoulli laws are

    q_+ = (11/15,4/15),   q_- = (4/15,11/15),

with TV distance 7/15. Any fixed classical continuation, even with independent
randomness, additional private memory and an arbitrary valid output-state
region, produces record laws a_+,a_- with distance at most 1/3 by classical
data processing. If each record error is at most epsilon, triangle inequality
requires

    7/15 <= epsilon + 1/3 + epsilon,
    epsilon >= 1/15.

The bound is tight for this two-preparation binary-record test: the ordinary
unrotated X read gives (2/3,1/3) and (1/3,2/3), each at distance 1/15 from
the target. Tightness for the pair is not tightness for the full channel or
for every preparation. All 256 deterministic binary response functions are
enumerated as an independent contraction control; stochastic responses are
their convex hull.

The priors also lie in P_ball={p:||A p||_2<=1}. Therefore enlarging the allowed
output to every valid-Bloch prior does not repair this obstruction. Even
enlarging the input domain from P_oct to P_ball keeps the problematic pair.
The result is an A2 error floor for this fixed representation, these allowed
preparations and this one target protocol. It is not an A2 error floor for
OI generally. Changing which hidden preparations represent a quantum state,
their conditional apparatus correlations, the decoder or the available
interface changes premises of the calculation and needs a new contract.

## State coverage is a separate issue

The pure target r*=(3/5,4/5,0) lies outside the octahedron. Every octahedral
state has max(|r*_x-r_x|,|r*_y-r_y|)>=1/5, since r*_x+r*_y-1=2/5.
Thus the worst of the two X,Y Bernoulli errors is at least 1/10. The point
(2/5,3/5,0) attains it.

This state-coverage gap can be removed by a different supplied preparation
domain: for every Bloch vector r the positive product prior

    p_r(lambda) = product_(a=x,y,z) (1+r_a lambda_a)/8

has first moments r. Admitting P_ball instead of P_oct then gives full Bloch
state coverage for the Pauli/Clifford interface. The operational quotient
does not select either domain, nor does it make all those priors physically
available. The non-Clifford kernel obstruction and its 1/15 pair bound survive
because their input priors are retained. This separates state representability,
preparation availability, interface congruence and operation availability.

## Exact checks and research decision

`checks.py` uses only the Python standard library and exact rational complex
arithmetic. It checks 19 admissible priors; 114 quantum projector branches;
24 Clifford actions; 1,064 adaptive full-record branch nodes with 608 leaf
records at depth three; the Fourier-kernel congruence; all 1,536 global reset
inverse cases; readiness; no-reset/workspace/seed countercontrols; the eight
prior span; the rational rotation; all 256 deterministic binary responses;
and both separate approximation bounds. Branch totals include zero-mass
branches. The finite checks validate the implementation; the all-finite-depth
and cube-preservation statements rest on the written proofs above.

Run `python3 checks.py` or `python3 -O checks.py`. Both emit the same structured
`result.json`; failed controls raise before the pass verdict is produced.
No Lean build, formal certificate, native reset or CI attestation is asserted.

The proposed route has therefore passed a useful positive test and failed
the next operation-coverage test under an explicit contract. The next A1
architecture must specify what changes: admissible preparation fibres,
operational decoder, hidden carrier/continuation kind, or resource dependence.
It must then establish full-record congruence and actual availability again.
The current native #910 phase has a different encoding and is not refuted
by this abstract eight-sign obstruction.
