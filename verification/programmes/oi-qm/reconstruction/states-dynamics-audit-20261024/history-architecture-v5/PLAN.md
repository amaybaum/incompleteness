## Candidate, fixed before calculation

Use the Kochen-Specker qubit sphere model, with pure-state preparation
density mu_n(lambda)=(n dot lambda)_+/pi on S^2 and hemispherical response
s=sign(a dot lambda). Unitary actions are explicit rotations of lambda.
The preparation cone is the finite positive mixture cone generated by all
mu_n, not every arbitrary positive sphere measure. A fixed decoder uses
mass and the scaled first moment (3/2)E(lambda). Mixed preparation procedures
can be context-dependent at the hidden level.

After each rank-one projective read, the candidate uses a specified fresh
reset to mu_(s*a), with all random resources supplied initially, retained old
lambda/workspace, fixed public completion ticks and no permitted return
coupling or private-resource read. A sample reset may use independent initial
uniform variables u,v and the explicit cosine-weighted hemisphere map.
This is a supplied continuum kernel, not the native lattice update.
