  carrier preparation:

  - below recurrence the only candidate is the half period `N/2`;

## 6. The parked-record case (decision A), in the four-way separation

[P] **Definition** (U1 C-RET, parked-record clause). A record held at `t₁`, whose support passes through
the hidden sector `H` and returns to a region readable under AD-1 by `t₂`, is *retained* when its return
is effected by `φ` within the budget. A return by recurrence is not retention. Three return kinds:
- *intact return:* readable at `t₂` without a private decoder;
- *scrambled return:* readable only with content still held in `H`, so not retention under C-RET;
- *coherent uncomputation:* no region holds path-correlated content any longer. This is Case Rv, not
  Case Pk.

### 6.1 Class (i), native mechanism

- **PK-1. Same-site one-tick parking is native on K1 under A-cur** [A-inf].
  - `φ(u_prev, u) = (u, A u − u_prev)` moves each current value into the retarded slice, which A-cur
    hides (SM:252, Lemma 1c (iii)).
  - It returns the value additively in `u_{t+2} = A u_{t+1} − u_t`.
  - At a one-site region this is VER's Sup-3: `Φ^1_{XX}(x, y) = (y, −x)`. Sup-3's condition, a zero
    current slice on the neighbours, is the condition under which the parked value is not mixed with
    neighbour content.
  - Scope: same-site slice parking only. Under A-reg nothing is parked at site level. Transport of
    content out of `V` into hidden sites and back is not excluded under either form (S-C4a) and is
    undecided as retention.
  - As a held record it is undecided (C-4; CALC R-7).

  global reading].

### 6.2 Class (ii), conditional quantum interpretation

The imports are named: P-super, unitary marker and environment dynamics, and the Born dictionary.
Write `|Ψ⟩ = (|L⟩|E_L⟩ + e^{iθ}|R⟩|E_R⟩)/√2`, with `|E_·⟩` the pure state of the whole complement,
hidden part included.
- Unconditional visibility is `V = |⟨E_L|E_R⟩|` (PREREG §3.5 benchmark, applied with the detector taken
  to be the whole complement).
- `D_whole` is the best path discrimination over the whole complement.
- `D_acc ≤ D_whole` is the best path discrimination over the accessible region alone.
- For this pure complement, `D_whole² + V² = 1` (PREREG:232–233 derives the pure-marker equality).
- For mixed markers the relation is the inequality `D² + V² ≤ 1` (Englert 1996 [LIT: ABSTRACT, theory];
  Schwindt 1999 [LIT: FULL], "The equality holds for pure initial states of the WWM, while the inequality
  applies to (partially-)mixed states").

The items:
- **QM-1. Parking and intact return leave `V` and `D_whole` unchanged.** A unitary acting on the
  complement alone preserves the overlap and the trace distance between the complement's conditional
  states (LIT §3, writer's inference there; consistent here). While parked and after an intact return,
  `V` stays where it was. On return, a complementary readout of the record gives conditional fringes
  whose full masses sum to the unsorted pattern (T4); a path readout gives none. This is Case S applied
  after Case Pk.
- **QM-2. A scrambled return leaves `V` and `D_whole` unchanged.** Conditional fringes then need a
  readout that includes the scrambling key.
- **QM-3. Coherent uncomputation raises `V` and lowers `D_whole`.** When the complement's conditional
  states re-overlap, by autonomous revival or applied reversal, unconditional fringes return. This is
  Case Rv.
- **QM-4 (restated: a trade-off, not an exclusion).** `V ≤ √(1 − D_whole²)`.
  - The relation permits partial `D` with partial `V` (for example `D = V = 1/√2` for a pure complement).
    It excludes only `D_whole = 1` with any `V > 0`, and `V = 1` with any `D_whole > 0` (LIT §3).
  - As a statement about processes under the pure-complement equality, `V` and `D_whole` cannot both
    increase (LIT C6).
  - A record's return in the Case Pk sense changes neither `V` nor `D_whole` (QM-1). What it raises is
    `D_acc`, and `D_acc² + V² ≤ 1` allows a partial rise of `V` together with a partial rise of `D_acc`.
  - Any comparison of `V` at one tick with `D_acc` at a later tick relies on QM-1's invariance: only the
    complement evolves between the two ticks.
- **QM-5.** GR:350 [M] states: "environmental decoherence, in which which-path records pass to
  unobserved degrees of freedom such as emitted radiation while the total evolution remains unitary".
  *Inference, class (ii), not GR:350's statement:* within this interpretation, `V` depends on
  path-correlated content anywhere in the complement (`D_whole`), not on whether an observer holds it.

### 6.3 Distinguishing observables, and what can be said against data

**Within QM, a record's return and a coherence revival differ in which distinguishability changes**
[A-inf, class (ii)]. Two named processes, replacing r1's undefined labels:
- *record return:* `D_acc` rises at fixed `D_whole` and `V`. This is path-basis backflow into the
  accessible region;
- *coherence revival:* `V` rises and `D_whole` falls. This is phase-basis backflow at the recombination.

The observable is a joint statistic over runs at one tick and one preparation: the unconditional
contrast `V` at the recombination, together with the best path discrimination achievable from the
accessible region (`D_acc`) in the same runs. It separates the two processes inside QM. It is defined,
in class (ii). LIT records no performed measurement of it in a parked arrangement.

**OI's revival on record is of the path-basis kind** [A-inf]. The book's revival measure `𝒩` is "the
total-variation distance of their visible population laws, equivalently the trace distance of the
corresponding diagonal states" (ch18 §18.3). With PK-6, this is the native counterpart of a record
return, not of a coherence revival. A phase-basis revival needs a coherent preparation, which is P-super
or an operation not shown available.
