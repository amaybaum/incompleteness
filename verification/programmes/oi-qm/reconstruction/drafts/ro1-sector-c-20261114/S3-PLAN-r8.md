(`verification/programmes/oi-qm/reconstruction/measurement-record-formation/preregistration.md` §1):
- **Representable.** "a mathematical representation exists".
- **Available.** "an executing physical process exists in the declared interface: every operation,
  setting change, readout, reset and inverse is a composition of native steps under the declared
  access, with its resources accounted".
- **Derived.** "available from the OI premises at `D` … with no added resource".

[P] S3 uses these levels and does not invent new ones. `Γ_exe(M)` is the set of *available*
programs. A program in `Γ_exe(M)` that needs no added resource is *derived*. D-real and D-glue
(RO1-DEPS-r4 §1.2) are *representable* results, and do not place a program in `Γ_exe`.

### 4.1 Recording and renewal, tested together (owner addition 1)

**Status: a hypothesis to assess, H-RR, not a claim.** No joint restriction is claimed to exist. The
owner's words (`amaybaum`, 2026-11-14): "Yes. Add both points to S3-PLAN-r2. They are important

**H-RR.** Record creation, retention and renewal, taken *together* in one fixed model, might impose a
restriction on executable non-quantum correlations that none of them imposes alone.

**Three cases, kept separate:**

| Case | What is on record | Bearing |
|---|---|---|
| **RR-1. Same-seed reuse with retained records** | [R, reviewed, not merged] The retained first record exposes the known PR implementation: complete-history signalling (SEL-EXCL-1 §7) | A partial discriminator, only if the continuation is executable (RO1-DEPS-r4 §5.1) |
| **RR-2. Fresh-cell renewal, supplied** | [R, reviewed, not merged] A stipulated supply of fresh independent cells avoids it: complete local-history no-signalling (SEL-EXCL-1 §3) | Defeats RR-1. The supply is assumed |
| **RR-3. Physically sourced renewal** | [R, record on main; reviewed, not merged] No producer is sourced on record: NATIVE-PREP-SRC-1 stop; MR-1 GAP "absence of a physically sourced quiet, ready region"; a seed bank is fresh only if not previously used or conditioned on (`REPORT.md`:57–69) | Open. The model must produce the fresh resources, not assume an unlimited supply |

**Evidence that would bear on H-RR** [P]:
- **Would support it** only together with AC-1 (§6). One fixed model with one shared interface would
  have to give a class-(i) result of this form: executing a fresh-cell PR program with complete
  retained records requires a renewal process, and the model's own production of fresh cells cannot
  supply that process with the independence it needs. A rate counts only where the rate limit is
  itself structural, that is, where it differs between PR and the quantum references on the shared
  interface. The *same* model's renewal must
  still suffice for the quantum references on that interface (coherent measurements, and Bell
  correlations at 2√2). The restriction must also be structural, about which records are retained,
  readable and renewed across the wings. It cannot be a resource count, because "Scarcity alone is not
  a separator" (SEL-DISC-1 P5): fair quantum outputs need comparable entropy (SEL-DISC-1 §4, l. 79).
- **Would refute it** if any of the following held, each subject to SNR-1 (§8.6):
  - a model on record with physically sourced renewal (RR-3) executing fresh-cell PR with complete

### 8.6 Outcome tiers, and what would close route M

**Route M stays open, conditional and unclosed until a decisive test runs.** Its tiers:
- **Strongest tier.** One native observer–record mechanism, in class (i), that both:
  - supplies coherent measurement operations (track (a): (iv), the phases, coherent preparation);
  - restricts executable non-quantum correlations (track (c)) without excluding quantum Bell
    behaviour.

  This needs a class-(i) result on Point 1(a), which the configuration level cannot give (check 4).
  So it would have to come from the observer-level lift (check 3) or from a σ12 rule (check 2), with a
  class-(iii) restriction on Point 3.
- **Weaker tier, still useful.** Recording explains memory and some measurement behaviour, in class
  (i): record formation, retention, the role of the retained record in exposing same-seed reuse, and
  which accounts of interference loss stay conditional. Quantum coherence (track (a)) and Bell
  soundness (track (c)) stay open separately.

**SNR-1, the sourcing rule for negative outcomes.** [P] A negative outcome counts toward closing
route M, or toward any other negative closure in this plan (H-RR's refutation, §4.1), only if every
capability it relies on is either **sourced** or **disproved** under the §3 criterion. That covers
preparations, markers, retention, erasure, settings and renewal.
- **Disproved.** A capability shown not available by a result on record is a sourced fact (#866's
  vocabulary: "derived / assumed / unsourced / disproved", §3). A negative that follows from it
  counts at that result's scope only, and at the result's recorded status.
  - A disproof whose verification is only reported, and is not verification on `main` (§A.46 item 7),
    enters as a **named premise**. A negative that follows from it is conditional on that premise.
    It counts only once the premise is independently verified, and is re-checked under the §3
    criterion that U1 fixes.
  - S-L3 is such a case (§8.4, check 1).
- **Unsourced or assumed.** An outcome that relies on an unsourced or assumed capability is reported as
  *unsourced* or as concerning an *assumed apparatus*, and it does not count.
- **Target laws.** The quantum references enter AC-1 and H-RR as target laws under `Θ`. They are not
  capabilities that must be executed (§4.1).
- **Conditioning.** The conditioning reading (α) is not a capability: outcomes are counted per reading
  of P-vii.

**What would close route M negatively, at its scope.** All of the following, each subject to SNR-1:
- (a′) **D-1 is resolved.** Its four outcomes are: undecided at (γ); unsourced at (β)–(δ); empty on
