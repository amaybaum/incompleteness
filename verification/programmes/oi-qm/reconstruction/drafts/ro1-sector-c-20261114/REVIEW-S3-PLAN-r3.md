## 5. Recommended clarifications (nonblocking)

- **N1. (β) and (δ) are coupled (ll. 501–513).**
  - S-L5 makes the marker's content after coupling `s_A + Φ_{AΣ} s_Σ`.
  - For a native marker with uniform `s_A` independent of the carrier, that content is uniform and
    independent of `s_Σ`. The marker alone then records nothing about the path: its own initial
    content masks the record.
  - The path information survives only jointly with whatever retains `s_A`, which is the ready-cell
    or history-record convention of MR-1's GAP and OA-1 Gap 1.
  - So for the only sourced marker, (β) generically fails unless `s_A` is held elsewhere, and the
    native-marker miss branch may be empty.
  - Stating this coupling keeps U4 from treating (β) and (δ) as independent. It sharpens D-1's
    discrimination; it does not change it.
- **N2. "At the rate" (l. 273).**
  - H-RR's support condition reads "cannot supply that process at the rate or with the independence
    it needs". The same paragraph excludes resource counts, because "Scarcity alone is not a
    separator".
  - "Rate" is a resource quantity. Either drop it, or say it counts only where the rate limit is
    itself structural (differs between PR and the quantum references on the shared interface).
  - Also: "Either a miss or a match would be falsifying at its scope" (l. 529) should name what a
    match falsifies, namely the reading that native K1 recording misses complementarity.
