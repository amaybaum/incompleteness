# Owner questions carried by GATE-A-AMEND-r4.md
Decisions the amendment leaves open; answering them adopts no E or C and changes no scientific
status. Details: `GATE-A-AMEND-r4.md` §5.2, §7 and §8.

**1. What kind of thing is the sector condition C?**
- *A condition on the observed statistics* (the model's experiment family). This matches #914
  c.6875548603: "the experiment families satisfying C are exactly those of QM_fin". C must be
  stated without mentioning quantum theory; agreement with it is a theorem to prove. Question 2
  answers itself only if C depends on nothing but the statistics of the labelled Gate A
  experiments, all of them, which are the statistics the comparison with quantum theory uses. If
  C also depends on the statistics of other experiments the model can perform (question 4, first
  option), question 2 stays open unless C is shown to give the same answer for any two models
  fully equivalent to the same quantum theory.
- *A condition on the underlying model* (substratum law, initial distribution, observer embedding,
  allowed preparations or readouts). This matches #1169 c.6875724719: "OI models outside C". C can
  then be read physically, but question 2 becomes a real question.
- A "regime" restriction counts only if it is stated as one of these two. Restricting which
  experiments are compared gives a narrower named target, and results on that target do not
  discharge part 2.

**2. Must every model that reproduces quantum statistics satisfy C?** This is a real question
under the second option of question 1, and under the first option whenever C depends on more
than the labelled statistics.
- *Yes*: the programme must also prove that no admitted model outside the sector reproduces quantum
  mechanics.
- *No*: models outside the sector may reproduce quantum statistics, and only coverage is required.
- No owner record states the "yes" form.

**3. Does "one common" mean one architecture or one single model?**
- *One architecture*: one fixed set of rules, with one model per carrier size and quantum theory,
  and parameters set in advance, never fitted. This matches #1169 c.6875724719: "one common,
  physically sourced substratum–observer architecture".
- *One single model*: one model whose subsystems realize every carrier, with one E for all carriers
  and typed embeddings between subsystems; each comparison is a part of that model. This matches
  the proposal the owner accepted in #914 c.6875548603: "every finite quantum experiment is
  realized by one common OI model". The same comment's restatement of the target reads "on one
  common substratum–observer realization".

**4. Which experiments must look quantum inside the sector?**
- *Every experiment a sector model can physically perform* (obligation S-A, the reading taken in
  the amendment). This rules out PR-box-type behaviour from unlabelled procedures in the sector.
- *Only the labelled experiments of the Gate A interface*. Then non-quantum behaviour from other
  procedures inside the sector is permitted, and this would be named as a restricted reading.
- Recorded positions: #1169 c.6703342848 says the owner approved proceeding after the explanation
  that "success means the same predictions across the complete agreed experimental interface". In
  the same comment, its author states that the accepted contract requires "a quantum counterpart
  for every behavior admitted by the physical OI interface". #914 c.6875548603 asks for "excluding stronger-than-quantum correlations
  in the physical sector".
