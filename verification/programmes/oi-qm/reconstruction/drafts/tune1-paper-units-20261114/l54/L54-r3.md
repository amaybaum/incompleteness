## 4. Results

**Notation.** `G_j` is the group of round-`j` records, `((V × V)^O)^τ` (in exchange mode, times the
label coordinate). For a law `P` on `G_1 × G_2`, `dep(P) := TV(P, P_1 ⊗ P_2)`. `α = a·x`,
`β = b·x` for the Bloch vector `x` of a state; `p_a = (1 + α)/2`, `p_b = (1 + β)/2`; `c = a·b`.
Hypotheses 1–8 are those of §0.

**Lemma 1 (translation covariance).** Assume 1–5. For complete label assignments `c, c′` fixed in
advance and every `u`, `h(c′, u) = h(c, u) + H_P(c′ − c)`. Hence the record law of `c′` is the image
of the record law of `c` under the coordinatewise shift by `H_P(c′ − c)`. *Proof.* S3:79–85, steps
1–3: `Φ_F` is a group endomorphism; the time-zero configuration is the sum of the embedded `r_O`,
labels and `u`; an exchange is `x ↦ (I − Π_C)x + ι(c_j)`. ∎ *Status: exact algebra.* It is Lemma
A-T (a), (b) read for two label sequences. It does not cover policies whose labels depend on
records (§8 item 6).

**Lemma 2.** (i) `dep` is invariant under coordinatewise bijections of `G_1 × G_2`.
(ii) `|dep(P) − dep(P′)| ≤ 3 TV(P, P′)`. *Proof.* (i) A coordinatewise bijection maps marginals to
marginals and products to products, and total variation is invariant under bijections. (ii)
`TV(P_1 ⊗ P_2, P′_1 ⊗ P′_2) ≤ TV(P_1, P′_1) + TV(P_2, P′_2) ≤ 2 TV(P, P′)`, and the triangle
inequality. ∎ *Status: exact.*

**Lemma 3 (target values).** Assume 7. For a state `σ` and reads `u, v ∈ {a, b}`, the target law
of the test `(c_u, c_v)` is `L_uv = Σ_{k_1,k_2} π(k_1, k_2) q_u(·|k_1) ⊗ q_v(·|k_2)`, with
`π(k_1) = Tr(P^u_{k_1} σ)` and `π(k_2|k_1) = Tr(P^v_{k_2} P^u_{k_1})` (AF:107; the projectors have
rank one). Since `q_u(·|u1)` and `q_u(·|u0)` have disjoint supports,
`dep(L_uv) = Σ_{k_1,k_2} ½|π(k_1,k_2) − π(k_1)π(k_2)|`, and the four deviations of a 2×2 table have
the common modulus `|π(1,1) − π(1,·)π(·,1)|`. That modulus is `p_a − p_a²` for `aa` and
`p_a(1 − p_a)|c|` for `ab`. Therefore `dep(L_aa) = 2p_a(1 − p_a)` and
`dep(L_ab) = 2p_a(1 − p_a)|c|`, and likewise with `a, b` exchanged. ∎ *Status: exact.*

**Theorem 1** (statement in §0). *Proof.*
1. By 8, `TV(W_uv, L_uv) ≤ ε` for the four tests, with the `L_uv` of Lemma 3 for the one `σ`.
2. By Lemma 1, `W_ab` is a coordinatewise shift of `W_aa`; by Lemma 2 (i), `dep(W_aa) = dep(W_ab)`.
3. By Lemma 2 (ii), `|dep(L_aa) − dep(L_ab)| ≤ 6ε`. By Lemma 3, `2p_a(1 − p_a)(1 − |c|) ≤ 6ε`; with
   `|c| ≤ 1/2` and `p_a(1 − p_a) = (1 − α²)/4` this is `1 − α² ≤ 24ε`.
4. The same with `a, b` exchanged: `1 − β² ≤ 24ε`.
5. `α² + β² = xᵀ(aaᵀ + bbᵀ)x ≤ (1 + |c|)|x|² ≤ 3/2`. Hence `2 − 48ε ≤ 3/2`, that is `ε ≥ 1/96`. ∎
*Status: exact algebra; re-derived by the review with the constant.* *Does not use:* heralds, Q6′,
the gate family, Q1, Q2, Q4, `c₀`, `L`, `|C|`, the form of the law, any assumption on the layout or
on the labels of later blocks.

**Corollary 1a (without the empty preparation).** Assume 1–7 and `K ≥ 3`. Let `P` be an unheralded
preparation of length `1 ≤ ℓ ≤ K − 2` whose labels are fixed in advance, with one weight vector `w`
on its prefix records and one assignment `r ↦ σ_r`, both the same for the four tests
(AF:125–127), credited at error `ε` on the tests `(c_u, c_v)`, `(u, v) ∈ {a, b}²`. Then
`ε ≥ 1/128`. *Proof.* Put `D(Q) := Σ_r Q(r) dep(Q(·|r))` for a law `Q` of `(r, h_1, h_2)`. `D` is
invariant under coordinatewise bijections, `r` included, so it is equal for the world's laws of two
tests even where a non-causal layout lets the test labels shift the prefix records.
`|D(Q) − D(Q′)| ≤ 4 TV(Q, Q′)`: `D(Q) = TV(Q, Q̃)` with `Q̃(r, h_1, h_2) = Q(r)Q(h_1|r)Q(h_2|r)` and
`TV(Q̃, Q̃′) ≤ 3 TV(Q, Q′)`. The targets give `D = Σ_r w_r 2p_{a,r}(1 − p_{a,r})` for `aa` and the
same times `|c|` for `ab`. Hence `Σ_r w_r (1 − α_r²) ≤ 32ε`, likewise for `β_r`, and each
`2 − α_r² − β_r² ≥ 1/2`, so `1/2 ≤ 64ε`. ∎ *Status: exact; the bound on `TV(Q̃, Q̃′)` was obtained
here by a hybrid and by the review a second way, by a direct `ℓ¹` split.*
*Exchange mode, adaptive prefixes.* The statement also holds when the prefix is an adaptive policy:
the two test labels are written after the prefix, so replacing `c_a` by `c_b` in the second test
round leaves `(r, h_1)` unchanged for every `u` and shifts `h_2` by the fixed image of
`ι(c_b − c_a)`, whatever the prefix policy, and `D` is invariant under a bijection of `h_2`.
With `K ≥ L + 1 ≥ 3`, the corollary applies to every such Q6′(i) witness.

**Proposition 2 (the form L5.4 asks about).** Assume 1–7; Q3′ and Q5′ at `(ε, K)` with
`K ≥ L + 1`; and admissible Q6′(i) witnesses `P_1, P_2` for `ψ_1 = a`, `ψ_2 = −a` whose labels are
fixed in advance. Assume further that both are unheralded, or **(C)**: no record of rounds
`1, …, j − 1` depends on the label of block `j` (a layout option, not a requirement of the schema,
P:206–209). Then `ε ≥ 1/48`. *Proof.*
1. Pad each `P_i` with `c(0)` to length `K − 2`; `P′_i` is admissible with the same herald
   (AF:122–123). As in Lemma I (S4:262–282, steps 2, 3 and 5 of FC-1), for `u ∈ {a, b}` the
   credited mean `x_i` of `P′_i` on `𝒯_K` satisfies `|u·x_i − u·ψ_i| ≤ 10ε`.
2. Let `W^u_i` be the law of the round-`(K − 1)` record after `P′_i` with label `c_u` in block
   `K − 1`, conditional on the herald. By Lemma 1, replacing `c_a` by `c_b` shifts the
   round-`(K − 1)` record by one element `δ`, the same for `i = 1, 2`. For unheralded witnesses
   that is a statement about marginal laws. Under (C) the prefix records are unchanged for every
   `u`, so the accepted event is the same event and conditioning commutes with the shift. So
   `TV(W^a_1, W^a_2) = TV(W^b_1, W^b_2)`.
3. Credit and linearity of the target in the state give `TV(W^u_i, L^u(x_i)) ≤ ε`, and

**R1 (statement; re-derived here; exact).** Under D4 a configuration of `O` is a pair (previous,

target law of `X(h_1)` therefore puts at least `1 − ε` on the two maximizers, and the world's
round-1 marginal is within `ε` of it. ∎ No label action and no additivity is used.
