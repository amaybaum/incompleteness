## 3. Branch nodes (depth-first)

**N1. What survives a herald?** Lemma A-T (d), the 0-or-1 dichotomy, is a statement about marginal
continuation laws and is lost under conditioning (S3:205–206). Lemma A-T (a), (b) is a statement
about values: `h = H_O r_O + H_P c + H_U u` with label-independent homomorphisms. Check: its proof
(S3:79–85) uses additivity of `Φ_F` and the additive decomposition of the time-zero configuration,
and no property of the law. **Verdict: the translation form survives heralds and every change of
law. Decisive branch: use (a), not (d).** → N2.

**N2. Translation covariance of record laws.** Lemma 1 (§4). Check: exact algebra. **Proved.**
Consequence: up to a known, label-determined shift of each round's record, the complete record is
one label-free random variable `z = H_U u (+ H_O r_O)`. → N3.

**N3. Which target statistics are not shift-invariant?** Class probabilities are not shift-invariant
(a shift moves a record across a class boundary), so the gate-then-read class statistics of a
one-axis family are not excluded by N2 alone; this is the content of S3:208–209. Functionals of the
complete-record law that coordinatewise bijections preserve are label-independent in the world.
Candidate: `dep_{12}(P) = TV(P, P_1 ⊗ P_2)`, the dependence between two rounds' complete records.
Check: Lemma 2, exact. → N4.

**N4. Does the declared target have label-dependent `dep`?** Lemma 3: for the Lüders reads of
AF:88–91, `dep(L_aa) = 2p_a(1 − p_a)` and `dep(L_ab) = 2p_a(1 − p_a)|a·b|`, with `p_a` the class
probability of the first read. They differ unless the prepared state is near an eigenstate of `a`;
the same with `a, b` exchanged; no state is near eigenstates of both when `|a·b| ≤ 1/2`. Check:
exact algebra. **Verdict: contradiction with N2–N3 for small `ε`.** → Theorem 1. This closes the
question at this node; the remaining nodes close the lines named in the task and the controls.

**N5. L5.4 as posed: heralded Q6′ witnesses.** Independent of N4. For two equal-length admissible
preparations, the read-`a` and read-`b` record laws are shifts of each other by one common element,
so the two total variations agree, while the target separates witnesses for `a` and `−a` by about 1
under read `a` and by at most about 1/2 under read `b`. Check: Proposition 2. **Verdict: fails
below 1/48; for heralded witnesses the proof needs a causal layout (C).**

**N6. Line (i): simplex structure.** Lemma 4: for a coset-uniform law, with every herald and every
function of the complete continuation record admitted, the prepare–measure fragment across a cut is
the full classical theory on a finite set `Ξ` of coset labels. It is simplex-embeddable at every
member. Check of the task's two sub-questions:
- *Does that alone exclude an `ε`-family?* No. Main's classical-dimension proposition bounds exact
  reproduction by a model with a fixed number `N` of ontic states (Main:558); `|Ξ|` grows with `n`,
  and finite deterministic `ε`-realizations of a qubit exist at every `ε` (Main:596–598; Main:600

  preparations and effects approximates a qubit; C-POS-1 is a classical hidden-variable model that
  meets the Q-conditions on its class (P:675).
- *Is the obstruction evaded as `n → ∞`?* The dimension obstruction is. The obstruction of
  Theorem 1 is not dimensional and holds at every `n`.
- *Pressure test of the reading "the simplex leaves room".* In class A every function of `ξ` is an
  available effect, so two preparations with nearly equal statistics on all effects have nearly
  equal laws on `Ξ`. With credit on tests whose complete records determine `ξ`, this points to a

**Verdict: line (i) is true as a structural statement; the dimension count alone does not decide

**N7. Line (iii): (SP) for class A.** Lemma 4 (c): in the form stated for `Z₀` (OA2:535), (SP)

**N9. Superseded route (recorded, not relied on).** For a coset-uniform law, credit is a

## 4. Results

**Notation.** `G_j` is the group of round-`j` records, `((V × V)^O)^τ` (in exchange mode, times the
label coordinate). For a law `P` on `G_1 × G_2`, `dep(P) := TV(P, P_1 ⊗ P_2)`. `α = a·x`,
`β = b·x` for the Bloch vector `x` of a state; `p_a = (1 + α)/2`, `p_b = (1 + β)/2`; `c = a·b`.

**Lemma 1 (translation covariance).** *Hypotheses.* (h1) `F` is additive (R-lin, any `q ≥ 2`,
either carrier; on `ℤ^3` a finite horizon involves finitely many sites). (h2) Initial-condition
mode with labels as block content, or exchange mode with non-adaptive label sequences. (h3) H-μ:
the law of the unread configuration `u` is the same for the label sequences compared. (h4) `r_O`,
the layout and `τ` are those of the one interface (D1). *Statement.* For complete label assignments
`c, c′` and every `u`, `h(c′, u) = h(c, u) + H_P(c′ − c)`. Hence the record law of `c′` is the
image of the record law of `c` under the coordinatewise shift by `H_P(c′ − c)`. *Proof.* S3:79–85,
steps 1–3: `Φ_F` is a group endomorphism; the time-zero configuration is the sum of the embedded
`r_O`, labels and `u`; an exchange is `x ↦ (I − Π_C)x + ι(c_j)`. ∎ *Status: exact algebra.* It is
Lemma A-T (a), (b) read for two label sequences, and M1 Lemma A (iii) and Corollary A′ ("The
physical joint law of all records does not depend on the setting", M1:424–425) in TUNE-1's terms.

**Lemma 2.** (i) `dep` is invariant under coordinatewise bijections of `G_1 × G_2`.
(ii) `|dep(P) − dep(P′)| ≤ 3 TV(P, P′)`. *Proof.* (i) A coordinatewise bijection maps marginals to
marginals and products to products, and total variation is invariant under bijections. (ii)
`TV(P_1 ⊗ P_2, P′_1 ⊗ P′_2) ≤ TV(P_1, P′_1) + TV(P_2, P′_2) ≤ 2 TV(P, P′)`, and the triangle
inequality. ∎ *Status: exact.*

**Lemma 3 (target values).** For a state `σ` and reads `u, v ∈ {a, b}`, the target law of the
two-round test `(c_u, c_v)` is `L_uv = Σ_{k_1,k_2} π(k_1, k_2) q_u(·|k_1) ⊗ q_v(·|k_2)`, with
`π(k_1) = Tr(P^u_{k_1} σ)` and `π(k_2|k_1) = Tr(P^v_{k_2} P^u_{k_1})`. Since `q_u(·|u1)` and
`q_u(·|u0)` have disjoint supports, `dep(L_uv) = Σ_{k_1,k_2} ½|π(k_1,k_2) − π(k_1)π(k_2)|`, and for
a 2×2 table all four deviations have the common modulus `|π(1,1) − π(1,·)π(·,1)|`. Therefore
`dep(L_aa) = 2p_a(1 − p_a)` and `dep(L_ab) = 2p_a(1 − p_a)|c|`, and likewise with `a, b`
exchanged. *Check.* `π(1,1) − π(1,·)π(·,1)` equals `p_a − p_a²` for `aa` and
`p_a(1 − p_a)[(1 + c)/2 − (1 − c)/2] = p_a(1 − p_a)c` for `ab`. ∎ *Status: exact.*

**Theorem 1 (R-lin fails Q5′).** Assume (h1)–(h4), a declared target with Lüders reads as at
AF:88–91, and `K ≥ 2`. Then `F(ε; 𝒯_R)` fails for every `ε < 1/96`. Consequently Q5′, Q3′ (which
contains Q5′) and every conjunction containing either fail at `(ε, K)` for all such `ε` and `K`.
*Proof.*
1. Suppose `F(ε; 𝒯_R)` holds. The empty preparation has one prefix record and one credited state
   `σ`. The tests `(c_a, c_a)`, `(c_a, c_b)`, `(c_b, c_b)`, `(c_b, c_a)` lie in `𝒯_R` and fit
   (`0 + 2 ≤ K`), so the world's laws satisfy `TV(W_uv, L_uv) ≤ ε` with the `L_uv` of Lemma 3 for
   this one `σ`.
2. By Lemma 1, `W_ab` is a coordinatewise shift of `W_aa`; by Lemma 2 (i), `dep(W_aa) = dep(W_ab)`.
3. By Lemma 2 (ii), `|dep(L_aa) − dep(L_ab)| ≤ 6ε`. By Lemma 3, `2p_a(1 − p_a)(1 − |c|) ≤ 6ε`; with
   `|c| ≤ 1/2` and `p_a(1 − p_a) = (1 − α²)/4` this is `1 − α² ≤ 24ε`.
4. The same with `a, b` exchanged: `1 − β² ≤ 24ε`.
5. `α² + β² = xᵀ(aaᵀ + bbᵀ)x ≤ (1 + |c|)|x|² ≤ 3/2`, the largest eigenvalue of `aaᵀ + bbᵀ` being
   `1 + |c|`. Hence `2 − 48ε ≤ 3/2`, that is `ε ≥ 1/96`. ∎
*Status: exact algebra.* *Uses:* Lemma 1; the Lüders form of the two reads with `|a·b| ≤ 1/2`;
`F(ε; 𝒯_R)` for the empty preparation on four two-round tests. *Does not use:* heralds, Q6′, the
gate family, Q1, Q2, Q4, `c₀`, `L`, `|C|`, the form of the law, any assumption on the layout or on
the labels of later blocks.

**Corollary 1a (no empty preparation).** Let `P` be any unheralded preparation of length
`ℓ ≤ K − 2`. Under the hypotheses of Theorem 1, `P` has no assignment credited at `ε < 1/128` on
`𝒯_R ∩ 𝒯_2`. *Proof.* Put `D(Q) := Σ_r Q(r) dep(Q(·|r))` for a law `Q` of
`(r, h_1, h_2)`. `D` is invariant under coordinatewise bijections, and `|D(Q) − D(Q′)| ≤ 4 TV(Q, Q′)`
(`D(Q) = TV(Q, Q̃)` with `Q̃(r, h_1, h_2) = Q(r, h_1)Q(h_2|r)`, and `TV(Q̃, Q̃′) ≤ 3 TV(Q, Q′)`).
The targets give `D = Σ_r w_r 2p_{a,r}(1 − p_{a,r})` for `aa` and the same times `|c|` for `ab`.
Hence `Σ_r w_r (1 − α_r²) ≤ 32ε`, likewise for `β_r`, and each `2 − α_r² − β_r² ≥ 1/2`, so
`1/2 ≤ 64ε`. ∎ *Status: exact algebra; the bound `TV(Q̃, Q̃′) ≤ 3 TV(Q, Q′)` was obtained by one
hybrid argument and not cross-checked a second way.* With `K ≥ L + 1 ≥ 3`, this applies to every
unheralded Q6′(i) witness.

**Proposition 2 (heralded witnesses, the form L5.4 asks about).** Assume (h1)–(h4); Q3′ and Q5′ at
`(ε, K)`; `K ≥ L + 1`; admissible Q6′(i) witnesses `P_1, P_2` for `ψ_1 = a`, `ψ_2 = −a`, heralded
or not; and, if either is heralded, **(C)**: no record of rounds `1, …, j − 1` depends on the
label of block `j` (the layout option of P:206–209). Then `ε ≥ 1/48`. *Proof.*
1. Pad each `P_i` with `c(0)` to length `K − 2`; `P′_i` is admissible with the same herald
   (AF:122–123). As in Lemma I (S4:262–282, steps 2, 3 and 5 of FC-1), for `u ∈ {a, b}` the
   credited mean `x_i` of `P′_i` on `𝒯_K` satisfies `|u·x_i − u·ψ_i| ≤ 10ε`.
2. Let `W^u_i` be the law of the round-`(K − 1)` record after `P′_i` with label `c_u` in block
   `K − 1`, conditional on the herald. By Lemma 1 and (C), replacing `c_a` by `c_b` leaves the
   prefix records unchanged and shifts the round-`(K − 1)` record by one element `δ`, the same for
   `i = 1, 2`. Conditioning on an event on the prefix records commutes with that shift. So
   `TV(W^a_1, W^a_2) = TV(W^b_1, W^b_2)`.
3. Credit and linearity of the target in the state give `TV(W^u_i, L^u(x_i)) ≤ ε`, and
   `TV(L^u(x_1), L^u(x_2)) = |u·(x_1 − x_2)|/2`. So `| |a·Δ| − |b·Δ| | ≤ 8ε`, `Δ = x_1 − x_2`.
4. `|a·Δ| ≥ 2 − 20ε` and `|b·Δ| ≤ 2|c| + 20ε ≤ 1 + 20ε`. Hence `1 − 40ε ≤ 8ε`. ∎
*Status: exact given Lemma I's steps, which were re-derived here for both reads; (C) is a
hypothesis for heralded witnesses only.* Step 3 holds for every pair of equal-length admissible
preparations: their credited means differ by a vector with `|a·Δ| ≈ |b·Δ|`.

**Lemma 4 (line (i) and line (iii)).** Assume (h1)–(h4), a coset-uniform law, (C), a cut after
round `ℓ`, every positive-probability herald on the prefix record admitted as a preparation and
every function of the complete continuation record admitted as an effect. Let `S` be the subgroup
`H_U(U′)` of prefix-and-continuation records and `S₀ = {t : (0, t) ∈ S}`.
(a) Given the prefix labels and prefix record `r`, the continuation record under continuation
labels `c_>` is uniform on `ξ + t(c_>) + S₀`, with `ξ = ξ(c_≤, r)` a point of the quotient of the
continuation record group by `S₀` (S3:199–200).
(b) The fragment across the cut embeds identically in the classical theory on the finite set `Ξ`
of reachable `ξ`: every preparation is a law on `Ξ`, every point law occurs (herald on one prefix
record), and the complete continuation record determines `ξ`, so every function on `Ξ` is an
effect.
(c) With OA-2's `Z₀` read as the label-corrected joint record, every reachable law of `Z₀` is
invariant under `0 × S₀`; (SP) as stated at OA2:535 fails when `S₀ ≠ 0`, and holds for `ξ`.
*Status: (a), (b) exact; (c) exact under the identification of OA-2's objects with TUNE-1's, which
is an unreviewed reading (3A:595–597).*

**Lemma 5 (rule-free necessary condition).** For any rule, law and mode: if `F(ε; 𝒯_R)` holds at
`K ≥ 2`, then for `(u, v) = (a, b)` or `(b, a)` the world's laws satisfy
`dep(W_uu) − dep(W_uv) ≥ 1/16 − 6ε`. *Proof.* `α² + β² ≤ 3/2` gives `min(α², β²) ≤ 3/4`, so
`p_u(1 − p_u) ≥ 1/16` for one `u`, and `dep(L_uu) − dep(L_uv) = 2p_u(1 − p_u)(1 − |c|) ≥ 1/16`;
Lemma 2 (ii). ∎ *Status: exact.* A passing family's labels must change the dependence between the
complete records of consecutive rounds; under the hypotheses of Lemma 1 no additive rule does.

## 6. What the result covers

**Hypothesis list for Theorem 1, complete.** (1) The rule is additive: R-lin at any fixed `q ≥ 2`;
the proof uses only that `Φ_F` is a group endomorphism. (2) `Λ ∈ 𝓛_fin`, or `ℤ^3` under H-∞.
(3) Labels enter as initial block content, or by exchange under H-write along non-adaptive
sequences. (4) H-μ, for an arbitrary law. (5) One `r_O`, layout and `τ` (D1, D2). (6) The record is
the complete history of `O` (D4). (7) The declared reads carry Lüders target instruments with fixed
conditional record laws and `a·b ∈ [−1/2, 1/2]` (AF:88–91). (8) `F(ε; 𝒯_R)` is posed for the empty
preparation and two-round tests, `K ≥ 2` (or Corollary 1a: any unheralded preparation, `K ≥ 3`,
constant `1/128`).

**Class closed.** Every interface of every R-lin family in the TUNE-1 schema, under every law on
the D3 list (uniform, uniform product, level-set, non-uniform product), on both carriers, in both
modes, with any control-region size and any Q6′ witnesses: it fails Q5′ at every `ε < 1/96` and
every `K ≥ 2`, in particular at `K = L + 1`. No separation-length restriction is needed, since
`F` is posed at every `K` (AF:274).

| 3A class | Effect |
|---|---|
| A (L5.4, init, coset-uniform) | closed by Theorem 1; Proposition 2 answers the heralded-witness form directly |
| B (L5.4, L5.5, exch, H-write) | closed by Theorem 1: Q5′ is tested on non-adaptive read sequences after the empty preparation, to which Lemma A-T (b) applies; adaptive witnesses are not reached |
| C (L5.6, level-set and non-uniform product laws) | closed by Theorem 1, which uses no property of the law beyond H-μ |
| D (R-nl, NL-A, NL-P) | not touched: Lemma 1 fails at additivity (S4:86–106). Lemma 5 is a necessary condition any R-nl candidate must meet |

**Not covered.** Rules that are not additive. Interfaces outside the schema: supplied per-tick
control, labels acting other than as cell contents. Record conventions other than D4: coarse or
non-additive reads (control K4; OA-2's open exit AW-OA2-1, OA2:601–602). Targets whose reads are not
of the Lüders form of AF:88–91. Nothing here is a statement about OI beyond the additive rule of
ledger item (vii) under TUNE-1's definitions.

**Relation to Main.** Main:562 and Main:600 item 3 (no fixed finite carrier reproduces the full
qubit theory) play no part: the obstruction here is not dimensional and holds on growing carriers.
Main:600 item 4 and the gluing theorem are not contradicted (control K2). For the statement at
Main:588, that finite reversible read-write dynamics does not itself generate the tunable
state-mixing operation, with its kernel support at configuration level: the present result is a
paper derivation at the level of record laws, for the additive rule only, against an approximate
operational target. It is consistent with that statement, uses none of its kernel theorems, and
adds no support to it beyond the additive class; it says nothing about non-additive bijective
rules, which that statement also covers. It is a consistency-axis result (§A.23).

**§A.31 classification.** Lemma 1: ELABORATING (M1 Lemma A and Corollary A′; S3 Lemma A-T (a),
(b)). Theorem 1 and Proposition 2: NEW for TUNE-1 (gem by criterion (a): they decide L5.4, L5.5 and
L5.6). Hidden assumption exposed (criterion (b)): the L5 node tree took the coset dichotomy, which
depends on the law and is lost under conditioning, as the operative constraint of R-lin, and opened
child nodes by witness type and by law; the law-free shift form already conflicts with Q5′ before
any witness is used. Assumption-watch marker: before splitting a failure class by witness type or
by law, test the conditions' own test family against the measure-free part of the structure lemma.
Lemma 4: ELABORATING (OA-2 Theorem E(b)). Lemma 5: NEW, rule-free.
