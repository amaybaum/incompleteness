# Triage of open Issues under the amended target (r1)

Paper-only, read-only planning record. It proposes no tier, readiness, authorization or status
value, edits no Issue and starts no work. Every change it names is a proposal for an owner
decision (#914 c.6876304390). Issues carry planning, never scientific status (`AGENTS.md` §A.45
rule 1); nothing here assesses whether any part of the target holds.

## 1. Method

**Target read against.** #914 c.6875046763 (five decisions; first target) as amended by #914
c.6875548603 (three parts: inclusion of QM in OI on one common realization; a sector picked out by a
physically justified condition C that is exactly QM; GR from the same substratum and observer; "Not
required. That every OI model behaves quantum-mechanically."). The parts are named as in the draft
`GATE-A-AMEND-r4.md` (model-reviewed, unreviewed at r4, not adopted): inclusion **I**; sector
soundness **S⇒** and coverage **S⇐**; access closure **S-A**; confinement **S-J**; necessity
**S⇐ᴺ** (an option, not adopted); Bell clause **B**; gravity **G**; common architecture **𝔄**.
*Approximation milestone* and *kinematics input* refer to decisions 2 and 4 of c.6875046763.

**What was read.** All 90 open Issues (130 open items, 40 of them pull requests) on 2026-11-14.
`NOTES-r1.md` lists the depth of reading per Issue and what was not read.

**Test for "affected".** An Issue is listed as affected when at least one of these holds on its own
text or governing comments: (a) wording that states a two-way or class-wide OI–QM requirement;
(b) an obligation the amended target no longer requires of all of OI; (c) an obligation the
amended target adds; (d) a prerequisite or ordering that changes; (e) a recorded owner decision that
needs reconciling. Where only the role of an Issue relative to the target changes, the row says so.

**Readings.** A statement marked *reading* is this triage's interpretation, not a statement of any
record. The amendment draft is itself a draft; where it carries an open owner question (its
questions 1–4) the triage carries the question and does not answer it.

**Count.** 39 affected, 51 unaffected.
