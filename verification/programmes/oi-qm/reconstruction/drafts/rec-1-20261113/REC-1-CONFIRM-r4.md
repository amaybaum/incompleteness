### 2. "No new physical premise" and the relation of H-xiv to H-μ

"No new physical premise" is accurate in the narrowed sense r4 states: nothing is adopted, and H-xiv
is carried into every conclusion. Two defects remain.

- **(a) H-xiv is identified with H-μ, which is too strong (RC-r4-1).** H-μ at P:145 is a time-zero
  condition: "the law of the configuration of `Λ ∖ O` … at time zero, independent of everything the
  observer does". Row 37 itself calls H-μ "the time-zero law". The review's point is that time-zero
  independence does not cover correlation that feedback creates over time or that herald conditioning
  creates. H-xiv is therefore not simply "the unadopted H-μ". The two are related, but r4 gives no
  argument that one contains the other.
- **(b) The non-vacuity control does not require H-xiv (RC-r4-2).** H-xiv must hold for *every*
  admitted procedure, including every feedback schedule written by φ. So an instance where any such
  procedure correlates a later setting with the response-relevant variable falls outside the
  conclusion. If no instance satisfies H-xiv, O-C would hold vacuously. P4(v) and O-V should
  therefore require an instance that satisfies H-xiv.
