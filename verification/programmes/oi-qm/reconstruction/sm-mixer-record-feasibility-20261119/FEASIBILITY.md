## Decisive falsifier on the raw binary history

Let $k\in\mathbb F_2^\Lambda$ equal one at all odd sites and zero at the even sites, and put $h=(k,0)$. Every even site has six odd neighbours and every odd site sees only even neighbours. Thus $Ak=0$ over $\mathbb F_2$. Linearity gives $F(z+h)=F(z)+F(h)$, and $F(h)=(0,k)$, $F^2(h)=h$. Both differences vanish on the even cut. Consequently $Y_t(z+h)=Y_t(z)$ at all integer times by invertibility, and in particular in every retained finite record. The two states have equal positive mass under the same counting law. Here $h$ relates initial states for a counterexample. The argument uses autonomous evolution with a passive read, so no method for physically setting $h$ is required.

For the candidate, use modal projections $U_e=\langle e,\sigma(u)\rangle,\ U_o=\langle o,\sigma(u)\rangle,\ V_e=\langle e,\sigma(v)\rangle,\ V_o=\langle o,\sigma(v)\rangle$. The putative quadratures at one tick of interest are

$$
\widehat a_e=U_e+i(U_o-\sqrt2\,V_e),\qquad
\widehat a_o=U_o+i(U_e-\sqrt2\,V_o).
$$

There is a binary configuration $z$ with $U_e=V_o=0,\ U_o>0,\ V_e>0$. On even current sites and odd previous sites take $\sigma=+1$, using the zero sums above. Take the odd current signs equal to the signs of $o$, and the even previous signs equal to those of $e$ at nonzero entries, with $+1$ at zeros. Under $z\mapsto z+h$ only $U_o$ changes sign. The difference of even squared amplitudes is

$$
(U_o-\sqrt2\,V_e)^2-(-U_o-\sqrt2\,V_e)^2=-4\sqrt2\,U_oV_e\ne 0.
$$

The odd squared amplitude is $U_o^2>0$ in both states, so the normalized even fraction $|\widehat a_e|^2/(|\widehat a_e|^2+|\widehat a_o|^2)$ differs as well. This falsifies exact recovery of that candidate statistic as a function of the complete passive even-site history, even under ideal retention and the common law. It also falsifies the exact real-wave recurrence for these projected signs on the full support. If the even component satisfied $U_e(t+1)=2sU_o(t)-U_e(t-1)$, the identical even histories would determine the same $U_o(t)$. The two histories have opposite nonzero $U_o$ at the chosen tick.

This bound concerns a particular projected-sign identification, the passive grading cut and full support. Conditional expectations given a raw history are different objects.
