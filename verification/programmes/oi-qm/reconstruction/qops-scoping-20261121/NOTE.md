## 0. Summary

1. **What OI derives (§1).** OI derives a representation layer: every finite observable law has
   an exact fixed-basis unitary and Born representation, `S ⇔ D ⇔ Q_fb` [P, partly K]. For a
   bounded experiment, a reversible realization reproduces any finite quantum instrument family to
   any finite accuracy, given the quantum data as input [P]. OI derives no native coherent
   operation. In the kernel, a theory generated by the substratum's monomial class has no
   composite unitary control and executes no layer flow at time one half
   (`StructuralClosure.lean:330`, `LiftAudit.lean:200`) [K]. Exact finite operational quantum
   mechanics is characterized relative to named conditions (five completion conditions; the
   layer-flow form), and none of those conditions is supplied by the stated substratum dynamics
   with observer access (`Main:564`, `GR:269`) [P, K].
2. **Gap list (§2).** Against the ingredients scalable quantum computation needs:
   - *Assumed:* qubit encoding, the clock, efficient measurement and the error model. These are
     the hypotheses of the conditional BQP model, `Comp:94`.
   - *Absent as native mechanisms:* coherent preparation, a coherent mixer, phases, a
     non-Clifford resource, selective classical control and a fault-tolerance supply.
   - *Derived only at the representation level:* the Born form and passive record persistence.
     The persistence is not shown for localized, accessible, re-armable records (MQ, AW-MQ-2).
3. **Smallest candidate mechanisms (§3), all hypotheses.**
   - *Gates and interference (H-QOPS-G).* An executable continuous layer flow of one native
     involution, with no phase intervention, read for encoded universality only. The corpus
     needs the flow together with the stated phase intervention to obtain *exact* operational QM
     (`GR:265–267`). Scalable computation needs less: approximation, and an encoded subspace.
     Whether the flow alone suffices for the native gates is the decisive test T1.
   - *Preparation and fault tolerance (H-QOPS-S).* A non-invariant low-entropy supply. PR1 shows
     that reversible native dynamics creates none in law. [LIT2] Fault tolerance needs fresh
     low-entropy qubits at a positive rate.
   - *Measurement (H-QOPS-M).* A bounded breach acting as a latch. This is the AW-MQ-2 task and
     NU's decisive question.
   - *Control (H-QOPS-C).* Native universality (NU) or a supplied program register.
4. **Dependencies (§4).** A positive NATIVE-UNIV answer is necessary for native classical
   control, but it cannot by itself supply interference: a universal permutation automaton stays
   in a monomial class. The BQP lower bound (BQ item I2) acquires native support for a hypothesis
   only where an H-QOPS item is established. The upper bound I3 and the width ceiling I6 do not
   depend on QOPS.
5. **Assumption-watch markers (§5).**
   - AW-QOPS-1: exact unitary control and computational universality are different targets.
   - AW-QOPS-2: fault tolerance and the preparation regress are one supply obligation.
   - AW-QOPS-3: the realization theorem is a classical simulation with supplied data.
   - AW-QOPS-4: native layer flows fix the all-ones ray.

   Their classifications are proposals (§5).

## 3. Minimal candidate mechanisms and the cheapest decisive tests (plans only)

### 3.1 H-QOPS-G: gates and interference from one executable layer flow, without a phase

**Hypothesis.** The physical dynamics makes the continuous flow `unit g t` (G2) executable for
the native shear and swap gates of a finite region, at intermediate `t`. It supplies no relative
phase.

**Why it is the smallest candidate [W].** G4 shows that the monomial class executes no layer flow
at `t = 1/2`. Any coherent mixer therefore needs at least a non-monomial operation. For a
permutation involution `g` that moves `a` to `b`, `unit g t` has entries
`(1 + e^{iπt})/2` at `(a, a)` and `(1 − e^{iπt})/2` at `(a, b)`. Both are nonzero for
`0 < t < 1`, so `unit g t` is non-monomial. G5 shows that the layer flow *together with* the stated
phase intervention gives exact QM. The phase intervention is a further assumption on the class
(`GR:267`). The computational question needs strictly less than G5's conclusion: approximate
gates on an encoded subspace. G6's `not_hControl_evenCycle` shows that a real antisymmetric
algebra fails *exact* control. [LIT2] Real-amplitude quantum computation is BQP-universal
(Bernstein–Vazirani 1997), and Toffoli together with a real basis-changing single-qubit gate is
computationally universal (Shi 2003; Aharonov 2003). So exact-control obstructions do not decide
the computational question (AW-QOPS-1).

**What it must preserve (controls).**
- G1 (the native step at `t = 1`).
- G4 for the monomial class: the hypothesis is an extension, not a reinterpretation.
- AW-QOPS-4: `unit g t` fixes the all-ones vector, since `(1 − g)·1 = 0`. Any algebra the flows
  generate lies in `u(N)` restricted to the complement of the all-ones ray, plus that ray's phase.
  G7 then applies if the generated class is an architecture: no quarter phase on the full carrier.

**Independent prediction required (§A.29), stated in advance.** Under H-QOPS-G, two consequences
follow, and neither is the problem the hypothesis was built to fix:
- the all-ones ray is a decoherence-free direction of every native-gate drive;
- every native-gate drive conserves the overlap with the uniform superposition.

At the visible level this is a predicted invariant, and it is falsified by any observed native
process that changes the uniform-state population. Whether such a visible observable is defined
is open. The prediction is recorded here before any test.

**Test T1 (exact Lie closure of the native layer-flow generators).**
- *Object.* A finite region `R` of `k` sites of `ℤ³`, with every site outside `R` held at a
  uniform background `c`. The members are:
  - U₂ (the rounded rule at `q = 2`), with `k ∈ {2, 3}`, giving `N = 2^{2k} ∈ {16, 64}`;
  - the linear member L₂ at the same `k`, as a comparison.
  The shear gate `shear_i` and the swap gate `swap_i` (G1) at each `i ∈ R` are permutation
  involutions `P_g` of the region's `N` configurations.
- *Computation.* The real Lie algebra `𝔏 = Lie_ℝ⟨ i(1 − P_g)/2 ⟩` is computed in exact rational
  arithmetic, as BQ's `checks/lie_closure.py` does for hand-chosen gates. Two further objects are
  computed: the commutant of `{P_g}`, which gives the joint invariant decomposition
  `ℂ^N = ⊕ B_j`, and, for each block, whether `𝔏|_{B_j} ⊇ su(B_j)`.
- *Decision rule (preregistered as a rule, not as expected numbers).*
  - **T1-pos:** some block `B` with `dim B ≥ 4` has `𝔏|_B ⊇ su(B)`. The layer flows of native
    gates are then encoded-universal for two qubits on that block, for this member and region.
  - **T1-part:** some block has `dim B ≥ 2` and `𝔏|_B ⊇ su(B)`, but no such block reaches
    dimension 4.
  - **T1-neg:** no block of dimension `≥ 2` has `𝔏|_B ⊇ su(B)`.

  The verdict text is generated from the measured block table.
- *Controls.* The verdict prints only if all of the following are green.
  - K1 reproduces BQ's values: dimension 2 for two commuting NOTs at `N = 4`, 9 for {NOT, CNOT,
    SWAP} at `N = 4`, and 49 for {NOT, CNOT, Toffoli} at `N = 8`. These values are independent
    of the native rule.
  - K2 is a countercontrol: adding one non-ones-fixing generator (a diagonal phase) must remove
    the all-ones invariant, so the decomposition code must report one fewer block.

### 3.2 H-QOPS-S: a non-invariant low-entropy supply for preparation and fault tolerance

**Hypothesis.** One supply supplies both fiducial preparation and the fresh ancillas that fault
tolerance consumes: a boundary or time-slice law with an incoming low-entropy stream (PR1's NEQ-T
or NEQ-O).

**Why it is the smallest candidate.**
- *Native side.* P1 excludes net supply in law from native history under any invariant law.
  The listed escapes are conditioning, a boundary law, an orbit or record-compatible law, or a
  stream.
- *Literature [LIT2].* Noisy reversible computation without a supply of fresh qubits loses its
  information within logarithmic depth (Aharonov, Ben-Or, Impagliazzo and Nisan, 1996). Threshold
  constructions consume fresh ancillas at a positive rate per logical operation.
- *Consequence [W, conditional on that literature and on a noise model].* A scalable
  fault-tolerant computer in OI needs a non-invariant supply. The obligation is the same as that
  of the preparation regress (AW-QOPS-2).

**Controls.** PR1's exclusion lemma and budget, which are to be preserved. AW-MQ-3: the background
is a choice within an open set, priced at 32 bits per site.

**Test T3 (paper; cheapest).**

  hypotheses exactly: unital noise, no fresh qubits, and its depth bound.
- *Step 2.* Map those hypotheses onto PR1's setting. An invariant law on a fixed block corresponds
  to no fresh qubits. The reversible native step corresponds to the noiseless part.
- *Decision rule.* The AW-QOPS-2 conditional is **established** at paper level if every ABIN
  hypothesis has a stated PR1 counterpart. It is **not established** if some hypothesis has none,

- *Countercontrol.* A NEQ-T stream at a positive rate must escape the bound in the same mapping.
- *Not decided by T3.* Whether OI's actual boundary supplies such a stream.

- *Controls.* `r = 0` must reproduce PR1's constancy.

### 3.3 H-QOPS-M: a bounded breach acting as a latch, for measurement records

**Hypothesis.** The native rule Q has a finite closed gadget on a uniform background with three
properties:
- a carrier's arrival drives its boundary layer out of `[c−1, c+1]` only transiently, then back,
  without radiation;
- the gadget leaves a record of bounded size, fixed in advance, held for a stated window;
- the rest of the apparatus returns to its no-carrier content, and readiness is restored for a
  second carrier.

This is the AW-MQ-2 task (MQ `RESULT.md:549`) and NU's decisive question (NU `NOTE.md:86`).

**Why it is the smallest candidate.** By MQ3, any use of a carrier outside its class needs a
breach. The searched breaches are unbounded fronts that consume readiness (MQ §5, X2). A bounded
breach is the least departure that could give a localized, re-armable record within Q itself, with
no new rule.

**Controls.**
- MQ1–MQ3, the sharp confinement.
- PR1's budget: the record's held predictability must be priced.
- U2's radiation failure mode R3, as a negative control.

**Test T4.** Shared with #866 and #1461. It is not owned here, and it is NU's step 2, which is
not authorized.
- *Search.* Exhaustively search closed gadgets of at most `m` sites with values in `{c ± 2}`,

- *Decision rule.* Fixed in advance as AW-MQ-2's four clauses: bounded record, restored `A ∖ L`,
  held readiness, `N ≥ 2` deliveries.

  - The breach-front configurations of S-B-long, which must fail the bounded-record clause.
