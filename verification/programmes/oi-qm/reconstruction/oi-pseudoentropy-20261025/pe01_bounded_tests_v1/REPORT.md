## Result

The verdict is `results/verdict.txt`, generated by `verdict.py` from `results/measurements.json` alone. A local
regeneration is byte-identical.

**Governing classification (condition (d) read as `abs`): COUNTEREXAMPLE.** It is a counterexample to the universal
form of `book/ch18-beyond.md:114`, the statement that randomness "is unpredictable by any embedded observer regardless
of its origin". It rests on the witnesses in one admissible cell (`G_clk`, n = 5, `emb_m2_all`, K = 7), each meeting
conditions (a)–(f) of §6. The reported witness is:

| | |
|---|---|
| Generator | `G_clk`, n = 5, primitive polynomial z⁵ + z² + 1 |
| Observer class | embedded, active (subclass `emb_m2_all`), m = 2 memory bits |
| Observer tables | ρ₀ = [1,0,2,3], ρ₁ = [0,2,3,1], a = [1,0,0,1]; guess χ ≡ 0 (observer index 2361) |
| Horizon | K = 7, window K′ ∈ [4, 7] |
| Prediction advantage | Pr[χ(v_{K′}) = X_{K′}] − 1/2 = 1/8, 5/32, 5/32, 1/8 |
| Footprint | m + ⌈log₂(K+1)⌉ + 1 = 2 + 3 + 1 = 6 = θ (θ = n+1). The table size is 31 bits, held in the dynamics and not charged. |
| Recurrence bound | K_cx = 20; the μ-median orbit length is 40 |
| Knowledge level | K1: the tables are chosen for G_clk(z⁵+z²+1). |
| Family-wide status | None: the observer gives no window on the other five primitive quintics, and no observer in the subclass gives a window at K = 7 on every member of 𝔉_clk(5). |
| Realization conditions | T-level (C1) holds; C2–C4 not checked |

**Verification layer.** The statement is an existence claim, verified by exact enumeration of the joint state with
rational arithmetic. It holds for this generator, this observer and this horizon only.

**Extent of the cell.**
- Under `abs`, 36 (observer, χ) pairs meet (a)–(f). They come from 18 observers, each counted with χ and with its
  complement.
- These reduce to 3 observers, up to relabelling of the memory states that fix v₀:
  - the reported witness, with the constant guess χ ≡ 0, which is sign-consistent;
  - one with a non-constant χ, also sign-consistent (advantages 1/8, 1/8, 11/64, 1/8);
  - one that alternates in sign (1/8, 1/8, −1/8, −1/8) and qualifies only under `abs`.
- Under `signed`, 12 pairs qualify.
- The verdict's "1 witness(es)" counts this one cell, not observers.

**Mechanism.**
- For the reported witness (χ ≡ 0), the guess is correct exactly when the post-intervention system bit
  c_t = x_t ⊕ a(v_{t+1}) equals the generator's next output Y_t.
- c_t is a function of the past record, and Pr[Y_t = c_t] − 1/2 at t = 3..6 is the same curve.
- The interventions select the clocking step, and so which bit of the hidden sequence is emitted.
- Under the reference Q_p, with the same policy and fresh fair outputs, the probability is exactly 1/2, so the
  intervention alone produces no bias.
- Within the enumerated scope, no admissible witness exists for a passive observer or for the autonomous-hidden
  generators `G_lin` and `G_filt`.
- In this cell, passive m = 2 has no window, and the K = 7 windows of passive m = 3 and linear-filter m = 3 and m = 4
  observers have footprint 7 or 8.
- The result is therefore tied to an active observer and to G_clk's visible-to-hidden backaction.

**Sensitivity analysis (condition (d) read as `signed`, not governing): COUNTEREXAMPLE.** The reported witness has
positive Pr − 1/2 at all four K′. Signed COUNTEREXAMPLEs are a subset of the governing ones, and the run checks this
(SUBSET).

**Other outcomes** (verdict sections 1–2):
- **No witness outside this cell.** None meeting (a)–(f) exists for `G_lin` or `G_filt` at any n = 3..8, for `G_clk`
  at any other n, or for any passive observer.
  - The independent review confirmed this for m ≤ 2 by its own exhaustive search over every admissible cell.
  - It found no window for passive linear filters with m = 3 at n = 7, 8.
  - For the general passive m = 3 sweep and the linear-filter m = 4 sweep, it relies on the run.
- **Witnesses that fail only (f).** In 26 (generator, n, observer subclass) combinations, a witness meets (a)–(e) but
  fails (f).
  - In each, the verdict attributes unpredictability to resources: a limitation of the resource model (§6 item 4),
    not a property of observation.
  - These include `G_clk` n = 5 `emb_m2_all` at K = 10, with footprint 7.
  - The combinations are at n = 4..8, for all three generators, and mostly for the larger observer classes (`plin_m3`,

  - Witnesses that fail (c) are reported as recurrence-scale measurements.

## Scope: what the result does not show

The verdict refutes the universal form for one construction. It does not show any of the following:
- **Other knowledge levels.** No observer is a witness across the family (K0).
- **Other observer or generator classes.** It shows nothing for passive observers or for autonomous generators
  (`G_lin`, `G_filt`).
- **Full OI admissibility of the generator.** Only T-level (C1) is checked. C2–C4 are not, so it is not shown that
  G_clk(z⁵+z²+1) is an admissible OI realization in the full sense.
- **Other resource models.** Admissibility holds with the footprint exactly at θ = n+1 = 6 (memory 2, clock 3,
  output 1), with the 31 table bits held in the dynamics and not charged.
  - If those bits were charged as observer memory, the witness would lie outside θ.
  - For comparison, `Main.md:560` places a protocol table in a read-only program register of the realization's hidden
    sector.
  - θ = n is not tested as a decision rule (A1).
- **Larger n or K.** It shows no predictability beyond K = 7 within a bounded footprint, and makes no asymptotic
  statement.
- **The actual world.** It says nothing about the realization of the actual world, nor about the 10^-32 statement
  (`ch18-beyond.md:56–58`).
- **Security.** No cryptographic, circuit-security or distinguishing-only claim is made.
- **Quantum theory.** No claim is made about quantum theory or its reconstruction.

## Literature

- **Tags.** Literature tags under amendment A3 are in `review/literature/TAGS-r2.md`, which has been independently
  reviewed (`review/literature/`). They bear on no verdict.
- **The counterexample is covered in kind, not in its parameters.**
  - Gomułkiewicz–Kutyłowski–Wlaź, "Fault jumping attacks against shrinking generator" (Dagstuhl Seminar
    Proceedings 06111, 2006; read in full), outline an attack in which one bit flip in the control register of a
    shrinking generator changes its clocking. Comparing the faulty output with the correct one gives linear
    equations for the control register.
  - That generator is clocked by a separate register. The attack reruns from the same state, uses unbounded
    computation, and recovers state rather than predicting. No parameter of the `G_clk`, n = 5, `emb_m2_all` cell
    is covered.
  - Rueppel's self-decimated LFSR (Eurocrypt '87, not read here) shares G_clk's step set {1, 2}, and in both the
    clock is set by the generator itself rather than by a separate register. The clock selector differs.
    - Agievich–Solovey (arXiv math/0505660, p. 2) describe Rueppel's step at time t as 1 if σ_t = 0 and 2 if
      σ_t = 1, where σ is a linear recurrence sequence. With their eq. (3), the step is a function of the
      register's current state.
    - In G_clk the hidden register steps by L_p when the visible bit x is 0 and by L_p² when it is 1
      (`pe01/generators.py:49–51, 107–108`). That bit is the running parity x_t = x_0 ⊕ Y_0 ⊕ … ⊕ Y_{t−1} of the
      output stream, and under μ it is independent of the hidden state.
    - In the embedded protocol the observer's action a(v) is added to x before each step
      (`pe01/embedded.py:179–182`), so the observer selects the step.
  - Agievich–Solovey (read in full) state Rueppel's period result without proof and do not address prediction.
  - No work read treats a self-clocked generator under an adversary that influences its clocking, or an adaptive
    bounded-memory observer acting within one trajectory.
- **Passive horizons of linear statistics on the linear generators are covered in kind.**
  - Alon–Goldreich–Håstad–Peralta (1992; §1–§3 read) give the mechanism, in the proof of Proposition 1 and in
    Lemma 1 (Vazirani). For a fixed feedback polynomial and a uniform start, a parity of the register output is
    exactly unbiased or constant, and it is constant iff the polynomial divides its test polynomial.
  - This governs every PROVED row on `G_lin`, `G_lin_sib` and 𝔉_lin whose statistic is linear in the record: B1 K0
    (n = 3, 4), B3k, B3w, the linear-filter observers and the m = 1 passive observers.
  - The horizon values also need the order of the polynomial, the filters' test polynomials or a divisor count,
    and the paper does not state these. They are not COVERED-literature.
  - The B1 K1 rows keep their mechanical tag (A2, L1).
- **The branching-program results do not cover the passive horizons.**
  - Reingold–Steinke–Vadhan (RANDOM 2013) and Bogdanov–Dvir–Verbin–Yehudayoff (ToC 2013) treat the observer model
    of the passive classes: oblivious read-once permutation branching programs, and width 2.
  - At zero bias their bounds give only K_zero(class) ≥ K_zero(U). Beyond that point they are vacuous on the
    `G_lin` record.
- **What PE-01 contributes.** A verified, bounded counterexample to the universal form of `ch18-beyond.md:114`
  under its declared model and resource accounting. It claims no new cryptanalytic technique.
- **No novelty is claimed.** No candidate-NEW tag is assigned. Under A3 it is not assignable to the counterexample or
  to any untagged passive horizon while the works below are unread.
- **Unread:**
  - Rueppel 1987, Gollmann–Chambers 1989 and Chambers–Gollmann 1988;
  - Beth–Piper 1984, and Meier–Staffelbach 1989 and 1994;
  - Kholosha 2001, Golić–O'Connor 1994, Živković 1991 and Kanso;
  - Gomułkiewicz et al., WEA 2005 (A5/1) and WEA 2006.
  - The annex records why each was not obtained.
