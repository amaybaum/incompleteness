## 3. Cells

### 3.1 Two lemmas used repeatedly

**Lemma P (law-independence of the affine obstructions; P-here from cited results).**
- **Settings.** MECH-1 Lemma A's hypotheses are: one fixed affine bijection, a setting in
  controller coordinates at time 0, and the rest of the initial law independent of the setting.
  Any setting-independent background law meets them. So the settings part of SET/M is BLOCKED for
  every candidate that only supplies a law (x5, x6, x7, x8), at any q.
- **NC.** Every classical probability law, read in Gross's frame at odd q, has a nonnegative Wigner
  function. Affine symplectic maps permute phase-space points, so they keep it nonnegative.
  Proposition AS says that in that frame an ontic bijection acts CP iff it is affine symplectic,
  and Proposition C says the native class supplies no Wigner-negative state, quarter phase or
  infinite-order encoded unitary. Protocols with nonnegative inputs are efficiently simulable
  (L: Veitch, Ferrie, Gross and Emerson, New J. Phys. 14, 113011 (2012); not re-read). So NC is
  BLOCKED, at odd q, for every candidate that leaves the rule affine (x5, x7, x8). The scope is
  windowed, as for P1-12.
- **No ontic bijection gives NC in Gross's frame.** Proposition AS with Proposition C gives more:
  at odd q, in Gross's frame, no ontic bijection, affine or not, has a CP non-Clifford encoded
  action. Non-affine bijections are not CP there at all.

**Lemma E (no generation of a sub-maximal invariant entropy density; P-off with standard facts).**
F and F⁻¹ are sliding block codes, so h(F_*μ) = h(μ) for every translation-invariant μ. Weak
limits of F^t_*μ, and of their Cesàro averages, have h ≥ h(μ) (C-ENV D1 R3; AW-CENV-2). A law of
density h₀ therefore arises at one time only from a law of the same density at another time. Such a
law is never generated from Haar (2 log q) or from the vacuum (0).

### 3.2 x4: a native readout effect outside the configuration algebra

**Q0 and every cell: BLOCKED (P-here).**
- By the Main Definition (Main:42) the observer is a subsystem V ⊊ S. Its distinguishable states
  are its visible configurations (Lemma 1, Main:46).
- An outcome is a set A ⊆ C_V of configurations at readout. In the Born form on the configuration

  Definition's reading of registration, a Layer-0 change.
- Read instead in an encoded frame such as Gross's, x4 adds no premise: native reads there are
  already phase-sensitive (MECH-1 §1). It is then inside 𝒞, where K1 gives NC BLOCKED (odd q) and
  SET BLOCKED.

### 3.4 x6: KVP, the Kähler-vacuum background on the real lift

KVP is the strongest single-principle candidate found. It would serve three gaps at once:

- **Measurement back-action:** it would supply back-action through the symplectic bracket (MR-1

**Q0: fails.**

  generate the KVP law from another law.
- **(S) fails.** Maximal entropy at a fixed mean of a conserved quadratic form Q gives the Gaussian
  with covariance ∝ Q⁻¹.

## 4. The minimal missing principle, scoped

Within the covered class, the gaps fail at three different places. **No single covered principle
reaches more than one of them, and none reaches any one non-circularly.**

1. **NC.** At odd q, in Gross's frame, no ontic bijection, affine or not, gives a CP non-Clifford
   encoded action (§3.1, Proposition AS with Proposition C). Classical-law inputs keep protocols
   Wigner-nonnegative. Changing the dynamics (C-NL) or the background law therefore cannot supply
   NC within that frame. What is missing is the correspondence itself between ontic configurations
   and the effective quantum description: a frame other than Gross's (KIN-level), derived rather
   than chosen. At q = 2 the question is open (MECH-1 M3).
2. **SET.** It is blocked by affinity, whatever the law (Lemma A). The missing principle is a
   non-affine dynamics, which is C-NL and outside this unit.
3. **SET/M (meter supply) and O-RET.** The missing principle is a law-level selection of a
   sub-maximal-entropy background whose free choices are fixed independently. The record shows:
   - entropy density is conserved (Lemma E);
   - ledger item (iv) with finite records selects Haar;
   - no corpus principle fixes the choice among conserved quadratic forms (x6) or among Lagrangian
     subgroups (x5).

**The one multi-gap dependency found is LIFT.** The coarse-graining bridge of SM:232 is open and is
not an exit from 𝒞. On it rest three things:
- CS: lane 2's canonical J_F (K7);
- the existence of a stationary, minimum-uncertainty-type background at all, which is absent over
  F_q (C-ENV Theorem 1) and present on the real lift;
- SMD-CL-1's reading (B). That is outside scope here and is named only as a dependency.

LIFT does not touch NC or SET. Proving or refuting LIFT is the highest-leverage open step this unit
identifies. A proof would turn K7's conditional CS result into an unconditional one.
