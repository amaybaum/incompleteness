# Applying the composition contract to the native X candidate

Research draft, 24 October 2026. This is an application audit of the existing
native-X candidate, not a new native construction. The accepted composition
contract on #912 is conditional; this report identifies exactly which of its
hypotheses the candidate supplies. No premise or certification is adopted.

## Initial finding from the actual source

The candidate supplies a native X read/reset stage with success probability
5/16, a fixed private output encoding, complete public-record factorization
under the inherited transport/frontier theorem, and one terminal menu read.
It explicitly does not supply another reset after that terminal read or
closure under reuse of its output encoding. Thus it can instantiate a
single-stage success branch and a terminal observation contract; it does not
yet instantiate the composition theorem for arbitrary subsequent stages.

The source's failure convention is a terminal **no-carrier** symbol, with
failure probability 11/16. Actual private physical fields continue evolving.
No post-failure qubit carrier/reset law is supplied. This audit will retain
that distinction instead of filling it with a chosen quantum failure state.

## Fixed decision rule, before the new diagnostic

1. Reconstruct the finite register kernel from the actual candidate's
   `accept(u,v,k)` and raw output map; retain all 64 fresh seed assignments,
   all eight selector-pad assignments and all eight supplied preparations.
   Use exact rational arithmetic only.
2. On the cone generated by those eight preparations, test the success-branch
   intertwining identity with the fixed Bloch decoder described below, and
   test the source's terminal failure-record/no-carrier law. A positive result
   is a conditional one-stage instance, not a full native application proof.
3. Check the actual raw terminal port records. Investigate reuse only under
   the explicitly proposed extension: terminal X exposes its extra Y field,
   then the same unchanged sampled carrier is offered for a later decoded Y
   observation. If the joint law differs from a reset that restores the
   source's post-X ensemble independently of that metadata, reject this
   no-reset extension only. The source permits only one terminal read, so
   this is not a counterexample to its existing claim, and it is not a
   simulation of later native ticks.
4. Positive controls must include the actual source kernel and a genuinely
   independent fresh post-X sample. A swapped herald count or a missing
   constant collision must be detected. Do not promote an exact finite
   algebra check into a proof of the inherited transport theorem.

These rules are fixed before the new computation. This is an ordinary
research checkpoint, not an owner-designated native-round freeze.

## Actual boundary and fixed decoder

The source fixes T=185, P=12*840^185, S=T+P, d=68 and R=S+d. The physical
boundary at R is the pair `(U_(R-1),U_R)` on the causal neighborhood relevant
to a continuation, including the old apparatus, keys, retained information,
unused resources and public-frontier bank. It cannot be replaced by the
three output triples alone. On success the fixed encoding is

    E(x,y,r,z) = ((x,1,y) at q_Y, (x,1,r) at q_R, (0,0,z) at q_Z).

The selected unknown bit x physically drives these outputs. Fresh source bits
u,v and z give `(x,x XOR u,x XOR v,z)`. The accepted `(u,v)` patterns 00,10,11
have respective counts 5,4,1 among the 32 `(u,v,k)` assignments. The remaining
22 assignments remain failures, not silently discarded samples.

For a measure mu on the decoded calibrated carrier, define its trace and
Bloch coordinates by the total mass and the signed expectations of X,Y,Z:

    D(mu) = (mass(mu) I + E_mu[(-1)^X] sigma_X
             + E_mu[(-1)^Y] sigma_Y + E_mu[(-1)^Z] sigma_Z)/2.

The incoming admissible cone for this diagnostic is the cone generated by
the source's eight preparations, with the stipulated independent apparatus
resources. The intended reference Bloch directions are X, Y, Z and
R=(4/5,3/5,0), with both signs. Positivity is required on this preparation
cone, not on every hidden point state. This decoder is a comparison map and
does not add an available physical operation.

Coordinates throughout are named and ordered `(X,Y,R,Z)`, as in this native
source, with Z at index 3. The eight-preparation cone is a supplied channel
diagnostic; the demonstrated native predecessor supplies only its post-X
family. The diagnostic does not establish native preparation or physical
arrival of the other six menu states at the same boundary. Its probabilities
are conditional on a valid incoming history. The predecessor's separate 5/8
herald and full flag-pair law remain source-inherited, not re-enumerated here;
5/16 is not asserted as the success probability of the whole earlier pipeline.

For public selected outcome x and each of the eight selector ciphertext
records c, the success identity to check is

    D(K_(x,c,G=1) mu) = (5/128) P_x D(mu) P_x.

Here P_x is the X eigenprojector, and K retains the whole offered decoded
carrier. The factor 1/8 is for the three independent ciphertext bits. The
astronomically long old public noise word contributes its source-stated
uniform factor; its independence is inherited analytically and will not be
claimed to have been enumerated by this diagnostic. The failure check is
the declared terminal record probability `(11/128) Tr(P_x D(mu))` for each c,
paired with no-carrier. It does not identify the hidden failure fields with
a reset qubit state.

## Access and closure boundary

The source allows the six old public ports through R, its new herald port,
and one chosen terminal output port. The fixed-X whole-port record is
`(x,1,Y)`: its extra Y bit is fair given x and successful reset, but it is
correlated with the outgoing sampled carrier. An outgoing-state guarantee
after exposing that metadata therefore needs a fresh proof. A pooled
post-X marginal is insufficient for the joint-record contract.

The seed bank is initially independent of input, old seeds and frontier
symbols. The source's geometry and chronological-bijection theorem supply
the stronger independence used in its joint-record statement. Those are
inherited hypotheses here. Any additional access to keys, fresh seeds or
earlier informative fields changes the protocol. A new continuation needs
its actual two-slice interface, native evolution, and conditional seed law.

## Exact application result

The bounded diagnostic passes in normal and optimized Python execution, with
byte-identical `RESULT.json`. It uses the retained native `accept`, `pulse`
and `output` functions and the exact calibration literals, not the earlier
abstract composition toy. An independent direct evaluation of the nonlinear
native H collision agrees with the source output function at the actual
large-delay phase for all four `(x,u)` values. The source's giant transport
and complete physical boundary are not simulated here.

All eight preparation generators map to their intended positive pure qubit
states under the fixed decoder. Exact enumeration verifies 128 success
record branches and 128 terminal failure-record branches, including zero
mass branches. The stronger full offered-carrier law is also checked: each
success branch has the source's post-X ensemble, not only its decoded Bloch
vector. Linearity extends these algebra identities to the cone generated by
the eight supplied preparations with the stipulated independent apparatus
law. This is a conditional success-branch instance of the contract.

The terminal failure convention is verified without constructing a qubit
failure state. Physical rejected `(u,v)` counts are 3,8,4,7 for 00,01,10,11,
out of 32 initial `(u,v,k)` assignments. This supplies the declared 11/16
failure mass and records, while retaining the hidden physical distribution.
No second-stage action on those fields is certified.

## A concrete obstruction to unchanged-carrier reuse

Consider precisely the extension fixed in the decision rule: after the
successful native X reset, expose its raw terminal X port `(x,1,Y)` and then
offer the same sampled carrier unchanged for a later decoded Y observation.
The actual two-bit record `(Y_metadata,Y_later)` is a uniformly random equal
pair. A fresh post-X sample independent of the already revealed metadata
gives two independent fair bits. Each single-bit marginal agrees, but the
joint TV distance is **1/2**. If both comparisons retain the same 11/16
terminal failure branch before this extension, their full laws differ by
`(5/16)*(1/2)=5/32`.

There is also a positive-cone failure for the same unchanged-carrier proposal.
After conditioning on terminal metadata Y=y, its normalized decoded Bloch
coordinates are

    (trace,X,Y,Z) = (1, (-1)^x, (-1)^y, 0).

The corresponding 2-by-2 Hermitian matrix has determinant **-1/4**, so it is
not a quantum state. All four `(x,y)` cases are checked exactly. The initial
preparation cone is therefore not closed under this proposed continued use
of the sampled carrier and the same decoder. A decoder change selected from
the revealed history would not meet the fixed-interface contract as stated.

This is not a refutation of the existing native-X claim: that source stops
after one terminal read and certifies no subsequent outgoing state. Nor is
this a claim about native evolution at a later tick. It rejects an explicit
shortcut from terminal-read correctness to reusable-instrument correctness.
A physical reset/back-action, a justified different reusable encoding, or a
more restrictive continuation would need its own joint-law proof.
