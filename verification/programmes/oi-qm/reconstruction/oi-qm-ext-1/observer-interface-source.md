## 1. Decision rule and outcome

Before an operation derivation, a candidate must name:

1. a physical ingredient absent from the screened constructions;
2. independent physical motivation, with preserved controls and an independent
   consequence if it proposes a new premise;
3. the precise implementation/interface premise changed;
4. an observer–apparatus process that executes that change.

The process may be deterministic; its records may be classical; access may be
indirect. The apparatus may be a subsystem of the same substratum. Neither a
physically separate apparatus nor direct decoding of every hidden variable is
required. The physical identification and admitted preparations, controls,
records, failure branches and remaining resources must be stated together.

**Selection outcome: no physically motivated candidate with a changed premise
and an executing process has been identified in the bounded sources reviewed.**
This is a valid negative deliverable. No new operation or Bell construction is
selected. It is not an impossibility theorem for OI, continuum interfaces, or the
external programmes mentioned below.

The increment is a current-source reconciliation of #1110's named references
and a comparison of the two proposed next routes against the same availability
gate. It is not a new phase/control obstruction: the relevant negatives and
earlier selection screens already exist. In particular, F2-1 and PC-1 had
candidate-selection gates; this record does not portray those earlier rounds
as having omitted them.

The missing ingredient is an **apparatus-level source for the operational
interface**: a physical reason, implemented by the observer's dynamics, that
determines which state preparations and interventions are available and how
they are read and reused. A map or a quantum operator assigned to a process is
not yet that source.

## 3. What the physical-access sources actually supply

### 3.1 A family of observers is permissible

[Main §1.4](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/5ea5a40218e4e660b39a9b149050ced4f95d9b48/papers/Main.md#L86) and

make the description relative to a declared cut. The question is not whether
OI selects one unique observer or one measurement for the entire world.
Different physical apparatus arrangements may define different experiments.

For a proposed experiment, however, the arrangement must physically realize
its declared access. Existence of a finite read alphabet does not establish a
particular product decomposition, a native readout slice, or the ability to
write a setting. #1110 separates those requirements in §4.1 (Q1–Q5).

### 3.2 Conditioning, preparation and probability remain separate

[Main:60](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/5ea5a40218e4e660b39a9b149050ced4f95d9b48/papers/Main.md#L60) allows a fixed hidden prior as realization data.
[#1110 §4.6.3](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/f67b9f0c81e0e404d78c042adfa47d71abf3ad45/verification/programmes/oi-qm/reconstruction/observer-interface-obsmu-20261104/ANALYSIS.md#L948) gives a conditional reduction of
laboratory predictions to conditioning on records. Its requirements include
a dynamically correlated system, retained records, reliability within the
stated scope, and the open identification H-PREP. Creating the preparation
still requires selectable interactions.

The accepted D-OB7 classification of record conditioning and the D-OB8 decision
to keep H-PREP open are recorded in
[#1110 §5](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/f67b9f0c81e0e404d78c042adfa47d71abf3ad45/verification/programmes/oi-qm/reconstruction/observer-interface-obsmu-20261104/ANALYSIS.md#L1080).
Neither decision supplies readiness, retained records, a setting source or a
physical preparation procedure. This unit preserves those decisions and
does not resolve the other pending choices.

A conditional distribution can describe an already specified preparation.
It does not establish that a device can select the conditioning event,
announce failures, or renew the consumed resources. The prior conditional
loader and measure assessment are therefore useful at their stated scope;
their missing source is not removed by renaming the event a preparation.

[Main Lemma 3](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/5ea5a40218e4e660b39a9b149050ced4f95d9b48/papers/Main.md#L56) explicitly distinguishes maximal-entropy
selection from uniqueness under invariance. The
[stochastic-interface audit](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/5ea5a40218e4e660b39a9b149050ced4f95d9b48/verification/audits/operational/stochastic-observer-interface-audit.md#L278)
records that invariance alone does not determine the ensemble for the stated
architecture. Its unique-invariant-law predicate is that audit's precise
question, not a requirement that every physical preparation be stationary.
A nonstationary preparation can be admitted if its physical source is supplied.

## 6. Common-realization check and the next proof obligation

The results can be combined only at their declared scopes:

The [three-thread synthesis](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/e306143d23f534e7bbbb6e72444c026a0d55ad66/verification/programmes/oi-qm/reconstruction/oi-qm-ext-1/parallel-synthesis-20261107.md)
already states the narrower compatibility of its own conditional finite
preparation and probability constructions. That result is retained; it is not
extended to a continuum or to the other dynamics.

The **single next sourcing obligation** is to exhibit, from an independently
motivated physical rule, one observer–apparatus process with a declared
operational-state identification and an available intervention that changes a
named baseline premise. This is the missing apparatus-level interface source,
not a proposed new axiom and not a demand to make the underlying ontology
quantum.

Its minimal evidence packet would contain:

1. The native state/update or explicitly proposed extension, with its motivation
   and an independent consequence.
2. The actual apparatus preparation and setting-loading path, including the
   preparer's/controller's information, failures and consumed resources.
3. The physical rule identifying observable states and readout, and a derivation
   of the complete intervention on the admitted domain.
4. The specific obstruction it escapes under a fixed physical identification,
   with residual state and access sufficient to state the next operation.

If a new premise is required, its scientific content must be stated before
requesting its adoption. “Add the missing quantum operation” is a resource
assumption, not a source proposal. A named fundamental law need not explain
why nature has that law, but must constrain outcomes independently of the
quantum table it is supposed to explain.

**Route decision:** neither the present row-14 material nor the checked
continuum proposals pass the selection gate. Keep the no-candidate outcome.
Further work would need a new physical source proposal, supported
by primary literature or an independently motivated OI principle. Repeating
the existing coupling, posterior, basis or cardinality calculation does not
advance this obligation. No operation derivation or pair test follows from
this unit.
