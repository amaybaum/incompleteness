### T1: an exact conditional loader, with a precise preparation gap

The original binary, two-slice ring rule can perform one ordinary forward
tick that transfers an already supplied previous-slice request into the working
controller. Both requests use one identical uncontrolled precursor law.
The output equals the entire accepted local preparation, including its hidden
fields, rather than merely reproducing a receiver marginal.

This positive result requires three fixed hidden fields and five hidden
correlations in the precursor. Relative to its specified visible request root,
that event has counting mass `2^-8`. It is a conditioning mass, not a measured
factory success rate. The calculation transports the readiness requirement;
it does not physically prepare it.

The complete declared controller learns the signal at the first operative
tick after loading and retains it through the second. A preparer that reads
both precursor signal operands would learn it earlier; whether a real preparer
does so depends on its implementation. Failure mass and the entire outgoing
ring are retained. Neither reset nor a compatible next measurement is supplied.

**Derived:** conditional same-rule loading and its full source/history law.
**Assumed:** the specified precursor, fair free bits and supplied request.
**Unsourced:** the physical factory, selectable request creation, additional
apparatus/read/clock records and resource renewal.
**Refuted at the stated scope:** a visible request with the otherwise
unprepared canonical hidden source does not guarantee the intended load.

### T2: a composition obligation with conditional history and Bell controls

For the accepted ring, separated setting and read regions outside one another's
dependency cones yield the Bell ceiling `|CHSH| <= 2`, for every common
setting-independent source law. This includes hidden memory and complete local
histories within the separated window. Better preparation or readout cannot
remove this ceiling while those hypotheses remain.

The source map separates Bell's response-complete local factorization from
the weaker marginal-independence causal condition used in the Barandes route.
It identifies the common preparation and tensor-factorized local operations
used by the BHK bound. The original stochastic representation does not by
itself identify actual interventions or higher-order histories.

The paper proves a sufficient comparison contract: a fixed positive
joint-state map that intertwines actual record-resolved transitions with local
normalized quantum instruments preserves branch laws, gives complete-history
no-signalling, and yields the quantum CHSH ceiling on the covered domain.
That is conditional mathematics. Its physical joint preparation, operations
and compatible continuation remain unsourced.

**Derived:** the scoped classical ceiling and the consequences of the explicit
quantum comparison contract. **Assumed in that contract:** the positive common
state, local instruments and closure on the physical boundaries.
**Unsourced:** a prepared pair and an intervention-preserving physical bridge.
**Refuted implication:** no-signalling alone selects quantum correlations.
No native Bell violation or general OI impossibility is inferred.

### T3: a common conditional measure, not a physical measure selection

Existing results distinguish an invariant law on a cycle from selection of
weights across cycles. Counting is the existing maximum-entropy baseline;
invariance alone does not choose the global weights when disjoint invariant
sets exist. A supplied numerical law can have Born form without its physical
distribution or the quadratic measurement rule having been derived.

Applied to the same ring, counting conditioned on readiness supplies the common
fair-bit law used by T1. It also supplies the observer's actual posterior once
the controller learns the bit. Keeping a fair marginal does not justify
resampling a used resource.

The finite-fibre argument sharpens a compatibility limit: finitely many uniform
finite resources, deterministic finite readout and positive conditioning give
rational probabilities. They cannot supply an atomless uniform seed or the
abstract Bell candidate's exact irrational calibration. This does not exclude
independently justified nonuniform laws, infinite resources or approximation;
each changes a premise and needs its own source account.

**Derived:** conditional source laws, existing Born representation and the
finite-counting restriction. **Assumed:** the selected baseline and structured
readiness, plus the distinct source/response inputs of the earlier studies.
**Unsourced:** an actual native preparation law across the required operations.
**Preserved:** writer-born's #1082 scope, paused analysis, review repairs and
owner decisions; none is decided or independently accepted by this assessment.

## 2. Can the results inhabit one physical realization?

| Component of one realization | Compatibility finding |
| --- | --- |

| Source and loading | T1's precursor pushes to the exact law assessed by T3. Their mathematical inputs agree. A physical producer and request operation have not been supplied, so physical availability remains open. |

Thus T1 and T3 are compatible as **conditional apparatus mathematics**.
All three papers are consistent with one another as a source assessment.
They do not furnish the ingredients of one demonstrated quantum realization.
There is neither a justified splice of the earlier models nor a theorem that
every possible extension is incompatible.

## 3. Single best next step for full equivalence

Prioritize **one source-backed, two-use prepared-pair interface obligation in
#868**, feeding #1060 and #1169. This is the existing joint-preparation/test
obligation, not a new issue or an adopted mechanism.

The first gate is physical candidate selection. Use the existing
state-dependent-geometry / preparation-indexed-coupling direction only as
motivation. Name one independently justified carrier, interaction and
preparation/resource law before deriving its Bell statistics. No concrete
long-edge pair has yet been selected by these papers. If the sources cannot
supply such a proposal, stop at that specific missing ingredient.

For a justified proposal, the bounded proof should:

1. Derive one complete prepared-pair and selectable-control law, retaining the
   preparer/controller knowledge, setting independence, heralds, failures,
   timing, spent resources and remaining state. T1's loading identity and
   T3's weighted-fibre test can be used only if their actual assumptions match
   this same model.
2. At two consecutive use boundaries, prove or refute one fixed
   record-and-state correspondence with local instruments. Compare complete
   Bob histories across Alice policies and conversely, including every
   permitted local read. Keep the classical shared-memory control.
3. Identify which screened-local premise a Bell-capable proposal leaves,
   without hiding accessible records. A sourced quantum representation must
   constrain every admitted preparation/operation in the bounded domain;
   a Bell milestone additionally requires an available value above 2 and at
   most `2*sqrt(2)`. Both are weaker than full equivalence.

An unsourced preparation, measure, restriction on access, reset or interaction
is a stopping result. Unequal local-history laws fail the isolated experiment.
A local candidate that remains screened can still be useful apparatus work,
but cannot be promoted to the Bell mechanism.

This priority is driven by the equivalence goal: another local readiness
improvement alone leaves a proved Bell ceiling intact. The precise factory
obligation remains with #866/#1147, the drive question with #865, and its
probability audit with #1059. They are required checks when a proposed pair
uses them; they are not closed or duplicated. #1060's separate geometry
obligations also remain.

Passing two uses would be a bounded milestone. The same source/interface
must still extend to all admitted depths, ancillas and compositions, support
the target quantum repertoire and exclude extra observable behavior before
either whole-model direction is claimed. This recommendation records the
next proof obligation; it does not start another investigation or adopt a
physical premise.
