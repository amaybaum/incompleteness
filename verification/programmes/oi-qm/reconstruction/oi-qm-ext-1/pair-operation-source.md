## 4. What a classical register description does determine

This section makes a baseline calculation explicit. It is **not** a deduction that every observer-level operational interface must use that baseline.

Let \((X,\Sigma,\mu)\) be a probability space and \(g:X\to X\) a measurable bijection with measurable inverse representing one complete joint process. The state includes apparatus, controller, records and residual resources. Set \(\nu=g_*\mu\); no invariant or equilibrium law is assumed. The total hidden carrier may be infinite.

Let \(R_i\) and \(S_j\) be finite measurable input and output register partitions, with \(w_i=\mu(R_i)>0\) and \(v_j=\nu(S_j)>0\). The process gives the conditional record kernel

\[
 T_{ji}=\frac{\mu(R_i\cap g^{-1}S_j)}{w_i}.
 \tag{1}
\]

Its entries are nonnegative and each column sums to one. The observation functions may include the entire finite accessible history, apparatus logs and failures; an output bin is not removed because it is inconvenient.

For the following Hilbert-space representation, embed register coordinates as normalized indicator functions

\[
 e_i={\bf1}_{R_i}/\sqrt{w_i}\in L^2(\mu),\qquad
 f_j={\bf1}_{S_j}/\sqrt{v_j}\in L^2(\nu).
\]

The map \(U_g h=h\circ g^{-1}\) is unitary from \(L^2(\mu)\) to \(L^2(\nu)\), by the definition of the pushforward and invertibility of \(g\). With the corresponding isometric embeddings \(E_{\rm in},E_{\rm out}\), the compression is

\[
 K=E_{\rm out}^*U_gE_{\rm in},\qquad
 K_{ji}=\frac{\mu(R_i\cap g^{-1}S_j)}{\sqrt{v_jw_i}}\geq0.
 \tag{2}
\]

It is a contraction, as a compression of a unitary between these two Hilbert spaces. Directly summing the intersection measures gives

\[
 K\sqrt w=\sqrt v,\qquad K^*\sqrt v=\sqrt w.
 \tag{3}
\]

These equations express transport of the constant function. They do **not** generally say that \(K\) fixes the literal all-ones vector. In a stationary equal-partition case with \(v=w\), it fixes \(\sqrt w\); that vector is proportional to all-ones only for equal cell masses. No uniform law is inferred or selected.

Zero-probability cells can be treated on the support for this calculation; their zero weights remain in the experimental record alphabet. This mathematical treatment is not postselection or a license to change the physical interface with the setting. Nor may (1) be iterated as an autonomous Markov law without a closure proof: retained hidden memory changes later conditionals. Reuse must follow the actual joint process and the appropriate conditioned source.

Equations (1)–(3) show why a nonlocal \(g\), a conserved-quantity partition, a larger hidden carrier, or an observer included in \(X\) does not by itself make this *particular compression* acquire signed or complex entries. They establish no Bell-locality theorem: a nonlocal \(g\) may violate Bell locality, and arbitrary stochastic tables include non-quantum behavior.

**The essential separation.** \(T\) is a record law; \(K\) is a representation on functions. Neither formula alone makes \(K\), its polar factor, or its logarithm a physical gate or a Kraus operator. A controlled joint process can be physically available while a mathematically constructed operator associated with it is not an intervention the observer can execute. The operation-source premise below cannot be omitted.

## 6. Controls that prevent an overstrong conclusion

**Classical records do not fix the coherent extension.** Identity conjugation and conjugation by a diagonal quarter-phase unitary act identically on every diagonal input state and have the same probabilities under the same basis readout. They differ on coherent inputs. Thus even a perfectly specified classical record kernel does not select between those extensions. Their availability and the coherent probes that distinguish them are separate questions. This elementary comparison prevents reading §§4–5 as a general hidden-variable impossibility theorem.

**Passive coordinate changes are not new operations.** Rewriting \(K\) in a complex basis also changes the representation of prepared states, measured projectors and the constant vector. Holding those physical identifications fixed while counting only the new matrix entries as a resource would compare different interfaces. PC-F1 gives a concrete caution: a diagonal gauge carries the fixed rotation into the ones-fixing class. [PC1Controls](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/5ea5a40218e4e660b39a9b149050ced4f95d9b48/verification/lean-mathlib/OIBridge/PC1Controls.lean), blob 009889311da8d41160d5b65538536d3e89162f6e, verifies that control. Robust ones escape under every such gauge is not necessary for the single ungauged rotation witness. The stronger phase/resource targets retain their own conditions.

**Escape is not sufficiency.** The real-amplitude control contains the rotation while lacking the quarter phase. A single operation outside both fixed invariants does not establish quantum control, a quantum pair cone, Tsirelson's bound or full equivalence.

**Observer inclusion is not a quantum disturbance theorem.** A reversible classical coupling \((b,m)\mapsto(b,m\mathbin{\rm XOR}b)\), with a ready memory, records the bit without changing it. This is a logical countercontrol to “finite record writing necessarily disturbs the measured value,” not a native boundary construction. #1007 additionally permits an infinite total substratum with finite observer resolution.

**Bell and reuse remain separate.** The current route uses a common uncontrolled preparation across settings. Including controllers does not itself make their settings statistically dependent on the response-complete hidden source. Measurement dependence or two-boundary selection would require an explicit alternative source specification; neither is adopted here. No-signalling must hold for complete accessible histories, and a pass alone does not exclude stronger-than-quantum laws. The screened-local Bell control and B3's same-model instrument requirement are preserved.

## 7. Exact successor obligation and stopping decision

The **first overall physical gap** remains the concrete pair update/preparer. The **additional interface gap** is a physically justified map from that same realization's native processes to its observer operations. Neither clause can substitute for the other.

A successor proposal must supply one common packet:

- A world carrier and update, physical pair-preparation procedure and independently motivated source family, with preparer residuals and setting controllers.
- A physically executed read/write coupling and complete record map, including failures and all apparatus information available before cross-wing communication.
- A fixed rule assigning observer states and operations to those processes, with the exact difference from the configuration/positive-compression baseline stated. If a premise is added, state it as an extension proposal and identify a consequence beyond the fitted resource.
- One native executing procedure and its **whole instrument**, with a proof of the claimed operation on every admissible input in the stated domain, output-state compatibility and resource accounting. No target matrix or freely adjusted source law may define that procedure.
- A check that the claimed invariant escape uses the physically fixed state/readout identification, followed by the existing quantum-instrument/composition bridge and complete-history tests.

A representation map need only be justified on the physically admitted preparation/continuation family; this paper does not demand access to every ontic point mass or every probability law. But restricting that family is itself a physical source obligation. A matrix representation or an equality fitted separately for each setting is insufficient.

**Stopping decision:** the checked boundary proposal supplies neither the required update/preparer nor this operation-source packet. No physically justified candidate operation can be derived from it at this scope. The unchanged finite configuration completion and the expressly assumed positive implementation completion are excluded by their existing controls; every other observer-level interface is not thereby excluded.

The single best next proof step is to source **one physically executing observer-interface map for the boundary proposal**, including its carrier/readout identification, rather than merely add a long edge.
