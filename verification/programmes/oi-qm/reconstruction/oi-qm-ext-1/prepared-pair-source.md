## 2. Result: the source gate stops before a pair experiment

The checked corpus motivates preparation-dependent coupling, but does not specify a prepared long-edge pair update and the apparatus that prepares it. Substratum explicitly leaves the Bell-compatible existence statement as H-Bell. The first unmet input is therefore the **physical pair dynamics/preparation**, before a Bell probability table or two-use experiment can be derived.

This is a bounded source finding about the sources in §3, not an exhaustive nonexistence claim about OI. No new pair candidate is selected. There is no physical pair here on which to report a no-signalling pass or failure.

The added mathematical result is a source diagnostic. For a specified deterministic realization, complete-history no-signalling is a set of linear balance equations on its initial preparation law (§4). If those equations were required for a preparation family whose affine span is the entire probability simplex, both wings' records would be pointwise local; always-binary first results would obey CHSH ≤ 2 (§5). Such a rich physically preparable family is **not** supplied or assumed. A deterministic Bell route retaining setting independence must instead source its physically available preparations and observer access so that nonlocal hidden responses have balanced observable histories.

This does not require direct hidden-state decoding, forbid indirect interference/recombination, select a Born law, or prove the quantum upper bound. It makes the missing source/access restriction explicit without choosing it to fit an answer.

### 3.3 Why the native geometry schema has not supplied a pair

Write the finite second-order state as \((u,v)\in(\mathbb Z_q)^N\times(\mathbb Z_q)^N\). For **any specified** feedback map \(f\), the form

\[
 F_f(u,v)=(f(u)-v,u),\qquad F_f^{-1}(w,u)=(u,f(u)-w)
\]

is reversible. The inverse follows by substitution. Consequently this reversible form alone selects neither the graph-dependent feedback, the pair-preparation procedure, nor a preparation measure. Uniform counting on this finite phase space is invariant for every such permutation; that mathematical fact also fails to select a physical prepared ensemble.

SM supplies one explicit ring feedback. At \(q=2\) it is

\[
 f_i(u)=u_{i-1}\mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}}(u_{i+1}\mathbin{\mathrm{OR}}u_{i+2}).
\]

Its actual one-step dependencies lie within radius two. The T1 loading construction uses that same rule and cannot turn its separated, screened measurement interfaces into Bell-violating ones merely by improving loading or readout. Main's bound is conditional on the whole measurement interval satisfying the cone and setting-independence hypotheses; bringing interfaces into causal contact changes the comparison.

A prepared long edge is a motivated *direction* in Main/SM/Substratum. Book §18.4 additionally names non-localized cosmological-boundary modes as a possible realization and expressly withholds established-mechanism status. This is an existing physical motivation to investigate, not an omitted already constructed pair. A graph picture still leaves unspecified how the one fixed world update creates, retains and uses the pair coupling, which preparer produces it, and which source law and observer access it supplies. No such concrete rule is selected by these sources. A fundamental extension could be investigated if independently motivated; this paper neither supplies nor adopts one.

Any future dependency audit must include inputs used to **select** a neighbour as well as values read from the selected neighbour. Main's functional-dependency graph is defined from the whole update across possible states. A snapshot of selected edges need not equal that graph. The explicit ring already accounts for its selector within the radius-two envelope; no contrary claim about that component is made.

## 4. Complete-history balance for one specified realization

These are conditional mathematical definitions. The physical data they would describe are unsourced by §3.

Fix a finite response-complete initial state set \(\Lambda\) and an initial law \(\mu\), before the selectable isolated measurement policies. Include apparatus, preparer residuals, memories and any consumed resources in the realization. A local policy may choose its second operation from its own first record. Let \(\alpha,\beta\) range over a fixed finite menu of such physically implementable Alice/Bob policies, if supplied.

There is one dynamics and source across comparisons. The policies must be implemented by its controllers, not arbitrary external oracles. A common \(\mu\) means the uncontrolled initial source is independent of the policy interventions; it does not claim that the closed world's state including the controller's setting bits is independent of those same bits.

For each policy pair, deterministic evolution defines complete two-use history maps

\[
 H_A^{\alpha,\beta}:\Lambda\to{\cal H}_A,\qquad
 H_B^{\alpha,\beta}:\Lambda\to{\cal H}_B .
\]

The alphabets include all records available to that observer before permitted cross-wing communication: outcomes, locally acquired preparer/controller information, failures, heralds, timing and permitted read access to the remaining state. A common alphabet can include unused symbols. The second use follows the actual first outgoing state; no reset or resampling is implicit. A fixed two-use assessment is not a theorem for every future operation.

For fixed \(\beta\), distant policies \(\alpha,\alpha'\), and Bob history \(h\), define

\[
 D^B_{\alpha,\alpha',\beta,h}(\lambda)
 ={\bf1}\{H_B^{\alpha,\beta}(\lambda)=h\}
  -{\bf1}\{H_B^{\alpha',\beta}(\lambda)=h\}.
\]

**Balance lemma.** Bob's complete history law is independent of Alice's policy exactly when

\[
 \sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\mu(\lambda)
 D^B_{\alpha,\alpha',\beta,h}(\lambda)=0
 \tag{B}
\]

for every displayed comparison. Alice has the analogous equations with the wings exchanged.

*Proof.* Each sum is precisely the difference between the probabilities of the same complete history \(h\) under the two policies. Equality at every atom is equality of the finite laws. This uses neither memorylessness nor pointwise independence of hidden responses.

Let \(C\) collect both wings' rows \(D\). Then the two-use no-signalling laws of this *fixed* realization and policy menu are exactly

\[
 {\cal N}=\{\mu\in\Delta(\Lambda):C\mu=0\}.
\]

Changing the permitted record access changes \(C\); changing an apparatus or adding a reuse operation requires checking the enlarged realization. This description does not choose a physical law by solving for a desired answer.

**Prefixes and reuse.** Full-history equality implies equality of every local prefix law by summing over extensions. For any Bob prefix with positive probability, the conditional law of the rest of Bob's record is also the same, because both numerator and denominator are equal. The converse from separate one-read marginals is invalid: it discards correlations between records. No assumption is made that the hidden-state posterior after Alice's operation is independent of that operation. The constraint is on Bob's accessible history.

A physically sourced preparation family must fall within \({\cal N}\) for the admitted isolated protocols. If a preparer log later becomes accessible, it must be included in \(H\); unconditional cancellation after discarding that log is insufficient. Direct decoding of a hidden bit is unnecessary: any permitted indirect recombination that changes an accessible history is already included.

## 5. Why unrestricted reweighting would restore the classical ceiling

**Preparation-span diagnostic.** Let \({\cal P}\subseteq\Delta(\Lambda)\) be the laws physically available for this same realization, controller menu and access boundary, with each law common across setting interventions. Suppose both wings obey (B) for every \(\mu\in{\cal P}\). If

\[
 \operatorname{aff}({\cal P})=\operatorname{aff}(\Delta(\Lambda)),
 \tag{S}
\]

then each wing's complete record is pointwise independent of the distant policy.

*Proof.* Every row \(D\) defines a linear functional that vanishes on \({\cal P}\), hence on its affine span. Under (S), it therefore vanishes on each point mass \(\delta_\lambda\), so \(D(\lambda)=0\). Equality of all history indicators makes the two history maps equal at that \(\lambda\). Point masses need not belong to \({\cal P}\) for this algebraic implication; the affine-span premise is the substantive extra condition.

If every trial yields a binary first outcome and each wing has two first choices, fix its continuation policy for each choice. Pointwise history independence gives first-response functions \(A_x(\lambda),B_y(\lambda)\in\{-1,1\}\). The CHSH integrand is

\[
 A_0(B_0+B_1)+A_1(B_0-B_1)\in\{-2,2\}.
\]

A common setting-independent law therefore gives absolute CHSH at most 2. Failures cannot silently be removed to manufacture the binary premise; any conditioning must be part of the physical preparation/experiment with its setting independence checked.

**Status of (S): not supplied.** Neither arbitrary concentrated laws nor a full-affine-span family is asserted to be physically preparable in OI. A Bell-capable deterministic realization in this setting must violate (S) if it is no-signalling for every admitted preparation. The physical question is which restricted family is available, why its complete history balances hold, and whether the same dynamics/access preserves them through allowed preparations and continuations. Restricting access or ensembles can be physically justified; defining the restriction only as “whatever makes the target table work” is not a source.

This diagnostic retains setting independence. A measurement-dependent alternative changes that premise and needs its own explicit physical justification; no pending owner choice is resolved here. It also does not demand that every imaginable observation procedure belong to the isolated Bell experiment. Communicating devices, changed records, or postselected samples must be compared with QM under those changed conditions.
