will bind the immutable author checkpoint supplied by the parent.

Compute the full Q/C/G evolution independently using the hash-bound prior
independent native helpers, a signed L16 strip and a nine-step full native

divisibility and cone coverage, previous independently checked Q/C baseline,
old-source/readiness geometry, inherited qY/q2/q7 values, complete incoming
q7 triples, and exact equality with the published author's structured table.

Measure G-to-q8 output maps at fixed old inputs. A bijection and a free initial
G outside adopted q7 readiness establish underdetermination at this endpoint;
do not infer a general impossibility. Enumerate G(u) pairs and measure both
the specified H-rotated native code and arbitrary common two-code native and
q7-input encodings. Verdicts are generated from those candidate sets only
after the controls pass. Independently verify any concise obstruction formula
against the computed table. No reset, joint-history or full-QM result follows.

Use structured JSON results and exit status. Do not infer controls from logs.
Run normal and -O once after final source changes, compare structured results,
and keep the exact hashes and source provenance separate from certification.
