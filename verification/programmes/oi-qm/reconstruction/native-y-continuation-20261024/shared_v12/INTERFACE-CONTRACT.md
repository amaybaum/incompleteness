## Histories, failures, closure and error

The old eight-port public-noise theorem covers a fixed H=L+Delta<=2L with
an explicitly independent initial bank and24Delta additional noise bits.
It does not imply independence from a later physical carrier using that
bank. New public ports or timing need their own joint geometry/source audit.
The pool sources reach an old public port no earlier than L+77/L+79;
their unchanged-prefix argument cannot be extended indefinitely by assertion.

Compare the full public record and outgoing carrier jointly, including raw
fields, timing, adaptive settings, all flags and zero-mass branches. Earlier
terminal failures remain terminal and retain their actual physical measures.
Conditional freshness against public history alone can miss incoming-carrier
correlations. Prove the needed joint law or retain the actual joint kernel.

Use one fixed source/target operational correspondence and protocol class.
For exact composition, check positivity, mass, every complete-record kernel
and preservation of the admissible boundary cone. Operational equivalence
can be weaker than hidden-state equality; selected-moment equality can be
too weak. For approximation, name the protocol menu, horizon, error metric,
outgoing-state comparison, source resources and total failure/error bound.
Finite checkers validate bounded cases; written general proofs are separate.

A1 needs full specified target coverage and physically available operations;
a known abstract qubit model alone is not a native source or full composite
reconstruction. A2 reproduction with supplied calibrations is distinct from
deriving why quantum probabilities are selected. A failure refutes its stated
construction, not OI generally. No F/E designation, special workflow launch,
merge, force-push or branch deletion is authorized by these checkpoints.
