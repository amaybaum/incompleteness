## 3. Probability and history

If M is independently uniform, each fixed complement gives an exactly uniform
terminal triple. Conditioning on the original input, seeds, established Y
operand, or remote herald is safe because those are functions of the complement
and independent of M.

The report correctly stops short of conditioning on the entire observed history.
M may be causally relevant to an earlier public port. Conversely, that causal
possibility alone is not proof of a leak, since earlier public words have their
own masks. The older public-history factorization theorem does not automatically
supply a joint theorem for a new output using old random coordinates.

The independent checker includes a native counterexample to the general faulty
inference: with an isolated uniform initial triple, an earlier axial read and a
later axial read both have uniform marginals but their joint support has only
eight pairs, rather than the sixty-four of an independent product. This is a
logic control, not a claim that those two reads are the actual old protocol.

## 4. Blanket preparation, parity, and M2

The proposed full current/previous cone captures current M1 at distance N-1,
current M2 at distance N, and the old previous input at distance N-2. A blanket
zero prescription would change declared resources and input values.

The exact local no-wrap spacetime invariant is `sum(v)+t mod 2`. The target has
even parity. Current M1 and the previous origin input belong to the inactive
sector, while current M2 is active. Thus an active-sector prescription can avoid
M1 and the old previous input but must explicitly retain M2. Current M_j for

this cancellation control. Nonlinear interactions with the other retained
