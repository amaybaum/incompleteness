## Key cells and the native permutation

A key of neighbor q9+e is an initial cell that influences q9+e at L+1 and
influences neither q9 at L nor another neighbor at L+1. Propagating
dependency sets through the rule (K2) gives exactly the current-slice
cells q9+(L+2)e and the previous-slice cells q9+(L+1)e; the propagated
cones equal the parity cone formula cell by cell.

| +x | ((L+2),S,77) | pinned, port 4's ball, radius S (-399) | port 4 at L-476 |
| -x | (-(L+2),S,77) | free complement (2) | port 3 at L+79 |
| +y | (0,S+(L+2),77) | free complement (2) | port 5 at L+79 |

| -z | (0,S,77-(L+2)) | pinned, ball about (0,S,-68), radius R (-134) | port 5 at L-75 |

The margin 2 of the three free keys is to the q9 L-ball and to
B_(L+1)(q10). The previous-slice keys, with previous ready radius r-1,
are free for -x, +y and -y (margin 2; the -y one reaches port 0 at 340),
pinned for +x and -z, and inside the previous q10 ball for +z. The outcome
rule uses the current-slice keys, as preregistered; the previous-slice
keys enter the necessary conditions of (iii).

The ready-ball list, the q10 extension, the ports, deadlines and bank
directions are compared with the lists in native_route_v13's
`geometry()`, read from the pinned file as literals without executing it.
Completeness of that inventory remains an input: a ready region missing
from it would change the classification of a key.

The -x key is the cell at which Native A places its fair source F9, chosen
there to make V uniform (native_x_v12 section 3). The +z key is N itself:
the own-N permutation already retained by native_route_v13 is this same
axial mechanism at q10.

For a free key and every fixed value of all other initial variables, the
key's own neighbor at L+1 is a permutation of the key triple by the unique
axial first arrival in arbitrary background (native_v11/REPORT.md,
"Native proof in arbitrary background"). p, q10 at L+1 and the other four
neighbors do not depend on it. XOR translation and H are bijections, so V
is a permutation of the key. K3 checks every step of this on complete
small native worlds, for current-slice and previous-slice keys.

For each of the 48 accepted table rows of the v14 fewest-seed law and each
of the eight residual sums of the other neighbors, a uniform key gives a
uniform V (384 cases, computed with the actual p and q10 codes). The
argument does not use the law. Under a complement law in which no key is
uniform, for example a fixed known complement, V is a deterministic
function whose leakage is not computed here; route_v13 does not specify
that law, whereas Native A fixes F9 as fair.

## What the read exposes, given the old record

Given p (public at L), Sigma -> V is a bijection with

    V.A = p.A XOR Sigma.B,  V.B = p.B XOR Sigma.D,
    V.D = p.D XOR Sigma.A XOR Sigma.B*Sigma.D,

checked on all 64 (p,Sigma) pairs. The read therefore exposes the whole
triple q10 XOR (other neighbors), never q10.A alone: whatever cleans the A
field must come from the other neighbors, and the same neighbors would
have to pad B and D. On the 48 accepted rows of the fewest-seed law,
q10.A XOR p.D = a XOR b, because p.D = r_old = s XOR a XOR b: inside V.D
the sign carried by q10.A cancels the sign of the old record. Under that
law q10 takes 4 distinct whole codes for each value of s across the 24
contexts, so a whole-triple exposure of q10 also exposes known-seed
information. These are diagnostics outside the decision rule.

## Where the stop lies

Delivery through V needs the free keys determined. Pinning them is a
readiness extension of the q9 cone at L+2, a change to the initial
preparation. A blank radius-(L+2) current shell about q9 would also meet
eight reserved bank coordinates, four of them active at distance exactly
L+2 (reproducing native_x_v12): port 0 at deadline+2, reaching the -y and
-z neighbors; ports 2 and 3 at deadline+2, reaching +x,+y,-z and
-x,+y,-z; port 5 at deadline+2, reaching +y and -z. Those bank variables
enter their own ports' records at tick deadline+2, before L. The free -y
keys also enter port 0's record, which might determine them; that is not
computed. Any such extension therefore needs its own joint public-record
audit, besides the V kernel. Reading the key neighbors at L+1 instead is
an access change. This test adopts neither and stops.

## Scope

Established, for this one-tick stage, this source geometry and this
declared read: V is a permutation of each free key, so no fixed readout of
V delivers s for every complement value; under a uniform free key the read
carries no information about q10.A. Not established or excluded: privacy
of V under a complement law in which no free key is uniform; whether port
0's record determines the -y keys; multi-tick stages, other reads or
ports, other layouts, readiness extensions; Native A's own geometry and
pool; the acceptance predicate, a carrier law or a reusable interface; any
access rule or preparation change; A1 or A2 reconstruction; OI generally.
