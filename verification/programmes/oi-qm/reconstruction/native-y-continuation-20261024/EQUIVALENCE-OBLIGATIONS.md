## Native integration boundary

The actual Native A phase at L+2 contains current V and six previous
neighbors, with the pool in Q8/Q10 and old X in a fixed inverse code.
The old source theorem gives joint conditional uniformity; it does not
implement the inverse as a public read or physical actuator. Masks and
workspace remain part of the native state. A decoder identity is not a gate.

The actual-scale E route is a different readiness/source variant replacing
optional uniform F10. It may not inherit Native A's six-bit pool unchanged.
The affine-source obstruction concerns a common raw binary code. A fixed
single-field readout of the q10 cell succeeds under jointly affine E/N:
q10.A carries s, and, per the separate review, more generally x XOR f(k) for
each affine f of the known seeds under a law depending on f, with B and D
jointly carrying s beside it. q10 is not a permitted read under Native A.
Distribution-level quantum instruments are untested. The small pointer
census concerns its seven declared families/window only. One native tick
into the whole-V read gives no fixed readout equal to s for every complement
value, for its construction only (`native_vread_v15/`). None of these
exclusions is an OI-wide no-go.

## Programme and wider coverage

The programme synthesis these paths start from, and the later #885 draft not
used as a baseline, are named in CURRENT-HANDOFF.md as provenance on #885's
preserved branch. Inherited reviewer-evidence retention and the
commissioned independent transport-proof review remain separate programme
obligations, which the internal reviews here do not discharge.

The backlog [#913](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/issues/913)
retains calibration/direction selection, initial preparation, local settings
and clock, composed observer-access closure, unrestricted depth/menu,
general instruments, composites/entanglement/Bell and later continuum scope.
A reusable single-qubit native device would still require those extensions
before full equivalence. No physical premise or operational map is adopted.
