## Boundaries of copied-source use

The original files are retained exactly so that a reviewer can inspect complete statements, hypotheses and the source-version differences. Exact copying is not scientific promotion of every paragraph in those sources.

- SEL-ACCESS-1 §2 proves S1 from its explicit full/restricted-menu-pair hypothesis. S1 does not require existence of a particular C0/C1 Page example or adoption of an OI class closed under arbitrary menu deletion. Those unconsumed Page details and their recovery remain outside this child.
- The SEL-EXCL-1 control is an explicitly constructed, globally coupled mathematical model with supplied fresh cells and restricted access. S2 and S5 use that declared control, not a sourced native apparatus or universal exclusion theorem.
- Synthesis MM labels in S4 identify the compared missing ingredients. The claim here is how the discriminator relates to those classifications; the wider synthesis's REC/TUNE/ARP/source conclusions and ranking are not additional physical premises of D5–D7. The original synthesis's open evidence and source gaps remain open for any later reuse of those conclusions.
- Confirmation reports and reviews record their actual reviewed objects and verdicts. Their embedded claims about all earlier hunks, working copies, raw CI or original Git ancestry are not independently re-certified by this child.
- Comments and literature mentioned in review bodies remain the reviewer's context except where S1–S7 name specific content as support. No per-reference support obligation is discharged merely by calling an entire source historical.

For a future use beyond these specified claims, the reviewer must trace the particular proof/status premise and its dependencies under §A.42(8), retaining missing support or narrowing the claim before it is treated as established. The present proposed preservation grants no blanket exception.
