under `../support/synthesis/`. Commits they name, such as `4940e8d6` (r1) and `65b70b2d` (r2), are on
#1276's branch and are not ancestors of `main`; they are provenance. The reviews are model reviews:
evidence of review, not independent mathematical verification.
