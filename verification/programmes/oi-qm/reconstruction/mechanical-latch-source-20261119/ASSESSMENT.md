## What the experiment supports

Reddy et al. (2019) fabricated a silicon sensor in which a controlled impact moves a spring-mounted proof mass. A hook catches at a discrete position and blocks the return after the impact. The impact drives the write, and the engaged latch holds the position without a dedicated power supply during detection and storage. In the tests, a separate reference accelerometer measured the input. High-speed optical microscopy followed the mass, and before-and-after images show different retained positions for tested impacts. The reported optical observation and the proposed capacitive readout are separate. [Paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sna.2019.05.036) · [Accepted manuscript, §§2.1 and 4, Figs. 12–15 and 18](https://www.academia.edu/83407078/A_zero_power_sensing_MEMS_shock_sensor_with_a_latch_reset_mechanism_for_multi_threshold_events_monitoring)

The fabricated geometry, initial unlatched position and mechanical input are supplied resources. The tests used a powered electrostatic actuator to reset the device between impacts. Reset releases the latch and returns the mass to its ready position, so the same position cannot carry the first result through reset. The paper reports the position after the impact. Its claim about long-term storage is broader than the retention interval shown by that evidence. The response also depends on the direction and frequency of the input, so a peak-acceleration number alone does not specify the experimental stimulus. [Accepted manuscript, §§2.1, 2.3 and 4](https://www.academia.edu/83407078/A_zero_power_sensing_MEMS_shock_sensor_with_a_latch_reset_mechanism_for_multi_threshold_events_monitoring)

## Reopening evidence

The first requirement is an independently motivated map for calibrated support acceleration and mass displacement on the same fixed histories. It would specify the local regions, clock and preparation law, with input timing and calibration independent of the latch result. A subsequent write test could distinguish a held latch position from transient mass displacement with no engagement, using a post-impact read and the independently recorded input. That comparison is an evidentiary criterion. It has not been performed here. The stop applies to this candidate and the sources checked.
