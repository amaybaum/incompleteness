## One operational target

The target is one available balanced operation on a fixed pair of physically distinguishable alternatives. Starting from either alternative, one application produces equal probabilities for the two outputs. Two consecutive applications with no intermediate read, reset or postselection transfer the input to the other output with probability one. The preparations and readout have fixed meanings, and the physical setup must supply a way to use or bypass the mixer under the same microscopic law. The equal weights and transfer are the comparison data. A successful source must account for the preparations, carrier, controls and record law on one realization. Let $\epsilon(n)=(-1)^{n_1+n_2+n_3}$. Work in the period-eight standing-wave sector of this equation. Use the inner product over a spatial period to normalize $f(n)\propto\cos[\pi(n_1+n_2+n_3)/4]$, and set $g(n)=\epsilon(n)f(n)$. This selects solutions of the existing equation. On the infinite lattice these are periodic modes, with a norm per period. This calculation supplies no physical finite-volume preparation of them. Each direction contributes $\cos(\pi/4)=s=1/\sqrt2$ to the normalized symbol, giving $Lf=sf$. Each edge joins opposite parities, so $Lg=-sg$. The two modes are orthogonal by self-adjointness over the period and have the same norm because multiplication by $\epsilon$ preserves it. [Dispersion relation](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/192bbf29402d23fd49acc1a6d1a65c8cd0ff41b7/papers/SM.md#L278-L282) · [Spatial checkerboard](https://github.com/amaybaum/incompleteness/blob/192bbf29402d23fd49acc1a6d1a65c8cd0ff41b7/papers/SM.md#L531-L539)

The fixed real basis $e=(f+g)/\sqrt2$, $o=(f-g)/\sqrt2$ has support on even and odd sites, respectively. On its span, $L=sX$, where $X$ exchanges $e$ and $o$. The real two-slice recurrence preserves that span when both initial slices belong to it. Writing $v_t=u_{t-1}$ and $D=(I-L^2)^{1/2}=sI$, use the exact classical quadrature coordinate

$$a_t=u_t+iD^{-1}(Lu_t-v_t).$$

The update of $u$ is $u'=2Lu-v$, and $v'=u$. Substitution, using $D^2=I-L^2$, gives $a'=(L-iD)a$. Thus one ordinary tick and its repetition give

$$M=s(X-iI),\qquad M^*M=I,\qquad M^2=-iX.$$

For $a_0=e$, $a_1=s(o-ie)$ and $a_2=-io$. The same calculation with $e,o$ exchanged applies to $a_0=o$. The squared quadrature weights are $(1/2,1/2)$ after one tick and they swap after two. This action uses the relative phase of the two standing modes in a fixed spatial basis. It uses integer ticks of the wave lift and requires no variable link coefficient or fractional power of the microscopic permutation. The norm and its division between the two modes are properties of a classical wave equation.
