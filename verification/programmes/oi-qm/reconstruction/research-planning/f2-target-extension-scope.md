## Decisions before the next source investigation

- [ ] Choose the conclusion: exact finite QM or a precisely defined dense endpoint;
      qubit-power registers or all finite local dimensions.
- [ ] Specify the extension's preservation obligations, including which existing rooted,
      monitored and back-acting laws it must preserve. They cannot be treated as interchangeable.
- [ ] Define what counts as an extension that supplies the resource, and what would merely
      assume the desired gate, flow, preservation clause or conclusion.
- [ ] Keep physical state-operation compatibility, carrier identification and test availability
      as separate obligations with separate evidence.
- [ ] Decide whether the unfinished bridge necessity investigation remains in scope.
      If retained, re-pose it against the current pair theorem and distinguish availability from
      invariance; identify its exact uncompleted node before proposing a computation.
- [ ] Review the research index's F1 terminology against the maintained main programme
      before using that index to prioritize or describe F2.

The corrected candidate discussion is an input to this decision, not a candidate selection.
Failure of a particular registration or polarization construction does not exclude all extensions.
