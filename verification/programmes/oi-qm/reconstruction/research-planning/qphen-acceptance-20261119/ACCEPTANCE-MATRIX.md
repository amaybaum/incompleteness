## Common evidence and decision rules

**One realization.** Before a future verdict, fix the microscopic update, carrier, law for complete initial and boundary states, observer access and operational interpretation. Use the same realization across rows. Each preparation, choice, delay, control and reset needs a physical input reachable under the update and an accounted supply. Any row-specific change to the physical setup must be a specified physically available intervention within that realization. Trace each result from preparation and trigger through write, retention and an executing public read. Charge each fresh meter, request, clock, register or boundary resource and account for the full residual state, including failed attempts. Give the observer only the resulting public records. Model-internal tracing belongs in the validation evidence.

**A/B and ablations.** A uses the declared deterministic substratum as baseline. B adds the observational-incompleteness principles specified for the same realization. For every premise or identification, record its exact source, use, typing (native, candidate strengthening, calibration or quantum input) and the arm that removes it. Report whether the ablation changes the predicted records, changes which mechanisms can be justified, or changes access to an unchanged history. Use dynamics-changing ablations as comparators and report any loss of the target separately. Include the r7c dependency types and D18 invariance checks where applicable. An identification, probability law or control with a quantum-specific resource belongs in the benchmark arm. SA-1(c), null-result invasiveness and Tsirelson soundness retain their stated standing. A classical table and an ideal quantum instrument are useful positive controls for the scoring procedure. Physical credit requires a sourced executing mechanism.

**Records and denominators.** Publish one event schema with schedule, setting and preparation provenance, eligibility/herald and its time, all ordered read windows, public result or `⊥` (no valid record by the deadline), diagnosable failures, double clicks, later reads and any exclusions. Use every booked attempt in the relevant arm's ledger. Preserve any diagnostic distinction between a valid silent outcome, a withheld request, a missed event and an unreadable record. State the denominator of every probability. A heralded denominator is permitted where the criterion and lab source specify a recorded, pre-setting, setting-independent eligibility event. Report the total eligibility rate and audit the causal assumption in that arm. Retain the joint counts and null bins even when displaying a conditional panel. A protocol-specific post-interaction reconstruction needs its own selection rule, exposure weights and validation. Report it alongside the raw attempt law with its assumptions and denominator stated. Set clocks, selection, time windows and any local coarse graining before seeing the test outcomes. Model inability to produce a scheduled attempt belongs in the record.

**Agreement and thresholds.** Specify the lab preparation, apparatus and readout correspondence before comparison, including times, geometry, losses and background. A mapped raw-direct miss counts for that realization and arm. A fit or corrected metric is a secondary comparison with its transformation and uncertainty disclosed. Record every parameter calibrated from target data. A correctness claim uses outcomes independent of that calibration (§A.23). For each matched required arm use the joint law on the agreed bins. Predeclare an equivalence tolerance from independently calibrated systematic/transfer uncertainty and lab resolution. Construct simultaneous 95% confidence bounds within the declared family of comparisons under a sampling model justified by the records. With memory, use a justified memory-robust method or report the comparison inconclusive. For agreement require the upper bound on total variation distance to be within tolerance. A lower bound above tolerance is a mismatch. An interval bridging tolerance is inconclusive. Write `L` and `U` for the lower and upper bounds. Use exact bounds when comparing exact, fully specified model laws. Assumption-dependent witnesses additionally require their stated absolute threshold below. For a witness defined by an effect strictly above a bound, a valid upper confidence bound at or below it fails that witness. An interval that spans the bound leaves it inconclusive. Where only published summaries or figures are available, identify the calibrated bins, include figure extraction error if used and declare which comparisons the source supports. Missing raw denominators, transport calibration or tolerances leave the affected comparison blocked. Exact settings, margins, stopping rule and adequate power are prerequisites to execution.

| Verdict | Rule for each row and for the named claim level |
| --- | --- |
| PASS | Valid reference and controls plus agreement on each required matched arm. Any claimed exclusion or native-operation credit also needs its witnessed threshold, scope assumptions and physical source evidence. |
| FAIL | A decisive miss under valid controls for a specified realization and claim, including a proved contradiction in its required ready/write/retain/read chain. Preserve the candidate scope. |
| INCONCLUSIVE | The comparison was attempted but the controls, evidence, sensitivity, mapping or bounds leave the claim undecided. The original rounds' VOID and other tokens retain their own meanings. |
| NOT-RUN / BLOCKED | The comparison or claim-level test is unexecuted. Identify the first missing prerequisite or authorization. A narrower published conditional result can still inform admission. |

Keep four distinct levels of credit. The first is a quantum-compatible record pattern. The second excludes a **named** classical model class with its assumptions checked. The third requires physical generation and composition of the relevant native operations. The fourth requires one common sourced preparation, operation, measurement and repeated-use interface that reproduces all scoped operational data. A finite list of matches alone cannot settle general OI–QM equivalence. Q-C2-1's own level and attribution rules are evaluated as written. Its reported joint Level 3 from P's apparatus and C's gadget would still involve two distinct instances.

### Whole-attempt repeat-read gate

Any allowed eligibility event must be recorded before the first request, independent of the active or withheld assignment and qualified by its own source. Apparatus readiness and all later measurement-dependent flags are excluded from eligibility. Publish the full booked count and the eligibility rate by arm. For each delay, use **all assigned eligible windows** as the denominator for each rate below. Readiness, actual issuance, success of the first read and validity of a later record cannot select that denominator. Include a scheduled second window following a failed first read, even if the apparatus aborts. Record the scheduled arm and actual event separately.

| --- | --- |
| Active readiness and request, each scheduled read | Count inability to establish the declared ready state by its deadline, and failure to issue the physical request in its scheduled window, as separate rates. Unknown readiness from missing diagnostics cannot contribute evidence of readiness. Require a validated diagnosis or proof before scoring the readiness gate. |
| Active missed or invalid read, each scheduled read | Count every active window with no valid current result by the deadline, including missing readiness, aborts, unreadable records and invalid codes. Verify physical request withholding. The absence of a valid result in an otherwise empty withheld window is expected. |
