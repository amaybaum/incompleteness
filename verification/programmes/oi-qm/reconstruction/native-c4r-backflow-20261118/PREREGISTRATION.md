## What would count as a witness

Fix a finite visible alphabet $V$, a measurable update $F$, one readout $\pi$, and a probability $\rho$ on the hidden coordinates common to every root. For $a,j\in V$ use the one-time laws

$$
\Gamma_k(a,j)=\int {\bf1}_{\{\pi F^k(a,h)=j\}}\,d\rho(h),\qquad
 d_k(a,b)=\tfrac12\sum_{j\in V}|\Gamma_k(a,j)-\Gamma_k(b,j)|.
$$

The events over $j$ partition the fibre, so each row is stochastic, and $\Gamma_0=I$ for the coordinate readout. Alternatively, one physical joint preparation may give fibre laws $\mu_a=\mu(\cdot\mid\pi=a)$ on positive-mass roots. This defines an observational conditional. To interpret it as selectable preparation requires a source or herald, its record and its common joint background. Define all rows used by a square rooted family, including a justified convention for any null roots, or fix a supported alphabet closed under the specified read. Independently choosing a different bath for each root supplies no such experiment. Stationarity is an additional requirement only for a claim that uses it.

Preregister distinct roots and $s<t\le K$ on that one family. A strict $d_s(a,b)<d_t(a,b)$ forbids a stochastic right divisor from $s$ to $t$ by TV contraction. No full return enters this implication. A gap between histories having a common present can give positive conditional mutual information while every rooted map remains P-divisible on the window.
