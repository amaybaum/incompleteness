# NATIVE-C4R-1 · Decision on a laboratory-accessible rooted revival

**STOP UNSOURCED. No physical candidate was admitted.** The preparations proposed for the prime linear rule lack a source for their joint input law. ## Where each source chain ends

**Nonuniform preparation.** The single-site binary linear comparison in the preregistration holds $F$ and $\pi$ fixed. NR-1 obtains a uniform law on a coset by pushing *independent uniform hidden coordinates* through a homomorphism. Replacing those weights removes that proof. It does not specify replacement weights. A common nonuniform law on hidden fields, including correlations among those fields, is a legitimate POSITED realization datum. If the hidden law also depends on the visible root, it is instead necessary to identify one joint preparation, both positive-mass root events, their conditional hidden laws and the physical relation between the root label and the recorded preparation. A hidden record already correlated with the label at the outset cannot on its own certify that a subsequent write created it. [Main, lines 54–60, 135](../../../../../papers/Main.md#L54-L60) · [A1-10, lines 25–31, 83–91](../../../../audits/foundations/a1-10-recurrence-premises/PREMISE-LADDER.md#L83-L91) · [NR-1, lines 148–162](../../../../audits/foundations/nr1-near-return-20261116/NR-1.md#L148-L162)

This observation has no such force for a correlated joint state or a genuinely held past record. It transports supplied inputs but supplies no producer for them, no selectable request writer and no accounted herald/retry channel.
