## 1. The question

Owner framing, 2026-10-15:

- **A.** Does subsystem locality constrain:
  - the per-time Gram freedom;
  - the cross-time threading freedom;
  - both;
  - or neither?

  Neither freedom is assumed to be gauge.
- **B.** Locality is defined at the time resolution the programme licenses, keeping:
  - discrete-layer locality, at finite depth with a strict cone;
  - continuous-time locality.

  The tested notion must not exclude ordinary quantum dynamics.
- **C.** No-signalling is stated with:
  - its observables;
  - its interventions;
  - its preparations.

  Each is fixed before it is proved.
- **The success criterion.** Locality must be shown to distinguish two histories that are
  observationally compatible, or shown not to. Showing that an evolution is local, or that
  locality implies no-signalling, does not show that locality selects.

## 2. Definitions

**Time.** The index is `ℕ`, with successor and order only (a17p:508). Every lift in this round is
*two-valued*: `U_t = A` for `t = 0` and `U_t = B` for `t ≥ 1`, and *constant* when `A = B`.

**Declared subsystem decomposition.** A configuration `C` declares:

- `V = V₁ × V₂` and `A = A₁ × A₂`;
- the anchor `a₀ = (a₀₁, a₀₂)`;
- the visible family `Γ`, which is not required to factorize.

The composite carrier `V × A` is identified with `(V₁ × A₁) × (V₂ × A₂)` by the reshuffle
`((i₁,i₂),(a₁,a₂)) ↦ ((i₁,a₁),(i₂,a₂))`. For operators `Uᵏ` on `Vₖ × Aₖ`, the operator `U¹ ⊠ U²`
on `V × A` has entries `U¹((i₁,a₁),(j₁,b₁)) · U²((i₂,a₂),(j₂,b₂))`. This is act 21's product
embedding at the level of operators.

The decomposition is input, not output. At `C21`, `Γ ≡ 1/16` is a product under every
identification `Fin 16 ≅ Fin 4 × Fin 4`, so `Γ` does not determine it.

**Locality notions.**

- **`L-disc` (discrete layers, strict cone).** Take a configuration with a coupling graph on sites,
  where every ancilla is attached to a site. A lift is `d`-local if every tick increment
  `U_{t+1}U_tᴴ` is a depth-`d` circuit of gates on edges. Equivalently for the cone: conjugating any
  site operator by the increment leaves it supported in the radius-`d` ball.
  - Integer-time samples of a continuous evolution generated by a local Hamiltonian whose terms do
    not commute violate `L-disc` generically, because only Lieb–Robinson bounds hold for them.
  - `L-disc` is therefore not imposed on coupled subsystems in this round.
- **`L-cont` (continuous time).** The evolution is `e^{-iHt}` sampled at integer `t`, with `H` a
  sum of local terms. Locality is a Lieb–Robinson bound with exponential tails, not a strict cone.
- **`SL-U` (subsystem locality across an uncoupled cut), the notion tested.** On a declared
  decomposition whose two parts are declared uncoupled, a coherent lift is admitted iff
  `U_t = U¹_t ⊠ U²_t` for every `t ∈ ℕ`. Any phase is absorbed into a factor.
  - On a cut that no edge crosses, `L-disc` gives a product increment at every tick, and `L-cont`
    with `H = H₁ ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ H₂` gives `e^{-iHt} = e^{-iH₁t} ⊗ e^{-iH₂t}`.
  - With a product `U₀`, both therefore yield `SL-U` at every integer time.
  - `SL-U` excludes no evolution of two quantum systems that are uncoupled and have separate,
    uncorrelated environments.
  - It does exclude a hidden sector that couples or correlates the two parts. That is the content
    tested in §6.
  - *Provenance:* Source A (46) posits this factorization "if there is to be any possibility of the
    two subsystems evolving independently". Act 7 declined to import it as a source claim
    (a07rr:96-111). Here it enters as an added hypothesis, said at each use.

**The two freedoms.**

- **`TRAJ`, the per-time Gram freedom.** Two coherent lifts differ in it iff they are
  `GramTrajEquiv`-inequivalent: their fibre-Gram tuples are `GramPhaseEquiv`-inequivalent at some
  `t`, with phases chosen per time (a17p:441-449).
- **`THREAD`, the cross-time threading freedom.** It is the freedom among coherent lifts in one
  `GramTrajEquiv` class: what fixes the cross-time representative within a trajectory
  (RM:235-237). Its components are tagged on each witness:
  - `L`, a left `𝒢_L` move;
  - `S`, a time-dependent strong right move;
  - `D`, time-dependent anchored phases.

  The pair `L`+`S` is act 14's `≈_T`.

**Carriers.** These are act 14's carriers, read as frozen (a14p:154-231):

- `𝒪₁(U)(t) = 𝔇_{a₀}(U_t)`;
- `𝒪₂(U)(t,s) = readback a₀ ((U_t U_sᴴ).map ‖·‖²)`;
- `𝒪₃(U)(t,s) = 𝔇_{a₀}(U_t U_sᴴ)`.

`𝒪₀ = Γ` is the compatibility carrier: every compared pair shares it.

- **The headline carrier is `𝒪₁`.** Its justification does not depend on locality:
  - In quantum mechanics, the operational content of an open system's dynamics is the map from
    preparations of the system, with its environment in a fixed reference state, to the system's
    reduced states. Every measurement statistic of the system at one time is a function of that
    map's output.
  - `𝒪₁` is that map, with the hidden sector at the anchor.
  - Its presupposition, that the reduced state's coherences are physical (a14p:198-207), is the
    standard one.
  - It needs no re-anchoring at a second time, so act 7's negative `D4b` does not bear on it.
- **`𝒪₂` and `𝒪₃`** are reported beside the headline. They presuppose re-anchoring at `s`
  (a14p:209-221).
- **No carrier is adopted as the physical one.** Every verdict is relative to a named carrier.

**The intervention response, used only in §6.** Fix `s < t`, a visible unitary `R` on `V` acting as
`R ⊗ 1_A`, and an operator `ρ` on `V`.

- The response is `𝔇_{a₀}(U_t U_sᴴ (R ⊗ 1_A) U_s)(ρ)`.
- It presupposes that visible interventions at tick `s` are physical, and that the history from
  `s` to `t` is `U_t U_sᴴ`. That is Source A's composition form (a11p:81); the hidden sector is not
  re-anchored.
- It is not one of act 14's carriers, and it is not adopted either.

## 3. Written statements [W] (proofs written in the result; each is checked exactly where listed)

- **RL (reduction).** For `x ∈ {0,1,2,3}`, every `SL-U` lift has
  `𝒪ₓ(U¹ ⊠ U²) = 𝒪ₓ(U¹) ⊠ 𝒪ₓ(U²)`. The product is the Kronecker product of readbacks, or the
  tensor product of channels.
  - If every `Γᵏ` is column-stochastic, `U¹ ⊠ U²` is coherent for `Γ¹ ⊠ Γ²` iff each `Uᵏ` is
    coherent for `Γᵏ`.
  - Hence the `SL-U` value set on every carrier is exactly the set of products of the factor value
    sets.
  - It follows that `SL-U` never selects within one factor.
- **PL (locus).** The fibre-Gram tuple of `U¹ ⊠ U²` is the pointwise product `G(U¹) ⊠ G(U²)`. So
  every `SL-U` lift's class lies in act 28's product locus at every `t`.
- **PC (product certificate).** If the matrix of a value is reshuffled across the cut and has rank
  at least 2, the value is not a product. By RL, no `SL-U` lift attains it.
  - Rank ≥ 2 is certified exactly by one nonzero 2×2 minor through a nonzero pivot. If all minors
    through one nonzero pivot vanish, the rank is 1.
- **NS (no-signalling from `SL-U`).** For an `SL-U` lift, take any `t`, any `s < t`, any operator
  `ρ` on `V`, and any visible unitary `R₂` on `V₂`. Then:
  - (prep) `Tr_{V₂} 𝒪₁(t)(ρ₁ ⊗ ρ₂)` depends on `ρ₂` only through its trace;
  - (int) `Tr_{V₂}` of the intervention response is independent of `R₂`;
  - the same holds with the factors exchanged.
- **EQ1.** Suppose `|A| = 1`, and the unitary `U_t` satisfies prep no-signalling in one direction.
  Then `U_t = λ(U¹ ⊗ U²)`. The argument:
  - the Heisenberg map preserves `B(V₁) ⊗ 1`;
  - it therefore preserves the commutant `1 ⊗ B(V₂)`;
  - Skolem–Noether makes both restrictions inner.

  So at `|A| = 1`, `SL-U` is equivalent to prep no-signalling at every `t`.
- **HZ (horizon).** For a two-valued lift, every carrier value at `(t,s)` equals its value at
  `(min(t,1), min(s,1))`. Checking `t, s ∈ {0,1}` is therefore complete for these lifts.

## 5. Decision rules (each verdict is generated from its measurements)

**Status checks.** These hold before any verdict:

- **Admissibility.** Every lift is unitary and admissible for `Γ` at `t ∈ {0,1}`, in exact
  arithmetic.
- **`SL-U` status.** Each lift's status is computed, not declared. It is `SL-U` iff both values
  reshuffle across the cut to rank 1, by PC. The computed status must equal the table's.
- **Class relations.** Every `TRAJ` pair is certified `GramTrajEquiv`-inequivalent at `t = 1`, by
  one of two certificates:
  - a differing `∼_D` invariant: act 17's 2-cycle, embedded as
    `Ψ = G^{(0,0)}_{(1,0),(0,0)} · G^{(1,0)}_{(0,0),(1,0)}`;
  - for the lift not admitted, a violation of `a28_s_locus_first_index`. That puts its class off
    act 28's product locus, hence off every `SL-U` class by PL.

  Every `THREAD` pair is certified equivalent at `t = 0` and `t = 1`, by an exhibited
  anchored-phase matrix `D_t` with `G(b_t) = D_tᴴ G(a_t) D_t`.

**Pair verdicts.** For a pair `(a, b)` and a carrier `𝒪ₓ`, read over the frozen times. The first
lift `a` is always admitted.

- **If `b` is admitted:**
  - **`KEEP-BOTH`:** the values differ at some frozen time. The freedom survives `SL-U` and is
    physical relative to `𝒪ₓ`.
  - **`SAME`:** otherwise.
- **If `b` is not admitted:**
  - **`SEP-PHYS`:** at some frozen time, `𝒪ₓ(b)` lies outside the admitted value set. There are
    two certificates for this:
    - `𝒪ₓ(b)` is non-product, by PC;
    - or a written argument fixes the admitted value set to one point, and `𝒪ₓ(b)` differs from
      it exactly. Only `CELIM` carries such an argument.

    `SL-U` excludes `b`, and no admitted lift has its `𝒪ₓ` value at that time.
  - **`SEP-REP`:** some admitted lift at the same configuration equals `𝒪ₓ(b)` at every frozen
    time. `SL-U` excludes a representative, not a value of `𝒪ₓ`.
  - **`SEP-OPEN`:** neither certificate is found.

**Cell outcomes.** For `(C, freedom, 𝒪ₓ)`, read from the cell's pairs:

- **`EXCLUDES-ALL`:** `Γ` reshuffles to rank ≥ 2 across the cut (exact), so by RL no `SL-U` lift
  exists.
- **`ELIMINATES`:** some pair is `SEP-PHYS`, and the `SL-U` value set in scope is a single point by
  a written argument. Only `CELIM` carries one: `𝒪₂ ≡ 1` there by RL, because factor 1's admitted
  lifts are diagonal phases.
- **`REDUCES`:** some pair is `SEP-PHYS`, and some pair is `KEEP-BOTH`.
- **`UNCHANGED`:** one of two written arguments holds.
  - Every lift in scope is `SL-U` (RL at `C1A` and `C1B`). This is reported as "present" if some
    pair differs.
  - The freedom is absent from the scope: one class (SH1-C2), reported as "absent".
- **`UNDETERMINED`:** any other case.

**Headline tokens.**

- **`P0-SL-DISTINGUISHES-TRAJ`** and **`P0-SL-DISTINGUISHES-THREAD`.** Each requires a `SEP-PHYS`
  pair on `𝒪₁` for that freedom at `C21` or `CCT4`. The same is reported beside it for `𝒪₂` and
  `𝒪₃`. Otherwise the token is `P0-SL-NO-PHYSICAL-SEPARATION-<freedom>`.
- **`P0-SL-SELECTS`.** It requires some cell at `C21` or `CCT4` to be `ELIMINATES`.
- **`P0-SL-REDUCES-ONLY`.** It requires some cell there to be `REDUCES` and none `ELIMINATES`.
- **`P0-SL-INERT`.** Every other case.
- `CELIM` is a control and enters no headline.

## 6. No-signalling: statement, checks and the converse

**Observables.** The visible reduced state of one part:

- `Tr_{V₂}` of `𝒪₁(t)`, or of the intervention response;
- the same with the factors exchanged.

**Preparations.** Every operator `ρ` on `V`, with the hidden sector at the anchor. By linearity, the
matrix units `E¹_{ab} ⊗ E²_{cd}` give a complete check.

- Prep no-signalling `2 ↛ 1` reads `Tr_{V₂} 𝒪₁(t)(E¹_{ab} ⊗ E²_{cd}) =
  δ_{cd} · Tr_{V₂} 𝒪₁(t)(E¹_{ab} ⊗ E²_{00})`.
- `1 ↛ 2` is the same with the factors exchanged.

**Interventions.** Visible unitaries `R₂` on `V₂` at tick `s = 0`, read at `t = 1`, and the same
with the factors exchanged. By HZ, `s = 0` and `t ≥ 1` reduce to `t = 1`. For `s ≥ 1` the history
after `s` is the identity, so the condition holds for every lift.

- **At `CCT4` and `CCC`, every unitary is covered.** The check is the sesquilinear form `B` over
  matrix units `E_{cd}, E_{ef}` of `V₂`. A sufficient condition is
  `B(E_{cd}, E_{ef}) = δ_{ce} · β(d,f)`, with `β` independent of `c`; then the response is
  independent of every unitary `R₂`.
- **At `C21`**, the check covers the 24 permutation matrices of each `Vₖ`. There it spot-checks NS.

The tokens:

- **`P0-NS-HOLDS`.** Every `SL-U` lift at `C21` and `CCT4` passes prep no-signalling at
  `t ∈ {0,1}` and intervention no-signalling, in both directions.
- **`P0-NS-NONVACUOUS`.** Each of the following violates its test exactly:
  - `F` violates prep no-signalling;
  - `PF` violates intervention no-signalling;
  - `We.X` violates prep no-signalling;
  - `CN` violates prep no-signalling.

  Otherwise the prediction is vacuous on these witnesses, and that is reported.
- **`P0-NS-EQUIV-A1`.** Every lift at `C21` that is not admitted violates prep no-signalling at some
  `t ∈ {0,1}`. EQ1 [W] requires this. A lift that passed would contradict EQ1, and voids the round.
- **`P0-NS-WEAKER`.** One of two witnesses passes every no-signalling test, both directions, with
  the intervention test covering every unitary:
  - a lift at `CCT4` that is not admitted, and is `SEP-PHYS` on some carrier;
  - or `Ucv` at `CCC`, where `SL-U` is `EXCLUDES-ALL`.

  Otherwise the token is `P0-NS-NOT-SEPARATED`. `P0-NS-WEAKER` means no-signalling does not imply
  `SL-U`. The surplus is then a consequence of `SL-U` that no-signalling does not restate:
  - at `CCT4`, a product `𝒪₁`, so no correlated visible noise;
  - at `CCC`, a product visible law.

## 7. Controls (a failed control voids every verdict)

  - conjugation is an involution and multiplicative;
  - `H₁`, `Hᵢ`, `F₁₆`, `P_τ`, `W_d`, `L_cc` and `Ucv` are exactly unitary.
- **K-frozen.** The script reproduces three recorded values, which pins its conventions to the

  - act 17's `G^{(0)}_{10} G^{(1)}_{01}`, `1/16` for `H₁` against `i/16` for `Hᵢ`, on the factor;
  - CT4's relative-candidate entry `(0,0)`, `1` against `0`;
  - act 28's two entries at fibres `(0,1)` and `(1,1)`, indices `(2,0)` and `(2,1)`, `1/16`
    against `i/16`, for `T`'s tuple.
- **K-17-embed, act 17's witness embedded without restriction.**
  - **The configuration.** `C21` is act 21's product configuration, and each factor is act 17's
    `TJ3`.
  - **No restriction on the factor lifts.** By RL, the admitted lifts with a fixed factor-2 lift are
