**Argued against: Reading 1 under §8.3.** CONFIRM-BG-r2 holds that F4 excludes GR:400 Reading 1 "in both
§8.3 branches", because the piece "is defined as a function of `H`" and N3′ applies. I read §8.3
differently, for four reasons.

1. **What gravitates under §8.3.** GR:573 states that, for a relative difference, "there is a classical
   microstate difference corresponding to this energy difference … Relative differences appear in the
   classical stress-energy tensor and gravitate". Where §8.3's identification applies, the gravitating
   item is a component of the classical stress-energy, defined on the microstate. It is not the emergent
   computation.
2. **Uniformity with K2(a).** F4 admits `T^cl`'s energy content because it is defined on the microstate
   (r3:384–386). It does so even though `T^cl`'s values, through the dynamics, covary with the metric. A
   component of `T^cl` that §8.3 identifies falls in the same category. Classing it differently, without
   a difference relevant to grounding, fails K3.
3. **N3′ concerns definition.** It applies to a quantity defined as a function of the metric (r3:287–289).
   The classical counterpart's covariation with `H` is ⊳_D, and K5 separates that from ⊳_G.
4. **The witness is unaffected.** Its realized source value `ρ_Z(R*)` is absolute (`R₀ = R*`,
   U2Ar3:469). It is defined from the class (U1r5:222–225) and not relocated, so F4 excludes it in both
   branches. Reading 1's constant part is "fixed by the substratum budget" (GR:400).

**The limit of this reading.** It is conditional on §8.3's identification applying to an epoch-differential
vacuum piece, which "rests on GR:400's statement and not on a derivation" (U1r5:506). CONFIRM-BG-r2's
reading is the one that holds if the gravitating item is taken to be the emergent computation as defined.
r3 states both readings and marks the point as disputed (r3:90–91, r3:518–532, r3:655–657). It is put to
the owner in QB7 (r3:775–782). It does not change the witness's classification or the outcome.

**Found while applying BC-3: F4-8.** Without §8.3, uniform F4 on Reading G leaves the running-vacuum
channel (GR:374–380) unrealizable, along with Reading 1 and any `H`-dependent Reading 2 source.

- If the channel were a stated premise, F4 would be rejected by (b2) in that branch, and that branch's
  outcome would be (B).
- The corpus states the channel as "a structural reading, not a derived law" (GR:354) and "not derived"
  (GR:633), and does not adjudicate its readings (GR:400).
- r3 therefore classes F4-8 as a cost on the present premise base (r3:558–567), and QB7 asks the owner
  whether the channel is to be held as a premise.
