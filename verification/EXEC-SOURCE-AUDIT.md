# The executability-source audit — does the substratum's dynamics with the observer's stated access make one layer flow executable?

Owner-called, written from `main` at `087d023`, the merge of the Q3 propagation. Preregistered
here and committed alone before any proof is attempted. The Q3 round (`DERIVED-Q3-AUDIT.md`)
closed the finite completion problem conditionally: under the closure `DerivedOI`, with its phases
an explicit hypothesis, exact finite endomorphic operational quantum mechanics is exactly the
executability of one layer flow of an involution with a moved configuration
(`derivedOI_qm_iff_layerFlowExecutable'`). Two sourcing assumptions remain, the relative-phase
intervention and that executability. The phase-source audit decided the first for the stated
access, negatively. This round asks the same question of the second.

## The question

> Does the substratum's own dynamics, the second-order update with its depth-two factorization
> into a shear layer and a swap layer, together with the observer's stated access, read access to
> the visible sector and write access through selectable local couplings, acting through the
> observer architecture (attachment of ancillas, readout, coarse-graining, feed-forward, discard),
> make the continuous layer flow of one involution with a moved configuration an available
> operation at every level and every intermediate time?

In the kernel: for a substratum `𝒮` (`SubstratumInterfaceAudit.Substratum`: sites, alphabet, a
second-order rule, the update `𝒮.φ = leapEquiv 𝒮.R.F` with its layers `shearEquiv 𝒮.R.F` and
`swapEquiv`), the observer theory `obsTheory 𝒮`, the theory the stated access generates on the
configuration space (`permTheory 𝒮.Conf`, the substratum-interface audit's Q2), and any theory a
class of operators the stated access supplies generates:

> `LayerFlowExecutable (obsTheory 𝒮) g` for a layer `g` of `𝒮` with a moved configuration?

with `LayerFlowExecutable T g := ∀ n t, T.availExt n Unit (fun _ => conjChannel (gateFlow (levelPerm g n) t))`
(`LiftAudit`), the ancilla a spectator, `t` every real, `gateFlow g t = unit (permMat g) t` the
one-parameter unitary group of the gate. The lift audit's Q2 decided it for configuration-level
generation in general; this round decides it for the actual substratum's layers under every
route the corpus states, and, where no route is stated, names what one would have to supply.

**What "without assuming continuous control" fixes.** A derivation counts only if it is a proof
of availability in a theory generated by operators the stated access supplies, with no
availability axiom, no enlargement of the class by a non-monomial operator, and no reading of
availability off a mathematical path. Placing `gateFlow g t` in a class by hand is the stipulation
the question asks about, not an answer to it.

## Five distinctions, frozen at the outset

**(D1) A mathematical path is not an operation.** The update is joined to the identity by an exact
norm-continuous path of automorphisms of the quasilocal algebra (`driveQ_isContinuousPath`), and
each layer gate is reached at time one by the unitary group `unit g t`. These are theorems about
matrices and automorphisms. Availability is `T.availExt n Unit (fun _ => conjChannel U)`. The
lift audit fixed this exclusion and its guard enforces it; nothing below reads the second off the
first.

**(D2) An emergent continuous-time description of the visible process is a representation, not
an availability.** The manuscripts describe the visible process on accessible timescales by a
continuous-time channel with a Hamiltonian generator at time zero (`[Main §2.3]`, "approximate
unitarity"), by a Hamiltonian flow on a phase-space representative (`[Main §2.2]`), and by
continuous-time data `T(t)` used for phase retrieval (`[Main §3.2]`). Each describes the
statistics an observer records; none is an operation the observer performs. A continuous
one-parameter interpolation of a finite permutation is "additional structure, chosen rather than
derived" (`[Main §3.2]`), and a continuous-time Hamiltonian law "is additional structure where
that formulation is wanted" (`[GR §3.3]`).

**(D3) Time-one availability with a continuous interpolation is not intermediate-time
availability.** The layers at time one are permutations and are available in the observer theory
(`obs_shear_avail`, `obs_swap_avail`); the existence of `unit g t` between the identity and the
gate does not make `unit g (1/2)` available.

**(D4) Coarse-graining, hidden memory and hidden clocks give classical maps.** The observer
architecture attaches ancillas, reads out, coarse-grains, feeds forward and discards
(`InstAvail`). Every family it realizes from a class of monomial operators is a finite sum of
monomial conjugations after coarse-graining and discard, and preserves diagonal states
(`realized_of_instAvail`, `preservesDiag_conj_of_monomial`, `preservesDiag_sum`): a hidden clock
or a hidden memory reached through these operations yields a visible law that is a classical
stochastic map, not a coherent intermediate-time unitary.

**(D5) The target is the literal predicate.** `LayerFlowExecutable T g` at every level and every
real time, for one involution that moves a configuration. One non-monomial gate at one time is not
the target; neither is a continuous family that is not the gate flow of a layer.

## The criteria, fixed in advance

**Positive.** A kernel theorem `obs_layerFlowExecutable` (or its analogue for a theory generated
by a class the stated access supplies) proving `LayerFlowExecutable (obsTheory 𝒮) g` for one
layer `g` of the wave substratum with a moved configuration, under the rule of the question: no
availability axiom, no non-monomial operator placed in the class, no availability read off the
path.

**Negative, for the stated access.** Kernel theorems that the observer theory executes no layer
flow of an involution with a moved configuration, its own shear and swap layers included, and that
every class the stated access supplies, being configuration-level, generates no such
executability; together with the census verdict that every stated continuous object is a
representation or a mathematical path (D1, D2).

**Underdetermined.** An entry that names no operation and is not excluded by the negative
theorems. X7 is expected to be the one such entry.

## The tests, each with its admissible outcomes

**T1. The necessary condition.** If the theory an architecture `𝓘` generates executes the layer
flow of an involution with a moved configuration, then `𝓘` is not configuration-level, and it has
an admissible operator at level one that is not monomial: `not_configurationLevel_of_layerFlowExecutable`,
`exists_nonMonomial_of_layerFlowExecutable`, from the flow at time one half, which does not
preserve diagonal states, realized as a finite sum of admissible conjugations. Admissible outcome:
both theorems.

**T2. The actual layers.** For every substratum `𝒮`, the observer theory executes no layer flow of
an involution with a moved configuration (`obs_not_layerFlowExecutable`), in particular not the
flow of its own shear layer or swap layer when that layer moves a configuration
(`obs_not_layerFlowExecutable_shear`, `obs_not_layerFlowExecutable_swap`); and the swap layer of
the wave substratum moves a configuration for every nontrivial alphabet
(`waveSubstratum_swap_moves`), so the wave substratum's observer theory does not execute its own
swap-layer flow (`waveSubstratum_not_layerFlowExecutable_swap`). Admissible outcome: the theorems,
or the positive criterion.

**T3. The write access with a continuous parameter.** A theory generated by a bijection-level
class, the classes the read-write families generate included, executes no layer flow of an
involution with a moved configuration (`bijectionLevel_not_layerFlowExecutable`), the
read-write operators being permutation matrices at every parameter value
(`readWriteOperator_eq_perm`). Admissible outcome: the theorem.

**T4. The path is not an operation.** In the theory of every configuration-level class, the gate
flow of an involution with a moved configuration is unavailable at level one and time one half
(`configurationLevel_not_avail_gateFlow_half`), and that flow is the region's stage image of the
CT2 gate unitary at that time (`gateFlow_stage`, cited): the object the drive theorem runs is
exactly the operation the stated access does not supply. Admissible outcome: the theorem.

**T5. The census, per entry.** X1, X3, X4 negative by T2, T3 and D4; X2, X5, X6 negative by D1 and
D2 as representations or mathematical paths, no operation named; X7 underdetermined; X8 a
stipulation and the consistency control, not a source. Admissible outcome: the verdicts as
listed, or a positive entry, which would be the theorem of the positive criterion.

**T6. The countercontrols.** (a) Executability is consistent: quantum mechanics executes every
layer flow (`layerFlowExecutable_of_control`, cited); (b) the criteria detect executability when it
is stipulated: a class containing the gate flows executes them (`onesTheory_layerFlowExecutable`,
cited), so the negative verdicts are not an artifact of the criteria; (c) the necessary condition
of T1 is not vacuous: the ones-fixing class is not configuration-level
(`onesClass_not_configurationLevel`), the gate flow at time one half lying in it and not being
monomial. Admissible outcome: all three.

**T7. The surfaces and the checks.** Module `OIBridge/ExecSource.lean`, defining no intervention

## What the outcomes mean, fixed in advance

**If the verdict is negative for the stated access.** The executability of one layer flow is an
additional physical assumption on the route to quantum mechanics, not a consequence of the
substratum's dynamics with the stated access, exactly as the phase intervention is. The
assumption is then named in the same two-part form as the phase-source audit's: the necessary
condition, a non-monomial admissible operator at level one, which no configuration-level class
supplies (T1); and the sufficient access tested, the gate flow of one layer of the substratum's
own update at every level and every intermediate time, the stipulation `LayerFlowExecutable`
itself, which with the closure gives quantum mechanics (the Q3 round). A non-monomial operator is
not by itself sufficient: nothing here shows that an arbitrary non-monomial admissible operator
yields the gate flow of a layer, and the round does not claim it. The frontier of Route A is then
two named assumptions, neither derived from the stated access, and the next decision is the
owner's: attack the phase, or seek a physical principle for fractional-time access to the
substratum's own layers.

**If one entry is underdetermined.** The entry, expected to be the observer-level lift, is
recorded with what it would have to supply: a non-monomial admissible operator at level one (T1),
and, to reach the sufficient access, the gate flow of a layer at every level and time. The
manuscripts' statement that the lift is not proved stands; nothing here decides whether it is
derivable.

**If the verdict is positive.** The observer architecture derives executability, Route A reaches
quantum mechanics from the stated access with the phase intervention as its one remaining
assumption, and the layer-flow form of `[GR §3.3]` is requalified in a propagation round.

**What no outcome establishes.** Whether the observer-level lift is derivable. Whether a richer
observer architecture than the stated one, in particular one with a non-bijective-valued
coupling, which the manuscripts name as the irreducible empirical question, sources the flow.
Whether the phase intervention is derivable: the phase-source audit, not reopened. Uniqueness or
minimality of either assumption. Route A in either direction. Bare OI.
