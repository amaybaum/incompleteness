# Audit B — the SM/GR concrete cut

`verification/audit-census.json`, audit `B`; `verification/lean/audit_census_probe.py`.

The charter: identify the concrete visible/hidden cut the physical papers use, and check which of
the abstract observer, quotient, minimality and coherence hypotheses are **instantiated** there, as
against merely available somewhere in the formal framework. The purpose is that the published
physical story match the formal machinery, not that the machinery exist.

## The cut

`[GR]` §2.1: the cosmological horizon. `Γ_V` is the interior, `Γ_H` everything beyond; the partition
is a consequence of GR's causal structure rather than a modelling choice, and every sub-`c` observer
shares it. The book's Chapter 7 §7.2 is the parallel source.

## Finding B1 — C4 is named at the cut, and was not discharged there

`[GR]` §2.2, headed *Verification of the conditions*, verified three of the four:

| condition | how, at the cut | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | ADM constraints correlate interior and exterior data on any Cauchy surface; physical evolution is generated by the boundary term; stronger than `H_int ≠ 0` | satisfied |
| C2 | `τ_B ≳ 1/H ~ 10¹⁷ s` against `τ_S ~ 10⁻¹⁵ s`; ratio `10⁻³²` | satisfied |
| C3 | `A/ε²` horizon modes against `~10⁸⁰` baryons | satisfied |
| C4 | — | *no entry* |

while three other places spoke as though the fourth were in hand. `[GR]` §8.4 listed "the C1–C4
conditions" among the dependencies of the `ħ` derivation. The book's Chapter 7 §7.1 said that §7.2
"verifies that the cosmological horizon satisfies the framework's four conditions (… history readback
C4)", and its §7.2 closed with "the four conditions hold at the cosmological horizon by structural
argument — C4's readback is the bidirectionality of the boundary coupling, which reads the same
degrees it writes." And `[Main]` §1.3 sent the question back: the cosmological realization "with its
capacity, its timescales, and its bidirectional read-write coupling — is developed in companion work
and is nowhere consumed by a proof here", with the C4 entry naming the read-write cycle as "a property
to be independently demonstrated there".

So each paper treated the other as the place where readback across the cosmological horizon is
established, and neither established it. The census makes this a count rather than a reading: before
the repair, `**(C1)**`, `**(C2)**`, `**(C3)**` each occurred once in `papers/GR.md` and `**(C4)**`
nowhere in the corpus.

**Why the bidirectionality sentence did not close it.** C4 asks for history readback: information
about the visible past carried by hidden degrees and routed into future visible conditionals — at
some order, two visible histories with the same current state inducing different next-step laws,
mediated through the hidden state. Bidirectional coupling is a strengthened form of C1; persistence
is C2. Neither, nor their conjunction, supplies the routing. `[Main]` §2.3 records the separation
directly: a tape-and-ledger realization of the fair coin satisfies C1–C3 with identically zero
backflow on arbitrarily long accessible windows. And the dependency runs the wrong way to help:
`[Main]` §3.4 derives C1 and C3 *from* C4, so verifying those two at the cut is not evidence for it.

**Why H-scramble does not close it either.** H-scramble governs typicality in the Page-curve
arguments of `[GR]` Appendix A. Scrambling does not by itself route a particular stored visible
record back into accessible visible statistics; it bears on entanglement-entropy averages, not on
next-step visible conditionals. H-Hawking is a KMS condition, not a readback theorem.

**The repair, conservative by design.** No C4 proof is manufactured. `[GR]` §2.2 carries a fourth
entry: C4 is a named realization condition at the cosmological cut, **not presently discharged**,
with exactly what remains stated — demonstrating that a visible-history record written into hidden
boundary degrees is routed back into future visible conditionals within the accessible window. The
book's §7.1 and §7.2 are corrected to the same status in both parallel sources, and the sentence
inferring C4 from bidirectionality is gone.

**C4 is not added as a hypothesis of the `ħ` derivation.** `[Main]` §1.3 treats C1–C4 as diagnostics
of a realization rather than hypotheses of the characterization, and `[GR]`'s calibration is carried
by H-slope together with the horizon and frame conditions — its own §8.4 inventory lists exactly
those, and none is a readback claim. The §8.4 dependency line says so: C1–C3 verified at the
cut, C4 named there and not consumed by the calibration. The core `ħ` result is untouched; the OI
physical-realization claim is now exactly as strong as its evidence.

**A downstream consequence, recorded rather than repaired.** `[GR]` §7.2's corollary on the
invisible gravitational budget is stated *under* conditions (C1)–(C4). With C4 not discharged at the
cut, its application to our universe is conditional on C4 in the same way its statement already
says; nothing in its wording changes, but its status at the concrete cut is now visible rather than
implicit.

## What the audit checked and found clean at the cut

**The `ħ` chain's named conditions.** H-slope, H-frame, H-spectrum, H-balance, H-shell, H-energy,
H-Hawking, H-scramble, H-blind, H-local-lift are each stated with their conditionality at the point
of use, and §8.4's inventory attributes the calibration to H-slope with the horizon and frame
conditions. That inventory is the concrete-cut instantiation the charter asks for, and it does not
lean on any quotient, minimality or coherence hypothesis from the formal framework.

**Minimality and the quotient machinery at the cut.** Neither `[GR]` nor Chapter 7 assumes the
horizon partition is passively minimal or itinerary-separating; the census entries of Audit A remain
at zero across the physical papers.

**The universality claim.** `[GR]` §8.5 quantifies over substrata "satisfying [Main]'s structural
conditions C1 … C4" as an explicit hypothesis of a conditional theorem, which is the correct form and
is left unchanged.

## What this audit does not claim

That C4 fails at the cosmological cut, or that it cannot be demonstrated there — only that the
corpus does not presently demonstrate it. That the `ħ` derivation, the Bekenstein–Hawking coefficient
or the running-vacuum form are affected: their conditions are the named horizon and KMS conditions.
That the SM-side lattice cut has been audited to the same depth: this round's concrete-cut census is
the gravitational one, and the SM cut is the next item under the same charter. That the census
exhausts the corpus — its vocabularies are those recorded in the manifest.
