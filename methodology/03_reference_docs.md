### §1 What counts as what — the strict-criteria taxonomy (§B.2.186)

Three categories of distinctive content, distinguished by what they actually claim:

**G1 — substrate-derivable parameter-free predictions.** Parameter-free, substrate-derivable, quantitatively falsifiable, AND independent of already-counted G1 content (the §B.2.186 criterion (d) for independence). These are the framework's strongest predictive content. **Defensible count: 22-23 effective independent.**

**Mechanism-distinctive dissolutions.** Framework explanations for outcomes shared with standard QM (Bell violations, KS contextuality, PBR ψ-reality, FR consistency). The *outcome* is what QM produces; the *mechanism* (substrate non-factorizability, trace-out contextuality, emergent ψ-reality, level-confusion diagnosis) is distinctive. These belong on the positive ledger but should not be presented as parameter-free predictions. **Count: 5** (Bell, KS, PBR, FR, MWI).

**Framing items.** Interpretive recasting of standard-QM observables without observable consequences. The MWI category-error diagnosis is the canonical example: no measurement distinguishes OI's "branches as emergent compression features" from MWI's "branches as parallel ontologies" on standard observables. **Count: 1.**

The §B.2.183 generous count of "29 G1" is the union of all three categories. The §B.2.186 strict count of 22-23 G1 + 5 mechanism + 1 framing is what survives the criterion-(d) independence filter and is what external presentations should use.

### §2 The 9 hard differentiators (OI ≠ standard account; testable in principle)

These are the framework's sharpest falsification targets. Each gives a different prediction from the relevant standard account; observation can adjudicate.

#### Theoretical-principle differentiators

1. **Strong CP $\bar\theta = 0$ from T-invariance.** OI predicts structurally. SM has no derivation; Peccei-Quinn posits an axion. *Falsification channel:* DM axion searches. If no axion is found across the parameter space, OI is differentiated from PQ-based accounts; if an axion is found, OI is constrained.

2. **No-GUT structural theorem.** OI forbids any GUT extension via the cubic-group commutant theorem (Appendix B.6b). GUT programs (SU(5), SO(10), Pati-Salam) predict proton decay; OI predicts none beyond SM rate. *Falsification channel:* Hyper-Kamiokande proton decay search.

3. **Three generations from taste-reduction.** OI forbids fourth generation structurally. SM allows N generations in principle; data rules out fourth phenomenologically. *Falsification channel:* any fourth-generation discovery refutes OI.

4. **BQP characterization (super-BQP impossibility).** OI promotes the open CS question of super-BQP to a theorem. *Falsification channel:* any demonstration of super-BQP physical computation refutes OI.

5. **Extended Church-Turing as theorem.** OI promotes the ECT conjecture to a theorem. *Falsification channel:* any physical computation provably outside ECT.

#### Empirical differentiators

6. **Information-theoretic ceiling on transcriptome-only ML methods.** OI predicts scaling failures structurally; ML community expects scaling solutions. *Empirical status:* observed failures (Theodoris-Geneformer, scGPT, others) consistent with OI's no-go; inconsistent with scaling-will-work expectations. Currently the most active empirical engagement front.

7. **Rett syndrome wider therapeutic window.** OI predicts structurally; Guy et al. 2007 confirmed empirically. **CAVEAT — pre-registration timing unverified.** If the framework's prediction predates 2007 it's a forward prediction; if it postdates 2007 it's a retrodiction. Strongest non-physics empirical confirmation conditional on this timing check. *Author action item:* confirm pre-registration date.

8. **MOND acceleration $a_0 = cH/6$.** OI derives. ΛCDM has no galactic-scale acceleration prediction; MOND fits $a_0$ empirically. *Empirical status:* strong differentiator at galactic scales; contested at cluster scales (Bullet Cluster + cluster mass discrepancies favor DM, but OI also reproduces these via dark-sector content per GR §7).

9. **Partially-quantum regime in biology.** OI predicts a distinctive intermediate regime at $\tau_S \sim \tau_B$ characterized by P-indivisibility measure. No other framework predicts this specific regime. *Falsification channel:* multi-time correlation measurements in candidate systems (cryptochrome magnetoreception, electron transport in photosynthesis).

### §4 The constraint-structure observation (§B.2.184)

This is a fourth dimension beyond G1, mechanism-distinctive, and framing — it captures qualitative depth of explanation rather than count of predictions.

**The observation:** the framework dissolves approximately 12 deep mysteries via one structural feature (substrate/emergent split with trace-out). The list includes: cosmological constant ($10^{122}$), measurement problem, MWI, Bell, KS, PBR, FR, dark sector, hierarchy problem, strong CP, Higgs criticality, absence of GUT signatures.

**Why it matters:** This is Deutschian hard-to-vary evidence — fixing the framework's substrate machinery to produce one dissolution forces or strongly constrains the others. A framework with N free mechanisms each independently fitted to N puzzles has high prior; a framework with one constrained machinery producing all N dissolutions has low prior with correlated evidence — the prior advantage typically dominates.

**Why probability bookkeeping captures it imperfectly:** Probability is about correctness, not about how-much-the-correctness-would-tell-us. A 50% framework explaining 12 deep puzzles is epistemically more valuable than a 50% framework explaining 2 minor puzzles. The 36-50.5% probability range integrates correctness honestly but doesn't capture qualitative depth.

**How to use it:** For audiences focused on theoretical structure, lead with this observation and the dissolution list. For audiences focused on empirical track record, lead with the §2 hard differentiators and the empirical record (Cabibbo, JUNO, GW250114, Rett). For mixed audiences, lead with both.

### §6 "Do not strip" caveats — six things this card must always carry

These caveats are not decoration; they are what makes the card honest. Removing them turns the card into overclaim material.

**Caveat 1 — Hard differentiators are falsification targets, not assertions of correctness.** "9 hard differentiators" describes the framework's *vulnerability surface*, not its *track record*. Six of the nine are not yet empirically decided (proton decay search ongoing; axion search ongoing; super-BQP not demonstrated either way; ECT not demonstrated either way; partially-quantum biology tests pending; ML ceiling tests ongoing but not closed). Presenting "9 hard differentiators" without acknowledging falsification status is misleading.

**Caveat 2 — Mechanism-distinctive items are NOT parameter-free predictions.** The Bell, KS, PBR, FR dissolutions reproduce what QM does — required of any QM-compatible framework. The distinctiveness is the *explanation*, not the *outcome*. Conflating mechanism-distinctive with G1 (the §B.2.183 generous-grading error caught by §B.2.186) inflates the framework's apparent predictive content.

**Caveat 3 — Rett syndrome (#7) timing is unverified.** If the framework's prediction of wider therapeutic windows for reader-writer disorders predates Guy et al. 2007, it's a forward prediction (strong empirical anchor). If it postdates 2007, it's a retrodiction (still consistent, but weaker). The card cannot resolve this; author confirmation is required.

**Caveat 4 — "29 G1" is generous-grading.** The §B.2.183 number is the union of G1 + mechanism-distinctive + framing. The defensible figure for external communication is **22-23 effective independent G1** plus the other categories shown separately. Using "29" without the breakdown reintroduces the inflation §B.2.186 walked back.

**Caveat 5 — The community engagement map identifies *opportunities*, not *endorsements*.** No community on the list has endorsed the framework as a class. The map says "these communities have a differentiator at stake," not "these communities accept the framework." Framing it as endorsement is misrepresentation.

**Caveat 6 — Constraint-structure observation is qualitative.** It is not a probability adjustment; it is a Deutschian hard-to-vary observation that probability bookkeeping captures imperfectly. Translating "dissolves 12 deep mysteries" into "should have probability X" is precisely the move §B.2.184 disclaims as not principled.

### §2 Pre-registered sectioning

Part V comprises six new sections (§21–§26), inserted between the current §19 (Gauge Freedom and Observational Incompleteness) and §20 (Conclusion). §20 (Conclusion) will need a brief revision to reference Part V; §19 may receive a short Part-V-referencing forward pointer.

**§21 — The Operational Question (~3-4 paragraphs).** Frames Part V relative to Parts II–IV. The well-posed question physics modulo gauge identifies is "what does the equivalence class produce, and what are its representatives?" §21 sharpens this to its operational form: given the substratum gauge group $\mathcal{G}_{\text{sub}}$, what substrate computations are determining of observable content, and what are gauge-forbidden? The answer is non-trivial and is what §22–§24 develop.

**§22 — The Gauge-Invariance No-Go (~6-8 paragraphs).** §B.2.173 in the project journal (off-repo), written for the paper. **Statement:** under $\mathcal{G}_{\text{sub}}$ state-relabeling, no substrate computation can determine bijection-specific quantities (4/3 directionality, CP phases, mass hierarchy assignment). **Proof sketch:** gauge-invariance under relabeling means observables depend only on equivalence-class invariants; bijection-specific quantities require fixing a representative; therefore they are not derivable from substrate-only computation, only from the empirical inputs that select the representative. **Worked examples:** (a) Cond 2 4/3 directionality (which pair-ratio gets the 4/3); (b) CKM CP phase; (c) mass hierarchy assignment within generations. **Status:** developed result, proof complete in the project journal (off-repo) §B.2.173.

**§23 — The Category-Error Finding (~5-7 paragraphs).** §B.2.174 in the project journal (off-repo). **Statement:** per Ch02 §2.5 (book) / equivalent in technical papers, there is no substrate-level Lagrangian; "$\mathcal{L}_Y$" is emergent post-trace-out. The substrate is the deterministic bijection $\varphi$, not an action functional. **Implication for §22:** the gauge-invariance no-go is one manifestation of a deeper category structure — substrate-level operations (graph computations, bijection iterations) are categorically distinct from emergent-level operations (Lagrangian field theory, perturbation theory). Searching for "substrate-level Lagrangians" attacks a non-existent target. **Worked example:** the §B.2.164–174 Lagrangian search arc, retired by this finding. **Status:** developed result, type-checking complete in the project journal (off-repo) §B.2.174.

**§24 — The Architectural Pattern: Part III Extended (~4-5 paragraphs).** Connects §22–§23 to Part III's "one architecture, four manifestations" pattern. Part III identifies one architectural feature (substrate/emergent split with trace-out) producing four no-go manifestations. §22–§23 identify the same architectural feature producing two further manifestations at the content level: (a) what's gauge-determinable, (b) what's category-coherent. **The unification claim:** Part III + Part V together give "one architecture, six manifestations." Stated as a thesis: every framework no-go traces to the substrate/emergent architecture, and the architecture is fixed before any no-go is considered.

**§25 — The Operational Audits (~6-8 paragraphs).** §B.2.172 (gauge audit, physics-sector) and §B.2.178 (cross-domain audit, ch10-17) as systematic applications of the methodology to the framework's own content. **What the audits do:** classify each prediction by gauge status (G1 substrate-derivable / G1-partial / G2 mechanism-distinctive). **What they find:** approximately 22-23 effective independent G1 predictions, with the strict-criteria taxonomy (§B.2.186) distinguishing G1 from mechanism-distinctive (5 items) and framing (1 item). **What the audits demonstrate as methodology:** systematic application of the gauge-invariance no-go to a framework's *own* content is itself a methodological move — the framework holds itself accountable to the standard Part IV proposes for foundational programs in general. **Worked examples:** the §B.2.178 cross-domain audit's mid-session correction (14 G1 → 6 G1 under strict criteria) and the §B.2.186 audit-of-audit's recursive correction (29 G1 → 22-23 effective + 5 mechanism + 1 framing) demonstrate the criteria-strictness discipline in action.

**§26 — The Operational Refinements (~3-5 paragraphs).** Three methodological refinements canonized through this work, presented at general-methodology fidelity (not just framework-internal): (a) verify before recording (§A.13 seventh, eight-instance strength) — positive findings, especially corrective ones, inflate at first pass and need strict-criteria filters; (b) recursive scrutiny (§A.13 seventeenth candidate) — corrective moves require the same scrutiny as the moves they correct; (c) the observable / derivable / structurally-permitted / structurally-forbidden distinction (§B.2.175) — the framework's open-question list should distinguish these four categories rather than mixing them. **The general claim:** these refinements are not framework-specific; they apply to any foundational program that takes physics modulo gauge as its methodology.

### §B.9.3 Distinctiveness and significance — consolidated standing summary (June 5, 2026)

*Consolidates material previously scattered across §B.9.1 (Differentiator Card — the falsification-target inventory), the §B.2 reference-class block, §B.8.6 (positioning vs emergent-QM programs), and §B.2.236 (the differentiated foundational-papers verdict). It duplicates none of them and supersedes none; it is the single findable answer to "what separates OI from its neighbours, and what would it mean if confirmed." Written recency-invariantly per §A.18: anchored on the reference class, distinctiveness stated as real-but-non-uniform, the freshest finding (the SC joint-forcing) held to its actual weight rather than the headline, significance expressed as conditional × probability. It does not move the bands.*

**The recency-invariant anchor (read this first).** The reference class is "foundational programs claiming to derive QED/QCD/GWS/GR from prior structure": ~5–10 active programs (string theory and successors, loop quantum gravity, asymptotic safety, causal set theory, group field theory, Wolfram's hypergraph rewriting, Adler's trace dynamics, possibly Padmanabhan/Verlinde emergent-gravity). The base rate for substantive correctness in this class is low — decades each, none yet established. ### §B.9.4 Stage-3 offline boost campaign — run-ladder specification

# Offline Stage-3 Campaign — runnable specification

**Status:** the engine (`hmc_stage3_twospecies_su2.py`) is built and gate-validated (§B.2.235); the runner (`run_stage3_campaign.py`) drives the first rung from the command line. This document is the run ladder that turns "the engine exists" into "here is what to run." Everything below is **offline (§A.13, multi-day MC)** and every output ships **EXPLORATORY-PENDING-REPRODUCTION (§A.17)**. A single run on one machine is a data point, not the campaign — each headline number must be reproduced on independent seeds and, ideally, independent machines.

**The question the campaign answers.** Two species with different bare limiting speeds on a common confining background: does the speed split δc → 0 in the scaling region as the lattice grows and the coupling approaches the continuum window (emergent-Lorentz attractor / BPS), or does δc persist (the boost-LV differential survives)? The single-machine smoke test cannot answer this; only the ladder below can, because the apparent smoke-scale shrink is uninterpretable (L=4 curvature, single β, few configs, overall-scale confound).

## Acceptance gates carried at every rung (from the validated engine)
- **Gate S (free):** with the gauge field off, both species reproduce the analytic dispersion to ~1e-10 and the bare split 1/√A₂−1/√A₁. Run once per (L, Lt) to confirm the operator reduces correctly at that size.
- **Gate A (weak):** at large β the gauge-fixed speeds return to the bare values within MC error.
- **Plaquette-preservation:** Coulomb fixing leaves ⟨plaq⟩ unchanged to <1e-6 and keeps max|U|=1 (catches the divergent-gauge-fixing bug class).
- **Finite jackknife errors on c₁, c₂, δc** at every measured point.
A rung's physics output is only admissible if S, A, and plaquette-preservation pass at that rung's lattice size.

## Rung 1 — SU(2), two scalar species (RUNNABLE NOW with the current engine)
Purpose: resolve δc cleanly (kill the L=4 large-momentum curvature) and map δc(β, L) across the SU(2) crossover. This is the rung the validated code runs as-is.

Ladder (each step reproduced on ≥2 seeds):
1. **Volume ladder at fixed β in the crossover** (e.g. β≈1.6): `L = 4 → 6 → 8 → 12 → 16`, `Lt ≈ 2–3·L`, `--nconf` rising 50 → 200 → 500 as L grows. Watch whether the free-limit split (Gate S) converges to the true 1/√A₂−1/√A₁ as L grows — that is the curvature control, and it tells you how much of any measured δc shrink is physics vs. finite-L.
2. **β-scan at the largest affordable L** into the scaling region: `--betas 1.2 1.4 1.6 1.8 2.0 2.4 3.0 4.0`. The decisive plot is δc(β)/bare-split vs. β at fixed (large) L, and then its drift as L increases. Convergence requires the *ratio* to fall *and* survive the L→large and continuum-approach limits — not the absolute δc, which shrinks trivially with overall speed scale.
3. **Autocorrelation / decorrelation check:** raise `--sweeps-per` until successive configs are decorrelated (measure integrated autocorr of the plaquette and of c₁); jackknife errors are only trustworthy past that point.

Invocation examples:
```
python3 run_stage3_campaign.py --smoke                         # sanity: reproduce §B.2.235
python3 run_stage3_campaign.py --L 8 --Lt 20 --betas 1.6 --nconf 200 --sweeps-per 8 --out v8_b1p6.jsonl
python3 run_stage3_campaign.py --L 12 --Lt 28 --betas 1.2 1.6 2.0 2.4 3.0 --nconf 300 --out scan_L12.jsonl
```
Compute: rung-1 cost is dominated by the per-config Coulomb fix + the two direct solves; the direct solver scales poorly, so **swap `spsolve` → `cg`** (the engine notes M is Hermitian-PD; cg is the production solver) before going past L≈8, and consider porting the hot loops to the existing C kit in `papers/oi_lattice_code/` for L≥12.

## Rung 2 — SU(3) (code extension required)
The mechanism needs the confining SU(N) of the actual gauge sector; SU(2) is the stand-in. Extension: replace the 2×2 SU(2) link/staple/heatbath and the 2-color covariant operator with SU(3) (3×3, reuse the Cabibbo–Marinari SU(2)-subgroup heatbath or a Metropolis on the Wilson action; 3-color solve). Re-pass Gates S/A at SU(3). Then repeat the rung-1 ladder. This is the first rung that speaks to the real gauge group.

## Rung 3 — dynamical staggered fermions (the determinant)
Quenched (rungs 1–2) omits sea-quark feedback. Add dynamical staggered fermions via RHMC (the fermion determinant), matter species as staggered fermions rather than scalars. This is the largest single extension and the point at which the run becomes genuinely multi-day/HPC. Re-pass the gates; repeat the ladder. Only with the determinant is the test faithful to the interacting theory's IR.

## Rung 4 — the charge-differential knob (faithful differential)
Rungs 1–3 induce the split via different *bare anisotropies* (A₁≠A₂) — a clean attractor probe. The *physically faithful* differential arises from different *gauge charges* (two species in different reps, e.g. fundamental vs. adjoint), since the boost-LV differential is ∝ the coupling spread. Add a rep parameter to the matter hop (link in the chosen rep) and measure δc between reps at equal bare speed. Agreement of the two knobs (bare-speed vs. charge) on whether δc→0 is the cross-check that the result is about the dynamics, not the probe.

## What would count as a result (and its honest ceiling)

A reproduced *persistence* of δc would be evidence against. Either way: 0pp on the framework's correctness band until external reproduction (§A.17); this is one computation, not peer review, and the perturbative evidence (§B.2.228 A) currently leans against convergence, so a positive result would be a genuine surprise to be triple-checked, not assumed.
