## Bottom line

**1. Avenue 1 (Track B, chart J1).** None of the OI structures checked implies J1.
- Each of them holds in the padded non-locally-tomographic pair, which I built exactly.
- Its realization by Main.md's gluing theorem (V16) is available for every finite experiment (Main.md:22, :562). So the realization shows the pair is in the theorem's scope; it cannot exclude any finite experiment.
- The substratum posits (vii) and (ix)–(xi) were not checked.

Two written directions hold under TestGen:
- **TestGen** means pair tests are exactly the coarse-grainings of words of available instrument branches followed by a product readout of V_A∪V_B. The readout's product form comes from posit (viii). The word form comes from the protocol model of Main.md:544–558.
- **Direction 1:** TestGen ∧ Descent ⇒ J1.
- **Direction 2:** TestGen ∧ (one branch violating Descent on the body) ⇒ ¬J1.
- **Descent** means every available instrument branch maps product-equivalent subnormalized states to product-equivalent subnormalized states. It is a linear analogue of CompletionAction.lean's StateRespect, read on the product-effect stage. The kernel's normalized StateRespect does not suffice for outcome-bearing branches.
- Neither direction has a kernel witness (§A.34).

Descent is not sourced:
- GR §3.3 (GR.md:216), W d (CompositeDimension.lean:95–97) and WK k (pr/829 :56–65) presuppose it, because they define operations on the visible carrier or on the product chart.
- Composite.lt (CompositeInterface.lean:245) assumes J1 itself.

**2. Avenue 2 (Track C, first fork).** For the domain alone the answer is no. The first fork as posed, for SP cones, remains open.
- The face generated by two non-orthogonal pure products that share no factor is not fixed by the domain. It can be any closed convex cone between the segment and an explicit 6-dimensional cone B6. There is a continuum of distinct Λ₄ faces, and there are non-ball faces too.
- Three results follow, each [W + X] on stated instances:
  - (a) "Close" product pairs cannot be distinguished in any domain cone, so SP forces non-product pure states into their faces.
  - (b) maxCone (BP), the canonical domain cone with the non-ball face B6, is not SP.
  - (c) BP is Φ₁-invariant.
- So BP is not a counterexample. Two questions remain open: whether every SP domain cone is symmetric, and whether SP implies Φ₁ preservation.

## Avenue 1: the chart J1

**Question.** Does any OI-level structure exclude a stage-consistent, non-locally-tomographic two-token system whose product shadow satisfies every #829 premise, once it is realized as a finite reversible OI system?

### B1. What the kernel padding control shows [K-read + W] — ELABORATING
- In `paddedPre` (CompositeInterface.lean:848, :885), no effect of the interface reads the padding coordinate.
- In the stage vocabulary (KInfFoundations.lean:63–72) a state is its probability vector over effects, so a coordinate no effect reads is erased by construction.

### B2. Exact padded table [X] (padded_pair_exact.py)

**Model.**
- A and B are qubits (the tokens). hA and hB are hidden bits with the fixed prior I/4.
- Local instruments: HA, VA = H S†, HB, VB, XB.
- Joint instruments: CN, and L = X_B iff hA xor hB. Which instruments count as joint depends on the site identification; see B5.
- Readouts: MZB, and RD (Z_A Z_B).
- Seven preparations, including Qe = (L, MZB, select 0) and Qo = (L, MZB, XB, select 1).
- 43 test words of length at most 2, each with 4 outcomes. Every test is a unitary word followed by RD; there are no intermediate measurements inside tests.

**Checks.**
- ST: the table is a valid finite stage.
- PF: all 84 local-word effects factor as E_A⊗E_B⊗I.
- TC: the product effects span Herm(4) (rank 16).
- SH: every shadow is a density matrix (exact LDL), and every product row equals the shadow's Born row. The comparison runs on an independent 4-dimensional code path.
- NS: no-signalling holds in both directions.
- NLT: Qe and Qo have the same shadow |00⟩⟨00| and agree on all 84 product effects. On (L, RD=(0,1)) they give 0 against 1, and 22 joint effects separate them.
- NLT+: the same holds for the shadow |+0⟩⟨+0|, with 24 separating joint effects.
- DIM: on the parity sector (dimension 32) the product effects have rank 16, depth-2 effects rank 24 and depth-3 effects rank 28.
  - The rule "rank > 16" is already implied by NLT, so DIM adds no independent evidence (review Q1).
  - In this test vocabulary the span saturates at 31 at every depth. Every effect has Tr E_e = Tr E_o, which caps the span at 31, and the review's exact closure reaches 31 (review R1; without L the closure has dimension 16).
  - Reaching 32 needs intermediate MZB branches inside tests, for example (MZB=0, L, MZB=0), whose traces are (2, 0) (review R1b).
- DSC: every instrument except L acts as U⊗I_hh, so it descends to the shadow. L does not descend.
- K829: shadow images are density matrices. That is invariance of PSD, not of the table's cone. The #829 premises hold for the continuum shadow cone Q3 [W]; see B4(f). The script's label now says this (review item 18).
- IF: the composite of local preparations is a function of the local states.

**Result:** 11/11 checks pass and 5/5 mutations are rejected.
- The mutation `L_descends` also breaks the preparations, because Qo's selection gets probability 0. The review's clean mutation `L_test_id` removes L from the tests only, and it fails exactly NLT and NLT+ (review probes/padded_probeA2.out). So NLT is a genuine control.
- `rank_short` perturbs only the checked expression. It tests the comparison logic, not the mathematics.

### B3. Realization within the scope of the cited construction [X] (oi_realization_exact.py)

The construction is the Main.md gluing theorem (:544–560, exact-ring variant). This answers the owner's safeguard (B) in a limited sense: the preparations, readouts and step maps are the theorem's own. But the theorem realizes every finite experiment (Main.md:22) and "can realize non-quantum finite instrument families" (Main.md:562). So B3 shows that the padded pair lies in the theorem's scope. It cannot show that OI makes L available in any stronger sense.

**Two register models.**
- **V16** is the theorem applied verbatim to a d=16 experiment:
  - visible counter, record and outcomes;
  - hidden branch register, predecessor stack and seeds;
  - one fixed step map per instrument;
  - exact thresholds with G = 4, so the grid error is zero.
- **VS** is a site-refactored variant, not the theorem verbatim:
  - hA and hB are classical hidden registers, hA a site in region A and hB a site in region B, fixed as initial conditions from a fixed product prior;
  - a 4×4 joint branch register R holds AB;
  - VS is operationally equal to V16 (LAW).
- CN and L are instruments of one composite experiment. They are not built by the theorem's composition clause (4) (Main.md:552), which builds separated composites from local CP maps only.

**Checks.**
- GRID: G = 4.
- LAW: for both models, the realized joint law equals an independently coded dense Born law on all 287 distinct instrument sequences (301 preparation–test pairs).
- INJ/BIJ: each of the 9 step maps is injective on its reached arguments (7023 for V16, 28092 for VS). The explicit inverse recovers each argument, and each map completes to a bijection of one finite set (7117 and 28468 states). These are consistency checks that the predecessor stack and finite completion guarantee; they are not independent evidence.
- SEED: each step reads only its own seed (sampled regression).
- SITE: these are properties of how the model was built, relative to the chosen site identification (see B5).
  - VS: no instrument other than L reads or changes hA or hB, and L depends on both (exact witnesses).
  - V16: every instrument other than L acts identically on the four hh blocks, and L conjugates exactly the odd-parity blocks.
- TBL: the realized table reproduces the NLT certificate.

**Result:** 12/12 checks pass and 5/5 mutations are rejected.

### B5. What would source J1 [W]
**Classification:** ELABORATING, a reformulation of J1 (compare Main.md:256 and ROADMAP.md:1007). The only NEW item is the marker that H2 and A, read on the chart, presuppose that the gates descend.

**TestGen.** Pair tests are exactly the coarse-grainings of words of available instrument branches followed by a product readout of V_A∪V_B. It is only partly supplied:
- Posit (viii) (Main.md:708) gives the product form of the final readout of V_A∪V_B.
- The word form ("intervention words ending in a visible readout") comes from the gluing theorem's protocol model (Main.md:544–558).
- The kernel stage (KInfFoundations.lean:63–72) has abstract effects and supplies neither.

**Descent.** Every available instrument branch maps product-equivalent subnormalized states to product-equivalent subnormalized states.
- It is a linear analogue of CompletionAction.lean's StateRespect, read on the product-effect stage.
- The kernel's normalized StateRespect does not suffice for outcome-bearing branches.
- The subnormalized form includes equality of branch probabilities, because the unit is a product effect.

**The two written directions.** Each is [W]; neither has a kernel witness (§A.34).
- **(⇐) TestGen ∧ Descent ⇒ J1**, by induction on word length.
  - It uses Descent in the subnormalized form.
  - With the normalized StateRespect (CompletionAction.lean:53) it fails for an outcome-bearing joint branch whose probability is not a product effect.
- **(⇒, contrapositive) TestGen ∧ (one branch violating Descent on the body) ⇒ ¬J1.**
  - It needs the reverse inclusion in TestGen: every available word followed by a product readout is an available test (sequential closure).
  - The inclusion "effects ⊆ words∘readouts" does not give it.
- DSC together with NLT is an [X] instance of the converse direction only.

**Locality is relative.**
- Instruments of the token-local tests descend relative to the chart those tests generate. That this chart is the token's ball is P1 together with closure of local tests.
- Which instruments are joint depends on the site identification (viii).
  - The padded table is realized identically with L reading only hB' = hA⊕hB, a region-B site. Under that identification L is region-local and still does not descend.
  - The relabeling is a bijection of the hidden pair that preserves the uniform product prior, and no other instrument touches the hidden pair.
  - The realization's own mutation `L_one_site` (L reads hB only) fails SITE and passes LAW.
  - The review's probe with L = X_B^{hB} passes ST, PF, TC, SH, NS, NLT, NLT+, DSC, K829 and IF. Only DIM's parity clause fails (probes/padded_probeB_hBonly.out).
  - (viii) itself says the dynamics does not select the identification.
- So "L is joint" holds only relative to VS's identification, and Descent cannot be narrowed to joint instruments only.

**Sufficient routes.**
- Sufficient but not necessary: every available instrument is a word in token-local instruments (relative to a fixed identification) and the H2 gates, and those gates descend. Stating H2 and A on the chart already presupposes their descent.
- Alternative: full local access to the region carrier, through the OI_Q region algebra (GR.md:282–286) and GR §3.3's maps on M_S (GR.md:216).
  - This gives local tomography for region carriers.
  - But it makes the token the whole region, and it is a posit (continuous off-diagonal controllability), not bare OI.

**Related corpus statements.**
- Main.md:256: a symmetry that factorizes as S_V⊗S_H descends to S_V. The U⊗I_hh instruments here are of that kind.
- ROADMAP.md:1007: W d encodes local tomography and the product form of composite tests.

**Assumption-watch marker (NEW).**
- H2 and A, read on the chart, presuppose that the CNOT gates and the local rotations descend.
- GR §3.3, W d and WK k presuppose Descent; Composite.lt assumes J1 itself.
- Carry this marker to K1 (NativeGate on W d) and to K3.

## Avenue 2: SP and the symmetric class

**Frozen definitions.**
- Domain: closed convex C with SEP ⊆ C ⊆ BP on Herm(4), with the trace as unit.
- Effects: E in C* with I−E in C*. These are the mathematical effects; physical availability is not used.
- SP: BMU Postulate 1 (classical decomposability, no uniqueness).
- "Symmetric": homogeneous and self-dual, i.e. the EJA cone (Koecher–Vinberg). This is C1's class.
- Strong symmetry (BMU Postulate 2) is a different property and is not used.
- The quantum cone is not imported. It enters only as an explicit case in C2(c).

### C1. The face in every domain cone [W + X: face_structure_exact.py, 11/11 checks, 5/5 mutations]
**Classification:** NEW (structural) for the corpus. The literature is unchecked: the facial structure of positive maps on M₂ (Byeon–Kye, recalled) may already contain the B6 description.

- Take x = ab and y = a'b', non-orthogonal and sharing no factor.
- The face lies in B6 = W∩BP, where W is 6-dimensional.
  - This is exact on the instance pair (W6).
  - The review supplies a general written argument: in the basis (ab, a'b', ab', a'b), the p1 conditions force M44 = M14 = M24 = 0 and the p2 conditions force M33 = M13 = M23 = 0. It also gives an exact control at four rational pairs (review R4).
- In coordinates (α, β, γ, δ) along x, y, |ab⟩⟨a'b'|+h.c. and |ab'⟩⟨a'b|+h.c.:
  - B6 = {α, β ≥ 0, √(αβ) ≥ |γ|+|δ|} [W; exact identities FW, ZETA and NEG on the instance pair].
  - B6 is the Q3 face (δ=0, QF) plus the Γ(Q3) face (γ=0, GF).
- The only products in W are x and y (PROD).
- Every closed convex cone F with cone{x,y} ⊆ F ⊆ B6 and z in relint F is the face of the domain cone SEP+F [W]. The script's SEPF check covers only the exact part (z = w(1,1,0,0), together with PROD).
- So the Λ₄ faces form a family, the κ-slices δ = κγ (LAM4). Λ₃ slices, the segment and B6 itself also occur.
- By contrast, the face generated by two products sharing a factor is |a⟩⟨a|⊗PSD in every domain cone.
- **Verdict on the first fork, for the domain alone:** no.
- `fw_sign` and `gamma_wrong` perturb only the checked expression.

### C2. SP constraints [W + X]
- (a) In an SP cone, every face is SP with the cone's effects.
- (b) **Close-pair lemma.** If |⟨a|a'⟩|² + |⟨b|b'⟩|² > 1, no domain cone distinguishes x from y. Only C* ⊆ BP is used.
  - Exact instance: 7200/28561 ≤ f(a,b') ≤ 49/625, a contradiction (independently reproduced in review R3).
  - So in an SP domain cone the face at a close pair is never the segment; it contains non-product pure states.
- (c) If C contains Q3, the face contains the Q3 face and C* lies in PSD. (DIST checks only that the relevant overlap is nonzero.) If C lies inside Q3, the face lies inside the Q3 face.
- Whether F ≅ Λ₄ is forced for SP cones: OPEN. So the first fork as posed, for SP cones, is open; only the domain-alone version is answered.

### C3. Does C1's step (D) extend? [W]
Not from face data. (D)'s rigidity comes from the global isomorphism to Q3, and every factor-free pair admits a continuum of Λ₄ choices. A reconstruction would need a Hilbert-ball / Alfsen–Shultz-type route, which uses more than BMU SP [Lit, recalled, not re-read].

### C4. The non-ball face built into the canonical candidate, maxCone [W + X: maxcone_sp_exact.py, 7/7 checks, 7/7 mutations]
**Classification:** NEW for the corpus. The literature on spectrality of the maximal tensor product was not checked.

**(i) Φ₁ and CNOT.**
- Φ₁ = (5/8)Ad_{I⊗D} + (3/8)id with D = diag(1, (3+4i)/5) (BridgeFiniteProtocol.lean:168, :245) preserves BP (PHI).
- CNOT does not [X]: SWAP ∈ BP, and ⟨cd|CNOT·SWAP·CNOT|cd⟩ = −11 at c=(1,−2), d=(1,2). This exact witness is from the review (R5).

**(ii) BP is not SP** [W + X; one instance]. Take w = w(1, 1, (1+i)/3, (1−i)/3), which lies in the relative interior of B6.
- Written lemmas:
  1. Frame members are extreme rays of B6, and the extreme rays of B6 are exactly the P-type and G-type rays. Reason: √(αβ)−|γ|−|δ| is concave and homogeneous, and a boundary point with γδ≠0 splits as t(α,β,γ/t,0) + (1−t)(α,β,0,δ/(1−t)) with t = |γ|/(|γ|+|δ|). The lemma and its exact check on 200 points (review R6) are from the review.
  2. Members of the same type are orthogonal, from E ⪰ 0 and Γ(E) ⪰ 0.
  3. A (2,1) frame gives E3 ≤ P_{S⊥}. Then ⟨a b̄|Γ(E3)|a b̄⟩ = ⟨ab|E3|ab⟩ = 0, and likewise at a'b̄'. So Γ(E3) annihilates S′ and cannot reach 1 on a G-type member.
     - (1,2) follows by Γ, and (2,2) contains (2,1).
     - x and y count as P-type.
     - γ(w) ≠ 0 and δ(w) ≠ 0 exclude the (p,0) and (0,g) types.
  4. That leaves only the two (1,1) decompositions, A1 ∈ {1/3, 2/3} (DEC). A (1,1) frame needs |⟨ψ|cd⟩|² + |⟨φ|c d̄⟩|² ≤ 1, from E1 ⪰ |ψ⟩⟨ψ|/‖ψ‖² and Γ(E2) ⪰ |φ⟩⟨φ|/‖φ‖².
- Exact: both decompositions violate that condition at the product ab, with value 46/39 (VIOL). The review obtains it independently as 53/117 + 85/117 (R2).
- Scope: non-SP is certified at this one state, together with the written lemmas.

**(iii) SEP+F is not SP**, for F ⊆ B6 at the instance pair [W + X] (SEPF).
- For a disjoint close pair, W(z)∩W(z') = 0 (combined rank 16, reproduced in review R4). So that face is the segment, and the close-pair lemma applies.
- Φ₁ was not tested on SEP+F.

**Pressure test (§A.31).** The favourable reading is: SEP, SEP+F, BP, C_W and polytopes all fail SP, so SP may force symmetry. It is rejected, because it is an induction over a handful of constructions.

**Not evidence.** Exploratory floating-point runs found frames for the normalized x+y, under the most permissive effects, whose members lie off the rank-one boundary. `gamma_mut` perturbs only the checked expression.

**Verdict.** SP plus the domain implying Φ₁ preservation: OPEN. Every SP domain cone symmetric: OPEN. BP is eliminated as a candidate.
