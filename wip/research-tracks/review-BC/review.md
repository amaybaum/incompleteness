### 3. Track C

**C1 (symmetric-cone rigidity): CONFIRMED-WITH-SCOPE-FIX [W + Lit].**
- **(A) Irreducibility: correct.** The pure-marginal lemma follows from block positivity: D ⪰ 0 with trace 0 gives D = 0, then B = 0 by polarization. Extreme rays of a direct sum lie in the summands, and the product rays form a connected, spanning set.
- **(B) Classification: correct.** In dimension 16 the only irreducible symmetric cones are Herm₄(ℂ)₊ and Λ₁₆. This should carry a [Lit] tag (JvNW / Koecher–Vinberg).
- **(C) Exclusion of Λ₁₆: correct.** Trace is strictly positive on maxCone∖0, so the base would be an ellipsoid. In a strictly convex body an affine effect attains 0 at a unique point, so frames have at most 2 members. The 4 product projectors are a frame in every domain cone (R3).
- **(D) Rigidity: correct but compressed.** The skew/meeting pattern of P(S_a) and P(S'_b) follows from F_a ∩ F_a' = 0 and F_a ∩ G_b = a ray. Two steps are missing from the write-up:
  1. N_a(|b⟩⟨b|) ∝ |τ(b)⟩⟨τ(b)| for every a, because F_a ∩ G_b is a ray. Faithfulness on null rays then gives N_a = c(a)N.
  2. The scalars factor as c(a)/μ(a) = d(b)/ν(b) = κ, and since products span, L' = κM⊗N.
- **Literature dependencies** that should be tagged [Lit] where used: three skew lines lie on a unique smooth quadric; SL(2,ℂ)→SO⁺(1,3) is surjective; Loewy–Schneider.
- **R4 checks only the easy direction:** that both forms are Lorentz similarities. The direction the proof needs is Loewy–Schneider. Suggested wording: "[Lit: Loewy–Schneider; R4 checks that both forms are Lorentz similarities]".
- **R1 is a single instance.** My G1 check proves Γ∘Ad_{A⊗B}∘Γ = Ad_{A⊗B̄} for symbolic A, B. G2 (Φ₁) and G3 (CNOT|+0⟩ = Φ⁺, with spec Γ(Φ⁺) containing −1/2) also pass.
