## 0. The question as answered

1. **Weakest sufficient test family.** Hold #829's H1, hadd, hsmul, H2 and A fixed. Then validity on T can replace hmax iff cl cone(G_k·T) = PSD(H_k) [W].
   - For rank-one tests this holds iff the token-0 weights q(t) are dense in [0,1]. The other tokens' directions are arbitrary.
   - So no finite family suffices, and no gapped family suffices, even an uncountable one.
   - One dense parameter is needed for every k.
   - (a)⇔(b) does not depend on the proof route; its rank-one density form uses level-set transitivity (#829 argument.md §4).
2. **Availability vs validity.** Record validity of performed tests is free [K]. Validity on the represented cone also needs the chart (J1, i.e. local tomography/product form) and the invariance. VAL does not imply AV, and AV does not imply VAL without the chart.
3. **Premise map.** Nothing in the list is sourced for a physical k-token system. hmax splits into three parts:
   - free record validity;
   - the chart (open);
   - dense token-0 test availability (open, and impossible at any finite stage).

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 [W] Theorem B: the test criterion

Setup: H_k = (C²)^⊗k. G_k is generated by CNOT(i→j) for i<j, and by the third-axis flow (all angles) and the cyclic map on each token j ≥ 1. Every generator has an inverse word (argument.md §2).

- **Lemma 1:** every element of G_k commutes with Π₀ = |0⟩⟨0|⊗I, because CNOT(0→j) is block-diagonal in token 0 and every other generator acts as I on token 0. Hence q(Ux) = q(x). Exact at k=3: GEN2. Negative controls that fail as expected (GEN-NEG): CNOT(1→0), the cyclic map on token 0, a rotation on token 0.
- **Lemma 2:** each weight level set is one orbit, that of the seed s_q = (√q,√(1−q))⊗|0…0⟩, up to phase. This is argument.md §4, written for all k; its k=3 instances replay (REACH3, DEMUX3, MUX-PREPARATION). ORB1 [X] checks an instance of block-diagonal transitivity, not a gate word. Independently, the Lie algebra generated by the gates has exact dimension 30 at k=3 and 126 at k=4 (dim su⊕su on the token-0 blocks), so each level set is one orbit without the multiplexor construction (independent review, LIE check).
- **Theorem B.** For a set T of PSD tests, (a) ⇔ (b):
  - (a) every K satisfying H1, hadd, hsmul, H2, A and K ⊆ T* has K ⊆ PSD;
  - (b) cl cone(G_k·T) = PSD.
- **Proof of (b)⇒(a):** ⟨g·t, ω⟩ = ⟨t, g⁻¹ω⟩ ≥ 0, so K ⊆ (G_k·T)* = PSD* = PSD.
- **Proof of ¬(b)⇒¬(a):** take C = (G_k·T)*. It is closed, convex, G-invariant, contains PSD, and is T-valid. By the bipolar theorem it is strictly larger than PSD.
- **Rank-one case:** if the weights are dense, Lemma 2 gives every rank-one projector as a limit of orbit elements. If they are not dense, then by Milman's theorem the extreme rays of the closed cone have weights in the closure of the weight set, so a rank-one x x† with an excluded weight is missing. ∎

**Corollaries.**
1. #829's upper inclusion consumes exactly the seed tests: token 0 on a half-meridian at polar angle θ with cos²(θ/2) = q, every other token along +z. The prior k=2 R_min family is the instance at k=2.
2. "Weakest" here means satisfying the criterion; there is no minimum family. Example: T_min = {Π_{n(θ)} ⊗ Π_z^{⊗(k−1)} : θ ∈ πℚ ∩ [0,π]}, which is countable. Dense sets have dense proper subsets, so no family is minimal.
3. **Exact k=3 countermodel (CM1–CM5 [X + W]).** H = S⊗I₄, with S = I − (626/625)wwᵀ and w = (3,4)/5.
   - H is not PSD and is outside maxConeK: its value on the product vector (3,4)⊗|00⟩ is −1/25.
   - H is nonnegative after any gate word on every test whose token-0 ray lies in F = {(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,2),(2,1),(1,3),(3,1)}.
   - The same holds for a gapped continuum family with endpoints (5,6) and (2,3), by endpoint certificates plus a written monotonicity argument.
   - cone(G·H) + PSD satisfies every other hypothesis. The lower bound holds for every gate word, since every gate word is token-0 block-diagonal by Lemma 1; CM3 re-checks 12 sampled words. On the ray (1,r) the S-form is negative exactly for r ∈ (97/79, 103/71), strictly inside the gap (6/5, 3/2) (independent review).
4. **Tests on token 0 must be native.** No gate moves token-0 weight, so the dense family of polar angles must be measurable directly on token 0.
5. **Preparation side.** H1 is consumed only at the seed products, but at every q ∈ [0,1] (a continuum), because rank-one matrices cannot be decomposed. If K is closed, which #829 does not assume, dense q suffices. The test side always reduces to a countable family because dual cones are closed.
6. **No finite stage can carry the test premise.** A FiniteStage has finitely many effects (KInfFoundations.lean:63–72), so by Theorem B one stage's tests never force K ⊆ PSD: finite T yields finitely many rank-one weights. This needs a chart from the stage's effects to PSD tests on H_k.

### 2.3 [W + X] Availability vs validity

- **AV ∧ J1 ⇒ VAL on the preparable cone.** Validity then extends to cl cone(T) for free, but to cl cone(G·T) only with H2 ∧ A.
- **VAL does not imply AV:** every product test is valid on an invariant cone, yet only the dense seed family needs to be available, and each finite stage has finitely many tests.
- **AV without J1 does not imply VAL:** in the padded model the product functional sees only the product shadow of the state.
- **ST1 [X]:** the K(Z_F) defect (trace 1) has all 64 rational sharp-product records in [0,1], so it is a valid stage table, yet P₊ gives −1/2. Only invariance excludes it.
- **[W + A]:** every finite stochastic law has a finite reversible realization (Main.md:22). So bare OI's finite-stage content is exactly "valid stage tables". Local tomography, the chart, the availability pattern and the invariance are all additional structure (compare Main.md:20 and :630).

### 2.4 [Lit] hmax as the maximal tensor product

Barnum–Fuchs–Renes–Wilce (quant-ph/0507108, abstract level): influence-free joint assignments for local quantum tests correspond to normalized positive maps, i.e. maxCone.
- So hmax is the weakest composition clause, not a quantum input.
- An OI source for it would go through operational no-signalling, which is required but still to be proved as part of H-Bell (Main.md:394), and through parallel availability and the chart.
- The cited theorem's dimension conditions were not checked.
