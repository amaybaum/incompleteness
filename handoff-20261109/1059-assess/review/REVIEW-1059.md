## Blockers

**B1. R2's "no fixed-basis argument can select p" / "unselectable" is wider than R2 proves.**
- R2 is the class identity $Q_{\rm fb}^p=S$, restricted to realizations whose readout is normalised
  on every visited column.
- R2 needs no column lemma. $S\subseteq D\subseteq Q_{\rm fb}^p$ follows from `D_imp_Qfb`'s
  0/1 weights (EQ:302), with $\lVert0\rVert^p=0$ and $\lVert1\rVert^p=1$. $Q_{\rm fb}^p\subseteq S$
  follows from the normalisation restriction, as in `sum_chain` (EQ:99). (S at EQ:147 is *every*
  finite stochastic law.)
- What R2 rules out is selecting $p$ by *which laws are realizable*.
- It does not rule out a fixed-basis selection from a constraint on *which operations are
  admissible*. The assessment's own R3 (⇒) is such an argument: one admissible non-monomial column,
  read in the fixed basis, forces $p=2$.
- §3's "the constraint that does hold unconditionally … p is … unselectable (R2)" therefore
  contradicts R3 in the same section. It is also conditional, on the normalisation restriction that
  defines $Q_{\rm fb}^p$.
- **Fix.** "The realizable law class does not depend on $p$ ($Q_{\rm fb}^p=S$ for every $p>0$), so
  no criterion on which finite-horizon laws are realizable selects $p$. A selection must come from a
  constraint on admissible operations (R3)."
- Brief claim 1 inherits this wording.

**B2. H-RF$_p$ must be stated so that R3 (⇒) is true, and its status must use the permitted
labels.**
- **(i) Renormalisation.** "A record's probability is $|{\rm amp}|^p$, normalised" also reads as
  $|{\rm amp}|^p/\sum|{\rm amp}|^p$. Under that reading R3 (⇒) is false. On the non-monomial
  column $(1/3,2/3,2/3)$, the renormalised readout is a law for every $p$, and the laws differ:
  $(1/5,2/5,2/5)$, $(1/9,4/9,4/9)$, $(1/17,8/17,8/17)$ (rv C3). R2's own definition uses
  normalisation as a restriction, not a renormalisation.
  - *Fix:* "the unrenormalised values $|{\rm amp}_j|^p$ are the outcome law, so
    $\sum_j|{\rm amp}_j|^p=1$ is required on every read".
- **(ii) Preparations in (⇐).** "Coherent preparations included" holds only if every preparation is
  $p$-normalised, or is generated by the admissible operations from a basis state. A supplied
  2-normalised preparation $(1/\sqrt2,1/\sqrt2)$ has $\sum|a|^p=2^{1-p/2}\neq1$ for $p\neq2$
  (rv C4). It is a non-monomial preparation column in disguise.
  - *Fix:* state the preparation class.
- **(iii) Status.** R3's column (ii) and §4.5 say H-LIN and H-RF$_p$ "are supplied (BORN-SRC-1
  review 9(i))". Review 9(i) says the opposite of what a reader takes "supplied" to mean in a
  column-(ii) cell: the readout-as-outcome-law premise is the Born *form*, which "OI supplies rather
  than sourcing it … The hypothesis is open, not weaker than what is open".
  - §4.5's prerequisite column then lists FI-1 alone for (a-pow), "under H-LIN ∧ H-RF$_p$, which
    are supplied". That reads as a reduction of (a-pow) to FI-1. It is the R1 overclaim BORN-SRC-1

  - *Fix:* label both hypotheses in the Code Review Rules' vocabulary: H-RF$_p$ is *posited* (the
    Born form, Main:580), open as a sourcing question. Keep it in the prerequisites of (a-pow) in
