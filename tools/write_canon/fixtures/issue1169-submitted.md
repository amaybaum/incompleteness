## Purpose

Adopt a **construct-then-reduce** strategy toward OI ↔ finite operational quantum mechanics:

1. Explicitly **augment OI by a sufficient set of physical/operational conditions**.
2. Prove, within precisely stated finite-dimensional model classes and a shared operational interface, that **extended OI and the selected QM target are operationally equivalent in both directions**, rather than merely compatible or able to mimic one example.
3. Then audit the added conditions individually: can they be **derived from existing native OI**, weakened, replaced with physical couplings, or shown independent/necessary by countermodels?
4. Retain a precise conditional theorem even if some conditions cannot be sourced; never relabel conditional equivalence as emergence from bare OI.

**Scientific motive:** #1143's reviewed S8-A assessment reports that the presently specified native interface does not source the non-monomial coherent operation/carrier needed by its alternating-W test. This is a **scoped sourcing gap**, not a universal impossibility theorem. We want a formally sufficient destination against which to investigate the missing native mechanisms.

## Existing theorem and required scientific advance

The repository **already has** a Lean-verified characterization of exact finite operational quantum mechanics in `OIBridge/GeneralCarrier.lean` (`exactAll_iff_physical_general`, `main_result`). Within an **assumed** finite-dimensional complex-matrix, linear-operation, tensor-product and quantum-instrument setting, exact operational QM is equivalent to these five conditions:

1. **Valid probabilities** — valid normalized outcome families.
2. **Trivial-ancilla consistency** — adjoining a one-state helper changes nothing.
3. **Inert spectators** — an untouched independent bystander does not change an operation.
4. **Full reversible control** — every reversible quantum operation is available.
5. **Iterated composition** — fresh mixed helpers, interventions and discarding can be composed consistently across larger systems.

The theorem proves both directions **within that mathematical setting**. Its independence/countermodel results (`RankGapTheory.five_way_minimality` and related results) establish that the conditions do not follow from the others on the stated model class; bare finite OI does **not** imply the full operational-QM completion (`oi_alone_not_qm`). The existing characterization is a **baseline to reuse, not a theorem to reprove**. It does **not** derive the assumed quantum state space, tensor structure, Born probabilities or physical availability of the five conditions from OI alone.

**The novel objective of #1169** is to find physically meaningful, independently formulated OI extension conditions and one coherent underlying dynamics that *generate or physically source* the necessary quantum structure, preparations, selectable interventions, instruments/readouts and compositions. Reuse the proved characterization as a benchmark, identify any actual new equivalence obligation outside its assumed class, and then minimize/source the additional conditions individually. In particular, a proof that assumes Hilbert spaces, arbitrary unitaries, quantum instruments or the Born rule from the outset is a **conditional reconstruction/embedding**, not a physical derivation of them from OI.

The Gate B names **Coh-1, Dyn-1, Prep-1, Meas-1 and Comp-1 are candidate research categories**, not the five conditions of `GeneralCarrier`, not restatements of them, and not established native OI properties. Gate A must explicitly map each candidate against the previously proved five-condition theorem and distinguish (a) existing mathematics, (b) additional physical assumptions, (c) actual physical sourcing, and (d) new proof obligations.

**Success criterion:** a genuinely new, noncircular conditional physical reconstruction with a common operational OI realization and a documented assumption-minimality/source audit, or a precise obstruction identifying the irreducible missing physical inputs. Do not treat reproving the preexisting abstract five-condition characterization as progress on this objective.

## Proposed gates (planning only)

### Gate A — Define the endpoints and the hypothesis ledger
- Specify the exact finite operational QM target: preparations, quantum instruments/measurement statistics, selectable interventions, sequential composition, persistence/readback, and (where claimed) multi-system composition.
- Define the observer-relative native OI model class, the extension interface, allowed experimental controls, access/record maps, and the equivalence notion. Separate state-level, probability-level, and full operational equivalence.
- Maintain a numbered ledger of **native OI results**, **new physical posits**, **mathematical representations**, and **imported QM facts**. Each clause must be falsifiable or independently meaningful; disallow a vacuous axiom of the form "OI reproduces all QM".
- Preserve current scoped negative findings and requirements on independence of settings, genuine system-setting coupling, ready states, accessible records and memory.

### Gate B — Candidate sufficient extension (to propose, not assume approved)
Test the need for explicitly sourced or posited conditions covering:
- **Coh-1:** a coherent operational carrier with accessible phase-sensitive distinctions (not merely a classical probability distribution);
- **Dyn-1:** controllable non-monomial transformations and composition compatible with interference, including the alternating-W witness;
- **Prep-1:** sufficiently rich, reproducible native preparations and setting independence;
- **Meas-1:** a native instrument/readout, stable public records, and **Born frequencies derived from the stated extension**, not silently inserted or cited as if native to bare OI;
- **Comp-1:** sequential and, if claimed, composite-system/tensor structure, consistent statistics and repeatability/access constraints.

These are *candidate requirement names*, not established native OI properties. Explicitly explore whether a smaller or differently formulated axiom set suffices. If an extension posits Hilbert space, unitaries, or the Born rule outright, label the result a reconstruction/embedding with limited explanatory value and do not count those clauses as derived.

### Gate C — Conditional equivalence proof
- State a theorem with complete assumptions, model scope and **both directions** of operational correspondence.
- Prove soundness (each extended-OI realization predicts the targeted QM operational law) and completeness (the target QM operations in scope admit extended-OI realizations, rather than isolated outcome tables).
- Include fixed control cases, an interference example, an explicit native record/measurement case, and a multi-step example. Distinguish theorem proof from illustrations.
- Independently review definitions, noncircularity, proof objects, tests and any Lean claims. Partial or conditional result should remain so labelled.

### Gate D — Minimize and physically source the extras
- For **each** added condition: try to derive it from existing OI; construct a countermodel if it is not implied; weaken/remove it and rerun the theorem.
- Report which assumptions are necessary (within the stated class), sufficient but redundant, independent, or **UNSOURCED**.
- Prioritize a physical OI subsystem interaction that generates the missing carrier/non-monomial operation; do not declare a Hadamard gate or coherent unitary native simply because QM admits one.
- If full reduction fails, provide a minimal conditional equivalence plus a finite list of irreducible physical inputs, not an unsupported claim that QM emerges from OI alone.
