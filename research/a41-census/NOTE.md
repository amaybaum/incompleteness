## Domain and group action

- **SIG** = F₄(z) ⊗ F₄(w), with z = (3+4i)/5, w = (5+12i)/13, index (a,b) ↦ 4a+b. It is scaled by 4 so that
  entries are unimodular i^p z^q w^r. It is taken verbatim from the landed act-38 probe head
  (`verification/lean/dita_local_escape_probe.py`, loaded up to its section 1 by `lib41.py`).
- **Straight line.** E is a straight line when SIG ∘ u^E is complex Hadamard for every unit u. As a
  Laurent-polynomial identity: for every row pair (i, i′), every level set of E_i − E_i′ has vanishing pair sum
  Σ_k SIG_ik conj(SIG_i′k).
- **D** = {E ∈ {0,1}^{16×16} : row 0 = 0, E a straight line}. This is act 38's domain. For {0,1} rows the
  condition reads: the masks E_i ∧ ¬E_i′ and ¬E_i ∧ E_i′ both have vanishing pair sums. The full sum vanishes by
  orthogonality.
  - The vanishing tables are exact integer subset sums of the Gaussian integers 65·SIG_ik·conj(SIG_i′k).
  - The naive per-monomial balance lemma is never used.
- **Γ.** It is generated by three kinds of element:
  - gauge: E ↦ E + r·1ᵀ + 1·cᵀ, for integer vectors r and c;
  - g = (perm, s) ∈ Stab, the 1024 index permutations of act 36's stabilizer of SIG in G_ext, including
    transposition. It acts by (g·E)[perm(t)] = s·E[t] on flattened positions, with s = −1 on the conjugating
    elements;
  - the sign E ↦ −E (u ↦ u⁻¹).

  Stab × {±1} has 2048 distinct elements, is closed under composition and is generated by 6 of them (C7).
- **Canonical form.** Take the lexicographic minimum, row-major over all 256 entries, of the gauge normal forms
  gn(ε·g·E), where gn(E)_ij = E_ij − E_i0 − E_0j + E_00, over the 2048 pairs (g, ε). This is a function of the
  orbit, so it is idempotent by construction. That is checked for all 664 888 orbits, and against an independent
  implementation on 3992 orbits (C7). For the witness it reproduces the discovery note's normal form: values
  −2..1, support 156.
- **Orbit classification** (fixed in PREREG):
  - NON-DIŢĂ: the canonical representative lies in no relaxed subspace. This is an orbit invariant (C8b and the
    built-in counters).
  - DIŢĂ: some D-solution of the orbit lies in a strict subspace.
  - RELAXED-ONLY: the remaining case.

  Strict membership is not gauge invariant. A gauge-trivial matrix is relaxed in all 18 structures and strict in
  none (checked). The strict class is therefore defined through the orbit's D-solutions.

## Method (`census41.c`, streamed, bounded memory)

1. **Tables** (`export41.py` → `data41.bin`, sha256 `5399ef64…`): 120 exact vanishing tables, the 2048 group
   elements, and the membership equations.
   - The complete-matching equations for 20 structures come from `export41c.py` → `data41c.bin`.
   - Admissible rows per index are 4899 / 12869 / 1327, excluding the all-ones row. Pair-compatibility bitsets
     take 745 MB.
2. **DFS** over rows 1..15 with bitset forward checking and MRV (lowest index on ties), in act 38's order.
   - All-ones rows are excluded, so each gauge class of D is visited exactly once. Its D-solutions are its 2^z
     choices on its z constant rows.
   - Top-level branches are the 1327 admissible masks of row 5.
   - The state is checkpointed to disk every 10 min, so the run is resumable.
3. **Symmetry reduction.** H_dom is the 64 elements of Stab × {±1} that do not transpose and fix row 0. It maps
   the set of D's gauge classes to itself.
   - Each class N is kept only if it is lexicographically minimal among its H_dom images, which gives 1 298 084
     representatives.
   - For each representative the distinct H_dom images are enumerated, together with the 2^z constant-row
     choices of each image. Together these are exactly the D-solutions of that H_dom orbit. Each is tested for
     strict membership, and the minimum D-support is recorded.
   - The representative is then canonicalized under all 2048 elements, with early-exit comparison, and merged
     into an open-addressing table keyed by the packed canonical form (225 entries × 3 bits).
4. **Memory** is bounded by the number of orbits, not solutions: 745 MB of tables plus a 2²¹ × 184 B hash table,
   about 1.2 GB. The checkpoint file is 117 MB. Run 1 (sorted-matching membership) took 1572 s; run 2 (complete
   membership) took 2542 s, single core, with container load 7–10.
5. **Per new orbit**, the run also records:
   - the 2048 gauge-normal images: the number of distinct ones and their minimum number of nonzeros;
   - relaxed membership of the canonical representative;
   - the canonical form of the canonical form and of a random image, which must both return the canonical form.

## Finding F1 (gem-finding, NEW): the sorted label matching hides Diţă structures

A Diţă form H[(a,b),(c,d)] = X[a][c]·Y_c[b][d] fixes, inside each row group, which row carries which label a,
because X is shared across groups. The act-36/37 structure search (`structures`, `dita_orientations`) and act 38's
linear membership test label every group in sorted order. Each rank-one condition involves one group and group 0,
so the valid matchings factorize per group; `matchlib.py` enumerates them exhaustively with exact monomial
arithmetic, cross-checked in Gaussian rationals. Every census structure has several valid matchings at SIG:
k1, k3 and k4 have 8, k2 has 64, e1 and e2 have 4, and t1, t2 and t3 have 128.

Consequences, each verified exactly:

- **SIG has a fifth 4×4 Diţă structure in each form**, reported by the act-36/38 search only as a
  "candidate, not exact". Call it k5.
  - It has column blocks (0,6,8,14),(1,7,9,15),(2,4,10,12),(3,5,11,13) and row groups
    (0,2,9,11),(1,3,8,10),(4,6,13,15),(5,7,12,14). These are k3's index sets with the roles exchanged.
  - Group 1 needs a non-identity matching.
  - It has an exact reconstruction SIG = X_{ac}·Y_c[b][d] with X and every Y_c unitary (`fifth.py`,
    `fifth.json`).
  - The exact structure count of SIG by shape and form is therefore (5, 5, 2, 2, 3, 3), not (4, 4, 2, 2, 3, 3):
    **20 structures in 10 classes** modulo the stabilizer, not 18 in 9 (`matching_audit.py`, `ctrl_c8b.py`).
- **H(−1) for the act-38 witness admits a 4×4 Diţă structure in each form**, besides the 2×8 one act 38 lists.
  - It has an exact reconstruction with unitary factors (`minus_one.py`).
  - This contradicts act 38's check "at u = −1 … nothing of the other shapes".
- **What survives.** Act 38's exceptional set {1, −1} is unchanged when all matchings are tested at all 40
  candidate points (`exceptional_audit.py`). The witness has no proportionality candidate at all at generic u, so
  its generic non-Diţă claim is unaffected. Act 37's W-arc generic candidates are not exact under any matching,
  so its generic claim is unaffected.
- **Not checked here:** act 37's W-arc exceptional-point searches, and the act-39/40 torus probes, which may use the
  same search. For this census the finding changes no orbit's class. It changes 3 456 per-solution strict
  verdicts, and k5 adds no relaxed membership beyond the 18.

This is an assumption-watch item for every use of `structures` / `dita_orientations` / `struct_eqs`: "exact" there
means exact under the sorted matching only. It needs propagating by the owner's process, not by this thread.

## What a native round would need

**Theorem-shaped statement.** Let D be as above. Then |D| = 171 552 256. D meets exactly 664 888 orbits of Γ.
Exactly 84 328 of those orbits, containing 18 959 360 elements of D, consist of exponent matrices E for which
SIG ∘ u^E, as a function of a free unit u, is for no Diţă structure of SIG, under any label matching,
identically diagonally equivalent to a Diţă form. Each of the other 580 560 orbits contains an element of D that
lies strictly in a Diţă structure of SIG. SIG has exactly 20 Diţă structures (10 index structures in each of the
two forms, 10 classes modulo the stabilizer).

Layers:

- **Kernel-shaped, small:**
  - the k5 reconstruction identity at SIG and the 4×4 reconstruction at H(−1), both finite Gaussian-rational
    identities like the act-36/37 census reconstructions;
  - the witness's realizability and exclusion lemmas (as in act 38).
- **Probe layer (exact computation):**
  - the enumeration, the orbit count and the classification. They are exhaustive over 1.7×10⁸ solutions and not
    kernel-feasible as stated. A certificate of 664 888 canonical representatives, each re-verifiable as a
    straight line, would still leave completeness to the search;
  - the census of Diţă structures of SIG under all matchings;
  - the stabilizer permuting the 20 structures.
- **Before freezing:**
  - re-preregister C1(d) in two parts: minimum over D-solutions 48, and minimum over gauge-normal images 44;
  - replace C8 with C8b;
  - pin `data41.bin` and `data41c.bin` hashes and the expected outputs;
  - add a second independent orbit-level implementation, for instance a numpy/Python orbit canonicalization over
    a streamed dump of the H_dom representatives, to replay-match the table. The present cross-checks are
    independent at the count level (C5, C6d) and at the canonical-form level on samples (C7), not for the full
    table;
  - fix the checkpoint/replay rule: the second run already replay-matched the first bit for bit on every
    enumeration field;
  - budget a CI run of about 30–45 min single core and about 1.2 GB RAM.
- **Settle F1's propagation first.** A native round that asserts the 18-structure census, or act 38's u = −1
  statement, would be asserting a count that F1 shows incomplete.
