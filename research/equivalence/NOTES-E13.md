**Self-duality, as used here.** For a body Ω ⊂ ℝⁿ (n = chart dimension), its state cone is
`C(Ω) = {(T, T·y) : T ≥ 0, y ∈ Ω} ⊂ ℝ × ℝⁿ`. `C(Ω)` is *self-dual* if there is an inner product `M` (a positive
definite matrix) with `C = C^{*M} := {w : ⟨z, w⟩_M ≥ 0 for every z ∈ C}` — a linear isomorphism of `C` onto its
standard dual, `C^{*std} = M·C`, by a positive definite `M`.

  scalars is non-compact.* Proof sketch: if `M₁`, `M₂` both self-dualize `C` then `h = M₁⁻¹M₂` is a positive
