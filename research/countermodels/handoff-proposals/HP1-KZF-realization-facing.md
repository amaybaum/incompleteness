## 3. What this implies for the realization question (proposal)

1. **A realization in which local single-token reversible operations act on the pair as composite operations is
   excluded** — not by an item at L, but by KZ5–KZ7: the only local reversible operations compatible with `K(Z_F)` form a
   finite group (KZ6); no rotation flow of either token survives (KZ7). In particular a realization with the composition
   clause of Main.md:552 (local instruments acting as `I_a ⊗ I_b`) applied to the native drive or to any one-parameter local
   rotation cannot realize `K(Z_F)`; stage 6 thread R6 (REASSESSMENT §6) recorded the assumption-watch marker, and KZ5–KZ7 make it a statement about this
   cone. The proposal to `research/bridge`: test whether its candidate realization theorem contains such a composition
   clause for continuous local operations; if it does, `K(Z_F)` is not in its range, and the clause is (b) itself (the
   disguise test of T6 §5).
2. **What a realization would have to provide instead:** a pair whose reversible dynamics is generated inside `T³_F`
   (Bell-diagonal phases, KZ5, KZ8) together with the finite local group of KZ6; product states and their Ĝ-images as the
   pure states (KZ1); the four defects as additional extreme states with `tr ρ² = 1` but one negative "eigenvalue" (KZ3),
   steering maximally (KZ4); and the exclusion of the four Bell-type caps (KZ2).
3. **Discriminating tests a hidden-level model could be checked against** (each a computable property of the cone):
   the facial invariant (KZ9), the Bell-diagonal slice (KZ10), the sum rules (KZ3), the 384-element local group (KZ6).
