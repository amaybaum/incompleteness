# NOTES-C3 — extreme-ray transitivity T

Node C3 of `README.md`. T (I3.161, PT-record): the group of reversible pair transformations preserving `K` acts
transitively on the normalized extreme rays of `K`. Read here, as at stage 4 (Y6), with `Aut(K)` the linear
automorphism group. Invariant (Y6 [A]): `c(x) = dim span{y ∈ K : ipW(x, y) = 0}`; for self-dual `K` and `g ∈ Aut(K)`,
`gᵀ ∈ Aut(K)` and `c(gx) = c(x)`. Script: `experiments/c3_transitivity.py` (run 1: 7/7, `VERDICT C3-INGREDIENTS-EXACT`;
replay identical). Evidence levels as in NOTES-C1.

## Verdict

**EXCLUDES-ALL: OPEN.** Proved here: T excludes every exotic cone with finitely many non-PSD extreme rays (under a
mild interior hypothesis, Lemma 3), in particular every Theorem-S surgery cone — K(E0), K(e_c), K_F2, K(Z_F) and all
finite orthogonal defect cones. The undecided case is precisely the exotic cones whose non-PSD extreme rays form a
continuum, each touching the pure members of `K` along a set with empty interior in its null quadric; every exotic cone
invariant under a torus with G16 (stage 4 S2) or under the gate flow κ at level (ii) is of this type (Z [A]; NOTES-C5),
and none of them is exhibited. This is the wall. No candidate principle is proposed (the favourable branch is not
established).

## 1. What every K with H1–H3 satisfies at P00 (Lemma 1) [W + X T2 + A Y6]

`F_{P00} := K ∩ P00^⊥ ⊆ PSD(|00⟩^⊥)`, and it spans `Herm(|00⟩^⊥)`; so `c(P00) = 9`.
- Support: for `y ∈ K ⊆ maxCone ∩ cnot maxCone` with `ipW(y, P00) = 0`, the first-order conditions of block positivity at
  `|00⟩`, applied to `y` and to `cnot y` (`cnot` fixes `|00⟩` and sends `|11⟩` to `|10⟩`), give `pauliW(y)|00⟩ = 0` ([A] Y6).
- Positivity (new here): with `pauliW(y) = 0 ⊕ Y3`, block positivity reads `w†Y3w ≥ 0` for `w = (a0b1, a1b0, a1b1)`
  [X T2, symbolic]; every `w = (p, q, r)` with `r ≠ 0` arises (`a1 = 1`, `b1 = r`, `b0 = q`, `a0 = p/r`), so `Y3 ⪰ 0`.
- Span: `K_gen ∩ P00^⊥` spans `Herm(|00⟩^⊥)` ([A] Y6).
Consequence under T: every extreme ray `x = g P00` has `c(x) = 9`, and its exposed face `F_x = g^{−T}F_{P00}` is linearly
isomorphic to a cone of PSD 3×3 matrices spanning `Herm(3)`.

## 2. Surgery-type defects (Lemma 2) [W + X T3]

Let `x` be a non-PSD extreme ray of `K` (H1–H3) and suppose the pure members of `K` on the null quadric
`N_x = {v : v†pauliW(x)v = 0}` contain a nonempty relatively open subset of `N_x`. Then `c(x) = 15`, so T fails.
Proof. `pauliW(x)` is indefinite of rank ≥ 2 (a negative eigenvalue since `x ∉ Q3`; a positive one since `tr > 0`); as a
real quadratic form on `ℂ⁴ = ℝ⁸` it has rank ≥ 4 and is indefinite, so its null cone is an irreducible real quadric with
smooth real points, and a relatively open subset is Zariski dense in it. A Hermitian `Y` with `v†Yv = 0` on that open set
therefore vanishes on the whole quadric and is a multiple of `pauliW(x)`. Hence the pure members span the hyperplane
`x^⊥` (dimension 15), all of them lie in `K ∩ x^⊥`, and `c(x) = 15 ≠ 9 = c(P00)`. ∎
[X T3]: for each defect of K(E0), K(e_{3/4}), K_F2 and K(Z_F) a pure member of `K` lies on its null quadric with every other
defect constraint strict — an open neighbourhood on the quadric stays in `K`.

## 3. Finitely many non-PSD extreme rays (Lemma 3) [W]

Let `K` (H1–H3, exotic) have finitely many non-PSD extreme rays `x₁ … x_n`, and let `S_K = {v : P_v ∈ K}` have nonempty
interior in `ℂP³`. Then T fails. Proof. `S_K = ⋂ᵢ {v†pauliW(xᵢ)v ≥ 0}` (PSD members of `K` impose no constraint on pure
states; `K = K*` is generated by its extreme rays). `S_K ≠ ℂP³` (else `K ⊇ Q3`, so `K = Q3`), so its boundary in the
connected 6-manifold `ℂP³` is nonempty and has topological dimension 5. Boundary points where two constraints are active,
or where `N_{xᵢ}` is singular (`v ∈ ker pauliW(xᵢ)`), lie in a set of dimension ≤ 4; so some boundary point `p` has exactly
one active constraint `i` with `N_{xᵢ}` smooth at `p`, and near `p`, `S_K` coincides with `{v†pauliW(xᵢ)v ≥ 0}`: a relatively
open piece of `N_{xᵢ}` lies in `S_K`. Lemma 2 gives `c(xᵢ) = 15`. ∎
Corollary. Every Theorem-S surgery cone `K(Z)` (finite, pairwise orthogonal defects with the SD certificates; open caps
disjoint) fails T; for these the interior hypothesis holds (the products' neighbourhoods: `S_K ⊇` the complement of the
open caps).

## 4. The compact case (Lemma 4) [W + X T1, T4]

If `Aut(K) = ℝ₊ × G_o` with `G_o ⊆ O(ipW)` compact and T holds, then `G_o` fixes `E00` and every normalized extreme ray
`x` (`x₀₀ = 1`, i.e. `tr pauliW(x) = 1`) satisfies `|x|² = 4`, i.e. `tr pauliW(x)² = 1` (pure-like).
Proof. Average an interior point over `G_o` to a fixed `e`; all pure products lie in one `G_o`-orbit of rays, so after
normalizing by `e` they have equal pairing with `e`; the 16 functions `{1, aᵢ, b_j, aᵢb_j}` are independent on `S²×S²`
[X T1], so `e ∝ E00`; `G_o` preserves `ipW` and `E00`, and `|prodState(a,b)|² = 4` on the spheres [X T1]. ∎
So a T-transitive exotic cone with compact automorphism group is a self-polar orbitope (`conv E = −E°` in the affine
hyperplane `x₀₀ = 1`) of a compact group `G_o ⊄ 𝒜` on the pure-state sphere `|x_rest|² = 3`, containing the Segre set of
products and its `cnot` image. [X T4]: K(Z_F)'s normalized defects lie on that sphere (spectrum `(−1/2, 1/2, 1/2, 1/2)`),
K(E0)'s and K(e_c)'s do not (`3`, `1 + 2c²`).

## 6. Disguise test and provenance (for the record)

T is a property of the pair cone alone — its own reversible symmetries are transitive on its extreme rays — and is not
stated through a single-token action, so it does not restate (b), IE1 or K2's local-action clause; it holds for Q3
(retention: `PU(4) ⋊ ℤ₂` is transitive on pure states). Sources: none at L (I3.161 is a PT-record candidate); its
single-system analogue is K∞-Trans (`BoundaryTransitive`, OPEN at L); its OI⁺ counterpart, reversible richness, is a
do-not-assume item (T6 §5). Were EXCLUDES-ALL proved, T would enter a governed round as a new pair-level posit,
CONDITIONAL, not derived; as it stands, no proposal is made.
