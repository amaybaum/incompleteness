## Outcome: SUFFICIENT, with one premise added and two scope conditions made explicit

The three named hypotheses do not suffice exactly as stated. A **fourth premise is required**: every member of G
is an automorphism of the body (`PreservesBody`). With it, the theorem holds in this form (draft
`seedOrbit_ball3_eq`, Draft:348):

    PreservesBody ball3 G  ∧  SharpSeed ball3 r  (P1)  ∧  BoundaryTransitive ball3 G  (K∞-R)
      ⟹  seedOrbit G r = directionalFamily,

where:
- `seedOrbit G r = {r ∘ g⁻¹ : g ∈ G}`;
- `directionalFamily = {ballEffect b : b 0² + b 1² + b 2² = 1}`, with `ballEffect` at KF:1026.

V4′ is **not a premise of the equality**. It adds only `seedOrbit G r ⊆ avail` (`seedOrbit_subset`). From
there:
- every `ballEffect b` with `∑ j, b j ^ 2 = 1` is available (`ballEffect_mem_avail`, Draft:369);
- (SEC) and `KInf1 ball3 avail` follow (Draft:382 and 389);
- the bridge into `NativeGateBall.lorentz_of_effects` at `p = 3` follows (Draft:406 and 422).

## The omitted-premise list (the main deliverable)

**O1. G-AUT: each g and g⁻¹ map Ω into Ω. Necessary and not contained in "boundary transitivity".**
- Countermodel (exact F3): take G = fullAut3 ∪ {½·id}.
  - It is boundary-transitive, every member maps the ball into the ball, and the seed (1+z)/2 is sharp.
  - But the transport along ½·id is v ↦ 1/2 + z, which takes the value 3/2 at e_z. That is not an effect, so
    seedOrbit ≠ directionalFamily.
  - With G = all affine automorphisms of ℝ³, G-AUT fails in both directions.
- For KF's own drive vocabulary, G-AUT is *supplied* on the generators: `preservesBody_drive` (Draft:161) uses
  `flow_preserves`, `flow (−t) = (flow t)⁻¹`, `J_preserves` and `J_symm_preserves`.
- So if K∞-R is sourced as "G generated by an `ElementaryDrivability`", O1 is discharged for the generators.
  Closure under words is a one-line induction (written; not drafted).

**O2. P1 must carry the zero value. "Certain + proper" is not enough.**
- Countermodel N2 (draft, Draft:626–688; exact F2): r = 3/4 + z/4.
  - r is an effect, certain at e_z and proper. Its minimum on the ball is 1/2.
  - For **every** G satisfying G-AUT, the transitive fullAut3 included, its orbit is disjoint from the
    directional family (`unsharp_seedOrbit_disjoint`, `not_seedOrbit_unsharp_eq`).
  - Yet (SEC) still holds (`supportingEffectComplete_unsharp`).
- The Lorentz consumer also fails (exact F2c, F2d). Take (x0, v) = (1, (0,0,2)).
  - It pairs nonnegatively with every orbit member: the minimum is 1/4 > 0.
  - But |v|² = 4 > x0² = 1.
  - The directional family rejects the same vector with −1/2.
- So (SEC) needs only a proper certain seed, while the Lorentz input needs the zero value.

**O3. BODY / coordinatization: the formula (1 + b·x)/2 is bound to `ball3` in the seed's coordinates.**
- Countermodel N6 (exact F6, F6b): take Ω_E = {x²/4 + y² + z² ≤ 1} and G = A·SO(3)·A⁻¹ with A = diag(2,1,1).
  - G is transitive and preserves Ω_E, and the seed (1 + x/2)/2 is sharp.
  - But the orbit is {(1 + b·A⁻¹x)/2}, and (1+x)/2 = 3/2 at (2,0,0) ∈ Ω_E.
- The general-body theorem (SEC with COVER, Draft:174) needs no coordinates. Only the identification with the
  RHS of `lorentz_of_effects` does.
- Related trap: KF's Lemma B (`eq_closedBall_of_frontier_subset_sphere`, KF:770) concludes `Metric.closedBall`.
  On `Fin 3 → ℝ` that is the sup-norm cube, not `ball3`. Any derivation of BODY from Lemma B must go through
  `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)`.

**O4. Index set: Σ b_j² = 1, not `Metric.sphere 0 1`.**
- On `Fin 3 → ℝ` the norm is the sup norm.
- Countermodel N4 (draft `cubeCorner_mem_sphere` and `not_isEffectOn_ballEffect_cubeCorner`, Draft:691/701;
  exact F4): b = (1,1,1) lies on that sphere, yet `ballEffect b` takes 6/5 at (3/5, 4/5, 0) ∈ ball3.
- The draft indexes by the quadratic form, which is exactly the `(∑ j, b j ^ 2) = 1` of NGB:106.

**O5. K∞-R must be literal transitivity, or the cover from the seed point. Weaker forms fail.**
- Countermodel N1 (draft, Draft:567–621; exact F1): the flow of `ball3Drive` alone, `Set.range rot3`.
  - It satisfies G-AUT, and the seed is sharp.
  - The orbit is {ballEffect e_z} (`seedOrbit_flow`), so the conclusion fails.
  - Kernel-form corollary: `not_boundaryTransitive_flow`. K∞-R is independent of P1 + G-AUT.
- Countermodel N5 (exact F5): ⟨cyc3, N_z⟩ uses KF's own J and NOT, has order 12, and moves the axis.
  - The orbit of e_z has 6 points, so it gives 6 directional effects, a proper subset.
  - An axis-moving gate without the continuous flow is insufficient.
- A countable dense G gives a dense proper subset. `lorentz_of_effects` quantifies over *every* unit b, so that
  case needs an extra limit/continuity step (D3) that is not among the hypotheses.

**Not needed. These questions were raised in the charter and are answered negatively.**
- The seed's axis being a boundary point is automatic (L3; `sharpSeed_certain_isBoundaryState`).
- The normalization (1 + b·x)/2 is forced by sharpness on `ball3`: `ball3_sharp_eq`, Draft:278, a sqrt-free
  proof, with certificates in exact C1–C9.
- G does not need to act linearly or centrally, and does not need to be a group.
- Complement closure is automatic, since 1 − ballEffect b = ballEffect(−b).

**Convention.** V4′ uses `r ∘ g⁻¹` and K∞-R uses the push-forward `g x = y`. If V4′ were stated with `r ∘ g`
for a G not closed under inverses, COVER would have to be stated for g⁻¹.

## What `lorentz_of_effects` consumes (NGB:105), checked against the RHS

- Hypothesis shape: `∀ b : Fin p → ℝ, (∑ j, b j ^ 2) = 1 → 0 ≤ x0 + ∑ j, b j * v j`, with `1 ≤ p`.
- With `conePair e x0 v := x0 · e 0 + (e v − e 0)` (= x0·e(0) + e.linear v), we have
  `conePair (ballEffect b) x0 v = (x0 + b·v)/2` (exact A3; draft `conePair_ballEffect`).
- The RHS family therefore gives, up to the positive factor 1/2, exactly the functionals (1, b) on the full
  quadratic sphere. `lorentz_of_seedOrbit` instantiates `p = 3` and converts with `Fin.sum_univ_three`.
- NB-1 assumes the full self-dual cone. The orbit delivers its extremal rays and nothing else (⊆ direction).
