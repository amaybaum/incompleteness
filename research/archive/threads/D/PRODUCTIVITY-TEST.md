# Thread D — productivity test (fixed before any branch was walked, §A.31)

A branch is a **gem** iff it yields a fact strictly stronger than the obvious restatement
"route R gives a ball in the literature" AND at least one of:
  (a) it decides, by exact computation or written proof, whether a named premise of the route
      excludes or admits one of {square gbit, torus orbitope, Stiefel orbitope, 3-ball,
      Caratheodory orbitope} or a new discriminating body, in a way the K-inf note (§11, §14) did not;
  (b) it shows which half of a compound premise is load-bearing (a discriminating countermodel);
  (c) it exposes a hidden assumption: an imported Hilbert-space / full-effects premise, or a
      corpus sourcing claim whose object is mistyped.
Otherwise: non-gem (coherence relabelling), record-only.

Favourable branches (a route "excludes all three") get a countercontrol: a body outside the three
that the route must also be run against.
