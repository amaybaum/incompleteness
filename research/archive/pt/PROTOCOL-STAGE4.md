## The question Q-EX
Let H1–H3 be as in `PROTOCOL-STAGE3.md` (products in `K`; `cnot K ⊆ K`, at level (ii) invariance under the even
class `G16`; `K = dualW K`). Stage 3 established EXOTIC: the surgery cones `K(E0)`, `K(Z_F)`, `K({F, cnot F})`,
`K(e_c)` satisfy H1–H3 and differ from `Q3` (`pt/INTEGRATION-NOTE-STAGE3.md`, audited). For a candidate additional
condition `P` on `K`, decide:
- **(R) retention:** `Q3` satisfies `P` (exact);
- **(E) exclusion:** every closed `K` satisfying H1–H3 and `P` equals `Q3` [W with exact ingredients], or at least
  every stage-3 countermodel fails `P` (exact) — stated separately as EXCLUDES-ALL / EXCLUDES-KNOWN;
- **(N) necessity:** for every candidate `P′` strictly weaker than `P` in the fixed lattice below, an exact `K ≠ Q3`
  satisfying H1–H3 and `P′`, or an existence argument (EBF) with the seed exhibited exactly, or UNRESOLVED with the
  residual named;
- **(O) observer-nativity:** what `P` says about the observer architecture (local agency on one or both tokens,
  pair-level reversible richness / transitivity, frame covariance), whether the corpus states it (file:line at L)
  and under which label (certified / added principle / flagged / Origin-type), and whether anything at L derives it.

The **minimum additional condition** is a minimal node of the lattice with (R) and (E) EXCLUDES-ALL, each strictly
weaker node carrying (N). Nothing restates `K = Q3`: a candidate `P` that mentions `Q3`, PSD, or "all pure states of
the quantum cone" is inadmissible as `P` (it may appear as an intermediate lemma).

## Two criteria to use (coordinator's reductions; re-derive before use)
- **Reachability ⇒ UNIQUE.** Let `Ĝ` be the group generated by `cnot` and the symmetry demanded by `P`. If the
  orbit `Ĝ·SEP` of the pure product states contains every pure state of `ℂ²⊗ℂ²`, then `K ⊇ Q3` (every extreme ray
  of `Q3` is in `K`), hence `K = Q3` by the owner's observation. Instance: full rotations on one token. With
  `G = SU(2)⊗I` the generated Lie algebra is `span{σ⊗I, σ⊗X}` = two commuting copies of `su(2)` acting on the
  `I⊗X` eigenspaces, so `Ĝ = {U⊗P₊ + V⊗P₋}` contains `CNOT = I⊗P₊ + X⊗P₋` and carries `a⊗b` to every
  `u′⊗|+⟩ + v′⊗|−⟩`, i.e. to every pure state; symmetrically for `I⊗SU(2)` (algebra `span{I⊗σ, Z⊗σ}`). So
  one-token full local rotation symmetry, with `cnot`, forces `Q3` [W, to be made W + X].
- **Bell-type defect ⇒ EXOTIC (via EBF).** For a maximally entangled `ψ`, `e_ψ = E00/2 − T_ψ/4` has
  `⟨e_ψ, e_φ⟩ = |⟨ψ|φ⟩|²/4 ≥ 0` for every `φ`, `⟨e_ψ, p⟩ ≥ 0` for every product, and `⟨e_ψ, P_φ⟩ ≥ 0` iff
  `|⟨ψ|φ⟩|² ≤ 1/2`. So if some maximally entangled `ψ` has overlap `≤ 1/2` with every state of `Ĝ·SEP`, the seed
  `cone(Ĝ·K_gen ∪ Ĝ·e_ψ)` is `Ĝ`-invariant and subdual and EBF gives an exotic `Ĝ`-invariant self-dual cone
  (existence; explicit when the orbit of `e_ψ` is finite and the surgery hypotheses hold). Between the two
  criteria (reachable set not everything, but every maximally entangled state within overlap `> 1/2` of it) the
  question is open and must be decided by other means or left UNRESOLVED.
