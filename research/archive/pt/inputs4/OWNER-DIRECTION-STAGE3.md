# Owner's direction for stage 3 (verbatim excerpts, received 2026-10-10 after the stage-2 report)

> ### A useful mathematical observation
>
> Let Q be the ordinary two-qubit quantum positive cone and K a candidate cone, using the same Hilbert–Schmidt
> duality. If both are self-dual and Q ⊆ K, then necessarily Q ⊆ K = K* ⊆ Q* = Q. Therefore, K = Q.
>
> This means one possible route to uniqueness is to establish that the observer-native constructions generate every
> quantum pure state, then use self-duality to exclude everything else.
>
> But there is a critical restriction: we cannot simply assume that arbitrary single-system operations extend to
> entangled composites. Doing so would introduce precisely the operation-level coherence that the framework is trying
> to derive.
>
> That is why the S2/S3 cone question remains genuinely important.
>
> ### What I would prioritize next
>
> Priority 1 — Resolve the cone uniqueness question. Determine whether an exotic self-dual cone can satisfy the
> certified product-state and native-gate conditions without importing additional local-on-entangled operations. An
> exact counterexample is as valuable as a uniqueness proof.
>
> Priority 2 — Derive the composition principle. Even if uniqueness holds, establish why an embedded observer must
> have self-dual composition. Otherwise quantum mechanics is selected by an added axiom rather than derived from the
> framework.
>
> Priority 3 — Complete the remaining equivalence obligations. Finite rank, local tomography, purification and the
> unresolved K2 obligations cannot be considered settled merely by proving cone uniqueness. Higher-dimensional
> composition remains a separate extension.
>
> Priority 4 — Return to the double slit. Once the necessary composite structure is derived, build the experiment
> within that structure and test interference, complementarity, quantum erasure and genuinely nonclassical
> correlations. These tests must distinguish the construction from classical hidden-variable alternatives.
>
> I would preserve the existing governance boundaries and make the precise, assumption-minimal self-dual-cone
> uniqueness problem the next research target, with the countermodel search running alongside the proof attempt.

Stage 3 runs Priority 1 only. Priorities 2–4 are queued, not launched.
