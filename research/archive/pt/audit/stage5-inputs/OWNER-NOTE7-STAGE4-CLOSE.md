* Sufficient symmetry: When the permitted composite symmetry is rich enough to carry product states to every pure state, the quantum cone is uniquely selected.

* No unresolved middle case: Within the audited classification, the proposed intermediate zone is empty.

The results also reveal why certain promising approaches cannot finish the proof. Finite symmetry groups can eliminate known Bell-type defects without eliminating exotic cones altogether. Likewise, the existing native drive does not provide enough rotational freedom.
