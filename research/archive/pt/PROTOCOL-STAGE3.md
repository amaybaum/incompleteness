## The question, stated exactly

Carrier `W 3` [K CompositeDimension.lean:97], pairing `ipW` (Euclidean on the 16 entries; `ipW w v = 4 tr(pauliW w
pauliW v)`), `dualW K = {w : ∀ k ∈ K, 0 ≤ ipW w k}`, `prodState` [K CD:161], `cnot` [K CD:775], `eball 3` the ball.

**Q-SD.** Let `K ⊆ W 3` satisfy
- **(H1)** `prodState x y ∈ K` for all `x, y ∈ eball 3` (the certified product datum; hadm (a));
- **(H2)** `cnot ω ∈ K` for all `ω ∈ K` (the certified native gate; hgate — hinv follows, `cnot` being an involution);
- **(H3)** `K = dualW K` (self-duality for `ipW`).

Is `K = Q3`, where `Q3 = {w : pauliW w ⪰ 0}`?

**Levels.** (i) exactly H1–H3; (ii) H2 strengthened to invariance under the whole even native class (S2's Theorem N:
the 16-element group generated by `cnot` and the orientation-even signed-diagonal locals). A uniqueness proof at (i)
implies one at (ii); an exact countermodel at (ii) is one at (i). Any further symmetry assumed of `K` — local
rotations on entangled states, frame covariance, the torus `actC Rz(θ) ∘ actT Rx(φ)` that commutes with `cnot` —
is a **flagged premise**: admissible in a countermodel search (a countermodel with more symmetry is still a
countermodel), inadmissible in a uniqueness proof except as a CONDITIONAL result labelled with the flagged premise.

**Outcome vocabulary** (fixed now): **UNIQUE** — `K = Q3` proved from H1–H3 [W, with every step's exact ingredients
named]; **EXOTIC** — an exact `K ≠ Q3` exhibited with H1–H3 each verified exactly (membership of all products by a
symbolic identity, `cnot`-invariance exactly, self-duality exactly — a numerical near-self-dual cone is never
EXOTIC); **UNIQUE-IN-CLASS(𝒞)** / **NO-EXOTIC-IN-CLASS(𝒞)** for a stated class; **UNRESOLVED** with the precise
residual. Q3 and PSD appear only as the comparison object and in model verification, never as a premise.
