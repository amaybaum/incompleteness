## 0. Answer

**c = 0: open. c = 1: open.** For neither value of c was (a) a proof or (b) an exact countermodel reached.

**Instance.** P6: KT restricted to the token set {0, …, 5}. Each nonempty S carries one closed convex cone K_S.
Products of states lie in K_S, and products of full dual effects lie in K_S*, for every bipartition. The pair cones are
Q3 = PSD₄ (c = 0) or the twin cone (c = 1), with the native gate and its inverse as standalone (2) operations. The
cones are closed, and the single-token cone is the Bloch ball. Uniformity, S₃ symmetry, filter invariance at five
tokens and co-self-duality are settled inputs (EQ4-P, audited); they were re-verified where used.

**The wall, sharpened beyond ML1–ML3 (c = 0).** Does a closed convex cone K₃ ⊆ Herm(8) with K₃ ≠ PSD₈ exist such that

- (A1) BS ⊆ K₃ ⊆ BS*;
- (A2) K₃ is invariant under every one-token CP map and every token rename;
- (A4) N(x, y, e, f) ≥ 0 for all x, y ∈ K₃ and e, f ∈ K₃*? Here N is the crossing-split glue network with Bell links
  (NOTES N1).

After uniformity, a family satisfies P6 if and only if its K₃ satisfies (A1), (A2) and (A4). Each direction has its own
witness (§A.34):

- "⇒": each condition is a P6 consequence. (A1) and (A2) come from EQ4-P. For (A4), K₄ contains the glued states and
  K₄* contains the glued effects.
- "⇐": the minimal family K₄ = cl L₄, K₅, K₆ satisfies every P6 constraint. This rests on the exhaustive crossing
  enumeration and the case list [X s1 E1–E4, B–G + W].

Co-self-duality (A3), K₃ = T(K₃*), follows from (A4) and (A1) [X s3 A–C]. Consequences:

- **ML2 is not a separate obstacle.** No non-trivial crossing has a five-token part, so K₅ never enters. K₄ enters only
  through the one glue network.
- **(A4) is the genuinely six-token constraint.** It fails for the five-token countermodel PN₅ at −1/64, and (A3)
  fails there at −1/16 [X s3 P]. So the mandatory N5 control is green for any route through (A4).
- **c = 1** has the same statement with B_tw ⊆ K₃ ⊆ B_tw*, twin links, and K₃ = K₃* following from (A4)
  [X s1 G, s3 A + EQ4-P p1].

**Where the canonical candidate stands (ML1).**

## 2. P/A/C ledger

- **P** is P6 as stated in §0.
- **C** for c = 0 is "K₃ = PSD₈", equivalently a GHZ-class element in K₃ within six tokens (E3, as audited).
- For c = 1 every admissible K₃ contains B_tw ⊄ PSD₈. The decision is therefore between excluding c = 1 (no P6 model
  with twin pair cones) and a model. EQ4-P left c = 1 "not excluded by the GHZ-diagonal sector conditions".
- **P ⇒ C:** not proved, for either c.
- **Exact model of P ∧ ¬C:** none constructed, for either c.
- **Reduction (proved, both directions witnessed):** P ⇔ (A1) ∧ (A2) ∧ (A4) for the minimal family [X s1, s3 + W].
  This restates P; it adds no principle.
- **Models of weaker premise sets** (settled, re-verified where used):
  - PN₅ models P(≤ 5 tokens) ∧ ¬C and violates (A4) [X s3].
  - K_A (c = 0) and K_tw (c = 1) model the GD-sector conditions ∧ ¬C [EQ4-P]. They satisfy (A4) on unfiltered
    generators [X s4]. They are not P6 models: no lift is known, and the orbit hulls are (F) not co-self-dual.
- **Candidate principles:** none was introduced to reach either conclusion. One remark is recorded and not used.
  - A homogeneity premise A_hom ("K₃ has an automorphism group transitive on its interior") would give C through the
    Koecher–Vinberg classification [W + L, unverified; NOTES N2.5].
  - For c = 1 this works directly, since K₃ = K₃* is Euclidean self-duality.
  - For c = 0 it needs a further step not justified here. K₃ = T(K₃*) is self-duality for the indefinite form
    tr(X T Y), and passing to a positive-definite inner product is unverified.
  - So P ∧ A_hom ⇒ C would be conditional on [L] and on that step. Necessity of A_hom relative to P is not claimed.
  - A_hom is not IE₂ in disguise: it is a symmetry property of the single cone K₃, not an operation on part of a
    composite. It is, however, not in P6.
- No result here uses purification, transitivity, Choi-state availability, IE₂ or any (o)-type step.

## 3. Missing lemmas

- **ML-SIX (the wall, c = 0).** Existence or exclusion of K₃ ≠ PSD₈ with (A1), (A2) and (A4). This single statement
  replaces ML1 + ML2.
- **ML-SIX-lift (the canonical route to (b)).** A co-self-dual, filter-invariant, S₃-symmetric cone between Lift(K_A)
  and Lift(K_A)*.
  - Exact on ρ = 1 of S10.
  - Float evidence says the orbit hull itself is not such a cone, and that Lift(K_A)* contains incompatible elements
    (X_t, Y), between which a lift must choose.
  - Natural next candidate: a cone whose Ξ-section is the self-dual C_A. Its boundary point X_t lies in Lift(K_A)*
    with a self-positive orbit (F).
- **ML-SIX-(A4).** (A4) for that cone. Exact on unfiltered GD generators. Float with structured starts on filtered
  orbit-hull nodes (y24).
- **ML-SIX-selfpos.** Exact self-positivity of the κ and ω orbit pairs of Lift(K_A). Pairs with W3, BS or Z are exact
  via Z and BS*; the float evidence is y24.
- **ML-SIX-proof (the route to (a)).** A K₃-independent negative glue-network quadruple: for every K₃ ≠ PSD₈ with (A1)
  and (A2), nodes x, y ∈ K₃ and e, f ∈ K₃* with N < 0.
  - No candidate route was found.
  - Any route must use (A4), and must fail on PN₅ (it does, at −1/64).
  - A route through homogeneity would need A_hom (§2).
- **c = 1:** the same lemmas with K_tw, B_tw and twin links.

## 4. Research questions

  ρ ≈ 5.6e-3) lies in Lift(K_A)*: for example a sum-of-squares certificate for the degree-6 inequalities
  ⟨Y, Ad(k) g⟩ ≥ 0 on GL(2, C)³, g ∈ {W3, κ, ω}.

    cone(BS ∪ Z) ∩ Ξ ⊆ C_A.
- RQ-3. Exact (A4) on filtered nodes. On GD the glue tensor has a Pauli-flow form: 16 products of four stabilizer
  coordinates (y11). Does that survive filters?
- RQ-4. The c = 0 homogeneity step: must a homogeneous cone with K = T(K*) be symmetric?
- RQ-5. Other twirl sectors (Ω₃, 8 dims; the X-shaped 16-dim sector; Z₃, 22 dims) did not converge in the float tests
  (y13). Where else does the orbit hull leave room?
