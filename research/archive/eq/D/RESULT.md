**The hypothetical K2 interface (assumed, never claimed).** `K2-hyp`: for every `n` the `n`-fold composite of the
elementary system is the complex quantum system `Q_n` on `ℂ^{2ⁿ}` with every state, every effect and every CP
instrument, composites `Q_n ⊗ Q_m = Q_{n+m}`. Class-level reading in the K3 vocabulary: `𝓘 S K` for every `K` when
`card S` is a power of 2.

**Face principles compared** (a face is *frame-generated of size k* when it is the smallest face containing `k`
jointly perfectly distinguishable pure states):
- **FP-S** (state/effect form of the Hardy / Masanes–Müller subspace axiom): each such face, with the restricted
  effects, is affinely the state space, with all its effects, of a capacity-`k` system; **FP-E**: systems of equal
  capacity are state/effect-equivalent.
- **FP-T** (restriction form): moreover every transformation of `A` mapping `F` into its cone restricts to a
  transformation of the face system, and every transformation of the face system arises so.
- **FP-O** (frame-face compression; the Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti ideal-compression form restricted to
  frame-generated faces): there are a system `B` and transformations `D: B → A`, `E: A → B` of the theory with
  `D(Ω_B) = F`, `E∘D = id_B`, `D∘E|_F = id_F`.
- **FP-C**: the composite of two face systems is the face of the composite generated by the product states, and the
  real span of the products is the real span of that face.

## 1. Finding

Against `K2-hyp`, the operations do not lift with the state/effect form of the subspace axiom: in the exact
countermodel `𝔐₁` every frame-generated face of every system is the state space of a system with all effects and
equal capacities are equivalent, yet the capacity-3 system has no swap; `𝔐₁′` adds an Alfsen–Shultz filter
`ρ ↦ PρP` for every face of every system and still has none. They do lift under the operational form FP-O: `K2-hyp`
+ FP-O + sequential and parallel composition give, for every finite carrier `S` and every ancilla extension
`S × Fin n`, a system with state space `D_S(ℂ)` whose instruments are exactly the typed Kraus instruments, hence a
`TypedOperationalTheory` with `ShadowQuantum` and the implementation class `fullClass` with `DrivesElementary`
(written proof; FP-S, FP-O, FP-T and FP-C hold exactly in complex QT on every tested frame, `n ≤ 6`). In the kernel
vocabulary the transformation content the lift needs is located exactly (Theorem A): drivability at the single
carrier `Fin 2` together with `Architecture`, `ContextStable` and `LabelInvariant` gives `DrivesElementary` at every
finite carrier (exact at `Fin 1 … Fin 7`, written proof uniform in the carrier). `ContextStable` cannot be dropped
(exact countermodel `𝓘_cm`: full on every `2ⁿ` carrier, an Architecture, label- and dagger-stable, no swap at `Fin 3`,
and its qubit theory is not QM because level 3 of the qubit is the 6-element carrier `Fin 2 × Fin 3`), nor can
`LabelInvariant` (written set-theoretic countermodel `𝓙`) or `Architecture.block` (written countermodel `𝓘_nb`).
Corpus consequences: under `StructurallyClosed` the hypothesis "`DrivesElementary` at every carrier" reduces to its
`Fin 2` instance; a structurally closed extension of
the substratum class is a quantum architecture iff it contains the single qubit flow `e^{−itX}` at `Fin 2` for every
`t`; the corpus class `MixC` (= `FlowR`) is a quantum architecture, so its generated theory is QM at every nonempty
carrier, where the kernel states it at `Fin 2` only. With composites of face systems at the kinematic level but no
transformation clause, every unitary on a face system is an exact probabilistic branch (gate teleportation) and is
available deterministically within `2(8/9)^N`; whether it is available exactly and deterministically is the named
wall (exact deterministic *generalized* port-based teleportation), and closure of the transformation sets removes it.
