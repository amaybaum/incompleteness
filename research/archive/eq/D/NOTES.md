### N3(a) — operational face principle FP-O (closed: THEOREM ROUTE, written + exact controls)

FP-O (frame-face compression, the Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti ideal-compression form restricted to frame-generated
faces): for every system `A` and every face `F ⊆ Ω_A` generated by `k` jointly perfectly distinguishable pure states,
there are a system `B` and transformations `D: B → A`, `E: A → B` with `D(Ω_B) = F`, `E∘D = id_B`, `D∘E|_F = id_F`.
Written proof (RESULT §4 T5): K2-hyp + FP-O + sequential and parallel composition give, for every finite `S` and
every `n`, systems with state spaces `D_S`, `D_{S×Fin n}` (composite compatibility from parallel composition and the
local tomography of the K2 composites), every effect, and every CP instrument (`E'∘K̃∘D` with `K̃ = ι' K ι^†` plus a
complement Kraus operator); soundness from complete positivity on the composites. Hence a `TypedOperationalTheory`
with `availT ⟺ IsTypedKrausInstrument`, i.e. `ShadowQuantum` by `shadowQuantum_of_typed` (`TypedCompletion.lean:838`),
and the class `fullClass` with `DrivesElementary`. FP-O ⇒ FP-T by composition: `φ⁻¹Tφ = E∘T∘D` on `Ω_B`.

### N3(b) — composites of face systems without a transformation clause (closed at the exact level; wall named)
