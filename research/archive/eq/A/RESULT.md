## 1. Finding

The qutrit counterexample does not force K∞-Geom to be scoped, only localized: stated inside the face generated by two
pure states — **GEOM2**, that face is relatively strictly convex (effect form **SF2**: a test proper on it is certain
on at most one of its states) — the principle holds in finite-dimensional complex quantum theory at every level (a pair
face is the state space of a two-dimensional subspace; the census effect `diag(1,1,0)` is the unit of its pair face),
and it is the strict-convexity part of the Alfsen–Shultz Hilbert-ball property (literature, unverified); the
elementary predicate is then field-neutral and effect-free, **ELEM2** = two distinct extreme points whose midpoint is a
state and not a boundary state (KF:130), anchored as **ElemVis** on the certain states of a sharp `BinaryVisible` pair;
it holds for the qubit, fails for every complex or real level n ≥ 3, and with GEOM2 makes the body relatively strictly
convex (Theorem B), which is the only geometric input the ball route needs, since under TRB-1's hypotheses boundary
transitivity is relative strict convexity plus transitivity on pure states (landed purity for one direction, the new
Theorem A for the other) and `RelStrictConvex`/`SingletonFaces` have no consumer outside `KInfFoundations`; ELEM2 ∧
GEOM2 exclude the square, hexagon, pentagon, torus, Stiefel orbitope and bipyramid, while the classical bit needs
`HasTwoSharpTests` or drivability, the rebit disk needs corrected drivability or DIM-1, and balls of dimension ≥ 4 need
DIM-1; for QA2, K∞-Seed is automatic as stated (and anchored by `sharpSeed_completion` under ElemVis), whereas K∞-V4 is
independent of the package plus K∞-Stage, Seed, Trans (exact or dense) and Drive — exact SIC+axis tower — but follows
from label-dual operations; for QA3, FiniteRank is independent of the whole single-system package (an exact ℓ²-ball
stage tower satisfies every statable seam with stage ranks n + 1), the finite-carrier route is closed (a polytope with
GEOM2 ∧ ELEM2 has dimension ≤ 1), and, new, under the package with V4 into stage effects and a dense boundary orbit,
FiniteRank is equivalent to operational compactness of the completed body (**Theorem R**, written; an exact compact
infinite-dimensional ellipsoid tower shows V4 load-bearing).

  automorphisms (enumerated) but not boundary transitive: (1,0) never reaches (1,1), kernel TB:772. The one-axis flow

  and violates SF(avail). The capacity scope (SA-LEDGER P-D) must therefore be read with `fullEffects`. ELEM2 is
  effect-free and immune.

  restated, as SF ⇔ RSC given SEC already was. The content is the uniform principle, true at every quantum level, and

  completed body is the system's full state space. This is a reading of K∞-Stage that is nowhere named (no hit in the
  corpus or the listed ledgers). The effect form with quantum effects is hereditary.
