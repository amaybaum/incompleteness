Below that bar: record-only (ELABORATING / CONFIRMING). Favourable branches get maximum skepticism and a
countercontrol (§A.31, §A.21).

**Decision rules (preregistered as rules, not expected numbers):**

- R1 (converse first). A principle is reported as compatible with QM only if `Q3` (with its unitary group) satisfies
  it by an exact check or a cited standard fact. If `Q3` fails it, the verdict is INCOMPATIBLE, never repaired.
- R2 (foils). For each principle, each foil (`min`, `max`, `PT_B(Q3)`, real two-rebit composite, classical bits) is
  tested. If any admissible foil other than `Q3` satisfies the principle, the verdict for "forces exactly `Q3`" is
  WEAKER CONCLUSION with that foil as countermodel.
- R3 (Lie classification). A dimension of a solution space is reported only if (a) the lower bound is an explicit
  exact subspace verified to satisfy every constraint symbolically or on the full constraint family used, and (b) the
  upper bound is an exact rational rank. Controls: local `so(3)⊕so(3)` must lie in it; `su(4)`-image and its `PT_B`
  conjugate must lie in it (both cones are admissible); countercontrol: a generator of a known positivity failure must
  not lie in it. A verdict printed over a failed control is void.
- R4. Every written step is marked `[W]`; literature not checked against the source is marked unverified.
