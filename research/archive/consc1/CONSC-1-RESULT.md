## 1. The four-level boundary

| level | what it is | consciousness | warrant | where the corpus states it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **physical registration** | an interaction leaves a correlation or durable record (a die, a photon, a detector) | not required | physical fact | ch01:151–153, ch01:290 ("No consciousness is required…"), Explainer:574, Main:570 |
| **operational observation** | the triple (S, φ, V), V ⊊ S coupled through φ; with C1–C4 the memory-bearing observer | not required | definition + selection condition | Main §1.2 Definition, ch01:26–28 (a deck of cards, an automaton satisfy it), ch18:295 |
| **empirical evidence** | a record bearing evidential relations; in the corpus, *perspective-neutral*: what is available at the partition V (records, statistics, the measured memory witness) | not required by any step | physical/statistical | Main:62, App. C:108, Methodology:439, 479 |
| **phenomenal consciousness** | what it is like to be a system | — | not derived; conjectural | ch18 status note (line 6), §18.10 |

The **indubitable floor** (Axiom 1) is not a fifth level but a *warrant*: its content is structural ("some tokened
differentiation occurs"); its indubitability is performative — secured from within any locus that entertains the
doubt, because doubting is itself a tokened differentiation. No step of that argument uses phenomenal content.

## 4. COBS-2 — the two arrows

```
causal/ontological:  physical world → physical registration / observer (V, C1–C4) → possible consciousness
epistemic:           record available at V → evidence → reconstructed physics
```

The corpus states the causal arrow clearly (ch00:101, ch18:281–295). Its epistemic notions ("epistemic access",
"what the observer can infer") are uniformly **access-relative at the partition** — perspective-neutral, needing
no consciousness — so the epistemic arrow as the corpus uses it runs from records, not from a conscious
perspective. No sentence turns consciousness into a cause of physical outcomes. Places where access-relative
limits are restated as limits on *human* knowledge (ch04:196, ch12:213, 219) run in the valid direction (physical
limits bound the knowledge of embedded agents). The one reverse step, epistemic-to-ontic, is Methodology L463/467
("constituted by the observer's inability to tell … apart"; "a difference that no observation could ever register
is not a difference in fact") — the framework's stated gauge thesis, argued there from the gauge classification;
recorded, not an error of this audit's kind.

## 5. COBS-4 — Chapter 18

**Semantics of "empirical evidence": (a), perspective-neutral.** That is how the corpus uses it (§1). Under (a)
no independent content contributed by consciousness is identified: a record's evidential relations are fixed by
its correlations at V, and a non-conscious embedded system can take them up (ch12:197, "AI is an embedded
observer"). Under (b), perspective-relative, "a conscious perspective is necessary for *experienced* evidence" is
true by definition and must not be reported as a finding. **Recommendation: do not add the converse as a
claim.** At most add one scope sentence (edit B8 below) saying the framework's evidence is perspective-neutral and
the (b)-question is outside it.

The present one-way statement needs its modality made explicit, and ch18 contradicts its own status note:

- **Direction 2** ("observation does not require consciousness", ch18:295) is a consequence of the definition —
  detectors and horizons satisfy it. **Direction 1** ("any conscious system must implement C1–C4", ch18:289, 293) is
  a conjecture about consciousness, stated as the chapter's "substantive position" with no modality label in §18.10.
- **Status-note contradictions:** line 6 says "*conjecture* … no empirical test is proposed … carries no
  evidential weight for the core framework"; line 323 says the account "makes empirical predictions"; line 347
  lists "consciousness as structural necessary condition" in the "**Cumulative case**"; line 349 says the content
  beyond conventional physics "is not speculative addition". Guardrails 6 and 8.
- **Metaphysical position by default (guardrail 4):** ch18:287 asserts "None of these is conscious in any phenomenal
  sense — there is nothing it is like to be the cosmological horizon", while ch18:297 says the framework "allows"
  panpsychism.
- Noted for the manuscript round, not resolved here: ch18:293 argues Direction 1 from C2 in its slow-bath form, which
  ch18:68 calls "not the condition itself", and ch18:327 says feed-forward networks "fail C2"; whether a
  feed-forward architecture fails C2 (hidden-record persistence) or C4 (readback) is a physics-accuracy question
  outside this charter.

## 6. COBS-5 — the self-referential loop

```
S → physical observer (V ⊊ S, C1–C4) → records available at V → evidence → reconstructed physics of S
                        └─ (possible, conjectural) conscious perspective
```

Retained in this form only. The arrows S → observer (definition plus the C1–C4 selection condition and the
characterization theorem) and evidence → reconstruction (the reconstruction theorem with its stated empirical
inputs) are supplied by the physics core; observer → records is physical; **observer → conscious perspective is
biological/emergent and not derived** (sufficiency absent; necessity conjectural, §5). The loop closes without
the conscious branch: consciousness is a conjectural attachment to the observer node, not a link the
epistemology needs. Closing the loop is self-consistency, not evidential support (guardrail 6).
