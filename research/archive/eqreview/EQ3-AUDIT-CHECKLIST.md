# EQ3-P audit checklist (coordinator; applied when the thread reports)

The owner's instruction is to leave the protocol and governance as they stand and to audit EQ3's actual proof or
counterexample against the checks below. No new premise is introduced. This is a review aid, not a protocol amendment.

## The four checks

1. **Both inclusions, for the whole cone.**
   - Treat (I) `T(K₂*) ⊆ K₂` and (II) `K₂ ⊆ T(K₂*)` separately.
   - Each must be proved for the entire stated cone, not for a product-generated subset.
   - A proof of one inclusion alone is recorded as a partial result, not as co-self-duality.
2. **Effect availability: the quantifier.**
   - Keep mathematically positive functionals apart from physically available measurements.
   - The Bell-state conditioning argument gives only `T(E₂⁺) ⊆ K₂` when conditioning is licensed only on available
     effects, `E₂⁺` being the cone they generate. It reaches `T(K₂*) ⊆ K₂` only if `E₂⁺ = K₂*`, or if conditioning is
     established for every positive functional.
   - Under the full-effect reading the gap closes: every effect is a functional with values in [0, 1] on the
     normalized body. With a proper cone, a strictly positive unit and finite dimension, every positive functional is
     a positive multiple of an effect.
   - The same quantifier applies to (II), through the Bell effects, and to the effect products in either inclusion.
   - Determine at the base which reading the composite interface (COMP-1) and EFF-1 actually give at the **pair**
     level. EFF-1's recorded reading is cone derived, full effect set conditional. Do not assume it.
3. **Four-copy consistency.**
   - The regroupings 01|23 and 02|13, and 03|12 if it is used, must refer to one four-token composite `K₄` in one
     carrier, with consistent token identifications. Independently chosen composite cones do not qualify.
   - Abstract isomorphism of the pair cones does not supply the common identifications the Bell-link argument needs.
     Check how token charts, pair orientations and link-induced transposes are fixed: Amendment 2, uniform
     composition.
4. **No circularity.**
   - Every step must be state-level (regrouping, products, conditioning) or a standalone two-token operation.
   - No step may extend a pair gate to a larger composite; that is the (o) tag of Amendment 1.

## Also check

- Replays are byte-identical, and floats are labelled and never cited.
- Each verdict prints over green controls, with a countercontrol for every favourable branch.
- Every exact countermodel is re-derived in independent code before it is reported.
- The outcome is placed in the owner's five-way table.
- Co-self-duality, if established, is not presented as quantum uniqueness. The follow-up question is whether
  native-gate invariance, local symmetries and the dimensional constraints select the PSD cone or its twin from the
  co-self-dual candidates.

## Level of success (owner's classification; apply to the final interpretation)

The EQ3 protocol is frozen as it stands, with Amendments 1 and 2, and is not amended further.

- **Restricted-effect result.** Inclusions hold only for the cone generated by available measurements, e.g.
  `T(E₂⁺) ⊆ K₂`. This is meaningful but is not full co-self-duality.
- **Conditional co-self-duality.** `K₂ = T(K₂*)` holds, but only with explicitly named assumptions: full-effect
  availability, uniform composition, or another. Name each assumption and the inclusion it enters.
- **Unconditional relative to the base.** Both inclusions follow from premises already established at `bcbc516f`,
  with no added effect, uniformity or idle-extension assumption.

**Sufficiency, insufficiency and necessity (owner's distinction).** Write `P` for the premises established at
`bcbc516f`, `A` for an assumption the route adds, and `C` for the conclusion (one inclusion, or `K₂ = T(K₂*)`).

- `P ∧ A ⇒ C` is **sufficiency**. The report says A is "used by this route" and sufficient together with P.
- An exact model of `P ∧ ¬C` shows only **insufficiency of P**: P alone does not give C, so A cannot be dropped
  without a replacement. It does not make A necessary, since a different assumption might close the same gap.
  Re-derive every such model in independent code before it is reported.
- **Necessity of A relative to P** is claimed only with a proof of `P ∧ C ⇒ A`. Without that proof the report never
  says "required" or "necessary", whatever countermodels exist.
