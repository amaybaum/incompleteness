## Status vocabulary (every result carries one)

- **CERTIFIED** — a theorem checked by the Lean kernel at L (file:line on `main`), or a §A.39 round landed on `main`.
  Exact facts about the source tree (import graphs, tree comparisons) are carried as [X], not as CERTIFIED.
- **CONDITIONAL** — a derivation whose premises include a named item at its status (assumed / open / design-run /
  PT-record / literature); the item is named every time.
- **CONJECTURE** — a statement with evidence (exact instances, numerics, a written proof not kernel-checked, a plan)
  and no certification.
- **FAILED** — an approach that was tried and refuted or found circular; kept with its evidence (§A.29: a closed
  route is progress of the same kind as an open one).
- **OPEN** — stated, not yet attempted or not decided.
- Evidence levels inside a thread: [K] kernel at L · [D] design module (built on a dev branch, not certified) ·
  [W] written argument · [X] exact computation (script, output, replay) · [A] audited stage record · [L] literature
  input named, not checked here · [U] unsourced.
