## Status vocabulary (every result carries one)

- **CERTIFIED** — a theorem checked by the Lean kernel at L (file:line on `main`), or a §A.39 round landed on `main`.
- **CONDITIONAL** — a derivation whose premises include a named item at its status (assumed / open / design-run /
  PT-record); the item is named every time.
- **CONJECTURE** — a statement with evidence (exact instances, numerics, a plan) and no proof.
- **FAILED** — an approach that was tried and refuted or found circular; kept with its evidence (§A.29: a closed
  route is progress of the same kind as an open one).
- **OPEN** — stated, not yet attempted.
- Evidence levels inside a thread: [K] kernel at L · [D] design module (built on a dev branch, not certified) ·
  [W] written argument · [X] exact computation (script, output, replay) · [A] audited stage record · [U] unsourced.

## Handoffs

Versioned, documented handoffs live in `research/HANDOFFS/` (protocol in its README). A thread never changes
another thread's assumptions silently: a result relevant to another thread is written up as a handoff by the
coordinator from the source thread's committed record, numbered and versioned, and the receiving thread records
its receipt in its own `inbox/` with a commit before relying on it.
