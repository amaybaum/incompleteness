## Status vocabulary (every result carries one)

- **CERTIFIED** — a theorem checked by the Lean kernel at L (file:line on `main`), or a §A.39 round landed on `main`.
  Exact facts about the source tree (import graphs, tree comparisons) are carried as [X], not as CERTIFIED.
- **CONDITIONAL** — a derivation whose premises include a named item at its status (assumed / open / design-run /
  PT-record / literature); the item is named every time.
- **CONJECTURE** — a statement with evidence (exact instances, numerics, a written proof not kernel-checked, a plan)
  and no certification.
- **FAILED** — an approach that was tried and refuted or found circular; kept with its evidence (§A.29: a closed
  route is progress of the same kind as an open one).
- **OPEN** — stated, not yet attempted or not decided.
- Evidence levels inside a thread: [K] kernel at L · [D] design module (built on a dev branch, not certified) ·
  [W] written argument · [X] exact computation (script, output, replay) · [A] audited stage record · [L] literature
  input named, not checked here · [U] unsourced.

### The three objectives (owner, 2026-10-11)

With `A` the applicable adopted non-hypothetical OI premises at L and `b` the composite action, the stage-6
countermodel gives `K(Z_F) ⊨ A_reached` and `K(Z_F) ⊭ b`, hence `A_reached ⊭ b`: the currently transferred
pair-level constraints (the 118 SATISFIES rows) are insufficient for (b). The 68 NOT REACHED rows have not been shown
irrelevant to (b): a missing transfer theorem is not a proof that no transfer is possible, so semantic independence
of (b) from the entire OI system is **not** established; a transfer found later would be a previously overlooked
consequence of OI, not a new principle. This is what makes B14 and O11 decisive. The countermodel, not the dependency
graph, is the decisive evidence for the pair-level statement. Round 4 and its successors therefore work three distinct
objectives:

1. **A sufficient bridge.** A precisely stated `H` with `A + H ⇒ b`. Candidate: H-OI_g at `{R_z(θ₀), J}`,
   pair-context availability of the two operations, with H-T its continuous portion; with H1–H3 and `ReachPure` the K2
   schema carries it to `Q3` ([D], `EqvK2Schema`).
2. **Independence and minimality of `H`.** The countermodel shows that some additional constraint is needed; it does
   not show that `H` is necessary or minimal. One direction is immediate for the conclusion, in the invariance reading only:
   `Q3` is preserved by every local unitary, so `Q3 ⇒ A_miss` where `A_miss` is cone-preserving admissibility (the
   form the K2 schema carries: `K` invariant under the flow and `cycEquiv`). It does not give (A), the operational
   availability of the operations through the kernel's protocols, which is the H-level premise H-OI_g: a quantum cone
   with a restricted set of available transformations separates the two, and S6's necessity direction states which
   meaning it proves. What remains is to show that the relevant weaker alternatives fail (recorded so far: each half
   alone — H-T leaves exotic cones conditional on claim (D), the finite half leaves an explicit cone; stage 5's weaker
   forms ε … κ, NOT, NOT+J, finite and flow-only) and to decide whether any principle supplying `A_miss` without being
   (b) in disguise is derivable from `A` (B14, O11) or independent of it. Any sufficient bridge must exclude K(Z_F), or
   say why K(Z_F) no longer satisfies the bridge's applicable premises (at present: K(Z_F) violates (A) for `R_z(θ₀)`,
   B10-2).
3. **From the bridge to equivalence.** `A + H ⇒ b` does not establish OI–QM equivalence; the geometric, composite,
   operational and infinite-dimensional obligations of the K ledger and Level III are discharged at their own levels
   (equivalence E15–E16 and the ledger).

The central question, in the owner's words: *what is the weakest independently justified principle that excludes the
established non-quantum countermodels and supplies the missing composite operations, without assuming quantum
mechanics itself?* Whether `H` is an independent additional principle or a previously overlooked consequence of OI
decides the strongest defensible formulation of the eventual result.

**C21 safeguard.** A countermodel to (b) and a countermodel to `Q3` are recorded separately. A failure of (b)
establishes a failure of `Q3` only where a theorem or definition at L proves that `Q3` entails the (b)-instance in
question — true for every local unitary, since `Q3` is invariant under them, so a (b)-failure for a local unitary is a
`Q3`-failure; a `Q3`-failure (`K ≠ Q3`) is not a (b)-failure for any particular operation (the order-384 and
Clifford cones satisfy (b) for their groups and are not `Q3`). Each Table A2 row is decided on its own terms, and no
row's verdict is inferred from another's. The (b)-instances recorded there are cone invariances, never availabilities:
a cone invariant under a group is not thereby a model in which the group's operations are available.

**Acceptance criteria for the decisive round-4 result** (owner, 2026-10-11; the execution plan is unchanged and no
thread scope expands):

- **Derived bridge.** A theorem transfers the relevant OI premises (inventory items by id, the NOT REACHED rows it
  brings to the pair level named) to the pair level and establishes availability without an introduced hypothesis.
- **Independent bridge.** A *full* model, satisfying the applicable OI premises at every level that applies to it,
  the NOT REACHED rows included so far as they apply, in which availability fails. A model checked only against the
  118 reached pair-level rows establishes pair-level independence and is labelled exactly that, never "independent of
  OI".
- **Precisely scoped OPEN.** The investigation isolates an unproved transfer, extension or reachability lemma, without
  claiming independence.

Pair-level insufficiency (`A_reached ⊭ b`, established) and full-OI insufficiency (not established) are kept apart in
every label; cone invariance and operational availability are kept apart in every statement.
