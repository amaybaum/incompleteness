## Status vocabulary (every result carries one)

- **CERTIFIED** — a theorem checked by the Lean kernel at L (file:line on `main`), or a §A.39 round landed on `main`.
  Exact facts about the source tree (import graphs, tree comparisons) are carried as [X], not as CERTIFIED.
- **CONDITIONAL** — a derivation whose premises include a named item at its status (assumed / open / design-run /
  PT-record / literature); the item is named every time.
- **CONJECTURE** — a statement with evidence (exact instances, numerics, a written proof not kernel-checked, a plan)
  and no certification.
- **FAILED** — an approach that was tried and refuted or found circular; kept with its evidence (§A.29: a closed
  route is progress of the same kind as an open one).
- **OPEN** — stated, not yet attempted or not decided.
- Evidence levels inside a thread: [K] kernel at L · [D] design module (built on a dev branch, not certified) ·
  [W] written argument · [X] exact computation (script, output, replay) · [A] audited stage record · [L] literature
  input named, not checked here · [U] unsourced.

## The central question: the composite action (b)

(b): single-token reversible operations, idle-extended to one token of a pair, preserve the pair cone in `W 3`.
Stage 6 (archived, audited) established **INDEPENDENCE** at L: the complete applicable premise set (199 items of a
633-record inventory) does not force (b); the countermodel K(Z_F) satisfies every non-hypothesis item and violates (b)
under every one-parameter local rotation (law `ipW(R_n(t) z_s, R_n(π/2) p_s) = −sin t/8`); the missing assumption
A_miss is (b) for `ball3Drive`'s flow and its `cyc3`-conjugate on one token (an instance of OI⁺-1; fails the disguise
test). Round 1 added, with every claim at its label:

- **A_miss restated (origin, HO-5):** A_miss ⟺ (b) for `R_z(t)` for all `t` together with (b) for the single discrete
  operation `J = cyc3`, since `J = R_z(π/2)R_x(π/2)` and `J R_z(t) J⁻¹ = R_x(t)` — CONDITIONAL ([W]; identities [X],
  independently confirmed). Equivalently (bridge, HO-2d): (b) for the two rational infinite-order rotations
  `R_z(θ), R_x(θ)`, `cos θ = 3/5`.
- **Embedding does not close the gap (bridge, V-1):** the H-level constraints of the realization theorem and branch (a)
  are satisfied by an exact finite embedded-observer realization of K(Z_F) (B4, HO-1); `EmbeddedObservation` and
  `ObserverRecursion` transcribed are satisfied by K(Z_F); locality of registers is Bell-local and excludes every
  candidate cone, `Q3` included (B1, HO-3); finite and locally finite substrata cannot carry the sufficient operations
  (B3, HO-2). The weakest H-level principle supplying A_miss is **H-OI_g**: availability in the pair context of one
  sufficient token operation (OI⁺-1 at level H) — assumed, unsourced, fails the disguise test. The route "from
  embedded observation alone" is FAILED (kept with evidence).
- **What a realization of K(Z_F) must look like (countermodels, HO-4):** its only local reversible symmetries form a
  finite group (order 384 with SWAP and the transpose; 96 local unitary Cliffords); no rotation flow of either token
  survives; so a realization theorem whose composition clause lets continuous local operations act on the pair cannot
  have K(Z_F) in its range — and that clause is (b) itself. Consistent with B4: its token `S`, `cyc3`, `R_z(θ)` are not
  pair instruments.
- **Mutual dependency Origin ↔ bridge (origin, O4-D):** the bridge needs SRC (a balanced mixer `J` available on one
  token from a premise passing the disguise test) from Origin; Origin needs SPEC (the mixer's spectator stability,
  matrix form = what remains for `oiPlusMin_iff_qm`, pair form = (b)) from the bridge; neither discharges the other.
- **SRC's only known mechanism does not reach the pair (origin round 2, HO-9):** the knowledge-balance readout makes
  `J = cyc3` available on one token with the exact witness, but its exclusivity is excluded on the stated access, and
  every product-register composite of such tokens is Bell-local — SRC via KB-D is token-only and cannot serve SPEC's
  target. The one open Origin premise is an exclusive measure-and-re-prepare readout.

  matrix level by `substratumClass_contextStable`, needing at the pair level the transfer clause (T), which is (b) for

  exotic cones (CONDITIONAL on claim (D)); together they force `Q3`. A transfer of the matrix theorem needs exactly
