## 3. The premise, its label and its disguise test

**H-T (the H-level transfer premise, one token).** In the pair context, with the native gate present, the hidden
intervention that realizes one infinite-order rotation about the frame axis on token B (`R_z(θ₀)`) is available (A),

for the finite-order rotations `R_z(2π/2ⁿ)` of a directed tower, at every stage.

- **It yields (T)θ₀.** B1.1 [D] gives (b) for each operation it covers. The closure lemma (NOTES-B3 §3) then gives
  (b) for the closed group generated: rotations `R_z(t)` for all `t` and `nflip` on token B. `θ₀/π` is irrational
  [X T7: `2 cos θ₀ = 6/5` is not an algebraic integer].

  - Dropping (A) leaves nothing: B6's `ctl_counter` keeps (W) and (P), fails (A), and the conclusion fails.
  - The readout-only form "`R(Π_*𝒫) ⊆ cone R(𝒫)`" is weaker, but given (W) and (P) it *is* (b_H) (B1.1, step 3):
    a restatement, not a premise.

    - Order 2: K(Z_F) is invariant under `R_z(π)` [X T6] and not under `R_z(θ₀)` [X T4].

- **Label: CONDITIONAL (H-T assumed; unsourced).** It is not at L. The realization theorem's H-level constraints do not

## 4. The continuous half is locally finite (T7–T9)

- **`R_z(θ₀)` is at no finite stage.** It has infinite order [X T7]. So no permutation of a finite substratum
  induces it: such an induced map has finite order and hence an integer trace when rational, while
  `tr(actT R_z(θ₀)) = 64/5` on `W 3` [X T7]. In the kernel's vocabulary, an operation datum of infinite order on the
  chart body is not stage-preserving (CompositionOrder.lean:378 [K]).
- **The circle it generates is the closure of finite stages.** Let `G_n = ⟨CNOT, 1⊗diag(1, ω_n), 1⊗X⟩` with
  `ω_n = e^{2πi/2ⁿ}`.
  - These are monomial groups. Modulo phase their orders are `4^{n+1}` = 16, 64, 256, 1024 for n = 1…4, and
    `G_n ⊆ G_{n+1}` [X T8].
  - For n = 1, 2 the same orders, 16 and 64, come from a direct closure on `W 3` tables (an independent computation)
    [X T8].
  - In general, the diagonal part is the lattice `{a + b = c + d}` modulo `2ⁿ`, and the permutation part has order 4
    [W].
  - The union's closure contains every `R_z(t)` on token B, `R_z(θ₀)` included (density of the dyadic angles) [W].
- **Realization on a directed tower [W].** Let `Λ_n = G_n·S_n`, for an increasing family of finite sets `S_n` of
  states with dense union in a `G_n`-invariant closed cone `K`, with readout `δ_λ ↦ λ`. Then every element of `G_n`
  is a readout-respecting permutation of `Λ_n`, and the stages are nested with the same induced maps.
- **What this shows.** The continuous half of (T) can be put at level H with finite-order, stage-preserving data
  only: (A) at each stage. The infinite order of `R_z(θ₀)` is not what blocks sourcing (T). What blocks it is (A)
  at stage 2 already: `R_z(π/2) = S` moves K(Z_F) out on either token (B4 R11, `−1/2` on token A; B8 X3, the
  flow law on both tokens for every `t` with `sin t ≠ 0`).
- **Not claimed.** That the tower sources (A): each stage's invariance of `K` is the premise. That the continuous
  and finite halves can share one tower: they cannot (B12).

## 6. The realization theorem and the certified matrix theorem at level H

- **Realization theorem (Main.md:544–558).**
  - Its own certified instance has an infinite-order rational rotation as one fixed injective step map at finite
    horizon: the 3-4-5 rotation (opglue_probes.py:9; Main.md:558, "Certified at `(d, K, |𝓕|) = (2, {2,3}, 4)`").
    Its Bloch image is a rotation about `y` with `cos = −7/25` and trace `11/25 ∉ ℤ` [X T10]. So the machinery
    supplies an isolated token's infinite-order datum at finite horizon.
  - What it does not supply is membership of `R_z(θ₀)` on token B in the *joint* family. With that membership,
    clause (4) applies `id ⊗ 𝓘_B` to the joint register. In B4 the construction's threshold step then meets the
    negative value `−2/5`, so the step map is undefined and the family is not an admissible input.
  - Under the theorem's constraints, H-T is therefore family membership (B8-3). Its matrix form is CERTIFIED
    independent of the sealed core with exact system QM and full composite unitary control (OIRealization.lean:360
    `finiteOI_not_implies_inert`; SpectatorBridge.lean:223, :233).
- **The certified matrix theorem read at level H.**
  - `substratumClass_contextStable` (StructuralClosure.lean:261) holds because the monomial class acts
    configuration-wise on the product basis: a permutation of configurations times phases, with `tensorOf 1 K`
    acting as `id × π`.
  - Read at level H as a statement about configuration bijections of a product configuration space with local
    readout, it is L-REG (NOTES-B1 §1, clauses 1–3). Phases enter as stage-crossing configuration variables.
  - Under L-REG, (W) and (P) follow from RESPECT of `π` (B1 §2), and (A) holds when all hidden distributions are
    available. So it would supply (T).
  - But L-REG is Bell-local. For any hidden space, local response functions in `[−1, 1]` give `|S(λ)| ≤ 2` pointwise,
    hence `|S| ≤ 2` after integration [W]. `phiW` is in every candidate cone and has `S = 14/5` (B1-0 [K], B1.2). So
    this reading hosts no candidate pair, `Q3` included.
  - Without local readout, (W) and (P) are no longer automatic, and the premise reverts to (A).
  - **Assumption-watch marker:** the transfer route tacitly identifies the matrix carrier's Kronecker composite with a
    product configuration space. That identification is L-REG (B1-4's marker, now for the transfer).
