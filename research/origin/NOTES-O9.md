## 2. Level one: the written argument

Notation: B = ℤ[i][1/2] (a principal ideal domain), A₊ = B, A₋ = √2·B, σ the automorphism of ℚ(ζ₈) with ζ₈ ↦ −ζ₈
(it fixes i and negates √2; A₊ and A₋ are its +1 and −1 eigensets in ℤ[ζ₈][1/2]). Products respect parity
(A₊A₊, A₋A₋ ⊆ A₊; A₊A₋ ⊆ A₋).

- **Claim 1 (shape of the class).** Every member of `MixR {π/4}` at any carrier is a · K₀ with |a| ≤ 1 and K₀ a
  contraction whose entries all lie in A₊ or all in A₋. Induction over `MixR` (StateMixingCoupling.lean:56): a scaled
  partial permutation is c · (0/1 matrix), |c| ≤ 1 (SubstratumInterfaceAudit.lean:231); `phaseGate` has entries 1, i;
  `mixImage n (π/4)` has entries 0, ±√2/2 ∈ A₋; projectors are 0/1; products multiply scalars and parities; `smul`
  multiplies the scalar (|a| ≤ 1); an ancilla block of a·K₀ is a times a block of K₀ (a compression, still a contraction);
  relabelling permutes entries [W; generators' parities X L1e].
- **Claim 2 (unitary members).** If a member K = a K₀ is an isometry, then 1 = ‖Kx‖ = |a| ‖K₀x‖ ≤ |a| ≤ 1 for unit x,
  so |a| = 1 and K₀ is unitary: conj K = conj K₀ [W].
- **Claim 3 (every available family).** For every `InstAvail` family F of the class on any carrier there is a finite
  uniform probability space of ancilla assignments ê and, for each ê, a trace-preserving Kraus family {R_{a,ê,j}} of
  matrices each in A₊ or A₋, with F_a = Σ_ê p(ê) Σ_j conj(R_{a,ê,j}). Induction over the five constructors
  (ImplementationLocality.lean:268): `op` by Claim 2; `readout` with 0/1 projectors; `coarse` takes unions; `bind` takes
  products (assignments of the continuations for every first outcome, uniformly); `discard` uses
  uniformAttach m = (1/m) Σ_e pureAttach m e (OperationalAssembly.lean:492, :515) and
  `discardWith_uniform_conjChannel` (AncillaClosure.lean:461): the new assignment adds the attached value e, the Kraus
  operators are the blocks `ancBlock R f e` (AncillaClosure.lean:457), and trace preservation for each assignment is
  the (e, e) block of Σ R†R = 1 [W].
- **Claim 4 (unitary channels).** If conj V is available (as `T.availExt n Unit (fun _ => conjChannel V)`), then
  V = u · W with |u| = 1 and W a unitary with all entries in A₊ or all in A₋. Proof: by Claim 3,
  conj V = Σ_ê p(ê) Σ_j conj(R_{ê,j}); every Kraus operator of a unitary channel is a multiple of V (if
  Σ_j R_j X R_j† = V X V† for all X, then ⟨y, R_j x⟩ = 0 whenever y ⊥ Vx, so R_j V⁻¹ is scalar); fixing one ê with its
  family trace-preserving, R_j = c_j V with Σ|c_j|² = 1. Put R′_j = R_j (parity +) or √2 R_j (parity −), all in B,
  pairwise proportional; let R′_{j₀} = g N with N primitive (entries with gcd 1 in B); then R′_j = λ_j N with λ_j ∈ B,
  N†N = ν 1 with ν ∈ ℤ[1/2] positive, and 1 = Σ_j |c_j|² = ν Σ_j ε_j |λ_j|² with ε_j ∈ {1, 1/2} and |λ_j|² ∈ ℤ[1/2]. So
  ν is a unit of ℤ[1/2]: ν = 2^k, and W = 2^{−k/2} N lies in A₊ (k even) or A₋ (k odd) [W].
- **Claim 5 (two states).** A unit column with entries in B is x/2^k with x ∈ ℤ[i]², |x₁|² + |x₂|² = 4^k; one with
  entries in A₋ is y/√2 with y ∈ B², |y|² = 2, i.e. x/2^k with |x|² = 2 · 4^k. Descent: for 4^k (k ≥ 1) the four
  integers have equal parity; all odd forces 4^k ≡ 4 (mod 8), so k = 1 and (±1)⁴; all even divides by 2. For 2 · 4^k
